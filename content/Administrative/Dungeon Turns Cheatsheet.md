# Player Reference: The Dungeon Turn 

Standard exploration happens in **10-minute blocks** called **Dungeon Turns**.

Every time you commit to a time-consuming task, the clock ticks forward.

- **Torches/Lanterns** burn down.
- **Spells** with durations expire.
- **The DM Rolls** to see if a Patrol approaches or a Complication occurs.
### What Costs 1 Dungeon Turn (10 Minutes)?

#### Exploration & Investigation
- **Search a Room:** Thoroughly tossing drawers, checking under rugs, and sifting through debris (Investigation).
- **Search for Secret Doors:** Tapping walls, measuring dimensions, and checking for hollow spots (Perception/Investigation).
- **Pick a Complex Lock:** Attempting to pick a lock carefully without breaking your tools (Thieves' Tools).
- **Disarm a Trap:** Carefully taking apart a complex mechanism.
- **Decipher Runes/Text:** Translating a paragraph of ancient text or a code (Arcana/History).
- **Loot the Bodies:** Stripping armor/weapons from enemies and organizing the loot.
- **Move Carefully Through a Room:** Carefully, deliberately, or quietly moving through an area where that is difficult to do (Stealth).

#### Magic & Rituals
- **Ritual Casting:** Casting a spell with the "Ritual" tag without spending a spell slot takes the normal casting time + 10 minutes
- **Active Scanning:** Keeping _Detect Magic_ or other spells active while moving slowly through a large area.

#### Social & Roleplay
- **Interrogation:** Getting information out of a prisoner requires more than just one question.
- **Negotiation:** Haggling with a merchant or convincing a guardian to let you pass.
- **Gathering Rumors:** Spending time mingling in a crowd (like at a Gala) to pick up gossip.

####  Rest & Recovery
- **Treat Wounds:** Using a Healer's Kit or Medicine check to stabilize or bandage a customized injury.
- **Don/Doff Armor:** Putting on Medium armor (1 min) is free/short, but putting on **Heavy Armor** takes exactly 10 minutes (1 Turn).
- **Short Rest:** A Short Rest takes 1 Hour. **Cost: 6 Dungeon Turns.**

#### Utility & Survival
- **Barricading:** Piling furniture against a door to create a safe room.
- **Foraging:** Searching the immediate area for food or water (Survival).
- **Repairing:** Fixing a broken wagon wheel, weapon, or ship engine (Tool Check).

---
### What is "Free"? (Does NOT cost a Turn)

These actions take seconds, not minutes. You can do them immediately.

- **Quick Look:** _"I glance into the room to see if anyone is there."_ (Perception).
- **Movement:** Walking down a hallway (unless you are stealthing/checking for traps every 5 feet).
- **Brief Talk:** Shouting a command or whispering a few sentences to an NPC.
- **Interaction:** Pulling a lever, opening an unlocked door, drinking a potion.
