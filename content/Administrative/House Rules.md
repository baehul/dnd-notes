The following are the rules we are going to follow for the campaign (subject to change). Any questions and you can ask me.

# System and Accepted Content

## System
We are going to use DND 2024 rules wherever possible. 
## Homebrew
Since this is my first foray into 2024, I am going to ask that we stick to only official content (basically meaning nothing that isn't something you can purchase on DND Beyond). Don't worry, there will still be lots of homebrewed magical items and other goodies but for the purpose of feeling out DND2024, I want to give it a proper try. This also means we are going to avoid too many homebrew rules but as I get more comfortable with the changes in the system, we can definitely revisit this.
## Classes and Subclasses
Any official class and subclass (yes that includes Artificers and Gunslingers) will work in this campaign!
## Spells
While most spells are acceptable, I think the following will either be modified or banned because I don't think they will fit well in this campaign. If you feel strongly about any of them, please talk to me and we can figure out a solution.

| **Spell Name**                                                                                        | **Change** | **Reason**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Any summon spell that summons large amounts of creatures                                              | Banned     | They will really slow down combat and make things less fun.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Silvery Barbs                                                                                         | Banned     | I like rolling nat 20's and so do you.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Resurrection spells other than Revivify                                                               | Banned     | I want death to feel like a real possibility and impactful. At high levels, dying is an inconvenience. If your character dies and you aren't ready to part with them, we can talk about ways for you to keep playing your character and it still has an impact on the story. |
| Teleportation Circle, Teleport, Plane Shift, and any other major teleportation spells other than Gate | Banned     | The story centers around how hard it is to move from place to place. In addition, travelling and downtime are important parts of the campaign. Teleportation breaks both of those.                                                                                           |
| Zone of Truth                                                                                         | Banned     | Make Deception Checks Great Again                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
