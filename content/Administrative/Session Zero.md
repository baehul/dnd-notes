WELCOME TO SESSION ZERO OF THE CAMPAIGN

This is going to be our campaign website and we will use to track lots of information from rules to lore and more. Looking forward to what is hopefully an amazing story and a great way to spend your Mondays!

# Tone of the Campaign
In my previous campaigns, I have found that the tone can become very heavy and serious. I have found over time that, while I enjoy a good twist, if every NPC has an underhanded motive and every narrative point is intense, it becomes difficult to inject levity into the campaign.

To that end, I think the main tones I am striving for in this campaign involves **heroism** and **ease**. 

When I say **heroism**, I mean your characters should be heroes or have the potential to eventually become heroes. They should do heroic things. That doesn't mean the characters can't be cynics or that they can't be ruthless pragmatists or even selfish bastards at time. It doesn't mean there won't be difficult choices with tradeoffs involved. What it does mean is that I want all of us to work together to avoid situations where someone comes out of a session feeling like they are a bad person **in real life** because of what happened in the session or choices they felt forced to make.

I also want the campaign to feel **easy**. Easy means different things to different players. That's alright. In fact, when I say easy, that's exactly what I'm talking about. I want people to feel free to explore role playing without feeling like saying the wrong thing will ruin the campaign. We should all trust each other to build a great story. I am also aiming for there to be lots of things that interest players - magic items crafting, base building, large narratives, lore, novel combats, etc. It's ok if some aspects don't entice you and you can't get enough of others. Finally, I want each session we do to feel like its a space you can just have fun - whether that's intense roleplay, silly scenes with NPCs, or anything else your heart desires. Don't feel shy to try it and if it isn't happening don't feel shy to tell me.

# Important Aspects of the Campaign
In some ways, this campaign is going to be very similar to the campaigns we have typically run in our group. In other ways, it will be very different. Here are some of the things that I think are going to be core aspects of the campaign.

- **Base Building and Downtime** - You guys are going to get access to a base of your own that you will have the ability to add to and build upon. Normally, when you have a base in DND, it becomes difficult to use because you are often far away from it for extended periods and you often don't have any real downtime. 
  
  In this campaign, we are going to try a different approach that will explicitly give you more downtime and time at your base - the tradeoff is that advanced travel has to take a hit. I touch on it in the [[House Rules]] but major teleportation magic is going to be banned because it makes lengthy travel obsolete. I am hoping the pay off is that you guys get the feeling that you have a cool base that you have built over the course of your campaign and is very much your own.

- **Clichés Can Be Fun** - This is going to be a campaign that leans a little into cliche and corniness. I encourage you to embrace that. If your character is serious, that's great! If your character is very silly, that's great too! This campaign will be serious at time and silly at times. The important thing is that  you feel comfortable with your character in both of those tones. I am hoping to be direct where I can be so that moments of ambiguity feel purposeful and I am going to lean on cliche to do so.

# Opening Scene

On a small [[Tectonic Island]] somewhere in the [[In-Between Isles]] [[Strata|Stratum]], we see a strange-looking tavern. Cloaked figures of various shapes and sizes disembark from three ships that have been moored on the island and hurriedly rush into the tavern lest they be spotted. # Character Creation
## Rolling For Stats
We are going to take a simple approach to generating stat arrays. Everyone will roll a stat array via the standard 4d6 drop the lowest method. From there, we will collect all the stat arrays, and all of them will be available to pick from.
## Classes and Subclasses
Any official class and subclass (yes that includes Artificers) will work in this campaign!
## Species
Any species will work though I encourage you to pick a species that would normally be considered "bad".
# Character Backstory
This section is going to cover some stuff to guide your character's narrative. Each will have some questions to consider - you don't have to answer them all, they are there to help spark your imagination (do try to answer at least one question per section though since that will help you feel more connected to everything else).

I don't expect all of this to be done by the time Session Zero starts but I would appreciate if you can come in with some ideas (at the very least think about [[#^856ac2|which group]] interests you the most)
## Personality
### General Background
Your character should generally want to be part of an adventuring party. They should also be a good-enough person. As long as both of these are true about your character, your character is good to go. If you need any seeds for inspiration:

- What is your character's favorite memory?
- What is your character's biggest regret?
- What is a silly phobia your character has?
- What is an small thing your character has an interest in?
### Setting specific background
There are some questions specific to this setting/campaign that you might find interesting to consider

- What kind of [[Tectonic Islands]] interest your character? Are there any you want to see/settle on?
- If the world could be restored to its original state, would your character do it? What would they change?
- What parts of the world has your character seen?
### Religion
There are certainly religions (check out the Religion folder for more info) that are popular throughout the world that you can feel free to pick. However, you have the freedom to create any religion you'd like for your character. You also have the freedom for your character to not be religious at all. Just make sure to talk to me about what you have imagined and we will work together to make sure it fits within the vision of the campaign.
- Is your character religious? (IT IS OK IF THE ANSWER IS NO)
- How was your character introduced to their faith? Were they born into it or did they learn about it later into life?
- Is your character's faith an optimistic or pessimistic one?
- Is your character's relationship with their religion positive?
## Groups
### Personal Affiliation

^856ac2

Your characters are each part of one of three organizations: [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult]], [[Blood Horde|The Horde]], or [[Ancient Ones|The Ancients]] (we will try to make sure each organization has at least one player unless everyone is interested in just one group). 

- How/why did your character become a member of this organization? 
- If there is another PC that is part of your organization, do you know them? What is your relationship with them?
- Are they fervent members or are they in the organization for more pragmatic reasons? 
- What are their goals for the future of the organization? 
- How does your character feel about the leaders/direction of this organization?
- Does your character mention to others that they are a part of this group?
### The Resistance
The group your character is a part of makes up one of the three branches of [[The Resistance]]. They should generally want The [[The Resistance|Resistance]] to succeed in its goals though they don't necessarily have to agree on what those goals are or what success looks like.

- What does your character think [[The Resistance]] should do?
- How does your character feel about the other groups that make up [[The Resistance]]?
- What are your character's dreams for the people of the [[In-Between Isles]] [[Strata|Stratum]]?
- Does your character mention that they are part of The [[The Resistance|Resistance]]?
### The Mission
Your character is about to be sent on a very important mission on behalf of [[The Resistance]] to rescue a member of [[The Resistance]] who is suspected to have been captured in the midst of a very important discovery that they claim reveals [[The Crusade]]'s plan.
- Does your character know about this mission before the meeting? Do they know they're about to go on it?
- Why did your character volunteer/get chosen for this mission?
- How does your character feel about the other PCs joining you on this mission?
- This mission will probably take you behind enemy lines. How does your character feel about that?
### The Crusade
In general, your characters are going to be running into characters that are part of [[The Crusade]]. They are an important force in the story and will most likely be prevalent throughout it.

- How does your character respond to seeing [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company]] ships? Have they done business with them before?
- Has your character been questioned by paladins of the [[Ecclesia Solari]]? What did they do?
- What does your character think of [[Upper Crust Houses|The Upper Houses]]?
- What does your character know/what are some assumptions they've made about the people that live in the [[Upper Crust]] [[Strata|Stratum]]?
