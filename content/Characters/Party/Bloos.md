**Flashcard / Quick Reference**

- **Visual:** A giant humanoid moth with soft, powdery wings and fuzzy antennae. Currently wearing a ridiculously oversized, half-eaten knit sweater.
    
- **Voice:** Fuzzy, and easily distracted. He occasionally emits soft insectoid clicks or a buzzing hum between words.
    
- **Mannerisms:** Erratic fluttering when startled. Constantly chewing on a stray thread of yarn. Will abruptly stop talking mid-sentence to stare vacantly at any bright or magical light source.
    
- **Want:** A safe, comfortable place to rest, more sweaters to consume, and beautiful lights to stare at.
    
- **Fear:** Sudden, loud grinding mechanical noises (due to the trauma of the Ostinato Accumulator) and absolute darkness.
    
- **Attitude:** Benignly chaotic, highly confused, and easily mesmerized.
    
- **Allies:** Anyone who provides a light source or a wool cardigan.
    
- **Enemies:** "The big loud metal monster" (The Ostinato Accumulator) that ate his home.
    
- **Capabilities:** Capable of natural flight and druidic magic, though easily distracted.
    

**Background & Current Status**

- Originally a druid, Bloos is currently cursed to live his life as a giant humanoid moth.
- His home was violently pulverized into tectonic mortar by the Ostinato Accumulator during House Cuprum's demonstration of Project Orchestra.
- He frantically fled the destruction into the void of the In-Between Isles.
- He recently crash-landed onto the party's newly acquired island, drawn in like a moth to a flame by the powerful magical lights emitting from the Lair's transformation into an arcane tower.
