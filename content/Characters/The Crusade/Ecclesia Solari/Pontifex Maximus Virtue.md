**Role:** Pontifex Maximus of [[Ecclesia Solari]], Member of Triumvirate

Takes the form of a Planetar. Virtue is the literal Voice of Solaris on the Shattered World. Pilgrims who have attended the Pontifex's sermons speak of a voice that resonates in the chest like a church bell, commanding absolute obedience and reverence. Serving as the active spiritual guide of [[the Crusade]].
