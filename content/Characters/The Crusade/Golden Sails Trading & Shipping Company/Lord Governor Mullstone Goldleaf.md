**Role:** Lord Governor of the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]], Triumvirate Member

Visionary halfling industrialist that acts as the chief executive of the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|Golden Sails Company]]. Widely considered the wealthiest individual in the world. Acts as the final authority on all logistical and financial policy for [[The Crusade]].
