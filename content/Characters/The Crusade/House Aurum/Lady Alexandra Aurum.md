**Role:** House Aurum's presence within the [[Ecclesia Solari]]

A daughter of [[House Aurum]], known publicly for her station within the
[[Ecclesia Solari]]. Such placements are common currency between the Great Houses and
the faith — the House lends its name to the church, and the church lends its good
standing back to the House.

# Family & Station
The seventh child of [[High Baron Magnus Aurum]] and Baroness Adelaide Aurum, and the
family's appointed scion of the church — a posting forced on her, which she has never
truly believed in and has always used for the House's gain rather than the faith's.

# The Wound
By House Aurum's own creed a member "who cannot fight on the front lines and bend magic
to their will is considered as much a failure as one who cannot expertly wield their
finances." Alexandra is precisely that failure — a superb abjurer with no war in her —
and she knows it. Under the Law of Three she is a seventh child: no special status,
unlike her brother Romauld (3rd, rumoured heir) or the publicly-dead ninth, Eobard.
Like all Aurum children she was subjected to the [[Resonance Crystals]] saturation
process. She was born only seconds before her twin, [[Alexander Aurum]] (eighth child,
the necromancer fixated on "pain threshold"); even her birth is a near-miss at
mattering.

# The Turn
In Session 4 the party bluffed into a charity gala by claiming to represent
[[House Aurum]] through Lady Alexandra Aurum — a connection they invented wholesale.
Alexandra heard of it. She concluded that [[High Inquisitor Varius]] had used her name
to force his way in — the church exploiting the scion it had leashed. That perceived
betrayal is why she abandoned her Ecclesia posting and returned to her family's fold,
determined to finally prove her worth to House Aurum. It is also why, in the Library,
she assumes Varius is the one hunting her. She has been wrong about her enemy ever
since: the party unknowingly caused her turn, and she still does not know they exist.
[NEW — the DM's premise, made explicit]

Her departure from the church is quiet. Publicly she is still presumed the House's
scion within the [[Ecclesia Solari]].
