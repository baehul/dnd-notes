**Role:** Ninth son of [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|Magnus Aurum]], long believed dead

The ninth son of [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|Magnus Aurum]], considered by [[House Aurum]] to be the fated scion of the House. He was said to have died in an ambush by a [[Blood Horde]] tribe before coming of age. Though once believed dead, the party discovered that he lives under the name of [[Collin McCambridge]], fighting alongside [[The Resistance]]. He told his companions so himself, and his sister [[Lady Alexandra Aurum]] knew him as Eobard. By her account, their father had told everyone that he was dead, and the wider world believes it still.
