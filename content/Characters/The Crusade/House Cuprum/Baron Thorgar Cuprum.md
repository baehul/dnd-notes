**Role:** Patriarch of [[House Cuprum]]

Thorgar is the dwarven patriarch of [[House Cuprum]]. A brilliant architect and a flawless legal scholar, but not considered a good politician. He is well-respected amongst the people of the [[Upper Crust]] and members of [[The Crusade]] outside of [[Upper Crust Houses|The Upper Houses]] who view him as a nuisance. He is considered a man with unimpeachable honor.

%% 
# DM Notes: 
Thorgar recently reviewed the massive engineering schematics for [[Project Orchestra]] submitted by his niece, [[Lady Dagna Cuprum]]. With his unparalleled architectural expertise, Thorgar realized the mathematical alignment of the islands doesn't just create a stable crust—it forms a massive, planet-wide magical firing array aimed directly at the sun.

He is quietly horrified and deeply suspicious of the logic behind the designs. Bound by his strict code of honor, he will not break his oaths to the Triumvirate without absolute proof. He is secretly compiling evidence of this catastrophic flaw and could become a powerful ally to the party if they can present him with the missing pieces regarding the Terramancers.
