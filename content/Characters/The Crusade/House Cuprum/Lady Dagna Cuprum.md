**Role:** Chief Engineer

[[Baron Thorgar Cuprum|Thorgar Cuprum]]'s eldest niece. She is responsible for most of the engineering efforts of [[The Crusade]] as a whole. She is famously pragmatic and is considered the more *flexible* member of [[House Cuprum]]. The other Great Houses of the [[Upper Crust Houses]] have been pushing for her to take on more responsibilities within [[House Cuprum]].
