**Luwei, The Whisper In The Reeds**, is an Ancient Green Dragon and a prominent member of the [[Ancient Ones]]. %%Luwei is a creature of immense age, cunning, and patience.%%He resides within the [[In-Between Isles]] on a mist-shrouded tectonic island called The Jade Fragment.

%%
Luwei’s involvement in [[The Resistance]] is driven by a singular, deeply personal obsession: the hatching of his heir.
%%
# Appearance
![[LuweiPortrait.png]]
He is a serpentine, wingless dragon. His scales are the color of moss and old jade, covered in a living layer of swamp vines and marsh grass that acts as perfect camouflage.

He is rarely seen fully; usually, he winds his massive body through the thick bamboo forests of his lair, with only his head visible. His long, tendril-like whiskers move independently, twitching to reflect his mood. His voice is described as a sibilant whisper that seems to originate from the wind in the grass rather than his mouth.

# The Jade Fragment

Luwei’s domain is a medium-sized [[Tectonic Islands|Tectonic Island]] known as **The Jade Fragment**. It is a humid, suffocating expanse of marshland and towering bamboo forests, perpetually covered in thick, magical fog.

%%
The island is covered in a unique flora known as Whisper Reeds. These plants share a hive-mind connection with Luwei who can hear every word spoken within the reeds anywhere on the island.
Luwei can mentally command the bamboo and reeds to shift, closing paths or opening new ones. He uses this to separate intruders, leading them into ambushes or trapping them in endless loops until they die of exposure.

# The Unhatched Egg

Luwei guards a single, massive dragon egg—the last clutch laid before [[The Shattering]]. The egg has remained in stasis for 150 years. Luwei believes that the egg possesses a divine sensitivity; it refuses to hatch into a broken, unstable world.
%%

%%
## Motivation & The Crusade

Luwei’s primary goal is to find or create a region of geological and magical stability profound enough to coax the egg to hatch.

Recently, Luwei has gathered intelligence suggesting that [[The Crusade]]—specifically the engineering division led by [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]]—is developing technology capable of forcibly reuniting the [[Tectonic Islands]] of the [[Upper Crust]] to seal the "imperfect" world below a solid shell.

While [[The Resistance]] views this as a doomsday scenario, Luwei views it with calculated opportunity. He has not shared this with his allies, but he is investigating whether this Crusade technology could be co-opted to create a perfect, stable paradise for his heir, regardless of the cost to the rest of the [[In-Between Isles]].

# Minions and Servants

The Jade Fragment is tended by a fanatic hierarchy of servants made up of green dragonborn/kobolds and grung who revere Luwei as a god-emperor.

- **The Tenders:** These servants are responsible for the physical care of the egg and the maintenance of the bamboo groves. They are forbidden from speaking above a whisper.
- **The Ambushers:** Servants who patrol the perimeter of the swamp. They utilize the shifting paths created by Luwei to strike intruders from the mist before vanishing.
