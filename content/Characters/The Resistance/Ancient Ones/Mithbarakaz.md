**Mithbarakaz, The Silvered Blizzard** is an ancient White Dragon who survived [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]] and now acts as one of the primary financiers of [[The Resistance]]. He resides in the [[In-Between Isles]] on a [[Tectonic Islands|Tectonic Island]] made of ice and silver called The Silver Moon. He is renowned for his hospitality.
# Appearance
![[MithbarakazPortrait.png]]
Despite being a White Dragon, Mithbarakaz's scales are not the matte white color commonly found amongst his relatives; rather, his scales are a shimmering, iridescent ivory like polished silver. Having lived for centuries amidst the silver-laced peaks of his lunar lair, his hide has integrated the mineral, giving him a natural armor that is both beautiful and terrifyingly resilient.
# Personality
Mithbarakaz is renowned for his hospitality. He is unfailingly polite, believing that civil discourse is the highest form of sapience.
%%
Beneath his polite exterior, he is always calculating how to increase his wealth. Everything is a calculation as to whether it will ultimately increase or decrease his wealth
%%
He acts as the primary supplier of money (in the form of silver taken from his island) and magical goods to [[The Resistance]].

# Silver Moon
**Silver Moon**, Mithbarakaz's home, got its name from its unusual round shape. The weight of the island, along with its icy composition, led to the island to collapse in on itself due to its own gravity during [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]]. Silver Moon itself has several [[Tectonic Islands]] that closely orbit it. That, along with its unusual shape, make Silver Moon incredibly difficult to access.
