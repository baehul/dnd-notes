**Broomhilda Bonebelly** is a master biomancer for [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult]] and a important figure within [[The Resistance]]. A Duergar of immense intellect and intimidating physical presence, she is the primary advocate for arguing that [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult]] should abandon the war effort and retreat into the deep [[Underworld]] to safeguard their knowledge.
# Appearance
![[BroomhildaBonebellyPortrait.png]]
 Broomhilda is a walking testament to her own science. While she was born a Duergar, years of radical self-experimentation have turned her into a fearsome amalgam of subterranean biology.
# Personality

Broomhilda is defined by pragmatism. She views [[The Crusade]] not as an enemy to be defeated, but as a natural disaster to be weathered. Her priority is the survival of [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult]] and the preservation of its accumulated knowledge. Despite her grotesque appearance and gruff demeanor, she is fiercely maternal toward her fellow members of [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult]]. She views the scholars, especially idealists like [[Professor Jitterhop]], as fragile things that need to be protected from their own bravery. 
## The First Subject
Broomhilda follows a strict code of bioethics: "The Creator is the First Subject." She refuses to perform an experiment on another living being that she has not first tested on herself. She views scientists who risk the lives of others without risking their own as cowards and amateurs.

# Role in The Resistance
While she publicly argues for retreat, Broomhilda is loyal to a fault. She will not abandon [[The Cult]] as long as they remain. She is also the person who provides the contact for [[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe|Captain Arabella]] since she sources things from her on occasion.
