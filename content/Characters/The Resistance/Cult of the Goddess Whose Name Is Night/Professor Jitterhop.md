**Role:** Lead Artificer, Dean of Research and Education

A frantic Deep Gnome often covered in strange stains, burns, and chalk. Leads [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|the Cult]]'s faction of scientists as its lead scholar. Head of education and scholarly studies at [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|the Cult]]. Maintains that [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|the Cult]] should have a policy of neutrality as neither [[The Goddess Whose Name Is Night]] nor science takes sides.
