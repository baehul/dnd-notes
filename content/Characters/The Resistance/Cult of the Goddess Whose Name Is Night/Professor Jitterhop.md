**Professor Jitterhop** is the Lead Artificer of the [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night]]. A Deep Gnome of frantic energy and boundless intellect, he serves as the head of education at the [[Monastery of Night]].

While he supplies [[The Resistance]] with advanced technology, Jitterhop is not a revolutionary. He is a pure academic who views the war as a distraction from the pursuit of knowledge.
# Appearance
![[ProfessorJitterhopPortrait 1.png]]
Jitterhop is a Deep Gnome with slate-grey skin, typically hidden beneath a heavy leather apron and a lab coat covered in strange stains, burns, and chalk dust.

# Personality

Jitterhop maintains a policy of absolute scientific neutrality. He rejects the tribalism of the war, viewing politics as "small thinking." Jitterhop provides weapons and tech to [[The Resistance]]%% and [[The Crusade]]%%. He views this funding as a necessary evil; if he didn't supply them, someone less scrupulous would. By controlling the supply, he ensures the profits fund the Cult's "true research."

Despite his frantic demeanor, Jitterhop is an incredibly supportive and wise teacher. He defends his students' work fiercely, encourages them to question everything (including him), and provides sage, unbiased life advice. He teaches that a good scientist must be able to entertain a thought without accepting it.

# Current Research: Project Genesis

Jitterhop’s current field of study is Artificial Tectonic Genesis.

He believes the housing crisis of the [[Strata]], where billions fight over limited land, can be solved by manufacturing new [[Tectonic Islands]].

To understand how to create land, he must understand how it breaks. Jitterhop is currently conducting controlled demolitions of islands. By exploding these islands and observing how the [[Resonance Crystals]] and [[Ley Lines]] react, he hopes to reverse-engineer the binding process.
