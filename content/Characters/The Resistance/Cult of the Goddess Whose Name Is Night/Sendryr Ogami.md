**Role:** Cleric of [[The Goddess Whose Name Is Night]]

**Sendryr Ogami** is a Drow cleric who wears a permanent silk blindfold and leads the religious wing of [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|the Cult]] of Night. She speaks almost exclusively in shifting verses from the [[Way Of Night (Book)|Way Of Night]], serving as [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|the Cult]]'s spiritual anchor. It is rare to get a straight answer from her but she is the biggest voice of support for [[The Resistance]] within [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|the Cult]].
# Portrait
![[SendryrOgamiPortait.png]]
Sendryr is a Drow of ethereal grace and beuty. She is always seen wearing a silk blindfold and her robes which are enchanted to look like moving stars.

# Personality
Sendryr speaks in an ethereal, soft tone, often answering questions with another question to force the listener to find their own truth. She is deeply religious, quoting verses from the [[Way Of Night (Book)|Way Of Night]] to contextualize the struggles of the war. 

Unlike the isolationists in the Cult, Sendryr cannot abide the suffering of the [[In-Between Isles]]. She views the pain of the common people not as a statistic, but as a spiritual wound on the world. This compassion drives her to push the assembly to support [[The Resistance]] more than they already do.

# Current Activities: The Pilgrimage
Sendryr is rarely found sitting still at the [[Monastery of Night]]. She spends much of her time on dangerous "Pilgrimages" across the [[In-Between Isles]].

She travels to the front lines where the [[Blood Horde]] clashes with [[The Crusade]], using her formidable divine magic to heal the wounded regardless of their faction. She spreads the [[Way Of Night (Religion)|Way Of Night]] along the way.
