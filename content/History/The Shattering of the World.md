The **Shattering of the World**,  also known simply as **The Shattering**, was a cataclysmic event that occurred 150 years ago in which the known world shattered into thousands of pieces known as [[Tectonic Islands]] and reformed into [[Strata|strata]] over the course of three days. Most extant knowledge of the event derives from disparate eyewitness accounts compiled in the century following the catastrophe. Though reports differ significantly as to the events that occurred and the causes of those events, several consistent features have been identified.

# Chronology
##  The First Day
On the first day of the Shattering, small fissures began to appear on the world's surface. Early eyewitness accounts describe as approximately 0.5 inches wide, leading many arcanists and scholars to initially question their significance .Scholarly consensus at the time held that the Shattering occurred over two days; however, later analyses suggest that along major [[Ley Lines|ley lines]], fissures measured up to 5 feet in width. This evidence has led many to accept the three-day model of the Shattering.

No comparable fissures were reported in the seafloor of oceanic regions. Instead, witnesses from [[aquatic civilizations]] observed unusual behavior amongst deep-sear creatures, which rose towards the surface in significant numbers. This phenomenon remains a subject of debate among contemporary researchers and is widely cited by proponents of the Inside-Out and Hybrid
hypothesis, who argue that this indicates disturbance originating within the world's interior.
## The Second Day
The second day of the Shattering marked the first visible and large-scale fragmentation of the world. Whereas the preceding day was marked with involved superficial fissures and underwater disturbances, contemporary scholars broadly agree that the second day involved the physical separation of continental landmasses in addition to the [[Strata|stratification of the world]].  Reports from multiple regions describe large flashes of white light accompanied by unprecedented levels of seismic activity as vast sections of terrain broke apart.

![[TheShatteringDay2Early.png]]

Many eyewitness records suggest that these fragmentations occurred along existing [[Ley Lines]] though this interpretation is widely contested amongst arcanologists. The landmasses began to rapidly drift from one another while previously molten subterranean [[Strata]] were exposed and rapidly cooled. Scholars disagree as to whether this rapid cooling caused further fragmentation of the lower layers or if the same forces responsible for the upper fractures had already affected the deeper [[Strata]] before cooling occurred.

## The Third Day
The third day of the Shattering is widely regarded as the most devastating phase of the event. Following the initial ruptures and separation of major landmasses during the second day, the fragmented continental bodies entered into unstable orbit around the planetary core. Contemporary accounts and later arcano-geological reconstructions indicate that many of these fragments collided with one another repeatedly over the course of several hours.

These collisions resulted in the further fragmentation of the continental plates into smaller structures now known as [[Tectonic Islands]]. The process caused immense structural damage to the already destabilized landscape and is believed to have been accompanied by extreme arcane and seismic activity.

# Calendar
The cataclysmic nature of The Shattering caused a fundamental shift in how people approached the calendar. In the immediate aftermath, people began to reference all events - both personal and historical - by when they happened in reference to The Shattering itself. Over the next decades, this usage became so prevalent that it coalesced into the standard method of referencing dates. Official record-keepers, historians, and scholars codified this system and officially adopted The Shattering Calendar system which designated the third day of the event as Day Zero of Year Zero.

The resulting standardized system uses two primary eras, reflecting the cultural obsession with the divide:
- **Before Fragmentation (B.F.):** All years preceding The Shattering.
- **Era of the Isles (E.I.):** All years following the event. The current year in most contemporary usage is **150 E.I.**
