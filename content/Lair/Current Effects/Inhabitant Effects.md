## Bloos

*A four-foot-tall, fuzzy humanoid moth whose two centuries of age are evident in his frayed antennae and wings. He flutters quietly around the base, looking to sate his insatiable appetite for the crew's finest woolens, drawn to the beautiful lights his massive compound eyes are uniquely capable of seeing.*

**Prismatic Perception.** Bloos's hypersensitive compound eyes can perceive magic, auras, and other phenomenon invisible to most others. 

**That Is GORGEOUS.** Bloos becomes entirely entranced and will abandon all current tasks to reverently flutter towards any beautiful lights he can see.

**You Have Any Spare Sweaters?** Anyone who leaves a sweater or woolen garment unattended within his vicinity does so at their own risk.
