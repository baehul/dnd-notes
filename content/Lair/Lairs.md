**Lairs** are powerful, symbiotic magical structures, such as a Witch's Hut or an Archmage's tower, that bond directly with their patrons. Rather than being simple constructed buildings, these structures possess an inherent magical presence that allows them to interact with both the environment and their caretakers on a fundamental level.

## Symbiosis and Growth

A Lair is not a static object; it is a reactive entity that relies on the protection and guidance of its patrons. It forms a symbiotic bond with its caretakers. As the patrons of a Lair grow in strength, knowledge, and capability, the Lair mirrors this progression, unlocking new structural capabilities, defensive wards, and utility.

Because of this deep connection, a Lair's physical form is rarely permanent. Its architecture, internal layout, and outward appearance are highly mutable, shifting to accommodate the needs, aesthetics, and collective will of its current masters.

## Navigation and The Ley Lines

Before [[The Shattering of the World]], Lairs possessed the ability to navigate the world for their patrons by following the unbroken threads of fate and magic that wrapped the globe. They could move autonomously, traversing the ancient landscape with ease.

Following [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]], the severance of the [[Ley Lines]] rendered this innate navigational ability inert. Lairs became stranded on their respective [[Tectonic Islands]], unable to interface with the chaotic, broken magical currents of the void.

### The Harmony Engine

Devices like the [[Harmony Engine]] can act as a specialized conduit to restore a Lair's mobility. By connecting the [[Harmony Engine]] to a Lair, the structure is able to tap into the raw magic and energy of the fractured [[Ley Lines]] once more. However, because the lines remain physically and magically broken, the translation is imperfect, making this navigation unpredictable and challenging compared to the pre-Shattering era.
