**Hydrospheres** are massive, spherical bodies of liquid water that drift through the Void amongst the [[Tectonic Islands]]. Ranging in size from small, floating lakes to massive, moon-sized oceans, these spheres serve as the primary habitat for the world's remaining aquatic civilizations following [[The Shattering of the World]].

# Hydro-resonance
The existence of Hydrospheres relies on a geo-arcane phenomenon known as Hydro-resonance. During [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]], vast quantities of [[Resonance Crystals]] were pulverized and dissolved into the world's oceans. This high concentration of magical particulate caused the water to coalesce into spheres. The dissolved crystals allow these spheres to "grip" the broken [[Ley Lines]], holding them in stable, drifting orbits within the [[Strata]].

# Boundary
## The Membrane
The most defining feature of a Hydrosphere is its outer boundary. The magical cohesion creates a surface tension of immense power, forming a "Membrane" that acts nearly as solid as glass. This barrier retains atmosphere and heat within the sphere but makes entering or exiting the water physically difficult.

## The Breach
Travel between in and out of a Hydrosphere is highly dangerous and requires specialized technology. Standard Weavesilk ships cannot enter a sphere; they would simply bounce off the Membrane or shatter their hulls against the surface tension. Entry requires specialized vehicles which are fully enclosed and reinforced.

# Aquatic Civilizations
Due to the difficulty of breaching the Membrane, the civilizations within Hydrospheres are profoundly isolationist. For 150 years, many have had no contact with the "outer" world.
