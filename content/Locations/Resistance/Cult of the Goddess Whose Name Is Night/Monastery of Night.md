**The Monastery of Night** servers as the headquarters for the [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night]]. Hidden deep within the twilight of the In-Between Isles, it operates less like a traditional religious temple and more like a university.

## Appearance and Atmosphere

![[MonasteryOfNightImage.png]]

The campus utilizes both the surface of a shadowed tectonic island and the hollowed-out caverns beneath it. Jagged obsidian spires reach into the perpetual twilight, while bridges woven from giant spider silk and iron span bottomless chasms below. Laboratories are carved into natural stalactites or the fossilized ribs of ancient behemoths.

The campus is illuminated by a chaotic, patchwork array of light sources. Bioluminescent fungi cling to the cavern walls, enchanted drifting orbs of cold fire patrol the pathways, and thousands of flickering, melting candles are clustered on desks and altars.

The daily life of the Monastery is entirely academic. Students and professors passionately argue over thesis papers, arcane theory, and theology as they walk the grounds.

## The Fluid Spaces Rule
In direct adherence to the *[[Way Of Night (Book)|Way Of Night]]*, which rejects absolute truth and rigid structure, the campus has no permanently prescribed rooms. A humid greenhouse filled with carnivorous plants for biomancy might be used as a meditation chamber by priests looking to deepen their connection with nature the following day.

This philosophy creates constant friction among the inhabitants. Devout followers of the *[[Way Of Night (Book)|Way Of Night]]* freely abandon and swap workspaces as their studies demand. However, researchers conducting highly volatile or precise experiments become fiercely territorial, aggressively defending their "unclaimed" labs to protect the integrity of their work.

## The Forum
The political and intellectual heart of the Monastery is a massive, tiered cavern simply known as **The Forum**. Because [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult]] operates on a democratic assembly model without a centralized leader, this is has become the primary venue where [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult]] decides on its course of action with [[The Resistance]].

Anyone from the most senior master to the newest initiate can take the floor to debate philosophy, propose resistance operations, or defend a scientific theory. In keeping with the commandments of the [[The Goddess Whose Name Is Night]], debates are a complete free-for-all. There is no designated speaker or judge; the crowd itself moderates the discussion.
