**Tectonic Islands** are the fractured, floating landmasses that constitute the habitable surface of the world following [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]]. Suspended in the void around the planetary core, these islands function as self-contained ecosystems. 

While they vary wildly in size from small, barren rocks to continent-sized plates, they share unique magical and physical properties between them.

# Orbital Mechanics and Stability
The movement of Tectonic Islands is not governed purely by gravity, but by the interaction between the world's fragmented crust and its broken [[Ley Lines]]. The Tectonic Islands do not flow along the broken [[ley lines]]; instead, they are held in relatively stable orbits by the [[ley lines]], which act as invisible magical tethers. These lines prevent the islands from spiraling into the core or drifting into the sun.

While anchored, the islands possess a natural, slow "drift." This movement is usually imperceptible to inhabitants on the surface. However, over the course of months, an island's neighbors may change. Occasionally, the drifting orbits of two islands will intersect. These events are rarely catastrophic. For the inhabitants, they often feel like an earthquake - enough to knock a person off their feet or rattle shelves, and sometimes strong enough to topple buildings.
# The Edge
The perimeter of a Tectonic Island is known as The Edge. It is a hard boundary where the rock ends and the void begins. 

Physics at the Edge behaves strangely due to the clash between the island's gravity and the vacuum of the void. Water that flows off the side of an island does not fall endlessly down into the [[Underworld]]. Instead, upon passing the threshold of the rock, the water instantly atomizes into a dense, magical mist. This mist rises along the sides of the island, forming a ring of clouds that eventually drift inward and rain the water back down.
# The Underside
The underside of a Tectonic Island is considered a "Dead Zone."

The magic that keeps the islands upright creates a chaotic gravitational shear beneath the rock. Gravity does not wrap around the island; "down" is always toward the planetary core. Because of this, and the exposure to raw, unfiltered magical radiation from the broken [[ley lines]] below, the Underside is uninhabitable. Structures built there are quickly torn apart by conflicting physical forces, and valuable minerals are rarely exposed, meaning there is no economic incentive for mining operations to dangle into the void.
# Isolation
Because each Tectonic Island is physically separated from the rest of the world by the Void, they effectively function as micro-worlds. It is common for two neighboring islands to have vastly different cultures, laws, and ecosystems. One island might be a dense jungle ruled by a tribal conclave, while the island floating a mile away is an arid desert plateau ruled by a wizard.

# Island Grading System

The **Island Grading System** is the standard cartographic metric used to categorize the habitable surface area of a Tectonic Island.

The scale ranges from Grade 0 (micro-islands suitable for single structures) to Grade 3 (massive landmasses capable of supporting a kingdom), which represents the largest stable landmasses found in the post-Shattering world.
