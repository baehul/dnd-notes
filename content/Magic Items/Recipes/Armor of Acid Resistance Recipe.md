 When successfully executed, this recipe results in [Armor of Resistance (Acid)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/5371-armor-of-resistance).
%% Treat this as and uncommon major item due to niche effect %%
## Components
- [x] 1 Ooze Core
- [ ] 12 Levels of Abjuration Magic
- [ ] 200 GP of alkaline salts, glass dust, and sealant oils
- [ ] 50 GP lead-lined basin
- [ ] One set of armor (leather or metal) 
## Ritual
### Infuse Enchantment
- [ ] **1. Channel the Ward:** Cast Abjuration magic into mixture of sealant oils and glass dust  
### Prepare Armor
- [ ]  **1. Neutralize the Core:** Place the Ooze Core into a lead-lined basin and cover it with the alkaline salts (Reagents).
- [ ] **2. Create the Gel (8 hours):** Slowly heat the basin over a low flame while stirring until the core creates a thick, translucent gel.
- [ ] **3. Coat the Armor (8 hours):** Coat the armor in the gel. Lacquer enchanted glass dust and sealing oils mixture onto of gel coating before it sets.
- [ ] **4. Polish Armor (8 hours):** Buff gel and enchanted mixture into armor using Leatherworker's (DEX) or Smith's (STR) tools until armor is slick (DC 14).
