When successfully executed, this recipe results in a [Instrument of Illusions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228786-instrument-of-illusions)

## Components
- [ ] 1 Spool of Phase Spider Silk
- [ ] 5 Levels of Illusion Magic
- [ ] 35 GP of quartz dust
- [ ] 5 GP of a clear, non-sticky oil
- [ ] A string instrument without strings in-place

## Ritual
- [ ] **1. Treat the Phase Spider Silk:** Briefly dip the silk into the clear oil so that the coil coats all the silk.
- [ ] **2. String the Instrument:** Inlay the silk into the string instruments frame
- [ ] **3. Channel the Enchantment:** While tuning the instrument, channel Illusion Magic into the instrument.
- [ ] **4. Seal the Enchantment:** Seal the enchantment into the instrument by coating the tuned and enchanted silk with the quartz dust.
