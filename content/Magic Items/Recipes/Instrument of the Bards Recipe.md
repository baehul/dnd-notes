When successfully executed, this recipe turns any stringed instrument into an [Instrument of the Bards](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/4657-instrument-of-the-bards). Whatever the instrument's form — lute, guitar, lyre, fiddle — the result uses the **Doss Lute** statblock.

*The strings are drawn from a Darkweaver's web and never quite catch the light. Plucked, they throw no shadow of their own.*

## Components
- [ ] 1 Bolt of Darkweaver Web (Aberration, CR 10 — Harvest DC 20, Arcana)
- [ ] 12 Levels of Abjuration Magic
- [ ] 175 GP of amber resin and warding varnish
- [ ] 50 GP of gold leaf
- [ ] One finished stringed instrument of good make

## Ritual
- [ ] **1. Reel the Web (8 Hours):** Draw and comb the web into even strands, one for each the instrument carries, working in full darkness. Any light on the raw web makes it brittle and it will part under tension.
- [ ] **2. Cure the Strands (4 Hours):** Draw each strand through warm amber resin until it holds its shape in lamplight. Uncured web slips back into shadow the moment it is plucked.
- [ ] **3. String the Instrument (4 Hours):** Strip the old strings and rehang the cured strands, tuning them up slowly over several hours so the neck settles without warping.
- [ ] **4. Channel the Ward (6 Hours):** Hold a single sustained chord and channel the Abjuration Magic into the strings until the sound outlasts the playing by a breath.
- [ ] **5. Seal the Rosette (2 Hours):** Lay gold leaf around the soundhole with Woodcarver's Tools to fix the enchantment in the body (DC 14). Left unsealed, the strings unravel back to shadow within a tenday.
