When successfully executed, this recipe fits the [[Harmony Engine]] with the **Keystone Lens**, a Lair addition that lets the Engine take in a key and spend it to steer the [[Lairs|Lair]] through the void to a place no one aboard has stood.

*An eye from the deep, set in cold brine among the Engine's crystal gears, open and unblinking. When a key is offered, it turns to look.*

%% Lair addition: a plot item excluded from the distribution, like the Harmony Engine itself. Costed on the Category G (Major Rare) Permanent row. DM default (2026-10-10), unlike the Crafting System rule: a natural 1 spares the eye; the reagents are lost. %%

## Components
- [ ] 1 eye of [[The Six-Eyed Tentacle of the Deep Icy Waters]]
- [ ] 26 Levels of Divination Magic
- [ ] 2,000 GP of cut Resonance Crystal and gold-alloy filigree
- [ ] 500 GP of cold deep-water brine and sealing wax

## Ritual
- [ ] **1. Open the Sphere (1 Day):** Part the Engine's seamless shell along its etched staves with Tinker's Tools. The seams exist only where the music is written.
- [ ] **2. Cut the Socket (2 Days):** Facet a cradle for the eye among the crystal gears without stopping them. A gear that stops will not start again on its own.
- [ ] **3. Etch the Iris (1 Day):** Cut containment runes in a ring around the socket, each one aligned to a stave on the shell above it.
- [ ] **4. Seat the Eye (2 Days):** Fill the socket with the cold brine, set the eye in it, and channel the Divination Magic until it opens. If the brine is allowed to warm, the eye clouds.
- [ ] **5. Settle the Hum (1 Day):** Hold the Engine's chord until the hum settles and the eye turns toward the open key aperture, then seal the shell with the wax (DC 17).

## Keys
Once fitted with the Keystone Lens, the Harmony Engine can take in a key, an object holding the way to a single place, and spend it to steer the Lair through the void to a place no one aboard has stood. A key is used up in the reading.
