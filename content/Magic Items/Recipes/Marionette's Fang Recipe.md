When successfully executed, this recipe results in a **Marionette's Fang**.

*A curved, translucent tooth bound in silver wire and mounted on a stickpin, worn at the throat. The fang always points inward, toward the wearer.*

> **Marionette's Fang**
> *Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a creature that can manifest Psychic Blades)*
>
> **Puppet's Thread.** While you are attuned to this pin, Psychic Blades you manifest count as a spellcasting focus for you, but only for a spell that requires an attack with the weapon used as the focus.

## Components
- [ ] 1 Tooth of a Puppeteer Parasite
- [ ] 8 Levels of Divination Magic
- [ ] 150 GP of powdered moonstone and drawn silver wire
- [ ] 50 GP of nerve-quieting oil (sweet oil cut with distilled valerian)
- [ ] 1 plain silver stickpin

## Ritual
- [ ] **1. Drown the Rider (2 Hours):** Submerge the tooth in the nerve-quieting oil until the root stops twitching. The parasite's will survives in the fang and will ride the wearer instead if this step is skipped.
- [ ] **2. Etch the Root (2 Hours):** Deepen the tooth's natural feeding grooves into fine channels with Jeweler's Tools, running root to tip.
- [ ] **3. Channel the Foresight (2 Hours):** Pack the channels with powdered moonstone and channel the Divination Magic into the fang until it leans of its own accord toward whatever the crafter looks at.
- [ ] **4. Set the Fang (1 Hour):** Bind the fang in silver wire and mount it on the stickpin, point turned inward, so the tooth is always aimed at the wearer's throat.
- [ ] **5. Seal the Thread (1 Hour):** Manifest a psychic blade and hold its edge against the set fang until the moonstone dims (DC 13). The puppetry runs backward from here on — the mind moves the blade, not the blade the mind.
