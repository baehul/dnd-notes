When successfully executed, this recipe results in a [Pearl of Power](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/4691-pearl-of-power).

*An ordinary pearl whose nacre has drunk in [[Resonance Crystals|Resonance Crystal]] sand. Held to a lamp, the iridescence sits deeper than the surface, somewhere down among the rings.*

%% Category F (Major Uncommon) — Uncommon rarity that effectively grants an extra spell each day, up to 3rd level. Charged: the pearl returns at dawn.

This is a variant [[Resonance Crystals|Tuning]], and it skips Faceting entirely — a pearl cannot be cut without destroying it, so the nacre's own growth rings stand in for the facets. Any crafter who has tuned a crystal before should get advantage on the final check. %%

## Components
- [ ] 1 measure of Resonance Crystal sand, salvaged from the shell that held [[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn|Sylas]]
- [ ] 1 pearl (100 GP)
- [ ] 12 Levels of Transmutation Magic
- [ ] 200 GP of powdered nacre and silver salts
- [ ] 25 GP of sea-salt brine

## Ritual
- [ ] **1. Read the Rings (2 Hours):** Hold the pearl against a strong lamp and map its growth rings. These stand in for facets, and every rune cut in the next step must follow one.
- [ ] **2. Etch the Nacre (6 Hours):** Score the containment runes shallowly along the mapped rings with Jeweler's Tools. Cutting through the nacre spends the pearl.
- [ ] **3. Draw in the Sand (12 Hours):** Stir the crystal sand into the sea-salt brine, suspend the pearl in it, and channel the Transmutation Magic in slow, even doses so the nacre draws the sand down into its rings, letting each dose settle before the next. Fed too fast, the sand fractures inside the pearl and it clouds from the inside out.
- [ ] **4. Seal the Runes (4 Hours):** Pack the etched runes with powdered nacre and silver salts, then burnish the surface until the runes vanish under a fresh layer and the pearl reads as unworked (DC 14).
