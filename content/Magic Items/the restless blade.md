*A long blade of dark, pitted steel, grown over with bone-pale ridges and a tangle of living swamp vines. Moss weeps from the crossguard, and the steel is never quite cold to the touch. When a fight draws near, the vines tighten around the grip, and it has opinions...*
***

*Weapon (Greatsword or Greataxe), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Cursed, Sentient*

**Bound Edge.** You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this weapon.

**Quarry.** When initiative is rolled, the spirit within the blade names aloud the foe it judges most worthy. Against that foe, the weapon's bonus is +2 instead of +1. When the Quarry drops to 0 Hit Points, the spirit names a new Quarry at the start of your next turn if a worthy foe remains; otherwise there is none.

**Reshape.** During a Long Rest, you can spend 1 hour holding the weapon while attuned to it. At the end of the rest, it becomes a Greatsword or a Greataxe (your choice). It takes the new form's damage die, properties and mastery property. Your attunement is unbroken, and everything else about the weapon, its curse and its spirit carries over.

**A Restless Spirit.** The blade is sentient (Chaotic Neutral; Intelligence 10, Wisdom 8, Charisma 14). It has hearing and normal vision out to 60 feet. It speaks aloud in the languages its wielder knows, but only while held. Its purpose is a glorious death.

**Curse.** This weapon is cursed, and attuning to it extends the curse to you.
- *Unwilling.* While attuned, you are unwilling to part with the weapon and keep it within your reach. You can't voluntarily end your attunement to it.
- *Glory's Pull.* Whenever a hostile creature damages you while you are Bloodied (including the hit that makes you Bloodied), you must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, on your next turn you must move toward the Quarry and, if able, take the Attack action against it. If there is no Quarry, nothing happens.
- *Scorn.* If you take the Disengage, Dodge or Help action, the weapon's bonus becomes +0, including against the Quarry, until the end of your next turn.

A *Remove Curse* spell ends your attunement, as it would for any cursed item. It does not lay the spirit to rest.
