**Ley Lines** (also sometimes called **The Weave**) are the visible, semi-corporeal bands of raw magical energy that permeate the void between the [[Tectonic Islands]]. Before [[The Shattering of the World]], these lines were an invisible, cohesive network that connected every point of the world to another and permeated the world with magic, facilitating instant travel and communication.
![[LeyLinesImage.png]]
# Physical Manifestation
Unlike in the Pre-Shattering era, modern Ley Lines are vividly visible to the naked eye. They appear as vast, luminous ribbons cutting through the darkness of the void. 

The most unsettling characteristic of the Ley Lines is their sound. They generate a constant, low-level psychic resonance that is perceived differently by every individual. To some, it sounds like complex, distant orchestral music; to others, it is the whispering voices of the dead. A common superstition among sailors is that the sounds are not random, but are auditory visions of the future. It is said that those who listen too closely may learn their fate, though they risk madness in the process.

# Magical Barriers
When scholars refer to the Ley Lines being "broken," they are referring to a metaphysical severance rather than a physical snapping of the energy. 

The primary manifestation of this break is the total failure of teleportation and long-range communication magic. Spells that attempt to bridge the gap between islands instantly fail or rebound unpredictably, as the path between "here" and "there" no longer exists in a straight line. This fracture means that despite being able to see a neighboring island, the inhabitants are magically isolated.
# Interaction and Manipulation
Direct interaction with a Ley Line is nearly impossible for an individual spellcaster. The raw energy is too vast and chaotic to be channeled through a mortal body or standard arcane focus. Safe interaction requires the use of highly specialized [[Resonance Crystals]]. These rare gems must be exposed to and uniquely tuned to the specific frequency of the Weave.
