The **Ostinato Accumulator** is a massive industrial arcanotech engine developed by [[House Cuprum]], led by [[Lady Dagna Cuprum]], along with the advisement of [[Professor Jitterhop]] of [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult of Night]]. It is the primary device contracted by [[The Crusade]] to execute [[Project Orchestra]].

# Description and Function

Unlike the delicate magic of the [[Harmony Engine]], which utilizes the [[Ley Lines]] to carefully move [[Tectonic Islands]], the Ostinato Accumulator is designed to rapidly fuse [[Tectonic Islands]] together.

The machine operates by seizing smaller, neighboring islands and systematically pulverizing them. The device grinds the rock down into a slurry of raw earth and fragmented [[Resonance Crystals]] which is then infused with [[The Terramancers|Terramancy]] magic. This highly volatile substance is then used as a magical "tectonic mortar" to glue the [[Tectonic Islands]] together, acting much like mortar between bricks in a wall. 
# The Showcase 

The Ostinato Accumulator was officially awarded the contract for [[Project Orchestra]] the day following the [[House Aurum]] Charity Gala. The competing prototype, the [[Harmony Engine]], designed by [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]], was still unfinished and unready for demonstration at the showcase. [[Lady Dagna Cuprum|Lady Dagna]]and [[Professor Jitterhop]] presented their device unopposed, securing the contract and the future of [[The Crusade|The Crusade's]]  efforts.

# DM Secrets
## The Terramancer Connection

The design of the Ostinato Accumulator was secretly guided and heavily influenced by **The Catalyst Imperative**, the extremist faction of the surviving [[The Terramancers|Terramancers]]. They manipulated Lady Dagna and Jitterhop into building this device because its violent, rapid fusion of the crust will expedite their own goal.
