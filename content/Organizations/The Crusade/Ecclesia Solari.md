The **Ecclesia Solari**, sometimes simply called **The Church**, is the powerful theocratic institution dedicated to the worship of [[Solaris and The Solari Faith]]. Led by the Pontifex Maximus, currently a planetar named [[Pontifex Maximus Virtue|Virtue]], the Church provides the ideological mandate and spiritual authority that underpins [[The Crusade]].

# Demographics
The Church accepts almost anyone with open arms. Many of their upper ranks are beings who have ascended by the blessing of [[Solaris and The Solari Faith|Solaris]] to more powerful forms such as celestials. However, their priests tend to come from species that are blessed with divine favor such as:
- Metallic Dragonborn
- Aasimar
- Kalashtar

# Leadership
The Church is ruled by the Pontifex Maximus, currently a planetar name [[Pontifex Maximus Virtue|Virtue]], who acts as the voice of [[Solaris and The Solari Faith]] on this world. The Church maintains a dual structure of administration and militant enforcement. 

The Paladins Templar are the Church's feared militant arm and investigative force. Composed of highly trained holy warriors and inquisitors, the Paladins Templar are responsible for hunting down dark magic users, tracking suspected blasphemers, and maintaining the faith within Solari territories. 

Day-to-day administration, education, record-keeping, and the study of divine magic are handled by the vast ranks of monks and priests. They administer the Church's enormous wealth and maintain the intellectual foundation of the faith.

# Political Ideology
The Ecclesia Solari is the only member of [[The Crusade]] unified by absolute, zealous faith. Their ideology is simple: [[Solaris and The Solari Faith]] is the only true authority in the world, and their mission is to enforce his dominion to all people. 

The Church views the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|Golden Sails Company]] and its profit motive as a necessary sin. The clergy sees their pursuit of wealth as misguided. The [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]]'s resources, logistical control, and substantial financial tithes are currently indispensable for funding the vast purification campaign and providing the magical components for Weavesilk. The Church tolerates this sin with the firm belief that once [[The Resistance]] and all agents of chaos are permanently dealt with, the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]] can and will be properly purified and its wealth consecrated entirely to [[Solaris and The Solari Faith|Solaris]]'s mission.

The Church views the [[Upper Crust Houses]]' claim to a divine right to rule as heresy, believing that only [[Solaris and The Solari Faith|Solaris]] grants authority. However, this heresy is strategically useful. The Church finds the Houses to be a necessary tool for the enforcement of the faith, as their aristocratic officers are effective at maintaining order and controlling the populace. The Church tacitly accepts [[Upper Crust Houses|The Upper Houses]]' secular rule, believing that through mandatory religious education and political necessity, the nobles will eventually be "educated" into becoming true, fully compliant believers.
# Prominent Figures
### [[Pontifex Maximus Virtue]]
**Role:** Pontifex Maximus of Ecclesia Solari, Member of Triumvirate

Takes the form of a Planetar. [[Pontifex Maximus Virtue|Virtue]] is the literal Voice of Solaris on the Shattered World. Pilgrims who have attended the Pontifex's sermons speak of a voice that resonates in the chest like a church bell, commanding absolute obedience and reverence. Serving as the active spiritual guide of the Crusade.
### [[Lord Commander Regus]]
**Role:** Lord Commander of the Paladins Templar

A golden colored dragonborn who is rumored to be able to take forms of power in combat. He is charismatic, fearless, and stands as the primary shield against the monsters of the dark. He can be seen on recruitment posters across the Upper Crust.
