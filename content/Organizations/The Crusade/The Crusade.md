**The Crusade** is the dominant theocratic and military power operating out of their headquarters, [[Cittadella Lumina]] within the [[Upper Crust]] [[Strata|Stratum]], the organization commands the active loyalty of an estimated 75% of the total populace. The Crusade frames its mission as a divinely mandated quest from the sun god, [[Solaris and The Solari Faith]], to unify the world and permanently end the darkness lingering from [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]].

The organization is built upon three groups that serve as its founding pillars: the [[Ecclesia Solari]], the [[Upper Crust Houses]] , and the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company]]. Together, these groups form the Crusade and actively combat the forces of [[The Resistance]] wherever they can.

# History

While the Crusade has sought to unify the [[Upper Crust]] since the its founding shortly after [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]], the nature of the organization shifted following the founding of [[The Resistance]] 30 years ago (120 E.I.). For a 25-year period, the Crusade was mainly characterized by diplomatic maneuvering, trade embargoes enforced by the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]] and border skirmishes. However, many experts credit the assassination of [[Lord Eobard Aurum|Eobard Aurum]] for the escalation of the Crusade's efforts from inquisitorial to active front-line conquest over the last 5 years.

# Governance
The Crusade is governed by a **Triumvirate**, ensuring that the three founding pillars share equal control over military, spiritual, and economic policy. This shared command structure provides the organization with highly centralized power and clear vision. The Triumvirate consists of:
1. **[[Pontifex Maximus Virtue|The Pontifex Maximus]]** ([[Ecclesia Solari]]): The spiritual and ideological head, focused on driving the mission of purification.
2. **[[High Baron Magnus Aurum|The High Baron]]** ([[Upper Crust Houses]]): The political leader, responsible for consolidating legal power and managing the aristocracy.
3. **[[Lord Governor Mullstone Goldleaf|The Lord Governor]]** ([[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|Golden Sails Company]]): The logistical master, who controls all vital transit between the [[tectonic islands]] and manages the war economy.
# Ideology
The core of the Crusade's power lies in its appeal to a populace searching for order, security, and a sense of divine purpose. The founding of The Crusade listed 3 divinely ordained tasks:
1. Winning lands and territories for children of the Sun.
2. Spreading the faith and rooting out all forms of darkness, chaos, and corruption.
3. Driving the monsters back into the shadows where they belong.
