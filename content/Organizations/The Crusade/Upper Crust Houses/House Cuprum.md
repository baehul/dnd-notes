**House Cuprum** is the Great House of [[Upper Crust Houses|The Upper Houses]] composed entirely of Dwarves. They are the builders of [[The Crusade]]'s fortresses and the authors of its laws. To the common citizen, they are the most trusted of the Great Houses, for one simple reason: A Cuprum never breaks their word.
# Culture
The Cuprum words can be found etched in everything they create: *"Ours Is To Endure*." This permeates the works of engineering that can be found throughout the [[Upper Crust]]. While [[House Aurum]] focuses on personal magic items specifically for martial use, House Cuprum is known for its engineering capabilities across multiple domains. Historically, House Cuprum has dominated in aspects of civil engineering due to a comprehensive set of masonry regulation they strictly follow. This set of regulation eventually grew into a vast web of laws that cover many aspects of society, leading to House Cuprum's role in authoring and preserving law.
## Code of Honor
House Cuprum is built on a rigid code of honor. To a Cuprum Dwarf, a verbal promise is as binding as any written contract. If a member of House Cuprum gives their word, they will go to any length to avoid breaking it; if they do break their word, they risk being shunned and disowned by House Cuprum.
# Prominent Figures
### [[Baron Thorgar Cuprum]]
**Role:** Patriarch of House Cuprum

Thorgar is the dwarven patriarch of House Cuprum. A brilliant architect and a flawless legal scholar, but not considered a good politician. He is well-respected amongst the people of the [[Upper Crust]] and members of [[The Crusade]] outside of [[Upper Crust Houses|The Upper Houses]] who view him as a nuisance. He is considered a man with unimpeachable honor.
### [[Lady Dagna Cuprum]]
**Role:** Chief Engineer

Thorgar's eldest niece. She is responsible for most of the engineering efforts of [[The Crusade]] as a whole. She is famously pragmatic and is considered the more *flexible* member of House Cuprum. The other Great Houses of the [[Upper Crust Houses]] have been pushing for her to take on more responsibilities within House Cuprum.
