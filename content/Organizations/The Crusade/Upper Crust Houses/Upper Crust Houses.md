**The Upper Crust Houses**, sometimes referred to as **The Upper Houses**, are a collection of noble houses representing the political authority, legal stability, and aristocratic tradition necessary to govern the vast territories liberated in the name of [[Solaris and The Solari Faith]]. They are a key member of [[The Crusade]] and are represented in the Triumvirate by the [[High Baron Magnus Aurum]].

# Demographics

## The Three Great Houses
While there are many families that make up the Upper Houses, the dynamic is dominated primarily by three families known as The Great Houses who constantly vie for influence and control of the High Barony.

# Leadership
The Upper Houses derive their authority not from wealth, but from blood purity. They are defined by their ability to trace their lineage back to the Pre-Shattering era, claiming direct descent from the old kings, queens, lords, and barons of the world before the cataclysm. This heritage grants them unquestioned legal and political leadership.
## [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|The High Baron]]
[[High Baron Magnus Aurum|The High Baron]] serves as the representative of the Upper Crust Houses within the Triumvirate. The position functions as the ultimate arbiter of aristocratic life, wielding significant military and legal authority.

[[High Baron Magnus Aurum|The High Baron]] acts as the supreme judge for internal disputes between noble families. Their word is final in matters of land rights, inheritance, and inter-House feuds. In addition, [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|the High Baron]] can commandeer the private house guards of any noble family to serve the direct interests of [[the Crusade]]. Refusal to answer this command is a violation of House law. A rebellious house faces not only the wrath of [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|the High Baron]] but excommunication by the [[Ecclesia Solari]] and embargo by the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|Golden Sails Company]], the threat of which is enough to keep even the most defiant houses in line.
### Election of [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|the High Baron]]
The position of [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|the High Baron]] is non-hereditary, preventing any single lineage from gaining permanent control. [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|The High Baron]] is selected by a council of the patriarchs and matriarchs of the three Great Houses. To secure the title, a candidate must also gain two key political endorsements: an official Blessing of Bounty from [[Pontifex Maximus Virtue|the Pontifex Maximus]] of the [[Ecclesia Solari]] and a Promise of Trade from [[Lord Governor Mullstone Goldleaf|the Lord Governor]] of the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company]].

Once elected, a High Baron holds their seat indefinitely, serving until they die or are deposed. A High Baron can only be deposed by the election of a new High Baron. Given the challenge in uniting the Upper Houses behind a new candidate who also has the favor of [[Ecclesia Solari|The Church]] and [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|The Company]], deposing [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|the High Baron]] requires a great deal of secret politicking and deal-making.
### Current High Baron: [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|Magnus Aurum]]
The current position of High Baron is held by [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|Magnus Aurum]],. the patriarch of [[House Aurum]].  Magnus has held the position for 20 years following the death of his father, the previous High Baron. Magnus is known as a shrewd political mind who has united the Upper Houses in [[the Crusade]]'s mission.

# Political Ideology
The Upper Crust Houses believe that their long, pure bloodlines were chosen and prepared by [[Solaris and The Solari Faith]] over many millennia to act as the true custodians of civilization. They view their inherited superiority as a sacred burden that allowed their families to endure [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]] and remain the only ones fit to guide the populace back to order. 

The Houses view the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|Golden Sails Company]] and its leadership with profound aristocratic contempt, considering them useful servants who handle the crude labor and logistics of commerce. They genuinely believe they are doing the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]] a favor by simply allowing them to conduct business and profit, but recognize the necessity of the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]]'s logistical power and ability to maintain the economy, ensuring their reliance remains purely pragmatic.

While most nobles are true believers in [[Solaris and The Solari Faith]], they firmly assert that the divine right to rule was bestowed upon their ancestral Houses, not the priesthood. They view [[Ecclesia Solari|The Church]]'s immense spiritual power as a threat to their own secular authority. They actively work to limit [[Ecclesia Solari|The Church]]'s temporal power, preventing the High Pontiff from establishing a true theocracy. They, do however, submit to [[Ecclesia Solari|the Church]]'s authority and require religious education, lest they be denied [[Pontifex Maximus Virtue|the Pontifex Maximus]]'s blessing when the time comes to choose [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|the High Baron]].
