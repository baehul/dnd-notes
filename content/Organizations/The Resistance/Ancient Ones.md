**The Ancient Ones** is an alliance of powerful, pre-[[The Shattering of the World|Shattering]] entities including dragons, liches, hags, giants, and primordial elementals which operates as a distinct faction within [[The Resistance]]. Unlike their allies, the Ancient Ones are not driven by ideology, vengeance, or scientific curiosity. Their primary motivation is self-preservation and isolation.

Composed of beings whose lifespans measure in centuries or millennia, the organization views the current war against [[The Crusade]] as a temporary historical blip. However, recognizing that [[The Crusade]]’s dogma poses an existential threat to their chaotic and magical natures, they have entered into a strategic pact with [[The Resistance]]. They provide their significant resources, both material and magical, to the coalition in exchange for concealment and the guarantee that [[The Resistance]] will act as a buffer force, keeping the actual fighting away from the Ancient Ones' lairs.

# Demographics
The membership of the Ancient Ones is exclusive, restricted to beings of immense power who originated on the material plane prior to or shortly after [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]]. Chromatic dragons, powerful undead, hags, ancient elementals, and giants are some of the most well-known members though many scholars theorize that many more powerful creatures make up their ranks. Their followers tend to be species that have historically served these powerful creatures such as:
- Chromatic Dragonborn
- Kobolds
- Genasi
- Centaur
- Firbolg
- Yuan-Ti

# Leadership
Because the members of the Ancient Ones are solitary by nature and possess massive egos, they do not submit to traditional voting or military command. Instead, they are governed by a council known as The Conclave of Eons. 

Status within the faction is determined strictly by age. The eldest among them, an unknown creature believed to be from the [[Era of Myth]], holds the deciding vote on all matters. The Conclave serves less as a government and more as a court of mediation. Its primary function is to enforce a strict non-aggression pact between members. To avoid lowering themselves to speak with "mortals," the Conclave appoints chosen representatives from their trusted amongst their servants to act as liaisons to the rest of [[The Resistance]].

# Political Ideology
The Ancient Ones’ political stance is driven by extreme risk aversion and a desire to preserve their few members. They refuse all direct combat; instead, they leverage their vast, millennia-spanning accumulation of resources, both material and magical, to act as the primary financiers and suppliers of [[The Resistance]]. They view their membership as a purely transactional arrangement with the lesser factions, whose short life spans make them ideally suited for the battlefield.

Operating as the coalition's primary resource engine, the Ancient Ones selectively open their hoards to outfit the [[Blood Horde]]. They supply [[Blood Horde|the Horde]] with enchanted weaponry and the gold necessary to fund the war effort. This material support is viewed not as charity, but as an investment in a "living wall" that keeps the fighting away from the Ancients' secluded lairs. Consequently, [[Blood Horde|the Horde]] is perceived as a mere swarm of expendables whose squabbles and honor codes are beneath the Ancient Ones' notice.

The Ancient Ones also serve as the key patron for the [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night]]. They supply [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|the Cult]]'s researchers with rare reagents, lost spell scrolls, and archaic materials necessary for research and engineering. [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult]] is regarded with patronizing tolerance - useful as logistical proxies who handle the mundane details of the war, though the Ancients frequently scoff at [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|the Cult]]'s "discoveries" as knowledge that they have possessed for millennia.
