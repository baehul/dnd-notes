**The Resistance** is a political and military coalition operating within the [[In-Between Isles]]. The organization was founded as a direct, unified response to the formation of [[The Crusade]], primarily to discuss the best action for inhabitants of the [[In-Between Isles]] to take in response. The coalition is primarily composed of the [[Blood Horde]] , [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night]], and the [[Ancient Ones]]. While the Resistance asserts that it represents the interests of all inhabitants of the Isles, contemporary scholars and military analysts estimate its active membership to encompass between 1-10% of the total population. In contrast, The Crusade maintains the active support of an estimated 75% of the [[Upper Crust]] [[Strata|strata]]. Internal critics within the Resistance have attributed its low general membership to a perceived lack of clear leadership and unified direction, citing the organization's current governance structure as a council composed of representatives from each of its three constituent organizations.
