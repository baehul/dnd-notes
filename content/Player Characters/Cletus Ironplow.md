# Name

Cletus Ironplow

# Description

Cletus is a Duergar with a short, stocky build. He has the characteristic grey skin of his kin, with a fairly short, black, mustache-less chinstrap beard along his jawline. He proudly sports a smooth, bald head when he’s not wearing his specially-designed helmet.

Without his armor, he wears a modified university uniform, with various extra pockets, belts, and pouches full of various tools and magical supplies. The custom work is shoddily done but seems to hold firm.

His armor is much of the same story. It is currently a set of custom-fit studded leather armor, with various add-ons and adjustments hastily attached. In the evenings or mornings, you often see Cletus fiddling with this armor and seeming to attach and detach various parts and magical devices based on the day’s expected activities.

# Personality

Cletus is a young but confident engineer, constantly seeking to prove himself and his work. He always seeks to understand how complex machines and systems around him work so he can incorporate them into his own designs. Despite his years spent in magic schools and academies, he has held onto his country bumpkin attitude and mannerisms. Cletus learns by doing. His favorite method is trial and error, with the underlying principles of “if it works, it works” and “function over form.” This fact is clearly displayed in all of his designs and decision making.

# Backstory

## Childhood

Cletus grew up on a farm run by his parents and family. His farm was somewhat isolated, on some grade 1 island. As the oldest sibling, he quickly learned the trade, needing to help out at a young age. However, in experimenting with ways to speed up his tasks, he accidentally started using magic. He was not even aware of it until his parents noticed. His parents were at first worried about the implications of having a son with magic abilities but did not specifically try to halt this behavior. Eventually his parents asked around and learned that the Cult was the best place for Cletus to learn about his abilities. Feeling that they would be doing him a disservice by forcing him to live a poor, busy farmer’s life without properly learning about his abilities, they managed to get him enrolled at a school within the Cult.

## School and the Cult

Cletus spent the remainder of his childhood and teen years in various schools associated with the Cult. He often brings up his “tip-top” grades at Cult academies and commends the Cult for finding a “needle in a haystack” by recruiting him. After years of success and academic prowess, Cletus wished to test his creations and skills in a more practical manner by officially joining the Resistance.

The REAL story:

As a child, Cletus showed a basic aptitude for magic, earning him a place at various magical schools and academies within the cult. Unfortunately, that ‘place’ often ended up being the bottom of the class. While he demonstrated some basic abilities with magic, he was never on par with any of his peers. Spells that others picked up on quickly seemed to just fizzle out when he tried learning them. Through sheer effort and grit, he was often able to learn the magic, just at a much slower pace than those around him.

One area he always found interesting was artificing. In his younger years, he was fascinated by the idea of tinkering with small machines and imbuing them with magic to see what they would do. While he was proficient at these small feats, he hit that familiar brick wall when attempting anything more advanced.

While the Cult follows its philosophy of exploring different truths, it only managed to shield him from ridicule from his teachers. His peers often looked down upon him, with the more conceited ones often taunting and bullying him for his lackluster magical ability. Even without this harsh treatment from his teachers, they tended to treat him with pity. This left him with a lack of belief that followed him and further hampered his attempts at learning magic and artificing.

One day, he was tinkering with one of his latest machines on campus. The usual group of bullies saw his unorthodox designs and prototype, laughing and asking him, “Is this your best creation yet, Cletus?”. After yet another failure attempting to imbue this creation with magic, he felt the despair seep in. But that despair was soon replaced with anger, rage, and fury. All his fellow students ever did was doubt him. Even his teachers doubted him, despite their attempts to hide it. He wanted more than anything to show them that they were wrong about him. The words echoed in his mind, *is this your best creation yet?* You know what, YES, this WAS his best creation yet. This sudden rush of belief and confidence swirled within him as his latest attempt succeeded with flying colors. For perhaps the first time, his hard work and perseverance truly had paid off. At first, thought of showing off his new skills to those who mocked him, but now he had bigger dreams. This single contraption would not impress them. It would not impress his teachers. He set out now to prove his abilities to everyone around him. And what better way to do that than showing off his designs on the front lines of this brewing conflict with the Crusade?

Cletus’s true passion and goal is using his farming equipment inventions to revolutionize agriculture. However, he believes he needs to prove himself with inventions for war first, since he is more likely to receive funding and recognition from the Cult and Resistance this way.
