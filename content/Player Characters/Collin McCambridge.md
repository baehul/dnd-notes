Collin is a more stern and quiet individual. Paranoid, %%about his true life being discovered%%he tends to keep to himself but is not against working with others so long as they don’t feel compelled to pry too much into his personal life. When he is talkative he's very sheepish and stutters somewhat frequently. He comes to life when talking about puzzles and mysteries especially ones pertaining to the arcane leylines and the origin of [[the shattering of the world]].

## Early Childhood

Eobard Lucidean Aurum is the ninth born child to [[High Baron Magnus Aurum]] and therefore next in line to be head of the Aurum noble house. Raised with eight siblings it felt as if the chaos at home never quite reflected the pressure he was forced to live in from the moment he was born. His mother was practically non-existent as she wouldn't burden herself with a responsibility such as taking care of children when she had more politically significant duties to attend to. His father on the other hand was more prevalent in his life with Eobard being the ninthborn son. [[High Baron Magnus Aurum]] kept a stern watchful eye over Eobard for the entirety of his youth, making sure Eobard excelled in both academia and martial prowess. Eobard, to his family's chagrin, was not extremely talented in the martial arts but was more proficient than his siblings in the academic arts. On Eobard’s thirteenth birthday his father called on him for a special coming of age ceremony.[[High Baron Magnus Aurum|Magnus Aurum]] told him “For generations the Aurum family had been the best magical practitioners Before Fragmentation and continue to strive for perfection in the Era of the Isles. [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]] may have caused some complications within the pre existing power dynamics but [[House Aurum]] decided to take hold of their future, making best of the situations set before them. The new access to the [[Ley Lines]] as you well know gives us a special opportunity along with the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company]] to harvest these”. [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|Magnus]] then pulls a small crystal out from an arcane machine that is taking [[Resonance Crystals]], specifically a large geode that a team of eight arcanists are etching arcane symbols into the finely cut sides of. “The entirety of our family has been using these to enhance our innate magical potential and allow us to further continue our hold as the greatest magical house this world will ever see”. Following his speech Eobard gets strapped to a chair willingly letting his father continue as they then begin an extremely painful and grueling exposure process allowing the magic of the geode to saturate directly into Eobard’s neurological and cardiovascular system. Feeling this surge of energy Eobard quickly saw his magical capabilities swiftly grow to being on par with his siblings. Sometime between his fourteenth and fifteenth birthday this began to change, he noticed that his magic was slowly but surely regressing in strength and dampening over time. As his father began to notice this struggle he continued to make Eobard go under more arcane enhancing but his efforts proved fruitless as this only quickened the regress of Eobard’s powers. Around the time he began noticing the loss of his magic Eobard began having strange lucid dreams. Many of which meant very little to him but felt a vague sense of reality to them. His fears were realised when he came home and saw the dream he had a few times, Cole and Callum’s hanging. Knowing now certain how evil his family is, Eobard hid the reality of his visions from his family. The most recurring one he had was of a glade somewhere in the [[In-Between Isles]] with swampy water and trees covering an opening in the ground where something greater lurked beneath the surface. Not long after Eobard’s seventeenth birthday he received a vision of his father and mother speaking privately in their bedchamber. They were speaking about how they were going to deal with Eobard’s magical issues even after session after session of exposure to large amounts of [[Resonance Crystals]]. Eobard knew this only meant bad news and would not put it past his family to kill him for the sake of the Aurum name. In this vision his parents discussed that a final supply of [[Resonance Crystals]] was coming in that day and this would be their final time trying to enhance my powers. Now knowing the exact date a ship was to come and the possible final date of his life, Eobard had begun to plan his escape from first experiencing that vision. When the day arrived he snuck his way off his castle's ground and stowaway on the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]] ship that took him to the [[In-Between Isles]].

## Non Familial Childhood Relations

Growing up Eobard felt like he had two people that he could truly call his friends, his personal butler Cole McCambridge and his butler’s son Callum who was training to be his fathers replacement and was roughly the same age as Eobard. When Eobard’s parents were off on political missions and he had finished his chores and classwork Cole would sneak his son into Eobard’s room and let them play together, as an act of care and pity for the life Eobard was living. To Eobard Cole was more of a father figure growing up than anyone and Callum was his closest brother, both of them finding the most joy in reading “The Adventures of Collin Thorpe” a Before Fragmentation era book series that accounted the life of a world renown detective and the cases he would undertake. Eobard’s parents would never let him read something non academic but Cole wanted Eobard to learn that finding joy was always possible when surrounding yourself with the right people. After turning fourteen, Eobard returned from a meeting with the other Upper Crust houses he was forced to attend as a showing of good faith between the future Baron of [[House Aurum]] and the other two houses. He came back to his father requesting his presence for specialised training, upon entering his father’s study Eobard was met with the grave sight of both Cole and Callum’s body hanging from the rafters in the study. His father had the same serious look and his face as if Eobard had just failed some lesson of his and exclaimed “This is what happens to those who go against my will, do not disobey me in such a manner again” and told Eobard to return to the operations chambers for extra arcane strengthening.

## Events After Running

After leaving his familial homeland Eobard began going underneath the pseudonym Collin McCambridge in hopes to distance himself from his family, covering his hair and eyes to try and keep his true lineage a secret. For the next year all of Collin’s resources went into finding this glad that kept coming to him in visions. He never made much progress without the help of his visions showing him the way to go. Anytime he felt as if he hit a dead end he would slowly be given visions that led him down a promising path towards his goal. On the exact day of his eighteenth birthday Collin made it to an island closer to the outskirts between the [[In-Between Isles]] and the [[Upper Crust]] with swampy terrain and a boggy atmosphere with a thin layer of mist covering the ground. This land seemingly uninhabited took a few days to traverse before finding the scene he’s been seeing for almost four years now. A clearing of the trees with murky water pouring into an opening in the ground that is thirty feet deep with the walls covered in vines and roots form the trees above. After traversing down into the pit below Collin felt a surge of magic rush into his eyes unlike any of the visions he’s had before and before a vision could come, a large gale of wind nearly blew him off of his feet and tossed the mask and hood he used to cover his head aside as the trees above rustled and the water crashed against his feet as if a hurricane was being formed around him. As the water and foliage covered his line of sight a long snake-like figure covered the sky and swirled around the vortex outside. Green scaled with moss in patches across the entire body and covered in vines and leaves across the spine with two large antlers, a grassy beard, and two long whiskers made of a thorny vine. Legs with taloned claws protrude out from the underside of its long body every thirty feet or so. This creature's body extends an unknown length deeper into the swamp above but the head careens down face to face with Collin as their golden eyes meet. A voice that sounds like the wind blowing reeds of bamboo and heavily leafed trees echoes throughout the pit originating from the dragon. The dragon says, sneering “Who are you and how have you found your way into my home?!”. Collin, scared and voice trembling stunned in silence for a moment but sensing the monstrous creature impatience spoke. “My name is Collin McCambridge, I..I came to seek aid in my quest for knowledge of the world”.

“Do not lie to me child, I have heard the lies of mortals more skilled in the craft and wiser than the likes of you. Truth or your life”.

Stunned, Eobard steadied himself and stared straight into the face of this creature with the determination of the last five years of his life fueling him “My name is Eobard Lucidean Aurum, Fated Scion of the Aurum house, Ninthborn and rightful heir to the great Aurum house. I’ve had visions of this place for years and see now that you are what my sight has been steering me towards. Now tell me beast, can you assist me in the downfall of one of the greatest noble houses this world has ever seen”.

Silent for a moment the dragon came closer and focused his large golden eye on Eobard’s face, smirking. “My name is [[Luwei]], The Whisper in The Reed. I shall help you, but first you must agree to obey my every command only then can we achieve your goal”.

“Done”

“Good, we now tell me all you know”

Eobard departs unto [[Luwei]] every facet of knowledge he has about his family's house, his family itself, and the arcane enhancements his father would force him and his siblings to undergo. Together they came up with a solid cover story for Collin McCambridge and for the past five years Collin has been working for [[Luwei]] and the [[Ancient Ones]]’ faction inside [[The Resistance]] with the promise of uncovering the true ramifications from the arcane enhancements and the downfall of his family.

# Cover Story

%%

Collin McCambridge was born into a service family underneath the [[Upper Crust Houses|Upper Crust House]] of [[House Aurum]]. It was always considered the highest privilege for a lower born to be in service to one of the greatest houses, especially the one currently in rule with [[High Baron Magnus Aurum]] being his patron. Collin was a butler that tended to the Aurum’s manor’s every need but more importantly he was given the opportunity to have nearly the greatest teaching available to a lower born. He attended school and had a knack for history and the arcane from a very young age. Being naturally curious and having a knack for getting into locked and prohibited areas got him in trouble frequently growing up. He would often sneak into the Aurum family's personal library to read books he was typically not allowed to read. On his sixteenth birthday his luck ran out. Collin snuck into an underground part of the manor and when opening a work desk with ledgers accidentally knocked over his lantern and burned parts of the desk and papers that were on it before getting to read any. Scurrying to leave he heard voices from behind him begin to shout, one of them clearly being The High Baron himself in rage telling them to find who created the mess. Running back to his father’s chambers in the service living area he could already hear the guards taking people he worked alongside out of their rooms and aggressively questioning them. His father, knowing Collin too well, knew that Collin was the source of the issue and with a maddeningly swift pace told Collin to tell him what he did. Collin, terrified, obliged and explained while his father grabbed Collin’s things and tied them in a sack. His father told him to use those talents of his to sneak out and that he’d be right behind him. Collin sneaking through service tunnels ran out of the manor grounds, this was the last time he ever saw his father. After two years of running and living in the [[In-Between Isles]] without a means to make a living for himself while tired and hungry had lost all hope of survival. At his lowest, ready to give up, is when he met his patron. An Ancient one who called themselves The Whisper in The Reed, who helped Collin get back on his feet with the promise of information and revenge. The only thing he asked in return was for Collin’s service to them and the [[Ancient Ones]]’ cause as a member of [[The Resistance]], which he has been loyally serving for the past five years.

[[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] reminds Collin a little of himself at a young age so Collin feels a kinship with him. Whenever Collin visits the Silver Moon he makes sure to set some time aside to speak to [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] about the book series they are both into, “The Adventures of Collin Thorpe” and about mysterious such as the [[Ley Lines]] and the events around [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]].
