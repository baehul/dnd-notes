## Early Childhood

Eobard Lucideam Aurum is the ninth born child to [[High Baron Magnus Aurum]] and therefore next in line to be head of the Aurum noble house. Raised with eight siblings it felt as if the chaos at home never quite reflected the pressure he was forced to live in from the moment he was born. His mother was practically non-existent as she wouldn't burden herself with a responsibility such as taking care of children when she had more politically significant duties to attend to. His father on the other hand was more prevalent in his life with Eobard being the ninthborn son. [[High Baron Magnus Aurum]] kept a stern watchful eye over Eobard for the entirety of his youth, making sure Eobard excelled in both academia and martial prowess. Eobard, to his family's chagrin, was not extremely talented in the martial arts but was more proficient than his siblings in the academic arts. On Eobard’s thirteenth birthday his father called on him for a special coming of age ceremony. [[Magnus Aurum]] told him “For generations the Aurum family had been the best magical practitioners Before Fragmentation and continue to strive for perfection in the Era of the Isles. [[The Shattering]] may have caused some complications within the pre existing power dynamics but [[House Aurum]] decided to take hold of their future, making best of the situations set before them. The new access to the [[Ley Lines]] as you well know gives us a special opportunity along with the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company]] to harvest these”. [[Magnus]] then pulls a small crystal out from an arcane machine that is taking [[Resonance Crystals]], specifically a large geode that a team of eight arcanists are etching arcane symbols into the finely cut sides of. “The entirety of our family has been using these to enhance our innate magical potential and allow us to further continue our hold as the greatest magical house this world will ever see”. Following his speech Eobard gets strapped to a chair willingly letting his father continue as they then begin an extremely painful and grueling exposure process allowing the magic of the geode to saturate directly into Eobard’s neurological and cardiovascular system. Feeling this surge of energy Eobard quickly saw his magical capabilities swiftly grow to being on par with his siblings. Sometime between his 14th and 15th birthday this began to change, he noticed that his magic was slowly but surely regressing in strength and dampening over time. As his father began to notice this struggle he continued to make Eobard go under more arcane enhancing but his efforts proved fruitless as this only quickened the regress of Eobard’s powers. Around the time he began noticing the loss of his magic Eobard began having strange lucid dreams. Many of which meant very little to him but felt a vague sense of reality to them. His fears were realised when he came home and saw the dream he had a few times, Cole and Callum’s hanging. Knowing now certain how evil his family is, Eobard hid the reality of his visions from his family. The most recurring one he had was of a glade somewhere in the [[In-Between Isles]] with swampy water and trees covering an opening in the ground where something greater lurked beneath the surface. Not long after Eobard’s 17th birthday he received a vision of his father and mother speaking privately in their bedchamber. They were speaking about how they were going to deal with Eobard’s magical issues even after session after session of exposure to large amounts of [[Resonance Crystals]]. Eobard knew this only meant bad news and would not put it past his family to kill him for the sake of the Aurum name. In this vision his parents discussed that a final supply of [[Resonance Crystals]] was coming in that day and this would be their final time trying to enhance my powers. Now knowing the exact date a ship was to come and the possible final date of his life, Eobard had begun to plan his escape from first experiencing that vision. When the day arrived he snuck his way off his castle's ground and stowaway on the [[GSTSC]] ship that took him to the [[In-Between Isles]].

## Non Familial Childhood Relations

Growing up Eobard felt like he had two people that he could truly call his friends, his personal butler Cole McCambridge and his butler’s son Callum who was training to be his fathers replacement and was roughly the same age as Eobard. When Eobard’s parents were off on political missions and he had finished his chores and classwork Cole would sneak his son into Eobard’s room and let them play together, as an act of care and pity for the life Eobard was living. To Eobard Cole was more of a father figure growing up than anyone and Callum was his closest brother, both of them finding the most joy in reading “The Adventures of Collin Thorpe” a Before Fragmentation era book series that accounted the life of a world renown detective and the cases he would undertake. Eobard’s parents would never let him read something non academic but Cole wanted Eobard to learn that finding joy was always possible when surrounding yourself with the right people. After turning 14, Eobard returned from a meeting with the other Upper Crust houses he was forced to attend as a showing of good faith between the future Baron of House Aurum and the other two houses. He came back to his father requesting his presence for specialised training, upon entering his father’s study Eobard was met with the grave sight of both Cole and Callum’s body hanging from the rafters in the study. His father had the same serious look and his face as if Eobard had just failed some lesson of his and exclaimed “This is what happens to those who go against my will, do not disobey me in such a manner again” and told me to return to the operations chambers for extra arcane strengthening.
