## Vase the Fragile

## Race: any

## Class: Battlemaster Fighter? Valor Bard? Swashbuckler Rogue?

## Appearance:

Vase stands at 5’ 7” and is very sensitive about it. He has medium length brown hair, brown eyes and a scar on his left cheek. He wears a blue long-sleeved jacket with red parts over a black t-shirt, a red scarf like ascot, black leggings, an oversized brown belt, with a silver buckle, brown knee-length leather boots with cream ends and grey soles and a sky-blue eyepatch lens over his right eye which he can use to magnify distant objects. His jacket sleeves are somewhat disconnected from the jacket, revealing a small part of his arms in the process (they are only connected to his jacket thanks to very small red connectors)

## Personality:

Vase has a driven and positive personality. and displays strong compassion for those he cares about and empathy for those who care about him. Vase is extremely driven to do well and show he is well deserving of the responsibility placed on him.

## Background:

Vase Argilla, otherwise known as Vase the fragile is the son of Captain Clay Argilla, the sky pirate captain of the stolen GSC Albatross. He grew up on “Windmill Island” one of two in-between islands that act in orbit of a satellite object colloquially known as the silver moon.

%%

* Windmill Island is the secret hiding place of the GSC albatross and its crew. On the surface the island is only the size of a small village, and hosts a small farming community with a trademark windmill. Due to the islands’s obtuse shape and intricate cave structure, a ship is able to fly in the “dead zone” of the island by starting low and letting gravity + wind naturally drift them just above the horizon line of gravity to allow the ship to enter a cave system on the upper belly of the tectonic isle. This is where Vase has grown up along with his ship mates and his father.
* Shrine island is the colloquial name for the sister island to windmill island. It’s uninhabited from human life as the size of the island is only as big as a city block. The island contains ruins, suspected to be a shrine, of some deity pre-shattering.
* Silver Moon is a moon that is literally made out of silver. There is a large mining operation being done by the GSC to move silver from the moon back to the outer crest.

%%

Vase became a crew member of the GSC Albatross at the ripe old age of 9. He earned the name “the fragile” after a ship battle resulted in Vase tripping down the stairs and scarring his face. Vase has years of experience as a sailor but limited combat experience. After years of piracy however, Vase’s father has taken his ship and his sky pirates to greater ambitions that Vase’s father insists Vase stays out of.

Instead, Vase was given as a ward to his uncle [[Mithbarakaz]], and worked as an errand boy or gopher for his uncle. Vase thinks of Mithbarakaz fondly, and knows he feels the same for him. Vase worked for 5 years under his uncle, quietly working towards moving more towards the resistance, making a habit of stowing away on resistance ships and trying to keep an ear on the door for any meeting involving the resistance.  

After years of persistence, Vase finally catches his big break. Mithbarakaz has hinted that the resistance is going to have a meeting regarding the disappearance of [[Lancaster Slickwhistle]], Mithbarakaz’s emissary cannot make the meeting, but the ship will pass by anyway.

## Relationships:

%%

* Vase’s Father: Vase’s Father, Clay Argilla, is the captain of the stolen GSC Albatross. He is a strict, but caring man that is very affectionate to Vase. He values punctuality above almost all else. He’s kept Vase out of the nasty business of piracy for the most part, and away from other more violent elements. He wields an incredibly powerful magical device that can take any object in motion anywhere in the world. The caveat is that the use cannot choose the item and it is immediately shot as a projectile at high speeds towards whatever it was pointed towards
* Teapot: Vase’s twin sister. She was with Vase on several pirate excursions before deciding to leave the pirate life behind after an excursion. She works as a farmer on the island and chooses not to support the resistance.

%%

* Uncle Mithbarakaz: A silver dragon nested within the silver moon. Mithbarakaz and Vase’s father have had a long standing professional relationship that Vase isn’t privy to. As a favor, Mithbarakaz acts as a sponsor for Vase within the ancient ones’ alliance and the resistance.

## The Compendium:

Vase keeps a collection of myths, legends, and biographies that document what life was like in the age of myth before the shattering. His book is a myriad of different passages and he hopes to fill it out with future biographies of other ancient ones and fill it with lore about the world

So far, the compendium contains:

* A biography of what Mithbarakaz has told him so far
* The foundational myths of the [[Old Path]]
* Select passages of the [[Way of Night (Book)]]

## Trivia:

* Vase wears a single goggle as corrective vision after an accident. He has since healed past the injury and no longer requires corrective lenses. He keeps it just because he thinks it’s cool
* If Vase wasn’t a sky pirate or resistance member, he would probably just find trouble elsewhere
* Despite the name, Vase has never taken pottery lessons
* Vase is in love with every woman he meets
* Vase has been in fights before, but has never killed someone, and doesn’t see a reason to.
* Vase is very curious about religion but shies away from doctrine. Still he thinks there is truth in every religion and is curious to explore them.
