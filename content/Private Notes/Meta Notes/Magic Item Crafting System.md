# The Art of Arcane Crafting

_A Guide to Forging Magic Items_

To create a magic item, you must possess its **Recipe**, which requires three distinct pillars to complete.

## Pillar 1: The Hard Component (The Soul)

This is the key ingredient that dictates the nature of the item. It cannot be purchased; it must be harvested from a creature, found in ancient ruins, or won.

### **The Harvesting Mechanic**

When a creature of interest is defeated, a character may attempt to harvest a Hard Component (e.g., a Dragon’s Heart, a Basilisk’s Eye).

**Outcomes:**

- **Success:** You retrieve the component in pristine condition.
- **Partial Failure (Miss by 5 or less):** The component is **Damaged**. It can still be used, but the Gold Cost (Reagents) required to craft the item increases by 50% to stabilize it.
- **Critical Failure (Miss by >5 or Nat 1):** The component is Ruined and useless.

## Pillar 2: The Soft Components (The Fuel)

To awaken the Hard Component, the crafter must pay a toll in magical energy and physical wealth.

### **A. Magic Levels (The Spark)**

Raw magical power is required to jumpstart the enchantment. This is measured in **Spell Slot Levels**.

- _Example:_ If a recipe requires "10 Levels of Evocation," a Wizard could cast _Fireball_ (3rd level) three times and _Magic Missile_ (1st level) once into the item.
    
- **For Non-Casters:** If the party lacks the required spell slots, they may substitute **Spell Scrolls** (which are consumed) or hire **NPC Mages** to perform this step (adding to the gold cost).
    

### **B. Reagents (The Conduit)**

Magic is volatile. Without a physical medium to conduct the energy, the item will shatter. This cost is paid in Gold Pieces (gp) representing gem dust, rare chalks, conductive inks, or alchemical oils.

---

## Pillar 3: The Ritual (The Method)

This is the narrative process of binding the parts together. It requires a specific amount of Downtime, a specific Tool Proficiency (or Arcana check), and carries a risk of failure.

### **The Crafting Check**

At the end of the required time, the lead crafter makes a Tool Check against the Recipe's DC.

- **Success:** The item is created.
- **Failure:** The **Reagents (Gold)** are consumed and wasted, but the **Hard Component** is preserved. You may try again after acquiring more reagents.
- **Catastrophe (Natural 1):** The Hard Component creates a magical feedback loop. The component is destroyed, the gold is lost, and the crafter takes force damage equal to the _Magic Levels_ required.

**Guidance for the DM:**

- **Recipe Acquisition:** Players should not automatically know recipes. They must find _Schematics_ as loot, study ancient murals, or pay a Master Crafter to write down the instructions.
    
- **Cooperation:** Encourage "Group Checks." The Fighter uses Smith's Tools to hammer the blade, while the Wizard channels the magic. If the Fighter helps, the Wizard gets Advantage on the final check.

# Recipe Examples

### Example 1: Consumable (Category A - Minor Common)

**Potion of Climbing**

- **Type:** Single (Consumable).
- **Hard Component:** Blood of a Giant Spider.
- **Soft Components:**
    - 2 Levels of Transmutation Magic.
    - **25 gp** of reagents (distilled mercury).
- **The Ritual:** 4 Hours, Alchemist's Supplies (DC 10). The blood must be distilled until clear.

### Example 2: Permanent Uncommon (Category F - Major Uncommon)

**Weapon +1**

- **Type:** Permanent.
- **Classification Note:** A +1 Weapon provides a bonus to hit/damage, making it a **Major** Item according to Categorization Rule 3.
- **Hard Component:** Meteoric Iron or metal scavenged from a Golem.
- **Soft Components:**
    - 12 Levels of Transmutation Magic.
    - **250 gp** of reagents (diamond dust for tempering).
        
- **The Ritual:** 3 days, Smith's Tools (DC 14). The metal is folded over itself 100 times while the magic is hammered into the grain.

### Example 3: Charged Rare (Category G - Major Rare)

**Wand of Binding**

- **Type:** Charged (Regains charges daily).
- **Hard Component:** Bone of a Mummy Lord.
- **Soft Components:**
    - 28 Levels of Enchantment Magic.
    - **2,400 gp** of reagents (gold filigree and obsidian).
- **The Ritual:** 1 Workweek, Woodcarver's Tools (DC 17). The bone is encased in wood and etched with binding runes filled with molten gold.
