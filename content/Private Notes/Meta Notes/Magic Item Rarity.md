## Categorization

### Quick Decision Flowchart

When you find a magic item, ask these questions to determine its Category (A-I).

1. **Is it Consumable?** (Potions, scrolls, single-use tokens).
    - **Yes:** It is **Minor** (Category A, B, C, D, or E) based on rarity.
    - **Exception:** If it has >10 charges or breaks the campaign (e.g., _Efreeti Bottle_), treat as Major.
2. **Is it Common Rarity?**
    - **Yes:** It is **Category A (Minor Common)**.
3. **Is it Uncommon Rarity?**
    - **Does it give Flight, +1 Bonuses, Resistance, Summons, or Stat Bumps?**
        - **Yes:** It is **Category F (Major Uncommon)**.
        - **No:** It is **Category B (Minor Uncommon)**.
4. **Is it Rare or Very Rare?**
    - **Is it permanent with broad combat/social utility?**
        - **Yes:** It is **Major** (Category G or H).
    - **Is it niche/situational (e.g. boat) or consumable?**
        - **No:** It is **Minor** (Category C or D).
5. **Is it Legendary?**
    - **Is it consumable or extremely niche?**
        - **Yes:** It is **Category E (Minor Legendary)** (Per user instruction).
        - **No:** It is **Category I (Major Legendary)**.

### The Categories (Reference Table)

#### Common Items

- **Category A: Minor Common**
    - **Definition:** All common items (permanent or consumable).
    - **Shop Price:** 45 – 85 gp.
    - **Examples:** _Potion of Healing_, _Cloak of Billowing_, _Moon-Touched Sword_.

#### Uncommon Items

- **Category B: Minor Uncommon**
    - **Definition:** Utility items that do not provide direct combat math, mobility, or major social hacks.
    - **Shop Price:** 225 – 425 gp.
    - **Examples:** _Alchemy Jug_, _Immovable Rod_, _Cap of Water Breathing_, _Lantern of Revealing_.
- **Category F: Major Uncommon**
    - **Definition:** Items that grant a +1 bonus, flight, resistance, stat setting (e.g. to 19), or extra spells.
    - **Shop Price:** 325 – 525 gp.
    - **Examples:** _Weapon +1_, _Winged Boots_, _Gauntlets of Ogre Power_, _Cloak of Protection_.

#### Rare Items

- **Category C: Minor Rare**
    - **Definition:** Situational items (limited use), niche utility, or consumables of Rare rarity.
    - **Shop Price:** 2,250 – 4,250 gp.
    - **Examples:** _Folding Boat_ (Situational), _Potion of Heroism_, _Bead of Force_.
- **Category G: Major Rare**
    - **Definition:** Permanent items with strong combat or broad utility effects.
    - **Shop Price:** 3,250 – 5,250 gp.
    - **Examples:** _Weapon +2_, _Armor +1_, _Ring of Protection_, _Wand of Fireballs_.

#### Very Rare Items

- **Category D: Minor Very Rare**
    - **Definition:** Niche utility or consumables of Very Rare rarity.
    - **Shop Price:** 22,500 – 42,500 gp.
    - **Examples:** _Nolzur’s Marvelous Pigments_, _Potion of Flying_, _Manual of Golems_.
- **Category H: Major Very Rare**
    - **Definition:** Permanent items that define a character's build or offer high-level spell power.
    - **Shop Price:** 32,500 – 52,500 gp.
    - **Examples:** _Weapon +3_, _Staff of Power_, _Spellguard Shield_.

#### Legendary Items

- **Category E: Minor Legendary**
    - **Definition:** Consumables or extremely niche mechanical items.
    - **Shop Price:** 112,500 – 212,500 gp.
    - **Examples:** _Apparatus of Kwalish_ (Niche), _Potion of Storm Giant Strength_ (Consumable).
- **Category I: Major Legendary**
    - **Definition:** Game-changing permanent artifacts.
    - **Shop Price:** 162,500 – 262,500 gp.
    - **Examples:** _Vorpal Sword_, _Holy Avenger_, _Staff of the Magi_, _Robe of the Archmagi_.
