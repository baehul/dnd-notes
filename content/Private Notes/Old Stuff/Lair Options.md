# Understanding Your Lair

Your Lair takes a unique shape that is steeped in its own unique magic, granting three distinct features to its residents:

* **Regional Effects.** Your Lair is not just a building or an island. These are atmospheric phenomena that constantly permeate your Lair. They don’t carry any specific mechanical benefits but rather have more narrative and roleplay-oriented effects.
* **Lair Defense.** Your Lair comes to your defense. Whenever combat breaks out on/in your Lair, whether defending your Lair from a wandering monster or waking up to an assassin infiltration, these effects trigger to aid you in battle.
* **Mooring Bonus.** Your bond with your Lair extends beyond a physical location. Whenever you connect your Lair to another location and leave your Lair, this bonus travels with you. 
  + This bonus is designed to buff the party as they leave their base to head out on a new mission/adventure and does not trigger every time you step off of your Lair.

# Lair Options
---

## Shady Speakeasy

*A sanctuary of low rafters and stale smoke. The laughter is loud but the glances remain sharp and lingering.*

### Regional Effects

* **Just My Night.** Coins, dice, and other objects of chance always have the result the dealer desires.
* **Wretched Hive of Villainy.** The tavern is a magnet for low-lifes from around the world looking to share the latest rumors they’ve heard over a drink.
* **The Good Stuff.** Alcohol consumed in the Lair is particularly refreshing and always hits the spot, curing even the sourest of moods.

### Lair Defense

* **Pocket Sand****.** Anything goes. During the first round of combat, enemies subtract 1d4 from their attack rolls when targeting allies.

### Mooring Bonus

* **Slip Out The Back.** Allies make a sneaky escape from the Lair. No one sees them leave and allies gain a +10 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks until their next long rest.

## Undead Castle

*A gothic manor of sharp angles and light-drinking stone surrounded by a garden of old roses and a silence that feels like a burial.*

### Regional Effects

* **My Dark, Twisted Soul.** The shadows of creatures here stretch unnaturally long - sometimes pantomiming inner thoughts, even detaching and creating physical disturbances.
* **Back In My Day.** Spectral servants occupy the Lair, tending to the grounds and the castle. If you listen closely, you can hear them occasionally reminisce about the life they had.
* **The Cellar.** Food and drink stored here never spoil, instead suspended in a timeless state that turns them bone-white or pitch-black.

### Lair Defense

* **One Foot In The Grave.** Grasping skeletal hands erupt from the ground to protect the masters of the Lair. During the first round of combat, enemies must treat the ground within 30 feet of allies as difficult terrain..

### Mooring Bonus

* **Wraith Walk.** Allies are one with the veil. Once before their next long rest, each creature can become incorporeal for 1 minute. During this time, they can move through (though not end their turn in) other creatures and solid objects as if they were difficult terrain.

## Witch’s Hut

*A leaning, impossible tangle of creaking wood and bleached bone. The air is heavy with the scent of dried herbs, sharp sulfur, and old secrets.*

### Regional Effects

* **Tea Time.** There is always a teapot boiling that pours out completely random, strange flavors such as “My Biggest Regret” and “The Smell of Rain”.
* **The Grapevine.** The local flora eagerly gossips with anyone who leans in close, whispering incredibly specific, petty opinions on recent visitors.
* **Mini-me.** Crude dolls can be found around the Lair resembling the residents, though there is always something *off* about them.

### Lair Defense

* **Twist Of Fate.** A strange magic falls upon the Lair. During the first round of combat, enemies have a bonus to their saving throws and AC equal to the party’s proficiency bonus. Enemies are vulnerable to the first instance of damage they take each combat.

### Mooring Bonus

* **Witch’s Brew.** A small cup of bright-red tea appears on the table. A small cup of bright-red tea appears on the table. Allies gain temporary hit points equal to their character level plus (their Constitution modifier x 2) that last until their next long rest. In addition, until their next long rest, allies gain double the amount of healing they would receive when drinking a Potion of Healing.

## Magical Library

*A twisting spire of stone and gravity-defying bookshelves. The air is thick with the scent of ozone, old parchment, and the low, electric thrum of raw creation.*

### Regional Effects

* **Arcane Intelligence Powered Assistant.** The library is sentient, with books flying from shelves to answer questions and pages turning themselves.
* **Wandering Scholars.** The spire occasionally attracts traveling scholars, ghosts of ancient historians, and exhausted couriers offering to trade obscure secrets and knowledge for a moment of quiet reading.
* **Self-Mending Tomes.** When given over to the library, damaged papers, spilled ink, and torn books slowly repair themselves over time, returning to mint condition.

### Lair Defense

* **Knowledge Is Power.** During the first round of combat, whenever an ally makes an attack roll or a saving throw, they can roll a d6 and add the number rolled to the total.

### Mooring Bonus

* **Cramming Session.**  Perfectly designed study guides and practice tests make preparation a breeze. Allies choose one skill or tool; until their next long rest, they gain a bonus to all ability checks made with that skill or tool equal to their proficiency bonus.
---

## Ancient Temple

*A stepped pyramid surrounded by lichen-stained statues peaks through the canopy. The air is heavy with petrichor and the sharp, copper tang of ancient, rain-washed altars.*

### Regional Effects

* **Forgotten Ones**. Statues of forgotten gods seem to shift their positions when unseen, watching residents with intense scrutiny.
* **Must Have Been The Wind.** The sounds of eldritch whispers and chanting carry the voices of the dead and forgotten.
* **Blood Thirsty.** Any liquid spilled on the floor - especially blood - is quickly absorbed into the stone without leaving a stain.

### Lair Defense

* **Unsettling Gaze.** The statues' gaze falls on the intruders. During the first round of combat, enemies cannot use reactions.

### Mooring Bonus

* **Whispered Warnings.** Prophecies of doom are difficult to ignore. Until their next long rest, all allies have advantage on Initiative rolls and cannot be surprised.
