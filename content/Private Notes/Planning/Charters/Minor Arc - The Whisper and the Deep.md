# Minor Arc: The Whisper and the Deep

> **DRAFT.** Drafted by the Showrunner as an advisory skeleton and updated with the DM's decisions from 2026-09-26. It has not been through the Continuity Auditor's gate and is not approved. The title is a working title. Session 28 has not been played, so the entry conditions must hold whatever happens to Alexandra. Session 29 (the bridge) needs its own charter.

## Premise
Free of the Library, the party answers Luwei's summons and pays his price. Otis makes amends by carrying Luwei's gift to the deep, and the party gathers proof of Mithbarakaz's double-dealing. The road to both runs through Vase's home at Windmill Island and Silver Moon. At the aboleth's hydrosphere they trade the gift for an eye: the locator that lets the Lair find the Terramancers at last.

## Milestones
- [ ] **Luwei's terms land.** The party answers the summons. Luwei asks two things: the Restitution (Otis carries a sealed coffer of Whisper Reeds, adapted to deep icy water, as a gift for the aboleth) and the Ledger (proof of Mithbarakaz's double-dealing). He supplies the hydrosphere's coordinates. (T-001 advance, T-002 advance)
- [ ] **Windmill Island and Silver Moon.** Vase's homecoming (needs the player's buy-in). Clay Argilla appears. Evidence of Mithbarakaz's double-dealing surfaces but is not resolved. (H-006 service, T-017 plant)
- [ ] **The Deep Hull relic** is taken from Mithbarakaz's hoard, forcing the Silver Moon visit.
- [ ] **The Deep Hull is built** as a downtime recipe on the trip to the hydrosphere. The Lair seals itself into an enclosed, reinforced shell to pass the Membrane.
- [ ] **A deliberate character beat** for Twilight (H-003) and/or Collin (H-001). Cletus's Engine augment (H-005) is a natural third.
- [ ] **The hydrosphere.** The party delivers the coffer. Otis's restitution is complete, and the reeds, which link to Luwei wherever they grow, become a listening post in the aboleth's home. (T-002 resolve)
- [ ] **The eye.** The Six-Eyed Tentacle of the Deep Icy Waters grants a boon in exchange for the great gift: an eye that acts as a locator, letting the Lair travel to the Terramancers. The arc ends with the party holding it. (T-004 resolve, T-012 advance)
- [ ] **News of Alexandra's excommunication** reaches the party during the arc (K-002 ends, T-014).

## Vertical obligation
Deliver Arc 2 the means to find the Terramancers (T-012). This fulfils the "find them" beat of "The Terramancers' Bargain." The Ledger strand is Arc 3 material arriving early: it seeds a fault line among the Resistance that the party must broker later.

## Horizontal obligation
Hand the next sibling a party that holds the eye and a route to reach and bargain with the Terramancers. Leave live: Luwei's leverage over Collin and his seeded Engine co-opt; the listening post; Mithbarakaz's double-dealing (unresolved); and the unidentified mole (T-009).

The party holds the Harmony Engine and the Lair, has left the swamp region, and has planted Auntie Dorian's cutting in the Lair. Varius's case is under way (K-002). Sylas is freed and debriefed. How Alexandra ends in Session 28 is unknown; nothing here depends on it except the tone of the excommunication news.

## Locked constraints
- The Terramancer betrayal and sun-plot stay locked for Arc 4. The eye is a neutral locator: no Terramancer intent is revealed. T-011 and T-015 must not foreshadow it.
- Mithbarakaz's double-dealing is planted, not resolved.
- Luwei's Engine co-opt is seeded, not sprung. He remains a working quest-giver through this arc. His betrayal stays personal and egg-driven, distinct from the Terramancers'.
- The mole (T-009) stays unidentified.
- The aboleth is an Ancient One in name only. It is not "The Eldest" (a separate DM plan). Do not build toward it here.
- The eye locates; it does not deliver. Lair travel stays navigation, not banned teleportation.
- The Lair (with the Harmony Engine) is always the party's vehicle and base.
- Lair additions are downtime recipes done during island-to-island travel. The Mudhollow plant is the exception.
- Published Lair pages must never mention Luwei, reeds or hive-minds.

## Risks
- **Fetch-chain fatigue.** Luwei, then Mithbarakaz, then the hydrosphere, then the eye. Give each leg its own dramatic core: Vase's homecoming and Clay's intrigue; the aboleth as an awe-and-dread scene.
- **Vase's private background.** Windmill Island's hideout, Clay's device, Shrine Island and Teapot are Vase's player's private material. Get his buy-in first.
- **The GSC tracks every Weavesilk sail.** Decide whether the Lair is trackable, since the arc passes through GSC-heavy places.
- **Telegraphing.** Two allied betrayals in a row (Luwei, then the Terramancers) can give away the Arc 4 twist. Stagger and differentiate them.
- **Engine-secret spread.** Decide exactly who learns what about the Engine's capabilities.
- **Under-served characters.** Twilight needs a beat; Vaxen's status is unconfirmed.
