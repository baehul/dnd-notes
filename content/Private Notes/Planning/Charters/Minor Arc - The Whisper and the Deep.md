# Minor Arc: The Whisper and the Deep

> **DRAFT.** Reconciled 2026-11-16 with play through Session 32 and with the retired Planning Backlog, whose live plan and open items were folded in here. The outline, including Luwei's terms, is still the DM's plan (confirmed 2026-11-16). Items marked [DM?] wait on the DM. The title is a working title. Adventures under this arc are islands; the voyages between them are downtime. Current adventure: [[Adventure - The Jade Fragment]]. For the current position read [[Current State]].

## Premise
Free of the Library, the party sailed for Luwei's island and freed Sylas on the way. On the Jade Fragment they answer Luwei's summons and pay his price. Otis makes amends by planting Whisper Reeds in his own garden on the party's Lair island, and the party gathers proof of Mithbarakaz's double-dealing. The road to both runs through Vase's home at Windmill Island and Silver Moon. At the aboleth's hydrosphere, where Twilight's wife Dawn is at work, they buy an eye with Mithbarakaz's money: the heart of Cletus's Keystone Lens. The Lens lets the Harmony Engine spend a key, and the Ring of Restless Revelation is the first.

## Design rules
- The Lair (with the Harmony Engine) is always the party's vehicle and base.
- Lair additions are downtime recipes done during island-to-island travel (the desk, the Deep Hull, the Keystone Lens).
- The eye does not locate. The Lens spends a key to set a course the Lair still sails: navigation, not banned teleportation. Keys are narrative, used up on reading, and handed out by the DM as a gate (Lair Shop.md:12).

## Locked constraints
- The Terramancer betrayal and sun-plot stay locked for Arc 4. The eye, the Lens and the ring's last reading are neutral: they set a course and reveal no Terramancer intent, and the ring's final act carries no new message. T-011, T-015 and Sylas's Terramancy scholarship must not foreshadow the betrayal.
- Mithbarakaz's double-dealing is planted, not resolved.
- Luwei's Engine co-opt is seeded, not sprung. He remains a working quest-giver through this arc. His betrayal stays personal and egg-driven, distinct from the Terramancers'.
- The mole (T-009) stays unidentified. Dawn's presence at the hydrosphere must neither identify nor clear it, and H-003 stays unresolved.
- The aboleth is an Ancient One in name only. It is not "The Eldest" (a separate DM plan, the Three Fates). Do not build toward it here.
- Published Lair pages must never mention Luwei, reeds or hive-minds.

## Risks
- **Fetch-chain fatigue.** Sylas, then Luwei's trial, then Mithbarakaz, then the hydrosphere, then the Lens. Give each leg its own dramatic core: Sylas's waking; Vase's homecoming and Clay's intrigue; Dawn and the aboleth as an awe-and-dread scene; the ring's last reading.
- **Vase's private background.** Windmill Island's hideout, Clay's device, Shrine Island and Teapot are Vase's player's private material. Get his buy-in first.
- **Twilight's background.** Dawn is in Twilight's player-authored background (Twilight Emberrest.md:40-42). Get the player's buy-in before she appears at the hydrosphere.
- **The GSC tracks every Weavesilk sail.** Decide whether the Lair is trackable, since the arc passes through GSC-heavy places.
- **Telegraphing.** Two allied betrayals in a row (Luwei, then the Terramancers) can give away the Arc 4 twist. Stagger and differentiate them.
- **Engine-secret spread, now live.** In Session 32 Collin told the tenders he has news for the master about magic that lets islands grow and merge. Decide exactly who learns what about the Engine's capabilities.
- **Under-served characters.** Twilight has had no hook serviced since well before the Jade Fragment; his beat is Dawn (H-003). Vaxen carries the restless blade (I-008) and needs room for it.
