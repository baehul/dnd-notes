# Session: 30 (downtime, under way)

## Premise
The Lair is already sailing for the Jade Fragment. In the downtime Sylas is freed from his shell, wakes with a clean memory gap, and opens his shop as the Curator in Exile. The party spends, crafts, and nears Luwei's island.

## Milestones
- [ ] Sylas is freed in a three-gate downtime skill challenge that fails forward: he is freed whatever the rolls, and failure costs time or resources, not Sylas. (T-006 resolve)
- [ ] The memory gap lands: his last memory is Smoke's Speakeasy (Professor Sylas Thistlethorn.md:34). His attacker and his discovery stay unknown. (T-031 plant, T-025 kept open)
- [ ] The shop opens only once he is free, with an opening stock. There is no restock until the first mooring, soft components cost +10% under way, and mail orders arrive at the next mooring (Lair Shop.md:6-8).
- [ ] Recipes built or begun with hard components in hand: the desk (I-003), Marionette's Fang (the tooth is held), the Pearl of Power (I-011; the sand exists only after the rescue) and the Armor of Acid Resistance (Ooze Core held, Lair Shop.md:33).
- [ ] The Bards web is only a lead pointing at the Jade Fragment (I-009); the ritual cannot start (Lair Shop.md:96).
- [ ] The Keystone Lens is not built (no eye; H-005), and the ring is not spent (I-012).

## Vertical obligation
Delivers the minor arc's first milestone: Sylas freed and the shop running, the economy the arc's Lair additions rely on. It puts a Terramancy scholar aboard (Professor Sylas Thistlethorn.md:20-22), serving Arc 2's "learns about the Terramancers" (Major Arcs.md:19). His scholarship stays neutral.

## Horizontal obligation
Ends with the Lair arriving at, or about to arrive at, the Jade Fragment, its first mooring. Luwei's demand on Collin and Otis is still waiting (T-001). The Bards lead, Luwei's leave to hunt and the shop's first restock give the next session reasons beyond the summons.
