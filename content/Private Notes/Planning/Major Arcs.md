# Major Arcs — The Shattered World

The campaign's top-level spine: a sequence of DM-owned major arcs. Each is a fixed ceiling the
Showrunner treats as read-only spec — every minor arc / adventure / session beneath it must
serve the major arc it falls under. High-level by design; an arc gets fleshed out when we
charter the minor arcs beneath it.

## Arc 1 — Meeting & the Lair *(done)*
The party comes together, finds the [[Harmony Engine]], and secures their own lair.
- **Hands off:** an established party with a base of operations, ready to be drawn into the
  Resistance's cause.

## Arc 2 — The Terramancers' Bargain *(in progress)*
The party learns about [[The Terramancers]], finds them, and convinces them to give
[[The Resistance]] a chance. The Terramancers agree to help — *if* the party can bring the
Resistance together.
- **Hands off:** the mandate for Arc 3 — a promised alliance, conditional on uniting the
  Resistance.

## Arc 3 — Uniting the Resistance
The party visits each organization within [[The Resistance]] and does what it takes to convince
them to work together, uniting them. They receive devices from [[The Terramancers]] to mobilize
the Resistance's islands.
- **Hands off:** a united, mobilized Resistance able to take the fight to [[The Crusade]].

## Arc 4 — War & Betrayal
The party leads the united Resistance against [[The Crusade]] and beats them. They discover
[[The Terramancers]]' plot to destroy the sun and are backstabbed by them. The endgame becomes a
forced alliance with the Crusade to stop the Terramancers.
- **Campaign end-state:** the Terramancers are stopped and the sun preserved (former enemies
  forced to cooperate); failure = the sun is destroyed.

## Fixed constraints
- These four arcs are the fixed, DM-owned spine and their order is locked; every minor arc lives
  beneath one of them and must serve it.
- The **Terramancer betrayal and the sun-plot** are the locked twist — surfaced in Arc 4, not to
  be contradicted or prematurely revealed by lower charters.
