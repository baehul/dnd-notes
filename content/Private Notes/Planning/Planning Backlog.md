### Design rules
- The Lair (with the Harmony Engine) is always the party's vehicle and base.
- Lair additions are downtime recipes done during island-to-island travel (the desk, the Deep Hull, the Keystone Lens).
- The aboleth is an Ancient One in name only. It is not "The Eldest"; the DM has a separate plan (the Three Fates). Do not build toward it here.
- The eye does not locate. It is the Keystone Lens's hard component. The Lens spends a key to set a course the Lair still sails (navigation, not teleportation). Keys are narrative, used up on reading, and DM-gated. The compass (T-028) is with House Aurum and is not a player option.
- Plant, do not resolve: Mithbarakaz's double-dealing. Seed, do not spring: Luwei's Engine co-opt. The mole (T-009) stays unidentified. The Terramancer betrayal and sun-plot stay locked for Arc 4.
- Differentiate Luwei's betrayal (personal, egg-driven) from the Terramancers' (ideological, cosmic) so the twist is not telegraphed.
