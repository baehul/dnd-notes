- **Exit the Library.** A Scroll of Teleport (not a robe find) lets the party leave. House ruling: it works within a tectonic island and fails between islands. It carries the caster plus up to 8 willing creatures. Anyone may try to read it, with a DC 17 ability check. Alexandra counts as willing only in a lucid moment, and Sylas in the shell counts as an object. Destinations: the Rat Race Private Detective Agency, in front of Auntie Dorian's hut, and the Lair (the moored Lair counts as part of the Sinking City).
- **Debrief** with Professor Sylas Thistlethorn (freed from stasis) and High Inquisitor Varius (the secret client, a shapeshifter). Resolves T-005, T-006, T-007 and T-008. Varius's promised holy texts to Vase will be a copy of the Codex Solari, handed over outright (DM decision, 2026-10-05); they are not a Library book.
- **K-002 starts** when the party hands over evidence. Varius needs about two weeks to take it, build his case, present it and get Alexandra excommunicated. It ends during the next arc as news the party hears (T-014). It depends on Alexandra's fate.
- **Auntie Dorian (I-001).** DM decision (2026-10-03): her service is the restless blade (I-008), a cursed, sentient Greatsword for Vaxen (it can reshape to a Greataxe) holding an unnamed restless spirit the party must carry and help find rest. It replaces the Mudhollow cutting, so there is no Lair plant, no Lair effect and no recipe. Dorian hands over the restless blade in Session 29; the service continues afterward as a long-term quest. Breaking the binding (Remove Curse) or abandoning the blade angers the spirits; that is the consequence, and the service stays open. Design is locked; see I-008 and `the restless blade - DM notes`. The cutting's planned tell, wilting near hidden watchers (a Luwei tell), is lost with it; the DM ruled on 2026-10-05 that there is no replacement tell.
- **Handoff.** Collin feels his patron's pull. Luwei already knows the party holds the Harmony Engine.

### The next minor arc: "The Whisper and the Deep"
Draft charter: `Charters/Minor Arc - The Whisper and the Deep.md`. In short:
- The party goes to Luwei. His terms: the **Restitution** (Otis carries a sealed coffer of Whisper Reeds, a gift for the aboleth; the reeds link to Luwei wherever they grow, so it is also a listening post) and the **Ledger** (proof of Mithbarakaz's double-dealing, planted but not resolved). Luwei supplies the hydrosphere's coordinates.
- **Windmill Island and Silver Moon.** Vase's father Clay Argilla (sky pirate captain of the stolen GSC Albatross, sponsored by Mithbarakaz) appears. The Deep Hull relic comes from Mithbarakaz's hoard.
- **The Deep Hull.** The Lair seals itself into an enclosed, reinforced shell to breach a hydrosphere's Membrane. It is built as a downtime recipe during the trip.
- **The hydrosphere.** The party offers the coffer to the Six-Eyed Tentacle of the Deep Icy Waters, who grants the eye: a locator that lets the Lair travel to the Terramancers.
- **End state:** the party holds the eye.

### Design rules
- The Lair (with the Harmony Engine) is always the party's vehicle and base.
- Lair additions are downtime recipes done during island-to-island travel (the desk, the Deep Hull).
- The aboleth is an Ancient One in name only. It is not "The Eldest"; the DM has a separate plan (the Three Fates). Do not build toward it here.
- The eye locates; it does not deliver. It starts the search for the Terramancers.
- Plant, do not resolve: Mithbarakaz's double-dealing. Seed, do not spring: Luwei's Engine co-opt. The mole (T-009) stays unidentified. The Terramancer betrayal and sun-plot stay locked for Arc 4.
- Differentiate Luwei's betrayal (personal, egg-driven) from the Terramancers' (ideological, cosmic) so the twist is not telegraphed.
