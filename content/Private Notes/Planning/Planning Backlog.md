### The next minor arc: "The Whisper and the Deep"
Draft charter: `Charters/Minor Arc - The Whisper and the Deep.md`. In short:
- The party goes to Luwei. His terms: the **Restitution** (Otis carries a sealed coffer of Whisper Reeds, a gift for the aboleth; the reeds link to Luwei wherever they grow, so it is also a listening post) and the **Ledger** (proof of Mithbarakaz's double-dealing, planted but not resolved). Luwei supplies the hydrosphere's coordinates.
- **Windmill Island and Silver Moon.** Vase's father Clay Argilla (sky pirate captain of the stolen GSC Albatross, sponsored by Mithbarakaz) appears. The Deep Hull relic comes from Mithbarakaz's hoard.
- **The Deep Hull.** The Lair seals itself into an enclosed, reinforced shell to breach a hydrosphere's Membrane. It is built as a downtime recipe during the trip.
- **The hydrosphere.** The party offers the coffer to the Six-Eyed Tentacle of the Deep Icy Waters, who grants the eye: a locator that lets the Lair travel to the Terramancers.
- **End state:** the party holds the eye.

### Design rules
- The Lair (with the Harmony Engine) is always the party's vehicle and base.
- Lair additions are downtime recipes done during island-to-island travel (the desk, the Deep Hull). The Mudhollow plant is the exception.
- The aboleth is an Ancient One in name only. It is not "The Eldest"; the DM has a separate plan (the Three Fates). Do not build toward it here.
- The eye locates; it does not deliver. It starts the search for the Terramancers.
- Plant, do not resolve: Mithbarakaz's double-dealing. Seed, do not spring: Luwei's Engine co-opt. The mole (T-009) stays unidentified. The Terramancer betrayal and sun-plot stay locked for Arc 4.
- Differentiate Luwei's betrayal (personal, egg-driven) from the Terramancers' (ideological, cosmic) so the twist is not telegraphed.
