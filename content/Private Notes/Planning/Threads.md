# Threads

DM-only tracker for open story lines, hooks, clocks and obligations. It lives under `Private Notes/` and never publishes. The Steward updates it after each session summary; the Loremaster, Showrunner and Continuity Auditor read it.

Seeded from the 27 session recaps (through Session 27) and updated through Session 28. Prep files were not used as evidence. Rows marked *check* need a DM confirmation.

## Legend
- **Live** — actively happening or being pursued.
- **Dormant** — a future hook or reference, not currently in play.
- **Resolved** — closed; the row moves to the archive at the bottom.
- **Owed / Done / Deferred** — used only for owed pages and records.
- **Knows** — who holds the information: the whole party, named characters, or the DM only. Private asides between the DM and one player are recorded here by character name.
- **Arc** — the Major Arc the row serves (see [[Major Arcs]]); a dash means none or unclear.
- **IDs are permanent and never reused.** Charters refer to rows by ID: T (threads), H (PC hooks), K (clocks), I (items and obligations), P (owed pages and records).
