## The spirit
- Unnamed. No stated origin; no tie to Vaxen's relatives, the Session 15 dream warrior, H-002, or the Mudhollows. It never names itself, never mentions Vaxen's family, and deflects every question about its past.
- It is Vaxen's mirror: swagger, arrogance, mischief. It stands where Vaxen used to stand, hungry for a worthy death. Vaxen leads it forward as much as it reflects them.
- Purpose (custom, not from the DMG list): a glorious death.
- **Its belief:** it can rest only by *dying* in battle against a worthy foe, not by killing one. The belief is wrong.
- What it imagines that means: the blade shattering against a worthy foe's weapon, or going down with its wielder in a fight worth the fall. It does not actually know what death means for a soul bound in steel.
- No DMG Conflict saves. Its pressure comes through Glory's Pull, Scorn, and talk.

## Stage 1 — Bound (Uncommon, F; cursed; sentient)
As on the player page.
Rulings:
- Graze (Greatsword form) or Cleave (Greataxe form) applies per the current form, as normal.
- If the Quarry changes before a compelled Glory's Pull turn, the pull aims at whoever is the Quarry when that turn starts.
- Relentless Endurance leaves Vaxen Bloodied, so the pull keeps working. This is intended.

## Stage 2 — Wavering (Uncommon, F; still cursed; still sentient; Wisdom rises 8 → 12)
- Scorn ends.
- **Steadied.** If an ally within 30 feet of Vaxen is Bloodied when Glory's Pull triggers, or when the compelled turn begins, Vaxen may ignore it.
- **Redirect Quarry.** As a Bonus Action, Vaxen makes a creature they can see that has damaged an ally since the end of their last turn the new Quarry.
- **Close the Distance.** Reaction, when an ally within 30 feet of Vaxen drops to 0 Hit Points: Vaxen moves up to half their Speed toward them without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
- Unwilling, Glory's Pull (as modified above), Quarry, the +1 bonus and Remove Curse are unchanged. Update the player page's stats only on your own timing.

## Stage 3 — Released (Rare, G; not cursed; not sentient)
- +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls. Quarry ends, and so do the curse and the voice.
- Requires attunement. Vaxen's attunement carries over and can now be ended normally. Reshape continues.
- **Close the Distance** (as above).
- **Stand With Them.** Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest. When a creature within Vaxen's reach hits an ally, Vaxen can use their Reaction to make one attack with this weapon against that creature.
  - Graze applies in Greatsword form. In Greataxe form, Cleave may trigger (once per turn, as normal).
  - Great Weapon Master's Heavy Weapon Mastery damage does not apply, because this isn't the Attack action on their turn.
  - Hew can't follow, because there is no Bonus Action off-turn.
  - It shares their single Reaction with Close the Distance and Opportunity Attacks.

## True rest condition (judged from story beats; never a counter)
- **A beat counts only if it costs Vaxen something they wanted.** Choices made just to advance the weapon don't count.
- **Bound → Wavering:** Vaxen gives up a fight they wanted, or a Quarry kill, to protect or save an ally.
- **Wavering → Released:** a truly worthy foe stands before them, the death they once sought is on the table, and they choose to live for their friends or family. The closing beat is a living-legacy moment: they say, and show, that their family name is carried by living, not by dying.
- **The spirit rests because it finally wants what Vaxen chose.** It was never the death.

## Voice guide
Never use an "Unkillable" taunt or any line that sounds like Vaxen's family.

Bound:
- "That one. The big one with the ugly shield. Make it worth singing about."
- "Help? *Help?* Swing me or sheathe me."
- "My past? Long, glorious, none of your business. Ask about my future. It ends magnificently. Against something worth dying to, steel and all."

Wavering:
- "You walked away from a good fight for that one. ...I'm not saying it was wrong. I'm saying I noticed."
- "Fine. The one who hurt them. That's worth something too, I suppose."
- "Stop asking what I was. I'm busy working out what I am."

Release (last words, then silence):
- "Huh. So that's what the door looks like."
- "I wanted the wrong ending. Yours is better. Go on, then."

## Consequences, not failure
Dorian's service is a long-term quest and is never "failed". Ending the binding with Remove Curse, or abandoning the blade, violates the old magic and angers the swamp's spirits (the same spirits as in Session 17: furious, souls "heavy"). How that anger shows is the DM's call. The blade's spirit is still restless and the service is still open.

## If Vaxen dies before the spirit rests
Handled at the time, with no mechanic. The DM leans toward the curse and the burden ending with Vaxen. The item grants no death-avoidance of any kind (House Rules: Revivify only, no loopholes).
