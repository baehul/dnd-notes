
- Crusade

		- Crusade ideology focueses on three things

	- Religion
		- Solari Faith
			- Beliefs
				- The physical sun IS solaris
				- The Shattering was Solaris trying to separate the good from the evil
				- Benevolent Father to Believers, Scouring Fire to corrupt and darkness
				- Other gods exist bu they are not divie. They feed off confusion and darkness, called Idols of Shaow
			- Practices and Rituals
				- Daily Worship
					- Greet and thank Solaris at Dawn 
					- Small prayer praising Solaris at noon
					- Thank Solaris and pray for strength to get through to endure the night at sunset
				- The hearth fire of the home must never go out between dusk and dawn
				- Ash from the hearth fire is put on forehead like a teeka
				- Things are burned to purify them
				- Faithful are cremated, unfaithful are buried
