Backstory

NAME has been part of the Cult of the Goddess Whose Name is Night for a long time. As a child, he showed a basic aptitude for magic, earning him a place at various magical schools and academies within the cult. Unfortunately, that ‘place’ often ended up being the bottom of the class. While he demonstrated some basic abilities with magic, he was never on par with any of his peers. Spells that others picked up on quickly seemed to just fizzle out when he tried learning them. Through sheer effort and grit, he was often able to learn the magic, just at a much slower pace than those around him.

One area he always found interesting was artificing. In his younger years, he was fascinated by the idea of tinkering with small machines and imbuing them with magic to see what they would do. While he was proficient at these small feats, he hit that familiar brick wall when attempting anything more advanced.

While the Cult follows its philosophy of exploring different truths, it only managed to shield him from ridicule from his teachers. His peers often looked down upon him, with the more conceited ones often taunting and bullying him for his lackluster magical ability. Even without this harsh treatment from his teachers, they tended to treat him with pity. This left him with a lack of belief that followed him and further hampered his attempts at learning magic and artificing.

One day, he was tinkering with one of his latest machines on campus. The usual group of bullies saw his unorthodox designs and prototype, laughing and asking him, “Is this your best creation yet, NAME?”. After yet another failure attempting to imbue this creation with magic, he felt the despair seep in. But that despair was soon replaced with anger, rage, and fury. All his fellow students ever did was doubt him. Even his teachers doubted him, despite their attempts to hide it. He wanted more than anything to show them that they were wrong about him. The words echoed in his mind, *is this your best creation yet?* You know what, YES, this WAS his best creation yet. This sudden rush of belief and confidence swirled within him as his latest attempt succeeded with flying colors. For perhaps the first time, his hard work and perseverance truly had paid off. At first, thought of showing off his new skills to those who mocked him, but now he had bigger dreams. This single contraption would not impress them. It would not impress his teachers. He set out now to prove his abilities to everyone around him. And what better way to do that than showing off his designs on the front lines of this brewing conflict with the Crusade?

Wants his farming equipment inventions to revolutionize agriculture, but thinks he needs to prove himself with inventions for war first.
