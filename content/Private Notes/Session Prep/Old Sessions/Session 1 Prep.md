## 1. The Strong Start (Cinematic Cutscene)
* **Visual:** Fade in on a room of blinding, absolute white light. There are no shadows here. In the center, [[Lancaster Slickwhistle]] (a Drow) is bound to a chair, his eyes squinting and watering in agony against the brilliance. Despite the torture, he attempts to maintain his suave posture. 
*  [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]] enters, his silhouette sharp against the glare. He adjusts his arcanotech monocle and places a cigarette into a holder with precise, jerky movements. 
* Ephraim circles the chair. "Remarkable. Three days. Most break within three hours under the lamps. You have a fortitude that is... inconvenient." 
* Lancaster offers a pained, bloody smile. "I've always found the decor in the [[Sun Spire]] a bit loud, Duke. Though I suppose subtlety isn't a House Aurum trait.". 
* Ephraim stops pacing. He leans in close. "The Showcase is in a few days. My brother, the High Baron, expects results. I expect results. Where is it?". 
* Lancaster feigns ignorance.
* Ephraim loses his composure, slamming a fist onto the armrest of the chair. "Enough games!" 
* Ephraim composes himself, smoothing his coat. He leans into Lancaster's ear, whispering over the hum of the lights: "Soak it in, Mr. Slickwhistle. Enjoy the light while you have it. Because very soon, for you and everyone else... it is going to run out." 
* The light brightens to a blinding white
