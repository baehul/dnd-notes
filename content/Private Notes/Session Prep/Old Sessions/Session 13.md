**1. Review Characters**

- **Collin (Eobard):** Has the _Ring of Restless Revelation_. Knows a specific Infernal phrase taught by Jitterhop.
    
- **Vase, Otis, Twilight, Cletus, Vaxen:** Will need to navigate Sendryr's test of faith and perception, and help decide the political/exploratory fate of the Resistance.
    

**2. Strong Start: The Obsidian Pantomime**

- **The Scene:** Collin dreams of a suffocatingly hot, obsidian cavern smelling of sulfur and ozone. Deep in the Underworld, Lestirmek Terramancers stand over a person-sized, glowing rock model of the Shattered World.
    
- **The Interaction:** Communication is difficult. Using precise, beautiful Terramancy, they pull out an "Upper Crust" island and an "Underworld" island. They cast magic, and both islands morph into familiar shapes (the Monastery of Night and a deep-strata island), each marked with a blinking light.
    
- **The Revelation:** They point to a central crystal in the room blinking in unison with the islands. With a sharp hiss of steam, they mold molten Resonance crystal and rock into a navigation console, gesturing that Collin's _Ring of Restless Revelation_ fits perfectly inside it as the key/compass.
    
- **Mechanics:** When Collin wakes up, the lingering magic requires a **DC 20 Constitution saving throw**. (Grant **Advantage** since the Terramancers used visual pantomime instead of grinding Infernal speech). On a failure, Collin takes **1 level of Exhaustion**. A **DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check** synthesizes the dream: _The Lestirmek want to talk, but the party must build/find this navigation device to safely steer their way down._
    

**3. Outline Scenes**

- **Scene 1: Waking Up.** Collin shares the details of his vision and the navigation device with the party.
    
- **Scene 2: Sendryr’s Test of Truth.** The party meets Sendryr in a quiet, star-dappled chamber of the Monastery. She initiates a combat trial to test if they can accept multiple truths simultaneously.
    
    - _The Mechanic:_ She conjures shadow-beasts (**Lion** statblock). If players close both eyes, they gain the **Blinded condition** (attacks against them have Advantage, their attacks have Disadvantage). If they keep _both_ eyes open, they only see the physical beasts. If they keep **one eye open and one eye closed**, they can see both the physical beasts _and_ the glowing magical runes/weak points Sendryr has hidden in the dark. _(No mechanical penalties for one eye closed to keep the fight snappy!)_
	    - - **Hint 1 (Focus on the physical vs. magical):** _Half in dark, (3)_ _Half in the blin-ding light. (6)_ _To walk the line you must live in both. (9)_ _Wink at the sha-dow's edge, (6)_ _Find the way. (3)_
	    
		- **Hint 2 (Focus on the mechanical solution):** _Do not blind, (3)_ _The sight lim-its the mind. (6)_ _Close one door to o-pen up the next. (9)_ _Keep one an-chored in light, (6)_ _I am Night. (3)_
		    
		- **Hint 3 (Direct combat hint if they are struggling):** _Look a-head, (3)_ _The beast bares its sharp teeth. (6)_ _But ma-gic weaves through the un-seen dark. (9)_ _Split your gaze to see both, (6)_ _Truth is two. (3)_
        
- **Scene 3: Sendryr’s Blessing.** Upon passing, Sendryr gifts them her **Resonance Crystal**. She warns them not to blind themselves to the many truths they will encounter on the difficult path ahead.
    
- **Scene 4: The Mock Engine (The Turning Point).** The party takes the crystal to Professor Jitterhop. He slots it into the mock Harmony Engine. The room fills with a gorgeous, resolving chord and the scent of ancient, damp earth. The math is perfect. But... it's too small.
    
- **Scene 5: The Dual Path.** Jitterhop explains the failure. To move actual islands, they need Lairs (sentient magical anchors) to act as steering wheels. To scale the engine up safely, they need the Lestirmek. The party ends the session with two massive, distinct quests to choose from.
    

**4. Secrets and Clues**

- The Lestirmek Terramancers are actively monitoring the Upper Crust, but lack the physical means to safely navigate the void to reach it.
    
- The _Ring of Restless Revelation_ is not just a communication device; it is the physical key to a massive subterranean navigation compass.
    
- The Harmony Engine alone cannot steer an island safely through the broken Ley Lines; it requires the symbiotic, sentient bond of an Ancient One's Lair to act as a rudder.
    
- Professor Jitterhop's math is flawlessly accurate, proving that Terramancy can restore the world, but the material cost requires resources only found deep in the Underworld.
    
- Sendryr believes that the Crusade’s greatest weakness is their absolute certainty in a single Truth; flexibility is the Resistance's greatest weapon.
    

**5. Fantastic Locations**

- **The Lestirmek Cavern (Dreamscape):** Oppressively hot, lit by the hellish red glow of magma but contrasted by the delicate, impossibly beautiful golden threads of Terramancy magic.
    
- **Sendryr’s Proving Ground:** A serene, circular meditation chamber in the Monastery. The air is cool and smells of night-blooming jasmine. Shadows here seem to move a half-second slower than the people casting them.
    
- **Jitterhop’s Laboratory:** A frantic mess of chalk dust and crumpled blueprints. When the mock Engine turns on, the chaotic lab is temporarily bathed in an aura of pre-Shattering perfection and perfect harmony.
    

**6. Important NPCs**

- **Lestirmek Terramancers (Dream):** Desperate, ancient, visually expressive.
    
- **Sendryr Ogami:** Serene, ethereal, speaks in riddles and verse.
    
    - _Hint 1:_ "Close one door to o-pen up the next. Keep one an-chored in light, I am Night."
        
    - _Hint 2:_ "Split your gaze to see both, Truth is two."
        
- **Professor Jitterhop:** Frantic and babbling, but becomes suddenly, reverently quiet when the mock Harmony Engine actually sings its perfect chord.
    

**7. Relevant Monsters**

- **Shadow Beasts (Lion statblock):** Conjured by Sendryr. Describe them as shifting masses of starlight and ink that hit with terrifying, physical force.
    

**8. Select Treasure**

- **Sendryr’s Resonance Crystal:** A vital component for the Harmony Engine.
    
- **The Terramancer Blueprint (Mental):** Collin's newfound knowledge of the shape and function of the Underworld Navigation Device.
