### 1. Review Characters

- **Otis, Vaxen, Collin, & Vase:** The swamp's curse specifically targets these four. The angry spirits recognize that they have turned their backs on their families. The spirits know that "severed roots rot," and that emotional rot acts as a beacon for the Mud-Hollows.
    

### 2. Strong Start: The Slog and The Ambush

The party enters the swamp and is immediately plunged into a grueling slog, sinking to their waists in dark water and ankles in thick, pulling mud. A dense, silvery fog hangs over everything, dampening sound. Amidst the miserable trudge, they spot a breathtaking, eerie sight: hundreds of glass bottles hanging from gnarled cypress branches, filled with twinkling beads, small rough gems, and liquids (blood, honey) mixed with alcohol, catching stray beams of moonlight. As they are distracted by the haunting beauty, the water erupts. A **Giant Crocodile** attacks, trailed by a frantic **Swarm of Piranhas (Quippers)** hoping to scavenge the leftovers.

### 3. Outline Scenes

- **The Ferryman's Toll:** After the ambush, a man approaches on a flat-bottomed punt, pushing through the water with a long wooden pole. He calls the party "Dry Boots" and warns they are lucky to have missed the Mud-Hollows. He offers passage, but his price is a personal story and for each of them to fill a bottle with something precious and strong alcohol to trick and trap the angry territorial spirits.
    
- **The Snapped Pole:** The silvery fog thickens, and the Ferryman loses his way. In frustration, he drives his pole down to pivot the punt, but it gets wedged in an ancient, submerged root. With a deafening _CRACK_ that echoes through the stillness, the pole snaps. The boat is adrift.
    
- **The Gauntlet to the Hut:** To reach the distant, flickering amber light of Auntie Dorian's stilt-hut, the party must physically and mentally push the broken punt through a 4-Stage Environmental Hazard _(see Fantastic Locations for mechanics)_.
    
- **Auntie Dorian’s Sanctuary:** The battered party reaches the stilt-hut, smelling of petrichor and crushed mint. The haggish witch doctor offers them food and shelter while the Ferryman makes repairs. She reveals the curse of the "severed roots" and warns that they will never leave the swamp unless the spirits are placated. She will defer her payment, extracting an ambiguous, magical price/favor on their return trip out of the city.
    
- **The Spirit Chase:** Auntie Dorian provides bottles filled with moonshine, dried vegetation, and crushed resonance crystals. The party must venture back onto the water. They have 1 round to chase the fleeting, luminescent red spirits across the combat grid before the spirits dive into the muck and mutate into **Mud Hulks**.
    

### 4. Secrets & Clues

- A few travelers came through the swamp recently, but they stubbornly refused the Ferryman's help, calling him a "heretic" and an "idol of shadow" before trudging off into the deadly fog alone.
    
- The Ferryman is no mere mortal, but an ancient spirit of the swamp. He will give the party a **gleaming gold tooth**—tossing it into the murky water will summon his punt on their return journey.
    
- Auntie Dorian’s presence predates the nearby city; she was here when it was nothing but a humble village. While visitors have dwindled, she has recently hosted **Blood Horde** travelers who still know the old ways and pay their proper respects.
    
- Auntie Dorian is an exile. She was cast out of the city for practicing the **Old Path**, driven into the swamp by a populace falling increasingly under the rigid, intolerant influence of the **Crusade**.
    

### 5. Fantastic Locations

- **The Silver Swamp:** A sprawling, flooded cypress forest blanketed in thick fog. Beautiful, bioluminescent flora and shimmering glass bottles contrast with the deadly, unseen depths.
    
- **The Gauntlet (Environmental Hazard):**
    
    - _Stage 1: Labyrinth of Fog._ **DC 13 Wisdom (Perception)** or **Intelligence (Arcana)** to navigate the shifting roots. _Failure:_ Boat crashes; **DC 12 Dexterity save** or fall Prone, taking **1d6 Bludgeoning**.
        
    - _Stage 2: Bioluminescent Mire._ Gorgeous cyan/violet glowing lotus vines choke the hull. **DC 14 Strength (Athletics)** or **Intelligence (Nature)** to push through. _Failure:_ **DC 12 Constitution save** or gain the **Poisoned** condition for 1 hour from soporific glowing pollen.
        
    - _Stage 3: Glass-Thief Swarm._ Shimmering, stained-glass-winged moths swarm the party's precious items. **DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** or **Wisdom (Animal Handling)** to swat/calm them. _Failure:_ Take **1d4 Slashing** damage; the swarm drops a minor trinket into the swamp.
        
    - _Stage 4: Chimes of the Severed Roots._ Moonlit illusions of abandoned family members stand on the water, begging the party to step out. **DC 14 Wisdom (Insight)** or **Wisdom saving throw**. _Failure:_ Character is **Charmed**, steps into the water taking **1d6 Cold** damage and gains **1 level of Exhaustion**.
        
- **Auntie Dorian's Stilt-Hut:** A haven of Old Path magic elevated above the muck, filled with drying herbs, bubbling cauldrons, and strange bone totems.
    

### 6. Important NPCs

- **The Ferryman:** An ancient, deeply superstitious swamp spirit bound to his punt. Respects those who honor the old rituals; dismissive of rigid "Dry Boots."
    
- **Auntie Dorian:** A haggish witch doctor and Old Path practitioner. Pragmatic, hospitable, but transactional. She sees the deep, rotting emotional wounds of the party and knows how to cure them—for a price to be named later.
    

### 7. Relevant Monsters

- **Giant Crocodile** & **Swarm of Quippers (Piranhas)**: Standard ambush predators.
    
- **Red Spirits / Mud Hulks (CR 3)**:
    
    - _Mechanic:_ Combat begins as a fast-paced chase. After 1 round, the graceful Red Spirits plunge into the mud, erupting as grotesque, lumbering Mud Hulks.
        
    - _The Ritual:_ When a Mud Hulk is reduced to 0 HP, the mud sloughs off, revealing the beautiful Red Spirit for a split second. A player within **15 feet** must use their **Reaction** to unstop their bottle and capture it.
        

### 8. Select Treasure

- **The Belly of the Beast:** An undigested, heavily waxed leather satchel inside the Giant Crocodile. Contains a sum of gold/platinum and a gleaming, pristine silver holy symbol of the Crusade (leftover from the travelers who refused the Ferryman).
    
- **Auntie Dorian’s Brew:** For completing the ritual, she gifts a corked vial of swirling, luminescent emerald liquid smelling of petrichor. It is a **Potion of Vitality** (or a custom _Swamp-Root Potion_ granting resistance to Necrotic and Poison for 1 hour).
    
- **Crystallized Spirit-Ember:** The byproduct of boiling the bottled spirits in the cauldron. A rough-cut, ruby-like reagent that is warm to the touch and pulses faintly like a heartbeat. A potent core of raw Old Path magic to be used in future high-level crafting or enchanting.
