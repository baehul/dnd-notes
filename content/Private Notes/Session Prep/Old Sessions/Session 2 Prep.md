## 1. The Strong Start
* The session opens immediately with [[Collin McCambridge]] experiencing a psychic seizure after the [[Resonance Crystals]] accident.
* **Vision Imagery (Project Orchestra):** He sees the shattered world slowly coming together like the shattering in reverse. He hears a hum like an interstellar chord—a perfect, terrifying musical chord (The Harmony Engine) that vibrates through his bones.
* **Vision Imagery (Backstory):** The scene shifts to a golden room (House Aurum). He sees a faceless noble (his father, [[High Baron Magnus Aurum]]) placing a golden crown on a pile of corpses. A voice whispers, "The ninth son returns to the mold".

## 2. Secrets & Clues
- [x]  [[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe]] is short on her delivery due to the lost barrel. She is desperate and terrified of her debt to the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]], making her vulnerable to taking risky jobs from [[House Aurum]].
- [x] The shipment is being received by [[House Cuprum]] agents, confirming they are supplying raw materials for [[Project Orchestra]].
- [x] The Cuprum agents are suspicious of the "contamination" in the hold and order a "Bio-Cleanse." This triggers the release of an Ooze into the hold.
- [ ] The docks of the [[Sun Spire]] are lined with massive **Magnetic Cranes**. These are currently moving scrap but could be weaponized or used for extraction later.

## 3. Scenes & Encounters
* **Scene 1: The Handoff (Roleplay/Stealth):** The party hides in the hazardous waste hold while Arabella argues with the [[House Cuprum]] receiver on the deck above. They overhear her desperation and the order to "cleanse" the hold.
* **Scene 2: The Biological Cleanser (Combat):** * **Monster:** **The Cleanser Ooze** (Reflavored Gray Ooze or Gelatinous Cube).
    * **Mechanic:** The hold begins filling with acid. The floor is safe for Round 1, then becomes submerged.
    * **Terrain:** Players must hop between floating piles of scrap to survive.
    * **Hazard:** Moving between piles requires a **DC 12 Dex Save** or take **2d6 Acid Damage**.
* **Scene 3: Arrival at the Spire:** The ship docks at the [[Sun Spire]]. Arabella is greeted by the **Sun Spire Head of Security** (Homebrew NPC). The party must disembark unseen into the scrap yards.
