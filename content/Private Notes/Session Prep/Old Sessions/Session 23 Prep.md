### Session 23: The Lead Belly's Betrayal

**1. Review Characters**
* **Vaxen & Otis:** The spotlight is on them early. The dying Orc will challenge their ties to the Blood Horde and their respective tribal honors. 
* **Collin:** His psychic link to the Resonance Crystals (from Session 2) gives him a visceral connection to the cause of the explosion. 
* **Vase:** Has a keen eye for GSC paperwork and lore; the library is his playground.
* **Cletus & Twilight:** Will likely need to support the social/physical navigation through the Sinking City and the library's magical architecture.

**2. Strong Start: Blood on the Water**
* The session opens immediately following the cliffhanger of Session 22. High Inquisitor Varius and the celestial Romanis fade into the shadows, leaving the party clutching their expedited GSC Library passes.
* **Sensory Details:** The harbor is a jarring contrast. The bleak, grayscale fog of the Sinking City is violently illuminated by the roaring, chaotic orange flames of the warehouse explosion. The smell of ozone and burning wood fills the air.
* **The Hook:** Over the crackle of the flames, they hear a wet, ragged cough from the edge of the docks. A severely wounded Blood Horde Orc is half-submerged in the murky water, clinging to scorched metal.

**3. Outline Scenes**
* **Scene 1: Delirium and Honor.** The Orc survivor is bleeding out and delirious, mistaking the non-Horde party members for GSC enforcers. 
    * *Mechanic:* Vaxen or Otis must take the lead, making a **DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation)** check to invoke their Blood God (The Skullsplitter or Steinn Yarta) to cut through the haze.
    * *The Reveal:* The Orc wheezes out the truth: Captain Arabella's weapons deal went sideways because one barrel was filled with pure Resonance Crystals. When the Orcs tried to take it, the halfling triggered a magic bomb and fled as *The Lead Belly* tore away.
* **Scene 2: The Grayscale Trek.** Leaving the docks, the party must navigate the dreary, colorless swamp-canals of the Sinking City to reach the archives. A moment for quiet roleplay as they process Arabella's betrayal amidst the hissing steam matrix devices that keep the city afloat.
* **Scene 3: Bypassing the Bureaucracy.** The party arrives at the GSC Library. They bypass the massive three-month waitlist by slapping down Varius's high-level GSC paperwork.
* **Scene 4: The Library of Lairs.** The party enters your pre-prepped dungeon to track down Professor Sylas Thistlethorn amidst the sentient books and GSC security renovations.
