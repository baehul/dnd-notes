### The Hub (Retooled to Foreshadow the Boss Fight)
**Unifying idea:** The Hub is a defunct control nexus the scholar-order once used to ward the library — a network of linked nodes, individually weak but collectively resilient, that degrades unevenly as nodes are destroyed. Alexandra either sabotaged this system in passing or reverse-engineered its logic into her mobile spires — so nothing here needs to be a literal copy of the boss fight, just built from the same bones.

- **The Refraction Bubble** *(unchanged mechanically)* — kinetic-threshold ward: sudden, violent, single-source force gets reflected as force damage; slow, sustained, or multi-source pressure gets through. An Arcana/Investigation check here can surface the real lesson: *this ward — like the network it belongs to — isn't beaten by one big hit, it's beaten by attacking load-bearing points and letting it degrade.* Direct primer for "don't nova the boss, kill towers."
- **The Drifting Dead Zone → reworked into a trigger gauntlet.** Split into 3–4 small glyph patches, each keyed to a different hidden condition (e.g., punishes bunching up, punishes standing still, punishes returning to the same spot). The party suffers through and deduces each by trial and error — a compressed, lower-stakes rehearsal of exactly how the six towers' blind triggers work later. This room carries the heaviest foreshadowing load — lean on it.
- **The Overcharged Guardian** *(unchanged mechanically)* — the ghost of an ancient cartographer forced to grip a valve wired with pulsing golden veins of magic, rigged to violently overload if mishandled, solvable only by grounding the charge safely through conductive material. Keep the "Golden-Eye" seal explicitly Alexandra's own abjuration signature, so when the boss fight starts, the players recognize the same magic and already have a frame for it. Also previews the danger of being forced (shoved/repositioned) into triggering something you don't understand.

## 8. Treasure / Magic Item Rewards
**A Drop of Spectral Ink** *(one-time use, no attunement)*
The ghost librarian sacrifices a piece of his own essence — hastening his true fading — to grant this to Vase. A single bead of shimmering black ink that clings to the fingertip until used.

*As a bonus action, apply the ink to a creature that is at 0 hit points. That creature automatically fails the next Wisdom saving throw it is required to make before the end of its next turn. The ink is then spent and turns to dust.*

Intended use: guaranteeing the party's existing **Scroll of Imprisonment** (picked up Session 25) actually lands on Alexandra Aurum once she's been dropped to 0 HP in the eventual boss fight, rather than risking her succeeding on the save at full power.
