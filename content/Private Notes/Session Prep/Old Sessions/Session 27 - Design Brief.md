# Session 27 — Design Brief (The Hub)

**Status:** skeleton + constraints, not a finished prep document. Hand to Adventure
Designer to build out; Showrunner to check fit against Arc 2.

## ⚠️ Superseded material — read first
The Hub as described in `Session 26 - Prep.md` and `Library of Layers Prep.md` was
designed for an EARLIER version of the Session 28 boss fight that no longer exists.
**The fire clock, the six burning wedges, the triage stakes, and tower brute-forcing
are all cut.** Do not design toward them. The current boss fight is
`Session 28 - Boss Fight.md` — that file is authoritative.

The three Hub rooms themselves (Refraction Bubble, Drifting Dead Zone, Overcharged
Guardian) are still usable as raw material, retooled per the beats below.

## Where this sits
Session 26 ended with the party in free-fall after triggering a lattice of contingency
wards. They land in the Hub. Session 28 is the boss fight against
[[Lady Alexandra Aurum]]. **No long rest between 27 and 28** — a short rest before the
final descent is expected.

## The one lesson that matters
> **A tower's disable is the thing its trigger forbids.**

Every tower in Session 28 inverts its own trigger. If the party internalises this
principle, they can DERIVE all six disables themselves. Session 27 does not need to
teach six answers — it needs to teach this one idea so thoroughly that they reach for
it by reflex.

## Teaching priorities
| # | Concept | Beat | If they miss it |
| - | ------- | ---- | --------------- |
| 1 | The disable inverts the trigger | 2 | Hard stall — no fallback exists in S28 |
| 2 | Hazards cannot be damaged | 2 | Wasted rounds swinging at towers |
| 3 | Triggers are legible cause-and-effect | 1 | The boss fight feels arbitrary |
| 4 | Disables require multiple people | 2–3 | One player tries to solo everything |
| 5 | Wards make things untouchable; breaking one opens a window | 4 | They ignore towers and swing at her |
| 6 | Only one hazard watches you at a time | 3 | Confusing first round in S28 |

**Deliberately NOT taught:** cover and line of sight. Teaching it would telegraph the
Watcher's mechanics. The party should discover it in the fight. (Note: the S28 arena
still REQUIRES cover terrain — that's an arena requirement, not a lesson.)

## The four beats
These are the mechanical skeleton. They are NOT the session — see "Story threads"
below.

**Beat 1 — Cause and Effect.** A hazard with one obvious trigger, low damage, firing
three or four times so the pattern is unmistakable. No solution required; they just
learn the place punishes specific behaviours and tells you which. *(New — nothing in
existing prep covers this.)*

**Beat 2 — The Inversion.** THE lesson, and it must be unmissable. A ward blocking
progress. **Let them attack it and accomplish nothing** — that failure is load-bearing.
The only way through is to deliberately do the forbidden thing, together. Example
shape: a ward that lashes out at sound, overloaded when the whole party makes noise at
once. *(Retool of the Refraction Bubble.)*

**Beat 3 — Conflicting Demands.** Two hazards with contradictory rules, so no position
or action satisfies both. The party must choose which price to pay and split work by
who absorbs what. **Debut the card mechanic here** — only one hazard watches each
person at a time, and they can see which. *(Retool of the Drifting Dead Zone.)*

**Beat 4 — The Warded Thing.** A capstone. Something protected by surrounding nodes;
attacks against it do nothing while they stand. Disable a node and it is briefly
vulnerable. A smaller rehearsal of Alexandra and her towers. *(Retool of the
Overcharged Guardian.)*

## Story threads — the beats are the skeleton, these are the meat
Four puzzle rooms in a row is a tutorial, not a session. These must carry it:

- **[[Luwei]]'s demand.** Unresolved and escalating. In Session 26 he demanded that
  BOTH [[Collin McCambridge]] and [[Otis Thulu]] come to him, and that Otis defaced his
  symbol. Collin has not told the party what this costs him.
- **The ghost librarian.** Drafted in `Session 26 - Prep.md` but never used — the sole
  survivor of the library's extinct scholar-order, who approaches
  [[Vase the Fragile]]. Still available.
- **[[Vase the Fragile]]'s vow.** He promised the Library that the party would leave
  with no books at all. Live constraint on any treasure.
- **Alexandra's recorded taunt.** A left-behind illusion in which she mocks
  [[High Inquisitor Varius]] — whom she wrongly believes is the one following her —
  telling him to turn around and forget this happened. Previews her contempt, and
  establishes that she has never once considered that someone beneath her notice might
  be the real threat. Grounded: Varius secretly hired the party and is chasing the same
  research.
  **Note:** [[Collin McCambridge]] is secretly Eobard. Whether his parentage surfaces
  here is an open DM decision — this scene is the first that naturally pressures it.

## Constraints
- **Exit at roughly 85% party health**, with a short rest available before the descent.
  Session 27 is formally the difficulty dial for Session 28.
- **No long rest.** Spell slots spent here are gone in the boss fight. Beats should not
  demand heavy casting.
- **[[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn]] does NOT appear in Session 27.** He is first seen
  in the boss room, frozen in stasis with Alexandra's knife in him.
- Unused floor entries in `Library of Layers Prep.md` Tables 1–3 remain available for
  pacing the descent between beats.
