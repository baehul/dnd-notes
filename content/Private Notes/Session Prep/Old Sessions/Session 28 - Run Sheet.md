# Session 28 Run Sheet — The Six Towers

## Setup
- [ ] Map: 6 tower footprints, quadrant lines, eighths (1–8 clockwise from N), lit-quadrant shape, 8 gold lit spots (2 per quadrant), all cover marked
- [ ] Red deck + blue deck, 6 tower cards each
- [ ] **Deal each PC one blue card before initiative**
- [ ] Clock: **16 segments**. Her HP: **380**

## Every Round
1. **Start of round:** rotate the lit quadrant one step clockwise.
2. **End of each PC's turn, in this order:**
   1. **Disable completed?** → that tower's 2 cards become blanks. The finisher takes no damage from it. Her ward drops (see Window).
   2. **Held card's trigger broken?** → DC 15 save. A success means nothing happens.
   3. **Discard and draw from the other deck** (blue → red → blue). This is next turn's card.
3. **Her turn:** Consign ×(burns/round) *or* Control, + Interference.

## The Towers — all DC 15, checked at end of the holder's turn

Riders last until the end of the holder's next turn, except prone.

## Cards
- **Doubled:** you drew the tower you just discarded → put it face-down. It doesn't trigger. Discard normally next turn.
- **Blank in a deck** = a free turn when drawn. **Blank in hand** → face-down at once.
- Each deck has one card per tower, so every tower is dealt before any repeats.

## Rulings
- **Ally** = a party member. Summons and familiars don't count.
- **Unavoidable turns don't trigger:** Incapacitated, or Speed 0 all turn from something you didn't choose. Self-inflicted (dropping prone, staying in an ally's grapple) still triggers.
- **Only your own turn counts.** Position is checked where you end your turn. Reactions never count.
- **Pendulum:** Actions are Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Influence, Magic, Ready, Search, Study, Utilize. Any spell = Magic. Smothering = Utilize. Readied = Ready. No Action twice = a repeat.
- **Watcher cover:** ruler line from its center to the token's center; crosses marked cover = covered. **Invisible** = out of its sight (safe, but doesn't count toward its disable). *Invisibility* ends on attack or cast.
- **Ceiling towers:** measure to their footprint.
- **Towers can't be damaged.** Disables are the only way through.
- ***Dispel Magic* on a tower:** suppresses it until the start of the caster's next turn (its cards don't trigger), automatically. It never disables.
- **Study** within 30 ft + **DC 15 Arcana** → learns that tower's disable in plain words. Counts as a fresh Action for the Pendulum.

## Alexandra
**AC** 15 +1/tower (21) · **HP** 380 · **DR** −1/tower per damage instance · **Saves** INT +9, WIS +6, +1/tower · **DC** 16 · **Spell atk** +8

**Window of Vulnerability.** When a tower is disabled, her ward is gone **until the start of the finisher's next turn**: AC 15, base saves, no DR. Everyone, her included, gets exactly one turn in it. Her reactions still work.

**Action:**
- **Consign:** 1 segment starts burning and is lost at the start of her next turn. **Smother** = any creature's Action (Utilize); *Dispel Magic* does it at range, automatically.
- **or Control:** *Dispel Magic* (strip a buff / break concentration) · *Wall of Stone* (cut the room) · *Slow* (up to 6, WIS DC 16).

**Bonus: Interference.** Swap two PCs' held cards after they've planned. If a PC gets the tower they just discarded → it goes face-down.

**Reactions** (a pool per round, see the stages):
- **Reprisal:** when attacked or forced to save → **+8, 3d8 force**. Not triggered by disables.
- ***Counterspell*** · ***Shield*** (+5 AC)

**0 HP → Final Contingency:** she falls unconscious and runes crawl outward. **They detonate after 1 minute**, destroying all remaining segments and savaging anyone nearby. ***Imprisonment*** seals her, contingency included. **Killed outright → it detonates immediately.** The party's options: burn the scroll and the Essence and seal her (keeping everything saved), kill her (losing everything), or run with what they have.

She has no escape magic and will not flee.

## Strain Tells (progress toward a disable; reset the crack marker when progress breaks)
- **Anchor:** groans and sags for each creature standing still beneath it
- **Rod:** sparks jump between allies and reach toward her
- **Blizzard:** frost around an isolated person; a crack stays in the ice into the next round
- **Pendulum:** its swing stutters as people repeat an Action
- **Watcher:** its eye darts as exposed people crowd one wedge
- **Shadow:** a lit spot flares when occupied, dims when empty
- **If they're stuck:** the gold runes match the Session 27 wards (the sphere broken by everyone throwing stones at once, the lightning drained along the metal to the water). The trick is doing the tower's own trigger with many people at once.
