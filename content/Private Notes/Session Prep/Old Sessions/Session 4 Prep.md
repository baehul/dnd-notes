### **Room 1: The Scrapyard (The Entrance Guardian)**


**Scenario**

The players disembark _The Lead Belly_ into a labyrinth of rusted metal and shattered arcanotech. The area is dominated by deafening, rhythmically moving **Magnetic Cranes** that move scrap. The path to the Spire is patrolled by **Automaton Sentries** (Animated Armor).

**Challenge Framework**
A traversal and stealth challenge. The party must navigate the shifting piles of scrap and avoid the sensor cones of the Automatons to reach the freight elevator leading to the Gala level.

**Time Costs (1 Turn each)**
- Mapping the patrol routes of the Automaton Sentries to find a gap.
- Moving slowly and carefully through a dense, unstable pile of sharp scrap metal
- Waiting for a Magnetic Crane to move a blockage in the path.
- Hacking or disabling a perimeter sensor node to create a safe zone.

**Room 1 Complication Table (d6)**
1. **Immediate Danger:** An Automaton Sentry detects movement and initiates "Purge Mode" (Combat).
2. **Environmental Hazard:** A Magnetic Crane activates unexpectedly, attempting to lift a PC wearing heavy armor (Risk of separation or falling damage).
3. **Resource Drain:** A jagged piece of scrap ruptures a container on a PC's belt (Potion leaks or equipment is damaged).
4. **Sensory Spook:** The party finds the crushed remains of a previous intruder, clutching a warning note about the "eyes in the walls."
5. **Barrier:** The freight elevator loses power; it requires a manual restart or an alternate climbing route.
6. **Roleplay:** One PC gets magnetized by a passing crane and is stuck to a wall or another PC, requiring a noisy or awkward extraction.

### **Room 2: The Charity Gala (The Puzzle/RP Challenge)**

**Scenario**
The freight elevator opens into a service corridor behind the "Hall of Gold." Duke Ephraim Aurum is hosting a gala to "honor" the displaced refugees (who are actually being used as props for propaganda). The room is filled with nobles, Crusade officers, and House Aurum guards. **Lancaster Slickwhistle** is not here; he is in the detention block. The players need the **Master Keycard** from the Head of Security, **Wolfram Zoloto**, who is currently drinking at the VIP table.

**Challenge Framework**
Social Infiltration. The party must blend in, gather intel, and maneuver close enough to Zoloto to lift the keycard without causing a scene.

**Time Costs (1 Turn each)**
- Circulating the room to gather rumors about where Lancaster is being held.
- Observing Wolfram Zoloto's routine to find a moment of vulnerability.
- Appropriate disguises to enter the VIP section.
- Creating a complex distraction (e.g., sabotaging the buffet or rigging the lights) to clear the guards.

**Room 2 Complication Table (d6)**
1. **Immediate Danger:** A guest recognizes a PC (perhaps from a Wanted poster or previous encounter) and alerts a guard.
2. **Roleplay/Social:** A noble engages a PC in a conversation about local politics; the PC must bluff effectively or draw suspicion (Disadvantage on next check).
3. **Spook (Paranoia):** Propaganda screens flicker and display a live feed of the party entering the room for a split second before cutting back to the Duke.
4. **Barrier:** The VIP section is cordoned off for a private toast by Duke Ephraim; the target is inaccessible for this Turn.
5. **Environmental Hazard:** A House Aurum arcanist performs a "Magic Sweep" of the room; active spells or magic items might be detected if not suppressed.
6. **Resource Drain:** To maintain cover, a PC is forced to make a significant donation (Gold) or drink a strong toast (Con save or intoxicated).

### **Room 3: The Lamp Room (The Setback)**

**Scenario**
Using the keycard, the party enters the detention block. They find **Lancaster Slickwhistle** strapped to a chair under blinding arcane lights (The "Lamp Room" mentioned in prep). He is conscious but weak (Exhaustion).

**The Twist/Setback**
Lancaster reveals he cannot leave without his **Arcane Focus**, which was confiscated. More importantly, the **Harmony Engine** isn't here. He hid it inside a hollowed-out Automaton chassis back in the **Scrapyard** (Room 1) before he was captured. To make matters worse, the moment they free him, a silent alarm triggers. They must retrieve his gear and the Engine _and_ escape, but the facility is now locking down.

**Challenge Framework**
A "Smash and Grab" under time pressure. They must stabilize Lancaster, find his Focus (in a nearby evidence locker), and backtrack to the Scrapyard before they are sealed in.

**Time Costs (1 Turn each)**
- Administering first aid/magical healing to Lancaster to remove his Exhaustion levels so he can move.
- Searching the chaotic Evidence Locker for Lancaster's specific Focus.
- Slicing into the security terminal to delay the lockdown doors.
- Carefully navigating the return vents to avoid the now-alerted guards in the hallways.

**Room 3 Complication Table (d6)**
1. **Immediate Danger:** A squad of "Sun Spire Elites" (heavily armored guards) breaches the corridor.
2. **Environmental Hazard:** A tranquilizer gas is released into the hall ways.
3. **Barrier:** A laser field activates, forcing the party to find a way through or around.
4. **Sensory Spook:** The lights in the hallway become blinding.
5. **Roleplay:** Lancaster is suffering the effects of the lamp room. He is blinded until the next room.
6. **Resource Drain:** The security system activates anti-magic dampeners; all spell slots cost one level higher to cast for the next hour.

### **Room 4: The Magnetic Defense (The Climax)**

**Scenario**
The party rushes back to the Scrapyard (Room 1). They find **Captain Arabella** standing near _The Lead Belly_, but she isn't prepping for takeoff. She's concluding a deal with **Wolfram Zoloto** and a heavy security detail. She has sold them out to clear her debt.

Lancaster spots his crashed, hidden ship (The _Slick-Whistle_) under a pile of scrap nearby. He rushes to repair the engine and retrieve the Harmony Engine hidden inside it. The party must defend him.

**Challenge Framework**
A wave defense. The party must hold off Zoloto's forces and Arabella's betrayal while Lancaster completes the repairs (requiring a set number of rounds or successes).

**Time Debt (Consequences of previous turns)**
- **Fast Arrival (Few Turns spent):** Arabella is wavering; she can be Persuaded to switch sides. Zoloto has only a standard patrol with him. Lancaster's ship needs minor repairs.
- **Average Arrival:** Arabella has fled with her payment. Zoloto has taken a fortified position with cover. Lancaster's ship needs significant repairs.
- **Slow Arrival (Many Turns spent):** Arabella has fled. Zoloto has deployed a heavy "War-Automaton." The Scrapyard is fully illuminated by searchlights (No stealth).

**Room 4 Complication Table (d6)**
None, combat focused

### **Room 5: The Ascension (The Reward)**

**Scenario**
Lancaster screams that the thrusters are hot. The engine of the _Slick-Whistle_ flares to life, blowing piles of scrap away. The party must make a final dash and leap onto the moving ramp as the ship rockets upward

**Challenge Framework**
A final cinematic skill check (Athletics/Acrobatics) to board the ship, or a "Help" action to pull a slower comrade aboard.

**The Rewards & Revelation**
- **The Loot:** Lancaster hands over a cache of Resistance-grade weapons stored on his ship.
- **The Item:** They possess the **Harmony Engine** (though it is dormant/locked).
- **The Revelation:** Lancaster confirms the torture was about **Project Orchestra**. He reveals that he doesn't know all the specifics but will share everything he knows with The Resistance upon returning to the tavern.
