### 1. Review Characters

- **Collin McCambridge:** Heightened paranoia regarding his true identity after recognizing his brother, August Aurum, at the gala.
- **Cletus Ironplow:** Possesses vital intelligence regarding Project Orchestra and the Cult of Night informant after eavesdropping on Duke Ephraim and Lady Dagna.
- **Otis Thulu:** Angered by House Aurum's propaganda after speaking with Goliath refugees.
- **Twilight Emberrest:** Recently recovered from temporary blindness caused by the Sun Spire beacon.
- **Vase the Fragile:** Successfully networked and gathered gossip from the gala guests.
- **Vaxen the Unkillable:** Issued a challenge to Lord August Aurum for a duel to the death at midnight.

### 2. Strong Start

- **Location:** Duke Ephraim's Bedroom.
- **Action:** Following the consumption of dry gin martinis, Lancaster Slickwhistle's blindness is cured, but he is impatient and fueled by adrenaline. He makes a rash, noisy move to exit the room and head for the downstairs workshop to retrieve his arcane focus.
- **Threat:** The high-pitched silent alarm has already triggered, and Sun Spire Elites are actively converging on their location.
- **Mechanics:** DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion/Intimidation) to force Lancaster to coordinate quietly. Alternatively, a Group DC 13 Dexterity (Stealth) check to cover his stumbling exit.
- **Failure Consequence:** Immediate detection by a passing patrol of House Aurum Guards. 

### 3. Outline Scenes

- **Scene 1: The Gala Descent (Social/Stealth)**
    - **Setup:** The party and Lancaster must cross back through the Charity Gala to reach the downstairs workshop.
    - **Complication:** Guards are subtly sealing the exits due to the silent alarm.
    - **Mechanics:** Group DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) or Charisma (Deception) to navigate the crowds. Escaping refugees grant advantage on Stealth checks.
- **Scene 2: The Downstairs Workshop (Exploration)**
    - **Setup:** The party enters the workshop to retrieve Lancaster's Arcane Focus.
    - **Action:** The party can scavenge experimental items and the Recipe for Armor of Acid Resistance.
    - **Mechanics:** DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) to quickly locate the focus and high-value loot. DC 14 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools) or Intelligence (Arcana) to bypass the evidence locker.
    - **Failure Consequence:** Triggering a localized shock trap dealing 2d6 Lightning damage.
- **Scene 3: The Taunt (Roleplay)**
    - **Setup:** En route from the Gala to the Scrapyard, Lord August Aurum spots Vaxen the Unkillable.
    - **Action:** August does not attack, but loudly reminds Vaxen of their midnight duel, mocking him and stating he hopes Vaxen isn't running away.
    - **Focus:** A psychological test for Vaxen to prioritize the party's escape over his pride.
- **Scene 4: The Scrapyard Ambush (Combat)**
    - **Setup:** The party arrives at the Scrapyard to find Lancaster's ship and the hidden Harmony Engine.
    - **The Trap:** Duke Ephraim, Wolfram Zoloto, and Captain Arabella spring their coordinated ambush to eliminate the intruders and recover the Engine.

### 4. Secrets & Clues

- **Secret 1:** Professor Jitterhop of the Cult of Night is the informant secretly supplying The Crusade with tectonic research data.
- **Secret 2:** Captain Arabella sold the party out to Wolfram Zoloto, paid with the gold taken from Duke Ephraim's hidden bedroom safe.
- **Secret 3:** Project Orchestra is currently undergoing active testing in the Argentum islands.
- **Secret 4:** The Harmony Engine was designed by Duke Ephraim to seamlessly fuse Tectonic Islands using Resonance Crystals and pre-Shattering Terramancy.
- **Secret 5:** The Harmony Engine is entirely dormant and mechanically locked; it can only be activated by exposure to a specific, perfect sonic frequency.
- **Secret 6:** Lancaster Slickwhistle discovered that if The Crusade activates the Harmony Engine to seal the Upper Crust, it will permanently condemn the In-Between Isles and Underworld to eternal darkness.
- **Secret 7:** House Aurum publicly claims that Eobard Aurum (the ninth son) was killed in an ambush by the Blood Horde five years ago.
- **Secret 8:** High Baron Magnus Aurum fabricated Eobard's death to cover up his disappearance.
- **Secret 9:** House Aurum forces its members to undergo painful exposure to Resonance Crystals to artificially enhance their innate magical potential.
- **Secret 10:** The high-pitched dog whistle alarm was a deliberate trap set by Duke Ephraim; he wanted Lancaster to escape specifically so the spy would lead them to the hidden Harmony Engine.

### 5. Fantastic Locations

- **The Downstairs Workshop**
    - **Features:** Benches cluttered with arcanotech tools, half-finished constructs, and volatile magical reagents.
    - **Key Element:** A secured drafting desk holding experimental blueprints, including the Recipe for Armor of Acid Resistance.
- **The Sun Spire Scrapyard**
    - **Features:** A labyrinth of rusted metal and discarded arcanotech at the base of the island.
    - **Hazards:** Active magnetic cranes sweeping overhead, providing dynamic cover or vertical mobility during combat.

### 6. Important NPCs

- **Lord August Aurum**
    - **Appearance:** Tall, brutish human.
    - **Goal:** Intimidate Vaxen and assert House Aurum's superiority.
    - **Roleplay Note:** Speaks with arrogant confidence; views Vaxen as beneath him.
- **Duke Ephraim Aurum**
    - **Appearance:** Older human male, blonde hair, golden eyes, wearing an arcanotech monocle and using a cigarette holder.
    - **Goal:** Recover the Harmony Engine and eliminate the Resistance intruders.
    - **Roleplay Note:** Speaks with a precise, cold German accent; obsessed with order and his own ego.
- **Wolfram Zoloto**
    - **Appearance:** Dwarven man with braided brown hair, armor, and a scarred-over eye.
    - **Goal:** Execute Duke Ephraim's ambush flawlessly.
    - **Roleplay Note:** Speaks with a Russian accent; highly tactical and disciplined.
- **Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe**
    - **Appearance:** Older Halfling woman in a worn uniform, constantly smoking a cigarette and rubbing her arms for warmth.
    - **Goal:** Protect her payout and survive the crossfire.
    - **Roleplay Note:** Raspy, wheezy voice; highly defensive and guilty about her betrayal.
- **Lancaster Slickwhistle**
    - **Appearance:** Handsome Drow elf, impeccably dressed despite recent torture.
    - **Goal:** Retrieve his focus, secure the Harmony Engine, and escape the Spire.
    - **Roleplay Note:** Suave, English accent; impatient and operating on pure adrenaline.

### 7. Relevant Monsters

- **Wolfram Zoloto:** Use _Dragon Army Officer_ or _Veteran_ stat block (Access to basic fire magic).
- **House Aurum Guards:** Use _Guard_ or _Thug_ stat blocks.
- **Automaton Sentries (If activated in the Scrapyard):** Use _Animated Armor_ stat block.
