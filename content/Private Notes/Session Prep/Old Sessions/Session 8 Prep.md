  ## 1. Review Characters

- **Vaxen the Unkillable:** Just utilized the Harmony Engine to hurdle a neighboring Tectonic Island at House Aurum's fleet.
    
- **Collin McCambridge:** Distracted and distant after experiencing a vision of High Baron Magnus Aurum welcoming him home with golden light.
    
- **Cletus Ironplow:** In possession of the Harmony Engine notes and intends to bring them to Professor Jitterhop, entirely unaware that Jitterhop is the Crusade's mole.
    
- **Twilight Emberrest & Vase the Fragile:** Survived the massive island collision and Duke Ephraim's fireball, which was absorbed by their newly awakened Lair.
    
- **Otis Thulu:** Still holding anger regarding House Aurum's propaganda and the displacement camps.
## Strong Start

- **Cinematic Cutscene:** Describe a peaceful island where a giant humanoid moth druid named [[Bloos]] is happily eating a sweater.
    
- **The Destruction:** The Ostinato Engine arrives to conduct the Project Orchestra demonstration.
    
- **The Mechanism:** The massive industrial engine seizes [[Bloos]]'s island, violently pulverizing the rock into a slurry of raw earth and fragmented Resonance Crystals. [[Bloos]] takes flight to escape the destruction.
    
- **Cut to Players:** Transition to the immediate aftermath of the island collision from the previous session.
    
- **The Transformation:** The Lair, fueled by the Harmony Engine, begins violently shifting its architecture from a Witch's Hut into a Magical Library.
    
- **Mechanic:** Require a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw from the party to avoid being knocked prone or taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage from rapidly shifting bookshelves and expanding stone walls.
    
- **The Arrival (DM Trigger):** At a time of your choosing, Bloos crash-lands onto the party's new Tectonic Island, mesmerized and drawn in by the powerful magical lights emitting from the transforming tower.
    
## Scene 1: The Ostinato Demonstration (Opening Cinematic)

- **Establish the Tranquility:** * Describe a small, peaceful Tectonic Island drifting in the void.
    
    - Introduce Bloos: A giant humanoid moth wearing a heavily chewed, oversized sweater.
        
    - Highlight his state: Comfortably intoxicated, lazily munching on yarn, perhaps staring at a small, shiny rock.
        
- **The Interruption:**
    
    - Introduce a deep, unnatural mechanical rumble that shakes the loose dirt of the island.
        
    - A massive shadow overtakes the island as the Ostinato Engine looms overhead.
        
    - Describe the sheer industrial scale of the arcanotech machine, bearing the rigid, brutalist design hallmarks of House Cuprum.
        
- **The Annihilation:**
    
    - The Engine seizes the edges of Bloos's island with sudden, violent force.
        
    - Describe the deafening sound of grinding rock as the island is systematically pulverized.
        
    - The ground beneath Bloos dissolves into a glowing slurry of raw earth and fragmented Resonance Crystals infused with Terramancy.
        
- **The Escape:**
    
    - Bloos, terrified and clutching his half-eaten sweater, frantically takes flight into the void just before the ground vanishes.
        
    - He flutters away erratically into the In-Between Isles, leaving the Ostinato Engine to process his home into "tectonic mortar".
        

## Scene 2: The Lair's Metamorphosis (Return to the Players)

- **The Immediate Aftermath:**
    
    - Transition back to the party immediately following the massive island collision.
        
    - The Harmony Engine, resting in the center of the room, begins to pulse with a louder, melodious thrum.
        
- **The Physical Shift:**
    
    - The leaning, creaking wood of the Witch's Hut begins to violently splinter and groan.
        
    - The floorboards harden and pale, transmuting directly into seamless stone.
        
    - The air grows suddenly thick with the scent of ozone, old parchment, and the low, electric thrum of raw creation.
        
- **The Mechanics of Change:**
    
    - The ceiling shoots rapidly upward, stretching the room into a twisting spire of stone.
        
    - **Mechanic:** Require a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** from all characters. On a failure, they are knocked Prone and take **1d6 bludgeoning damage** from buckling floors and expanding stone.
        
- **The Awakening of the Library:**
    
    - Massive, gravity-defying bookshelves erupt from the curving walls, spiraling up into the darkness.
        
    - The Lair's Arcane Intelligence Powered Assistant awakens: Books fly from the shelves like flocks of birds, their pages turning themselves as they orbit the room.
        
    - The violent shaking subsides, leaving the party standing in the quiet, charged atmosphere of their newly bonded Magical Library.
