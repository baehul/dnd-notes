## 1. Review Characters

- **Vase the Fragile:** Assisting Collin McCambridge in opening the infernal puzzle cube; looking for ancient lore.
    
- **Collin McCambridge:** Successfully opening the puzzle box recovered from Duke Ephraim Aurum's hidden safe; dealing with the psychic weight of the Aurum legacy.
    
- **Cletus Ironplow:** Intending to bring the newly acquired Harmony Engine notes to Professor Jitterhop.
    
- **Twilight Emberrest:** Deeply concerned about whether his wife, Dawn, remains safe at the Monastery of Night given the suspected mole.
    
- **Otis Thulu:** Tending to the emotional and dietary needs of the newly arrived moth-druid, Bloos.
    
- **Vaxen the Unkillable:** Experiencing a rare moment of vulnerability and reconsidering his singular desire to die in combat.
    
- **Campfire Prompt:** "As the Magical Library drifts through the chaotic void of the In-Between Isles, which one of you is the first to check the sentient library's shelves for a book about your own family, and what are you afraid the self-turning pages might reveal?"

## 2. Strong Start

- **Location:** Collin McCambridge's bedroom inside the Magical Library.
    
- **Visual:** The intricate exterior of the infernal puzzle cube finally gives way with a heavy, metallic click, revealing the [[Ring of Restless Revelation]] inside.
    
- **Sensory:** The obsidian band feels unnaturally warm against the skin. A blue crystal set into the ring plots an ever-changing course across the stars, while the band itself rumbles faintly with euhedral echoes.

## 5. Fantastic Locations

- **The Monastery of Night (Upper Campus)**
    
    - **Visual:** Jagged obsidian spires reaching into perpetual twilight; bridges woven from giant spider silk and iron.
        
    - **Sensory:** The chaotic, patchwork illumination of bioluminescent fungi and melting candles.
        
- **The Forum**
    
    - **Visual:** A massive, tiered cavern acting as the political and intellectual heart of the Cult.
        
    - **Sensory:** The echoing acoustics of dozens of scholars fiercely debating arcane theory simultaneously.
        
- **The Lower Caverns**
    
    - **Visual:** Pitch-black, hollowed-out subterranean tunnels beneath the tectonic island, filled with fossilized ribs of ancient behemoths.
        
    - **White Space:** A series of massive, jagged claw marks gouged deeply into the fossilized bone of the cavern walls, their origin and age completely unrecorded in the Cult's otherwise meticulous academic archives.
