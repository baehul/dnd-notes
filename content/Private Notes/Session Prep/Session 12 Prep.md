### 1. Review Characters

- **Collin McCambridge:** Secretly discovered Jitterhop is the mole and possesses the Ring of Restless Revelation.
    
- **Twilight Emberrest:** Suspicious of Collin after the laboratory incident; deeply worried about his wife Dawn's safety with a mole in the Cult.
    
- **Otis Thulu:** Recovering from the catatonic shock of the Arbor Imperatus encounter.
    
- **Cletus Ironplow:** Possesses the Harmonic Tuning Fork and trusts Jitterhop with the Harmony Engine notes.
    
- **Vase the Fragile:** Successfully defused the Minotaur duel; unaware of the unfolding treason.
    
- **Vaxen the Unkillable:** Experiencing newfound vulnerability; questioning his singular desire for a glorious death.
    
- **Campfire Prompt:** "As you wait in the absolute silence of the Temple of Night for Sendryr to appear, what is one quiet memory of this broken world you hold onto that reminds you why fighting the Crusade is worth it?"
    

### 2. Strong Start

- **The Reunion:** The party reconvenes at the entrance to the Temple of Night after splitting up in Session 11. Collin harbors the explosive secret of Jitterhop’s treason, while Twilight harbors intense suspicion of Collin.
    
- **The Descent:** Sendryr Ogami is waiting for them at the bottom of a sunken obsidian arena. She informs them that to acquire the final tool, they must prove they can navigate the overlapping, contradictory truths of the _Way of Night_.
    

### 3. Outline Scenes

- **Scene 1: The Temple Service (Pillow Scene)**

	- **Sensory:** The sharp smell of cold stone and burning sage; the echoing, overlapping murmurs of dozens of voices bouncing off polished black glass.
	    
	- **Visual:** An open-air amphitheater made of obsidian. Dozens of Cult members (priests, biomancers, and arcanists) are scattered across the silver sand floor, not sitting in rows, but clustered in chaotic, shifting circles.
	    
	- **The Reading:** A deep-gnome priest stands at the center, reading a new verse into the quiet dark:
	    
    > 	**A blind man.** (3) **Stares at the empty sky.** (6) **Does the sun still burn without his eyes,** (9) **or does he make the dark?** (6) **He is Night.** (3)
	    
	- **The Debate:** The moment the verse ends, the congregation immediately erupts into respectful but fierce arguments over its meaning.
	    
	    - _A Religious Adherent_ argues it proves Solaris is a false god reliant on mortal belief to exist.
	        
	    - _A Biomancer_ loudly counters that it is a literal biological thesis about perception shaping reality—if the eye is removed, the concept of light ceases to exist.
	        
	    - _A Mage_ insists it is a metaphor for ignoring Crusade propaganda to find inner truth.
	        
	- **Purpose & Ma:** Pure atmosphere. No encounters, no checks. Let the players stand in the shadows and simply watch the Cult operate in its natural state—a group that finds unity in disagreement—before Sendryr steps out of the crowd to silence them and address the party.
        
- **Scene 2: The Test of Two Truths (Combat)**
    
    - **Setup:** Sendryr drops the weavesilk replacement tool into the center of the arena and conjures the trial.
        
    - **Mechanic:** Opening both eyes reveals the conjured monsters (Targeting/Attacking normal). Closing both eyes reveals the glowing arcane traps (Avoidance/Disarming normal). Closing one eye and keeping one open allows the PC to see both, but imposes Disadvantage on depth perception (Ranged attacks and Dexterity Saving Throws).
        
    - **Aftermath Beat:** The heavy, synchronized panting of the party; the silver sand settling onto the arena floor; Sendryr softly clapping from the shadows.
    - Hints
	    - **Split your gaze.** (3) **Hold both truths in your mind.** (6) **Keep one eye upon the waking world,** (9) **let the other sleep now.** (6) **You are Night.** (3)
	    - **One is dark.** (3) **One remains in the light.** (6) **You must walk the line between the two,** (9) **do not choose a side now.** (6) **See them both.** (3)
	    - **Blind one eye.** (3) **Let the other look on.** (6) **the runes and beasts are both truth and lies,** (9) **hold them in your vision.** (6)  **They are Night.** (3)
	    - 
        
- **Scene 3: The Confession of Night (Social/Lore)**
    
    - **Setup:** Sendryr hands over the tool. She drops her cryptic demeanor and speaks plainly to Collin and the party.
        
    - **The Reveal:** She admits she knows Jitterhop is feeding data to the Crusade.
        
    - **The Justification:** She hopes "Project Genesis" (artificial islands) will solve the housing crisis, stopping the Crusade's need to conquer Resistance lands without further bloodshed. She feels trapped by her pacifism and hopes Jitterhop will eventually see reason.
        
- **Scene 4: The Crossroads (Decision Point)**
    
    - **Setup:** Sendryr burdens the party with the ultimate choice on how the Resistance moves forward.
        
    - **Path A (The Terramancers):** Use Collin’s Ring of Restless Revelation and venture into the Underworld to find the Lestirmek, seeking the original pre-Shattering source to counter the Harmony Engine.
        
    - **Path B (The Resistance):** Take this treason to the other legs of the Resistance (Ancient Ones, Blood Horde) and force a brutal, internal reckoning to stop Jitterhop.
        

### 4. Secrets & Clues

- Sendryr has known about Jitterhop's connection to the Crusade for months but has hidden it from the Cult's democratic assembly to prevent a civil war.
    
- Jitterhop truly believes his "Project Genesis" will create a utopia, viewing the Crusade's funding and the Resistance's resources merely as tools for science.
    
- Sendryr's adherence to the _Way of Night_ philosophy prevents her from executing Jitterhop; she believes truth must unfold naturally, stating, "I am Night."
    
- The Terramancers (Lestirmek) in the Underworld hold the original pre-Shattering blueprints that the Harmony Engine's magic is imitating.
    
- The Blood Horde and Ancient Ones are entirely unaware of Jitterhop's treason; revealing it to Gnarl Thulu will likely result in the Horde attempting to slaughter the Cult's scientists.
    

### 5. Fantastic Locations

- **The Temple of Night Arena**
    
    - **Visual:** A sunken circular pit lined with polished obsidian. The floor is covered in fine, silver sand that ripples without wind.
        
    - **Sensory:** The temperature drops significantly the closer one gets to the center; light seems to physically bend away from the arena floor.
        
    - **White Space:** Deep, ancient scorch marks pattern the edges of the upper spectator stands, forming silhouettes of massive creatures that do not match any known species in the current Strata.
        

### 6. Important NPCs

- **Sendryr Ogami**
    
    - **Appearance:** Drow female, permanent silk blindfold, robes enchanted to look like moving starfields.
        
    - **Goal:** Test the party's ability to perceive multiple, conflicting truths at once, and pass the burden of the Resistance's future to them.
        
    - **Key Info:** Knows Jitterhop's true allegiance; holds the weavesilk replacement tool.
        
    - **Ma Mannerism:** Tilts her blindfolded head toward the chaotic ceiling, letting a long, heavy sigh stretch until the ambient noise of the cavern fills the void, before whispering her response.
        

### 7. Relevant Monsters

- **Sendryr's Conjurations (Monsters):** Abstract forms made of shifting starlight and shadow. _DM Tool: Use the **Shadow**, **Animated Armor**, or **Star Spawn Grue** stat blocks, reflavored as semi-translucent magical constructs._
    
- **Sendryr's Conjurations (Traps):** Invisible fields of magical force. _DM Tool: Treat as the **Glyph of Warding** spell (dealing 3d8 Radiant or Necrotic damage, DC 14 Dex save) or the **Web** spell (DC 14 Dex save to avoid restraint)._
