# Vase
**Stage 1: The Father (Captain Clay Argilla)**
- **The Face:** His father, looking older, battered, and soaked in the hazardous acid the _Lead Belly_ hauls, clutching a mortal wound.
- **Targeted Fear:** Guilt that playing "hero" in the Resistance is actively painting a target on his family's back.
- **Key Quote:** _"You're playing at war, Vase. You think you're protecting us? You're just a boy painting a target on our backs."_
- **The Resolution:** Vase must assert his own agency and verbally separate his path from his father's, taking the metaphorical "helm" of the situation.

**Stage 2: The Mentor (Uncle Mithbarakaz)**
- **The Face:** Tarnished, cracked silver dragon scales that are actively bleeding out golden light.
- **Targeted Fear:** Powerlessness and his namesake—the fear that he is truly too "fragile" to save the people he cares about.
- **Key Quote:** _"I survived the breaking of the world, but I could not survive your foolish rebellion. When they came for me... where were you?"_
- **The Resolution:** Vase must push through the overwhelming despair and prove he isn't "fragile" by aggressively shattering the illusion's authority or stating his resilience.
**Stage 3: The Unseen Knife (Collin McCambridge / Eobard Aurum)**
- **The Face:** Shifts from a warmly smiling Collin into the arrogant, ruthless sneer of Eobard Aurum holding a bloody dagger.
- **Targeted Fear:** Paranoia that Vase's optimism is a fatal flaw, and that his trust in his friends will get him killed.
- **Key Quote:** _"Did you really think we were friends? I am Aurum. You were always just a pawn, and you were too blindly optimistic to see the knife coming."_
- **The Resolution:** Vase must actively choose _trust_ over paranoia. He overcomes the spirit by rejecting the premise that compassion and trust are weaknesses.

# Vaxen
**Stage 1: The Pathetic End (The Expectation)**
- **The Face:** One of their older siblings, appearing exactly as they did in their final, embarrassing, non-heroic death.
- **Targeted Fear:** The curse of the bloodline—that no matter how hard Vaxen fights, they are destined to die a meaningless, pathetic, or funny death, making the title "The Unkillable" a cosmic joke.
- **Key Quote:** _"Look at us, little sibling. The great warriors of the Horde. I tripped on a wet root. You’re going to die choking on tavern stew. Stop pretending. There is no glory for our bloodline."_
- **The Resolution:** Vaxen must assert their own worth in the present moment, rather than fixating on their eventual end. They must strike back by declaring that their actions in life define them, not the manner of their death.

**Stage 2: The Empty Hearth (The Regret)**
- **The Face:** The armor and weapons melt away, revealing their parents or a beloved older sibling dressed in simple, casual clothing, sitting by an illusory fire looking profoundly sad and lonely.
- **Targeted Fear:** The crushing realization that Vaxen was so obsessed with preparing for a "good death" that they completely ignored having a good life with their family while they were still here.
- **Key Quote:** _"You spent so much time sharpening your axe for the end, you never just sat by the fire with us. We just wanted our child. Now, we are nothing to you but a disappointing legacy and skulls on a string."_
- **The Resolution:** Vaxen must drop their arrogant warrior facade. To progress, they need to acknowledge the wasted time, showing genuine grief or remorse for not being present, rather than treating their family as a martial failure.
**Stage 3: The True Curse (The Breaking Point)**
- **The Face:** The spirit takes the form of Vaxen themselves—but older, battered, heavily scarred, and utterly alone, clutching the broken weapons of Otis, Twilight, Collin, and the rest of the party.
- **Targeted Fear:** The ultimate misunderstanding—that their family _never_ wanted Vaxen to die gloriously to redeem them. They just wanted Vaxen to _live_. The pursuit of a glorious death is a selfish illusion that will ultimately just leave Vaxen entirely alone.
- **Key Quote:** _"Did you really think bleeding out in the mud would make us proud? We didn't want you to die for us, Vaxen, we wanted you to live! Keep chasing your glorious end... you'll just outlive everyone else you love until there is no one left to mourn you."_
- **The Resolution:** Vaxen must actively choose _life_ over the pursuit of a glorious death. They overcome the vision by declaring their intention to survive and protect their current "found family" (the party). They must redefine "The Unkillable" from a mocking curse into a vow to endure for those they care about.
# Cletus
**Stage 1: The Abandoned Plow (The Doubt)**
- **The Face:** His father (or mother), looking deeply aged, exhausted, and physically broken, their hands bleeding from working the hard dirt of the Ironplow farm alone.
- **Targeted Fear:** The guilt of abandoning his family and his duties. The fear that his departure directly caused their suffering and hardship while he was away living comfortably at the academy.
- **Key Quote:** _"We needed a son to pull the plow, Cletus. We needed your hands in the dirt. Instead, we got a ghost who ran away to play with glowing toys while our backs break."_
- **The Resolution:** Cletus must drop his signature bravado. To progress, he cannot boast or deflect with a joke; he must genuinely acknowledge the hardship his absence caused without letting it break his resolve to move forward.
**Stage 2: The Wasted Potential (The Regret)**
- **The Face:** Professor Jitterhop, but his face is cruel, twisted, and disappointed. He holds up Cletus's most ramshackle, duct-taped, "trial-and-error" inventions, letting them fall apart in his hands.
- **Targeted Fear:** Imposter syndrome. The dread that he isn't actually a good artificer, that he will never have anything real to show for his time away, and that his "function over form" style is just a pathetic excuse for his lack of magical aptitude.
- **Key Quote:** _"Did you actually think you could revolutionize the world with this scrap? You're no artificer, boy. You're just a truant farmhand who is too embarrassed to go home empty-handed."_
- **The Resolution:** Cletus must assert the value of his unique, messy methods. He overcomes this stage by standing proudly by his "if it works, it works" philosophy, breaking the "perfect" academic illusion with his own chaotic, hands-on ingenuity.

**Stage 3: The Unasked-For Savior (The Breaking Point)**
- **The Face:** The spirit takes the form of Cletus himself—but a cold, hyper-advanced, fully metallic version whose face is entirely obscured by pristine, flawless Armorer plating. He stands in a "perfect," fully mechanized farm completely devoid of family, warmth, or life.
- **Targeted Fear:** The ultimate alienation. The realization that his family _never asked_ him to revolutionize agriculture, and that his obsession with building the "perfect machine" to buy their forgiveness will only separate him from them forever.
- **Key Quote:** _"They didn't ask for a revolution, Cletus. They just asked for you. But you don't fit in the dirt anymore. Keep trying to buy their love with machines, and soon you'll be nothing but cold metal too."_
- **The Resolution:** Cletus must reconcile his two halves. He defeats the vision by acknowledging that his worth to his family is not contingent on what he invents for them, but simply on being their son. He must prioritize his connection to his roots over his technological obsession.
# Colin
**Stage 1: The Collateral (The "McCambridge" Family)**
- **The Face:** The father and brother from his "Collin McCambridge" life—the ones who were killed when his magical abilities were discovered by House Aurum.
- **Targeted Fear:** The core dread that his mere presence is a death sentence. The belief that keeping his secrets and hiding his true nature inherently destroys the innocent people who care for him.
- **Key Quote:** _"You know what happens to the people who shelter you, Collin. We bled for your secrets. How long until your new friends bleed, too? You aren't a companion; you are a plague disguised as a friend."_
- **The Resolution:** Collin must step out of his isolation. To progress, he must verbally reject the idea that he is a curse, declaring his intention to actively protect his new family rather than just running away to keep them safe.
**Stage 2: The Crosshairs (The Found Family)**
- **The Face:** Rapidly shifts between Vase, Vaxen, Twilight, Otis, and Cletus—but their faces are pale, battered, and they are choking on the golden chains the spirit wields.
- **Targeted Fear:** The present regret that by staying with the party because he likes them, he is selfishly painting a House Aurum target directly on their backs.
- **Key Quote:** _"You think you're rebelling? You're just drawing them a map. The longer you pretend to belong here, the tighter the gold wraps around their necks. Leave them, or you are signing their death warrants yourself."_
- **The Resolution:** Collin must accept the risk of connection. He overcomes this stage by asserting that the party is strong enough to stand _with_ him, actively breaking the "golden chains" of his self-imposed isolation and trusting his friends' autonomy.

**Stage 3: The Prodigal Son (High Baron Magnus Aurum)**
- **The Face:** High Baron Magnus Aurum. He does not look angry; instead, he looks terrifyingly warm, proud, and welcoming—an exact mirror of Collin’s recent vision.
- **Targeted Fear:** The terrifying, gravity-like pull of his bloodline. The fear that his father will inevitably crush the party just to retrieve him, and that the only way to truly save his friends is to surrender to Magnus and become Eobard Aurum again.
- **Key Quote:** _"You can play the rogue, Eobard, but you are still Aurum. Born of Steel, Crowned by Gold. Stop this foolish rebellion. Come home to me, my prodigal son, and I will let your little pets live."_
- **The Resolution:** Collin must reject the false mercy of his father. He defeats the vision by owning his true identity on his own terms—acknowledging that he _is_ Eobard, but firmly declaring that his loyalty lies with the Resistance and the party, permanently severing his father's hold over his soul.
# Otis
**Stage 1: The Forsaken Root (The Tribe Chief / His Father)**
- **The Face:** His father (or the traditionalist Tribe Chief), looking profoundly disappointed and clutching a withered, dead sapling.
- **Targeted Fear:** The guilt of straying from his peaceful, animistic upbringing and losing his connection to the First Tree/Great Cycle.
- **Key Quote:** _"You carry a club made of our sacred wood, but you swing it with a heart full of poison. You have forgotten the Great Cycle, Otis. You are no longer a son of the First Tree; you are just another butcher."_
- **The Resolution:** Otis must reconcile his faith with his actions. He must verbally reject the rigid pacifism that allowed his people to be enslaved, asserting that sometimes a protector must use violence to defend the forest.
**Stage 2: The Blunt Weapon (Gnarl Thulu)**
- **The Face:** Shifts into Gnarl Thulu, covered in fresh wounds, looking enraged and disgusted by Otis's perceived softness.
- **Targeted Fear:** His failure to be the ruthless warrior Gnarl wants him to be; the imposter syndrome that his hesitation and gentle nature will get his allies killed.
- **Key Quote:** _"I tried to toughen you up, but you're still just a soft little sapling! You hesitate! Your weakness is going to get us all slaughtered. You don't have the stomach for the Blood Horde!"_
- **The Resolution:** Otis must reject the binary choice between being a pacifist victim and a mindless butcher. He overcomes this stage by asserting his _own_ brand of strength—controlled, righteous anger used only to protect his "pack," rather than Gnarl's reckless violence.
**Stage 3: The Grand Distraction (The Faceless Silver Knight)**
- **The Face:** The wood burns away to reveal a towering, immaculate, faceless Silver Knight of House Argentum. Beneath its heavy boots, the spectral forms of Otis's mother and the Thulu tribe are locked in cages.
- **Targeted Fear:** The dread that the party's massive, complicated quest is just a distraction. The fear that by getting swept up in world-altering events, he is playing right into House Argentum's hands by abandoning the simple goal of freeing his people.
- **Key Quote:** _"Look how far you've wandered, Goliath. Chasing floating islands and ancient magic while your family rots in our camps. You haven't rebelled; you've just distracted yourself. We couldn't have planned it better."_
- **The Resolution:** Otis must break the false dichotomy between his personal goal and the party's path. He defeats the vision by declaring that the party's fight _is_ his people's fight, recognizing that dismantling the Crusade's power on a grand scale is the ultimate way to shatter the chains holding his tribe.
# Twilight
**Stage 1: The Inadequate Bandage (The Doubt)**
- **The Face:** A shifting, battered amalgamation of Vase, Otis, and Cletus. They look exhausted, wounded, and dismissive.
- **Targeted Fear:** The doubt that his "therapy" and listening ear aren't actually doing anything to solve their massive, life-threatening problems. The fear that he is just applying bandages to mortal wounds.
- **Key Quote:** _"You sit and you listen, Twilight, but talk doesn't stop the bleeding. We are all breaking apart, and your soft words and innkeeper's wisdom aren't enough to hold us together. You don't know how to save us."_
- **The Resolution:** Twilight must assert the value of healing and presence. He must reject the idea that he has to "fix" their problems instantly, declaring that simply giving them a safe place to rest in the dark is a valid and necessary strength.

**Stage 2: The Crushing Tide (The Regret/Escalation)**
- **The Face:** The faces shift to Collin and Vaxen—the two members drowning in the darkest, most dangerous legacies (House Aurum, Blood Horde curses). They actively pull away from him.
- **Targeted Fear:** Compassion fatigue and the fear of inadequacy. The terrifying realization that his friends' problems are vastly bigger than what a friendly bartender can handle, and the dread that their trauma will eventually drag him down with them.
- **Key Quote:** _"We don't need a bartender, Twilight. We are fighting a war against gods and empires. Our burdens are going to crush you, and when you finally break under the weight of our trauma, who is going to pick up the pieces?"_
- **The Resolution:** Twilight must embrace the weight without letting it break him. He overcomes this stage by declaring his own resilience, proving he is not just a soft ear, but a Cleric of Night who is strong enough to endure the darkness alongside them.
**Stage 3: The Martyr's Hubris (The Breaking Point)**
- **The Face:** The spirit takes the form of Twilight himself—but monstrously warped. He is a frozen, martyred stone statue holding up the entire world, completely alone, with his friends trapped and suffocating _beneath_ the rubble he is failing to hold up.
- **Targeted Fear:** The hubris of the caregiver. The fear that by trying to figure out how to support _everyone_ and be the sole "backbone," he is actually stripping them of their agency. If he insists on carrying the whole party's weight, his inevitable collapse will destroy them all.
- **Key Quote:** _"You want to carry it all for them? You think you are the only thing keeping them standing? That isn't love, Twilight, that's arrogance. Keep trying to carry the whole shattered world on your back, and you'll just bury them when you fall."_
- **The Resolution:** Twilight must realize that true support doesn't mean carrying the weight _for_ them; it means standing _beside_ them. He defeats the final vision by actively sharing the load, declaring his trust in his friends' own strength and stepping out from under the "martyr's burden."
