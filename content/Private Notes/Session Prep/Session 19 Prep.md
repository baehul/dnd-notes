## Mystery

* **Scene 0**:
	* silas and his identical twin brother mulvin are deep in a dark forgotten part of the library.
	* silas realizes in horror what their breakthrough is. "we have to tell everybody".
	* mulvin reveals themselves out fo teh  illusions and stabs silas
* **Scene 1: The Hook (Rat Race Detective Agency)**
    * **The Client:** "Mulvin" (actually the disguised Inquisitor) hires the party to find his twin brother, Silas.
    * **The Hook:** Silas and Mulvin were researching Terramancy to stop the city from sinking. Silas made a breakthrough, missed their meeting to go to the Library, and has vanished.
    * **The Lead:** "Mulvin" says they last saw each other at a local speakeasy.

* **Scene 2: The Alleyway Ambush (The Speakeasy)**
    * **The Ambush:** As the party investigates the foggy, monochrome alleyway outside the speakeasy, they are jumped by desperate Sinking City back-alley thugs.
    * **Combat Note:** Use standard Thug/Bandit stats; play up the gritty, splashing mud and the sudden burst of colorful magic if the party casts spells.

* **Scene 3: Interrogation & The Clue**
    * **The Interrogation:** The party shakes down a surviving thug. 
        * *Mechanic:* **Influence action (DC 13 Charisma - Intimidation/Persuasion)**. Advantage if they rough them up or leverage the GSC.
        * *Reveal:* The thug spits out that a "fancy lady" (Alexandra Aurum) and some heavily armed, well-trained enforcers snatched the gnome. 
    * **The Clue:** Searching the alleyway mud.
        * *Mechanic:* **DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception)**. 
        * *Reveal:* They find a crumpled, blood-spattered GSC transaction receipt for a "Class-A Library Access Token" stamped with the time of the abduction.

* **Scene 4: The GSC Office (Following the Lead)**
    * **The Investigation:** The party heads to the local GSC office to track the receipt and the description of the heavy enforcers. 
    * **The Vibe:** Pristine, bureaucratic, and shiny—a stark contrast to the muddy, desperate city outside. The party sees that the GSC guards here do *not* match the description of Silas's kidnappers. 
    * **The Discoveries:** By bribing, charming, or sneaking a look at the GSC records, the party learns two things:
        1.  The Library manifest shows that both Silas and Mulvin have *already* checked into the Library.
        2.  Mulvin recently signed a lease and paid a heavy sum of Frosted Silver for a large warehouse down by the docks.

* **Scene 5: The Black-Market Docks (The Warehouse)**
    * **The Trail:** Following the lease paperwork from the GSC, the party heads to the docks to search Mulvin's warehouse.
    * **The Docks Vibe:** A shady area where unregistered Weavesilk sails and illicit frosted silver are being traded in the shadows. 
    * **The Discovery:** The party kicks open the warehouse doors expecting to find the captured Silas or clues to the Terramancy research. Instead, they find the *real* Mulvin tied to a chair.

* **Scene 6: The Twist & The Counter-Offer**
    * **The Reveal:** The "Fake Mulvin" traveling with the party drops their *Disguise Self* spell, revealing themselves as a cold, calculating Ecclesia Solari Inquisitor.
    * **The Truth:** The Inquisitor and Alexandra Aurum were working together to seize the Terramancy research. Alexandra double-crossed the Church, took Silas, and went to the Library herself.
    * **The Offer:** The Inquisitor hands the party a heavy pouch of Frosted Silver (doubling their original pay) to act as bloodhounds: infiltrate the Library, find Silas, and retrieve the research for the Church.

* **Scene 7: The Library Approach (Open-Ended)**
    * **The Obstacle:** The GSC has a monopoly on the Library and guards it heavily. Only those with Class-A Access Tokens are allowed inside.
    * **Player Agency:** The party must brainstorm and execute a plan (heist, bribery, forgery, or sewer-crawling through the pressure valves) to break into the sinking depths of the library to confront Alexandra Aurum.

### 5. Fantastic Locations
* **The Sinking City Streets:** A beautiful tragedy. Neon and harsh yellow arcanotech lights reflect in the flooded, cracked streets. Massive, industrial grates constantly hiss with scalding steam, blanketing the city in a perpetual, claustrophobic fog that hides the stars.
#### 1. The Approach (Nearing the City)
> "As the Ferryman poles the raft forward, the vibrant, glowing flora of the swamp begins to wilt and change. The terrain abruptly drops off, sloping downward into a massive, island-wide sinkhole—a colossal bowl of churning mud and deep water. Resting at the very bottom of this crater is the Sinking City. 
>
> A thick, unnatural fog pours out from the city center, rolling over the swamp water to meet you. As the raft breaches the fog bank, you watch in awe as the color is literally stripped from the world. The rich greens of the swamp, the brown of the wood, the color of your own clothes and skin—it all washes away into stark, high-contrast shades of black, white, and grayscale. **The only colors that survive the transition are the magical auras you carry: the vibrant liquid of Auntie Dorian's potion, the faint hum of your enchanted weapons, and the lingering golden sparks on Collin's skin.** Below you, half-drowned in the sinkhole, the jagged silhouettes of sagging bayou mansions and industrial towers pierce the monochrome smog. The only sound is the rhythmic, mechanical hissing of massive steam grates echoing through the mist."

#### 2. Inside the City (The Streets)
> "Stepping onto the streets of the Sinking City feels like walking into an old photograph. Everything is draped in heavy, grayscale shadows. The cobblestones beneath your boots are cracked and slick with an oily, black mud that oozes up from between the stones. Massive, iron 'Pressure-Valves of the Deep' line the street corners, violently venting blinding white steam into the air, keeping the oppressive, colorless fog thick around your ankles and shoulders. 
>
> The architecture is a tragic clash of eras: elegant, rotting plantation homes with sagging wrap-around porches and wrought-iron balconies, now bolted together and held aloft by brutalist, riveted GSC ironwork. The locals move quickly with their heads down, their faces hidden beneath the wide brims of waxed hats and heavy, dark trench coats. It is a city of sharp shadows, cold steam, and desperate silence, punctuated only by the harsh, colorful glare of magical neon arcanotech cutting through the monochrome dark."

#### 3. Flavorful Elements (To sprinkle in as they explore)

* **The Single Splash of Color (The Magic Rule):** Emphasize how jarring and beautiful magic looks here. When a player casts a spell like *Firebolt*, the orange and red flames reflect off the wet, grayscale cobblestones. If someone uses *Cure Wounds*, the soft blue light illuminates the alleyway. This makes magic feel incredibly special, but also means that **stealth is easily broken by spellcasting.** Any color immediately draws the eye of the GSC guards or the desperate locals.
* **The Weeping Architecture:** Because the city sits in a sinkhole, the moisture never leaves. Water constantly drips from the Spanish moss hanging off the iron balconies, sounding like the ticking of a clock. Puddles of ink-black swamp water reflect the stark white streetlamps.
* **The Bayou Blues (Soundscape):** To cover the sounds of the groaning, sinking earth, buskers play mournful, echoing music in the alleyways. You might hear the slow, wailing slide of a brass trumpet or the rhythmic, swampy twang of a banjo echoing through the heavy fog.
* **The Steam-Blind Intersections:** The GSC Pressure-Valves vent sporadically. As the party walks, a valve might suddenly blow, completely blinding an intersection in a thick cloud of white, hissing steam for a few seconds—perfect for a stealthy tail to lose the party, or for the alleyway thugs to launch an ambush.
#### Other parts of city
* **Drowned Ground Floors:** The city is actively sinking. Remind the players of this by describing how the ground floors of many older buildings are completely submerged in hardened mud. Doors are uselessly buried, and citizens have to use rickety wooden walkways and rope bridges to enter buildings through the second-story windows.
* **The Speakeasy Alley:** Cramped, smelling of cheap liquor and wet mud. The glow of the bioluminescent swamp flora tries to peek through the grime but is stamped out by heavy boots.
* **The Dockside Warehouse:** Rotting wood and the smell of saltwater mixed with the metallic tang of illicit frosted silver. Unregistered Weavesilk sails are being traded in the shadows.
