## 2. Strong Start

They are in **free-fall** where Session 26 left them.

The Library catches them. Not magic exactly — **drifts of loose paper**, centuries of pages worked free of their bindings and settled waist-deep, that take the impact and give. ***Feather Fall*** makes it graceful rather than merely survivable.

They come up out of the paper into a **safe hub**: still, dry, unlit except for what they brought. Nothing here wants to hurt them. Three passages lead off it. A fourth is a **collapsed tunnel**, and it's the way onward.

## 5. Fantastic Locations

### The Hub
Safe. Dry. Deep drifts of loose paper. Three passages and one collapse.

### Room 1 — The Refraction Bubble
A glowing abjuration sphere encasing the override, taller than a man, pale script crawling its surface.

- **Rule:** it mirrors **exactly one force per round** — swing, spell, shove, thrown stone — straight back at the source. It does not scratch, dim, or care.
- **The answer:** everyone applies force **at once**. It can only mirror the **single greatest** vector, so it throws the strongest of them across the room and buckles under the rest.
- **Cost:** **3d6 force**, automatic, to whoever pushed hardest, each round they try. No save — clean cause and effect.
- Repeated single attacks will eventually shatter it rather than collapse it. Messy, loud, and it costs them.

### Room 2 — The Crossing
A pit **≈100 ft across, 60–80 ft wide**, chest-deep mud below, spanned by slick beams **about a foot wide** with **four crossing points spaced 25–30 ft apart**. The override is on the far side. **Three components** must make the crossing.

**Two wards, claiming people each round:**
> **The Snare** — claimed creatures **cannot move** this turn. **DC 13 Strength** save. Fail: **2d6 force**, knocked **prone**.
> **The Tithe** — claimed creatures **must end their turn holding nothing.** **DC 13 Dexterity** save. Fail: **2d6 force**, and the component is torn from their hands into the mud.

**No damage on a successful save** — same rule as Session 28.

**Deal the claims:** six tokens, three of each, shuffled and dealt **face-up** at the start of each round.

| Action | DC |
| :--- | :--- |
| Walk a beam at half speed | No check |
| Move full speed along a beam | **DC 12 Dex (Acrobatics)** — fail: fall in |
| **Dash** along a beam | **DC 15 Dex (Acrobatics)** |
| Hand off to an adjacent creature | Free (object interaction) |
| Toss a component up to 15 ft | **DC 13 Dex** — fail: it sinks |
| Haul back onto a beam | **DC 13 Str (Athletics)**, costs half movement |
| Recover a sunken component | **DC 14 Str (Athletics)**, one action |

**How it resolves:** Tithe-claimed PCs are **carriers** (must hand off before their turn ends). Snare-claimed PCs are **anchors** (can hold indefinitely, can't advance it). A component only moves when a carrier and an anchor are in reach of each other — so the party strings out as a bucket brigade that **re-forms every round** as the claims change.

**Budget 5–8 rounds.** Round one is deployment and they'll get it wrong.

*Optional clock: the mud rises, shortening the beams.*

### Room 3 — The Bound Cartographer
A crumbling vault of pre-Fragmentation cartography — rotting globes, rusted brass astrolabes, copper Ley-Line compasses on stands. **Standing water, ankle-deep, in one corner.** The override is a heavy iron wheel set into a pressurised arcane pipe.

- **The tell:** the golden magic **arcs toward metal**. **DC 13 Arcana or Investigation** — the seal holds a charge it wants to shed, and it is *anchored*, not self-sustaining.
- **Attacking the ghost:** does nothing. The seal swallows it and **lashes back for 3d6 radiant.**

| Anchor | How it comes down |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The Conduit** | Haul the brass stand out of alignment — **DC 14 Strength (Athletics)** |
| **The Charge** | Earth it into the standing water with chains, compasses, brass — **DC 13 Arcana**, or an action |
| **The Escapement** | Jam the fine mechanism — **DC 14 Dexterity**, tools or Sleight of Hand |

- **The window:** drop any anchor and the ghost goes **slack for the rest of the round.** It re-forms at the start of the next round. **Disrupt early and five people can act; disrupt last and nobody can.** Let them waste the first one.
- **The wheel:** **three successful turns**, each an action taken **inside a window** — **DC 13 Strength.** It means gently moving a dead man's fingers aside, over and over.
- **On the third turn:** the seal breaks, the gold drains out of him, and he lets go of the wheel for the first time in a very long time.
