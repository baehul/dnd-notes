## Player Characters
- **[[Collin McCambridge]]:** Shaken by the vision of **High Baron Magnus** and the "Golden Coin Sun." Paranoid about his identity being revealed in the Spire.
- **[[Cletus Ironplow]]:** His attempt to jam the lever failed. Can use his Artificer knowledge to identify the **Arcane Anvil** in the trash or the mechanics of the **Automatons**.
- **[[Otis Thulu]]:** Noticed strange writing on the barrels. Will be confronted by the "Charity Gala" propaganda regarding displaced tribes.
- **[[Twilight Emberrest]] & [[Vase the Fragile]]:** Naivety tested by the harsh reality of the industrial Docks vs. the fake benevolence of the Gala.
- **[[Vaxen the Unkillable]]:** Looking for a fight; the Ooze battle is immediate gratification.
## Strong Start
- **Scene:** The "Bio-Cleanse" begins immediately.
- **Mechanics:** The hold fills with Acid.
    - **Acid:** Moving through the acid requires a **DC 12 Dexterity Save** or take **2d6 Acid Damage**.
    - **Stealth Tension:** Casting spells or making loud weapon attacks forces a Stealth check (DC 14).  Taking damage forces a **Constitution Save (DC of damage)** to maintain Stealth. 
    - **Arabella Persuasion**: Arabella rolls deception (+0) vs Dagna's insight (+2) on init 20. Each success or fail bumps the dice bonus/penalty
- **Monsters:** 2 **Gelatinous Cubes** (The "Bio-Scrubbers") drop into the liquid.
- **Objective:** **Captain Arabella** successfully persuades **Lady Dagna** to leave the ship or combat is ended.
## Scenes
- **Scene 1: The Bio-Cleanse (Combat/Hazard).**
    - Survive the acid and oozes while Arabella talks down the inspectors.
- **Scene 2: The Silent Voyage (Transition/Roleplay).**
    - The ship travels from the inspection point to the **Sun Spire** (1 day).
    - **Tone:** The hold smells of acid. A moment for Otis to discuss the writing he saw or for the party to plan their disguise.
- **Scene 3: The Docks of Gold (Exploration).**
    - **Visual:** Massive **Magnetic Cranes** lifting the hidden crystal barrels off and loading "Trash" onto _The Lead Belly_.
    - **Discovery:** Finding the **Arcane Anvil** in the scrap heap.
    - **The Betrayal:** Arabella nervously leaves to "take care of business" (selling out the party/paying her debt).
    - **Obstacle:** Sneaking past **Automaton Sentries** patrolling the perimeter.
- **Scene 4: The Charity of the Sun (Social Infiltration).**
    - **The Event:** A "Charity Gala" hosted by **House Aurum**.
    - **Atmosphere:** Displaced "undesirables" (Refugees/Laborers) lined up for handouts. Propaganda posters show happy refugees in camps (direct conflict for Otis).
    - **Goal:** Blend into the line to bypass the outer gate and enter the Spire's lower levels.
## Secrets
- [ ] **The Writing On the Barrels:** The writing on the barrels is Deep Speech (language of [[The Terramancers]]) marking the quantity and quality of the [[Resonance Crystals]]
- [ ] **The Lamp Room:** [[Lancaster Slickwhistle]] is being held in the "Lamp Room"
- [ ] **Project Orchestra Deadline:** The [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]] is under immense pressure. He must present results at the upcoming "Showcase" or face severe political consequences.
- [ ] **The Trash Heap**: Valuable magical equipment (like the Anvil) is frequently thrown away by Ephraim simply for having minor aesthetic flaws.
## Locations
- **The Acid Hold:** Cramped, industrial, rising green liquid, smelling of bleach.
- **The Sun Spire Docks:** Dominated by rhythmic, deafening **Magnetic Cranes**. Piles of scrap metal.
- **The Gala Courtyard:** Gaudy golden banners draped over industrial soot. Meager food tables set up for the poor, surrounded by armed guards in pristine uniforms.
## NPCs
- **[[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe]]:** Nervous, chain-smoking, guilt-ridden. Trying to justify her impending betrayal.
- **[[Lady Dagna Cuprum]]** (Voice): Authoritative, pragmatic, impatient with incompetence.
- **[[Wolfram Zoloto]]**: Russian voice, head of security
- **Automaton Sentries:** Cold, unfeeling constructs guarding the Docks.
