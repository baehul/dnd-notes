# Session 30 Prep: Freeing Sylas

## 1. Review the characters
Skill numbers are approximate (DM's D&D Beyond screenshots, 2026-10-10).
- **Cletus** (Artificer 7, Armorer): Arcana **+10**, Investigation **+7**, Nature **+7**. Tinker's, Thieves', Carpenter's, Weaver's, Leatherworker's and Smith's Tools. His grinding tools spook Bloos; his Weaver's Tools make the sweaters. Only PC with Tinker's Tools.
- **Collin** (Rogue 4 Soulknife / Wizard 3 Diviner): Investigation, Perception, Insight **+10**, Arcana **+7**. Thieves' Tools, Calligrapher's Supplies. Hair and eyes fully gold since the teleport scroll (H-001).
- **Twilight** (Cleric 7, Trickery): Perception **+I st10**, Religion and Insight about **+7**, Medicine **+4**. Herbalism Kit. Carried Sylas out of the Library with Vaxen's help.
- **Vase** (Fighter 1 / Bard 6, Valor): Persuasion, Deception **+10**, Acrobatics **+7**. Lyre. Holds the Ring of Restless Revelation (I-012).
- **Otis** (Barbarian 7, World Tree): Survival, Animal Handling, Perception **+5**. Carpenter's Tools. Woke holding a green leaf (H-008, I-013).
- **Vaxen** (Fighter 7, Champion): Athletics **+7**. Three-Dragon Ante Set. Newly attuned to the Restless Blade (I-008).
- **Nobody has Jeweler's Tools proficiency.** Recipes are unchanged; the party has to solve it (learn, find someone, or work around it).

## 2. Strong start
- The Ferryman sets them down on their island. **It has taken on the swamp:** mud, damp boards, soft footing. Scenery only (the island slowly takes on the character of where it sits).
- **Bloos** is at the dock, thrilled by the shell Twilight and Vaxen carry. **The glow is one only Bloos can see.**
- **The Lair casts off** toward the Jade Fragment (the players already know how the Engine steers). First job: work out Sylas's state.
- **Bloos volunteers, distracted:** he sees *tangled colours with bright knots*. PCs may ask follow-ups. The party learns the rescue is **layered and involved**, not the specific gates.

## 3. Potential scenes

### The rescue: six days, three gates
- **Each PC does one thing per day:** roll on Sylas, **Help** someone's roll (advantage; 2024 rules: the helper must be proficient in the skill or tool used), craft (8 ritual hours), or a personal project. A PC who spends **half a day** on a fix can't make a rescue roll that day (the other half can go to crafting).
- **Gates**, in order; several can fall in one day:
  - **Gate I, Read the Shell: DC 13, 3 successes.** Arcana, Investigation, Perception, Religion, or Bloos's Prismatic Perception.
  - **Gate II, Loosen It: DC 15, 4 successes.** Thieves' or Tinker's Tools, Vase's lyre listening for resonance, Carpenter's Tools (cradle), or Arcana.
  - **Gate III, Steady and Draw: DC 17, 4 successes.** Athletics to hold the shell, Herbalism Kit or healing, Insight or Persuasion to guide Sylas through waking, or Arcana.
- **Every failed roll:** **+1 to the failure tally**, and roll on the current gate's complication table.
- **End of day 6:** any missing successes complete automatically, **each +1 to the tally**. Sylas is always freed.
- **Waking tier (by tally):**
  - **5 or fewer:** clear-headed.
  - **6 to 9:** groggy and weak, **1 level of Exhaustion** until a long rest.
  - **10 or more:** shaky. **1 level of Exhaustion** plus slow, halting speech; fully himself the next day.
- **Scale:** six PCs over six days is 36 PC-days; the rescue takes about 18 to 20 rolls. An all-in party finishes around day 3 or 4.
- Rolls with features that change them (Remarkable Athlete, Portent, Guidance, Bardic Inspiration) work as normal.

### Complication tables (d6 on every failed roll, on that gate's table)

**Gate I: Read the Shell**
1. **Misread.** Lose the most recent Gate I success.
2. **Conflicting readings.** The next roll can only be made **with Help**.
3. **Shifting patterns.** The shell reads differently by lamplight than daylight; the next roll must happen in the other half of the day.
4. **Eye strain.** That PC's next roll has **disadvantage**.
5. **The shell hums when handled wrongly.** The next roll has **disadvantage**.
6. **Dead end.** The approach just used can't be used again on Gate I.

**Gate II: Loosen It**
1. **A fracture runs the wrong way.** Spend **half a day** bracing it (Carpenter's or Tinker's Tools), or the next roll has **disadvantage**.
2. **The crystal rings back.** The approach just used can't be used on the next roll.
3. **A tool fails.** **Disadvantage** until someone spends **half a day** repairing it (Tinker's Tools).
4. **The shell shifts in its cradle.** **Half a day** to adjust the cradle before the next roll.
5. **Crystal dust.** The next roll needs a **Helper** to clear it and hold the light.
6. **Gives too fast.** Lose 1 Gate II success.

**Gate III: Steady and Draw**
1. **The knife shifts.** **DC 13 Athletics or Medicine** to hold it, or lose 1 Gate III success.
2. **His body reacts.** Spend **half a day** stabilising him (Medicine, Herbalism Kit, or healing).
3. **The shell clings.** The next roll can only be made **with Help**.
4. **The holder tires.** The PC who made the last Athletics roll loses **half a day**.
5. **Rushed pull.** That PC can't roll on Sylas tomorrow (they can still craft).
6. **He stirs too early.** Sylas half-wakes and struggles, wordless. **DC 15 Insight or Persuasion**, or the next roll needs **Help**.

### Bloos: the sweater trade
- Cletus spends **half a day with Weaver's Tools** on a sweater. Bloos then stays focused for **two days**, giving **advantage on one roll per day** on any gate.
- Without one, his quirks are roleplay: he bolts at grinding tools and stares at glows.

### Crafting
- **Supplies (DM ruling, explained to players above the table):** the party can spend gold at shop prices (**+10% while under way**) and the Lair produces the pieces they need, including the set of armor. Jeweler's Tools can be bought this way; **proficiency is still the party's problem**.
- Purse: **8,835 gp**. Reagents run about 200 to 275 gp per recipe under way.
- **Armor of Acid Resistance** (Cletus; Ooze Core held): 24 hours. Needs a set of armor. Final step: **DC 14 Leatherworker's (DEX) or Smith's (STR) Tools**.
- **Marionette's Fang** (Collin; tooth held): 8 hours. Needs **Jeweler's Tools**. Final check: the psychic blade, **DC 13**.
- **Self-Mending Desk** (Lair addition): 8 hours. Carpenter's Tools; Woodcarver's or Carpenter's at **DC 13**.
- **Pearl of Power** (Cletus): 24 hours. **Only once the shell's Resonance Crystal sand exists, after the rescue** (I-011). Needs **Jeweler's Tools**. Realistically only begun this session.
- Magic Levels per each recipe page (scrolls or NPC casters may stand in).
- The Instrument of the Bards is not part of this session.

### Personal projects
Left open. A day on a project gets whatever scene the DM runs at the table.

### Waking scene (Gate III complete)
- Sylas comes out at his tier. **The knife is drawn.**
- **Twilight** guides him gently through waking, then asks a question of the player's choosing. Sylas has nothing to give: **his last memory is Smoke's Speakeasy**.
- **The shop opens** with its opening stock. It orders under a pen name (Varius keeps believing the party is missing), the locked specimen case stays shut, and it sells no lead to the eye.

### Closing scene: the Jade Fragment
- **Eerie silence.** The **fog parts as if expecting them**, and the **whole swamp sways in the wind together**.
