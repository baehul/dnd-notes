# Session 31 – Prep: The Jade Fragment
*Covers the island run (Session 31) and the audience with Luwei (first half of Session 32). Prep only, not canon.*

## 1. Review the characters
- **Collin:** the trial is about putting him in his place. The island that always parted for him now works against him. He's known here as **the Whisper's Eyes** (a title new in this prep). Luwei uses Otis as the stick and Alexandra as the lever (T-001).
- **Otis:** he crushed Luwei's message (T-002). He's treated coldly, his debt hangs over Collin, and he's looped alone in the reeds. He has the green leaf from the apple tree (H-008).
- **Vase:** wears the ring that once blocked Luwei (I-012). The party knows he's Mithbarakaz's nephew, and the Ledger ask is made in front of him. ⚠️ **Get Vase's player's buy-in first.** The Ettercap web is for his Instrument of the Bards (I-009).
- **Twilight:** came for answers about the old world. He gets patronising tolerance from the inhabitants and wonder from the Old Path rite.
- **Vaxen:** the restless blade wants a worthy foe (I-008), and the wyverns can be it. Keep Luwei's voice distinct from the blade's whisper: sibilant, coming from the grass.
- **Cletus:** keep him clear of Engine talk.
- **Alexandra:** the "Aurum brought uninvited." *Decide before play whether she comes ashore (the Showrunner advises yes).*
- **Sylas:** *decide before play. The Showrunner advises keeping him aboard and away from Luwei.*

## 2. Strong start
Read to Collin as the fog closes:
> *The island you normally can navigate without issue, the bamboo forest that always seemed to part its way for you, the fog that never seemed to obscure your vision, all feel like they work against you now.*

Then the Landing, straight into the guard drakes.

## 3. Potential scenes (a linear path)
Party level 7, six PCs. Combat budgets follow your Combat Prep note's Level 7 benchmarks: **total CR 21, max single CR 11**.

1. **The Landing** *(obstacle)*
   - Fog, bamboo stakes in the shallows, and dragon-carved gateposts swallowed by the bamboo.
   - Collin's familiar paths dead-end. **Collin's navigation checks have disadvantage**, so someone else leads.
   - **Survival / Perception DC 15** to find solid ground. A failure costs time, or **1 level of Exhaustion** for whoever wades the mire.
2. **Green Guard Drakes** *(combat)*
   - **About 6 green guard drakes** (Guard Drake, *MotM*, CR 2 each; total CR 12, a test-level fight).
   - They're released down the path ahead of the party and herd them forward.
   - They break off when **Bloodied**, or when a reed-whistle sounds somewhere in the fog.
   - It's a test, not a hunt, and nobody blames the party for hurting them.
3. **The Village** *(roleplay, rest, restock)*
   - Kobold and grung stilt-houses among the bamboo.
   - Collin is recognised as the Whisper's Eyes, and the old deference wavers. A child runs to him and is called back.
   - Otis gets coldness. Twilight gets patronising tolerance. Alexandra is recognised as an Aurum, and Collin vouches for her.
   - **The shop's first restock** happens here.
   - **The dragon-dance rite:** kobolds under a long silk-and-bamboo dragon sing the Old Path song. **The song is the key to scene 4.**
   - **The Ettercap lead:** the grove the Tenders won't walk at dusk (Lair Shop Sure Thing).
   - The path bends around a guarded grove. **Never explained.**
   - Short rest available.
4. **The Whispering Reeds** *(puzzle)*
   - A wall of reeds that whispers back every word and won't part.
   - **Solution:** sing the village rite song (**Performance DC 13**, or simply sung in character). Fallback: **History or Religion DC 15** to recall its shape.
   - **The Otis beat:** the reeds close and Otis is looped alone for a few minutes. *Private aside to Collin:* he knows for certain it's Luwei. The voice speaks for the first time: *"Why shouldn't he be punished?"* The reeds reopen when Luwei chooses.
5. **The Ettercap's Grove** *(combat)*
   - Webs strung between scaled bamboo high in the canopy, and spiders dropping from above.
   - **2 Ettercaps (CR 2) and 8 Giant Spiders (CR 1)**, total CR 12. Scale up to 3 Ettercaps and 9 spiders (CR 15) if it's running easy.
   - **Harvest:** 1 bolt of Ettercap web, **Nature DC 16** (I-009).
   - **Likely end of Session 31.**
6. **The Wyvern Roost** *(final fight, opens Session 32)*
   - A bamboo bowl at the crown of the canopy.
   - **3 Wyverns** (CR 6 each), total CR 18, near the cap of 21. The fight is aerial and terrifying, but these are beasts, not Luwei's kin.
7. **The Kneeling and the Audience** *(social)*. See NPCs below.
   - A Tender speaks first, because Luwei doesn't address mortals.
   - Collin kneels and gives his report, and the reeds check it against what they've heard. The party can speak for him.
   - What Collin reveals about the Engine is his player's choice. Luwei probes and isn't answered.

## 4. Secrets and clues
These aren't tied to any particular scene; use them wherever they fit.
- Collin was once one of Luwei's favoured agents, and the islanders know him as the Whisper's Eyes.
- Luwei was the voice of Solaris that sent Alexandra into the Library, to deliver an Aurum to Collin. *Told to Collin alone, in a private aside.*
- Luwei knew nothing of the compass or the Library's Terramancy, and doesn't care.
- Luwei wants Alexandra for her **name** and her **wards**, both said aloud. Her **research** is only hinted at: he asks how much she understands of "this magic."
- Luwei is secretly pleased to have Alexandra. **The tell:** his whiskers twitch as if pleased. Use it once, on Alexandra only.
- The guarded grove the path bends around. *DM only: the egg. Never explained.*
- Luwei will sabotage Otis in fights to come. Collin alone sees it, it never drops Otis, and it never touches the coffer. Away from the island it comes as a curse or mark, not through the reeds.

## 5. Fantastic locations
- **The fog shore:** jade light, bamboo stakes, gateposts carved with dragons, Draconic spirit-path markers.
- **The scaled bamboo:** ridged, scale-patterned bark, and stalks that ring like chimes. Shed skins as long as rivers hang like silk. Pseudodragons dart overhead.
- **The village:** stilt-houses, cookfire smoke in the fog, offerings at the bases of reeds, the dragon dance.
- **The roots:** pre-Shattering bones of something enormous under the bamboo.
- **The Whispering Reeds:** a wall of sound that hands your words back to you.
- **The canopy crown:** the wyvern roost, a bowl of bent bamboo above the fog.
- **Luwei's grove:** he's rarely seen in full; usually just his head among the bamboo, moss-and-jade scales, whiskers twitching, a voice like wind in grass.

## 6. Important NPCs
- **Luwei, the Whisper in the Reed**
  - **Register:** the cold banker, with exactly one flash of wounded pride. The fog recoils, the ground turns out to be his coils, and a whisper becomes a roar for one word. Then silence.
  - **The ledger of slights:** the ring that shut him out, Otis crushing his message, Collin sparing the Aurum he delivered, and an Aurum brought uninvited into his fog. That last is publicly his worst insult and privately what pleases him.
  - **Verdict:** "tolerated, not forgiven," left open. If Collin fails to prove useful and loyal, the debt will be settled violently.
  - **Stick:** Otis's punishment hangs over Collin and grows, but is never carried out.
  - **Carrot:** concealment restored for Collin and extended to Alexandra, and more progress on the House Aurum promise.
  - **Lever:** make Alexandra useful to him.
  - **Asks:**
    - **The Restitution:** Otis carries a sealed coffer of reeds to the hydrosphere. It pays for the crushed message. *DM only: it's a listening post. Unsaid.*
    - **The Ledger:** proof of Mithbarakaz's double-dealing, asked **publicly**.
  - **Rules for playing him:** he never lies, only withholds; every threat is spoken aloud; he boasts about his schemes.
- **The Tenders** (kobolds and grung): texture only, not rivals. They speak for Luwei first and waver over their old deference to Collin.
