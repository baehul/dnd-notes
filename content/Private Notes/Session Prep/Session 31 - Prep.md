# Session 31 — Prep (Lazy DM)

**Party:** 6 PCs, level 7. Aboard, non-combatants: [[Bloos]], [[Lady Alexandra Aurum|Alexandra]], [[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn|Sylas]] (unconscious), [[Lancaster Slickwhistle]].
**Voyage clock:** about 3 of 6 days remain when the session opens.
**K-001 (DM-only):** with the one-week slip, Operation Green Thunder is about 14 days away on arrival; the party believes about 7.

**Shape:** Strong start (Collin & Alexandra) → voyage montage → a five-room trial on the Jade Fragment ([[Luwei]]'s island), a walk-through of Luwei and the [[Ancient Ones]] in the way the [[Monastery of Night]] showcased the Cult. Collin leads; the island reroutes. Luwei states nothing until Room 5. Session plays through his terms and ends on a hook.

**Recurring motif — the maw (Rooms 1–4).** Each room, a maw nearly clamps down on Otis and never does. **Only Collin sees it.** Deliver privately to Collin's player (note or whisper). It escalates each room and echoes Otis's Session 25 dream (a crocodile with unnatural eyes; swallowed by the ground). The punishment never lands.

### Beat 1 — Voyage montage (short)
- **Sylas:** still unconscious on the library table where he was freed. Nobody moved him; nothing mysterious. Stable.
- **The shop:** not open — it opens once Sylas has had a chance to recover.
- **Lancaster:** in the library with a drink, chatting with Bloos. Watches; doesn't interfere. He is on leave over his Ancient Ones investigations (T-009) and is now about to moor at an Ancient One's island — give him one line about it (curiosity, or a dry warning) and let him stay aboard.
- **The restless blade** whispers to Vaxen — one or two lines.

### Room 1 — Entrance (combat)
- Collin feels the fog that once parted for him and the reeds that once showed his path turn foreign and hostile. The party pushes in for a while; it becomes clear to everyone that Luwei will not be kind.
- Fight to the finish: **8 green Guardian Drakes** (Fizban's), **two waves of 4** (wave 2 arrives round 3, out of the fog). Beasts of Luwei's domain — not the canon Ambushers.
- **Maw #1:** the mud under Otis softens and gapes, then closes before it holds.

### Room 2 — Roleplay: the Tenders' village
- **Scene type (PR-SOC-01):** Platform (rest) with an embedded Clue. *Wants:* the fond Tender wants Collin back in Luwei's favor. *In the way:* the reeds listen; the other Tenders watch and report. *Learned from:* half-sentences by the fire.
- Tenders (green dragonborn, kobolds, grung; revere Luwei as god-emperor) host by duty; cool pity for Collin, a fallen favorite. **Short rest available.**
- Everything said around the fire reaches Luwei through the Whisper Reeds (**DM-only** — nobody at the table knows). He may quote it in Room 5.
- **One unnamed Tender** remembers Collin fondly. In careful half-sentences: Luwei's mood; that he is "testing"; that something on the island matters to him more than anything. **The egg is never named or shown.**
- **Maw #2:** Otis's sleeping spot sags like an opening throat.

### Room 3 — Setback: lost in the fog (skill challenge)
- **PR-SKILL-01 — form: cost per failure.** Goal: find the way to the island's heart. **8 successes, DC 18.** Lap the table; each skill once; players pitch skills (Survival, Perception, Nature, Investigation, Arcana, Insight, Athletics, Stealth…). Collin gets **advantage on one roll** (island knowledge).
- After the third roll, the bamboo **splits the party** (suggest 2/2/2). **No fights.** Collin is cut off from Otis.
- **Maw #3 (closest yet):** Collin glimpses it closing on Otis's group, and can't reach him.
- **Failure costs (in order):**
  1. Hazard (bog spores / sinkhole): **DC 15 Con save, 3d8 poison, half on success**, party-wide.
  2. Each PC spends **2 Hit Point Dice** with no recovery.
  3. Each PC gains **1 Exhaustion level** (2024: −2 to D20 Tests, −5 ft speed per level).
  4. Overall failure.
- **Overall failure:** they still get through, worn — and Collin had to ask the reeds for help. Keep it one-way: he speaks, nothing answers that he can hear, and the way simply opens. Luwei remembers.
- The split groups arrive at Room 4 from different directions.

### Room 4 — Climax: the Grove-Coil
- Homebrew boss, on D&D Beyond: https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6867287-grove-coil
- **Gargantuan, CR 12 (8,400 XP), 247 HP, AC 16.** 14 segments in 10-ft cells; the body follows the head's trail. Any segment targetable; shared HP.
- **Rupture:** 30+ damage to one segment in a round severs everything behind it. Cut at segment 8 or lower = it can never loop again.
- **Loop:** closes when the head ends beside segment 8+. Snared creatures get **one round of warning**, then **Constrict: DC 16 Str, 33 (6d10), Grappled (escape DC 16)**; half on success.
- **Ways out:** cross the body (**20 ft + DC 13 Athletics/Acrobatics**), rupture a segment, fly above 10 ft, Misty Step-type teleport, swim under at deep water (**DC 13 Athletics**), incapacitate the head.
- **Legendary actions (2):** Whip; Coil (head moves 10 ft — can close a loop, can't Constrict, breaks a loop already set).
- **Dials:** Grove Actions at initiative 20; Bloodied arena shrink; Trial Mercy; easing (**215 HP / no Coil / Constrict 5d10**) if Room 3 went badly.
- **Maw #4:** if Otis is snared in a loop, Collin sees the maw. On a close save, it snaps shut just short.
- **Arena: TBD (pinned).** Draft exists: 120 × 120 ft clearing, bamboo thickets, mud, deep channel, three entries for the split groups.
- **The end:** when the snake falls, Luwei's **Ambushers** emerge from the fog and surround the spent party. Luwei stops them and reveals himself. **Not a fight.**

### Room 5 — Resolution: Luwei's audience
- **Scene type (PR-SOC-01):** Negotiation with a Monologue inside. *Wants:* Collin back in his place; Alexandra under his thumb **by Collin's request**; proof against Mithbarakaz. *In the way:* Collin's pride; the party at his shoulder. *Learned from:* Room 2's hints, the Beat 0 outcome, the grievances aired here.
- **Setting:** the clearing where murky water pours into a 30-ft pit walled in vines and roots — where Collin first met him.
- **Presence:** serpentine and wingless; moss-and-jade scales under living vines and marsh grass; a large golden eye; two large antlers; a grassy beard; two long whiskers of thorny vine that move with his mood; taloned legs every thirty feet or so along a body that runs off into the swamp. A sibilant whisper like wind through bamboo, seeming to come from the reeds rather than his mouth. (Antlers and legs also make clear the Grove-Coil was never him.)
- **Manner:** patient and soft-spoken; he never raises his voice. His invitations are commands — the old bargain, "obey my every command," restated politely.
- **Beats:**
  1. **The grievances,** unhurried: the silence (the ring blocked his dreams), the skull, and the Engine — a **pointed hint only**: he lets Collin know he knows of it and wonders aloud why Collin never mentioned it. He asks for nothing.
  2. He quotes something said at the Tenders' fire.
  3. **Alexandra:** she left the Lair and went into the Jade Fragment looking for Collin; she is in the fog, within his reach. He asks Collin what her fate should be. Goal: Collin **asks** Luwei to take her as a favor. (Echo of his old bargain — to deliver House Aurum into Collin's hands — and of "a member of the house" in Session 26.)
  3a. **One question.** Luwei allows the party one question about the old world or the aboleth (Twilight wanted to ask Collin's patron this — T-004). What he answers is the DM's call; keep to canon: the aboleth is not "The Eldest."
  3b. **The armistice.** If Silver Moon comes up (T-032), Luwei speaks of it as an Ancient One would: the war is a passing blip, and what matters is that the fighting stays far from the old ones' lairs.
  4. **Terms:** Collin brings him **proof of Mithbarakaz's double-dealing**. Luwei already knows Mithbarakaz works both sides (it is why Collin watches him), so this is an escalation, not news; no surprise in his voice. Luwei hints that the money for the aboleth's favor will have to come from Mithbarakaz, and that he is sure Collin will find a way. He never says "blackmail" and never openly accuses Mithbarakaz. (He doesn't know Vase is the nephew.)
  5. Play through Collin's answer and the terms. End on the hook: Mithbarakaz, Silver Moon, the aboleth.
- **Collin's answer (branch notes):**
| Collin says | Luwei |
|---|---|
| Asks Luwei to take her | Pleased. "A favor, then." The debt is noted; her fate becomes Luwei's lever. |
| Refuses | Mild amusement. "As you wish. The fog is large." Terms stand; she's still in the fog, at his mercy. |
| Demands her release | He yields — and remembers. The party leaves with her. |
| Asks to speak with her first | He permits it: a short scene with Alexandra in the fog. |
| Deflects or stalls | "I am patient." The terms still land. |
