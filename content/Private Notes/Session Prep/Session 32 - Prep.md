# Session 32 — Prep (Lazy DM)

**Party:** 6 PCs, level 7. [[Lady Alexandra Aurum|Alexandra]] comes ashore. [[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn|Sylas]], [[Bloos]], and Winfrey stay on the Lair.
**Lesson:** Collin learns he has no standing, period, without [[Luwei]]'s favor.
**Shape:** Ambush → Tenders' village → lost in the fog → boss, cut off by Luwei. **End on the interruption.** The audience is a backup scene only.
**Clock (K-001, DM-only):** about 14 true days to Operation Green Thunder; the party believes about 7. This session costs about one day.
**Naming:** the table knows "the Whisper in the Reed"; "Luwei" is unconfirmed at the table. Canon pronoun: he.

## 1. Review the characters
- **[[Collin McCambridge|Collin]]:** center of the lesson. His gold hair is no longer hidden. His player decides whether he recognizes the first whisper.
- **[[Otis Thulu|Otis]]:** summoned by name, crushed the message. The snake's first target; captured on overall failure (**cleared with his player**).
- **[[Vase the Fragile|Vase]]:** promised to calm the patron; his chance is at the fire. Keep the Mithbarakaz-uncle secret protected.
- **[[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]]:** the blade wants a death in battle, so the cancelled fight is its beat.
- **[[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]]:** treats venom (**DC 15 Medicine**); practical craft in the fog.
- **[[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]]:** can ask about the old world at the fire. Keep the answer neutral.

## 2. Strong start
At the fog's edge, the reeds don't part for Collin. Alexandra's wards flare gold at nothing (a falling reed, a bird, an elbow), and she insists she has it under control. Then the dart.

## 3. Scenes

### Beat 1 — The ambush (combat)
- **Opening:** a sleep dart bursts on Alexandra's ward. The ward sticks and **seals the vapor in with her**. She collapses in a golden shell and **handlers drag it off by end of round 1**. This can't be stopped.
- **Reaching the shell:** an action adjacent to it in round 1, **DC 15 Strength**, earns a **ward splinter** (prop): warm, tugging toward her. Worth **1 automatic success** in Beat 3; proves she's alive in the audience; fades when she wakes.
- **Enemies (about CR 21 on paper; deliberately overpowered, since the whisper ends this fight at round 3):** Ozhkaar, 4 grung handlers, 4 green guard drakes.
  - **Advantage in round 1** from the fog. Those who reach bamboo vanish and return from a new angle next round.
  - **A drake whose handler falls goes wild** (see stat block). The whisper doesn't call it off.
- **End of round 3:** wind through the bamboo, a sound at the edge of hearing. They freeze and stand down: these lives are **"claimed."** If Ozhkaar is dead, a handler says it. The whisper also clears the Mark.
- **Collin's player decides** whether he knows the voice. If yes, the voice only, no words.
- **Ozhkaar and Collin:** doesn't recognize him. If he names himself, a flat pause, and nobody can tell whether they don't know or don't care. A gold-haired man beside Alexandra may read as just another Aurum.

### Beat 2 — The Tenders' village (Platform with Clue, PR-SOC-01)
**Short rest available.** A Tender says Alexandra lives and the only way to her is forward.
*Wants:* Ribbik wants Collin restored. *In the way:* the reeds, and watching Tenders. *Learned from:* the fire and half-sentences.
1. **Sorted:** seating, food, and address show rank. Collin sits low; even absent, Alexandra's name earns a pointed place.
2. **Around the fire:** the party can learn what pleases their master and what to offer. **Keep to grievances (silence, skull). Never the proof against Mithbarakaz.** If asked about a grove they won't walk at dusk, plant the Ettercap.
3. **Away from the fire (Collin alone):** Ribbik, in half-sentences: the master is "testing"; something matters to him above all (**never name the egg**); he answers those who ask. Then gone.
- **DM-only:** everything said reaches Luwei through the reeds. Track any talk of the Engine. Don't explain how he first learned of it.
- **Maw #1 (private to Collin):** Otis's sleeping spot sags like an opening throat.

### Beat 3 — Lost in the fog (skill challenge, PR-SKILL-01)
**Goal:** reach the island's heart. **6 successes before 3 failures, DC 18.** Lap the table; each skill once; players pitch skills; spells grant advantage only. **No advantage for Collin.** Party stays together.
- **Forks (max 2, after laps 1 and 2):** two openings, no roll. One can be the route Collin remembers. Webs on one, if planting the Ettercap.
- **Each lap:** whispers nearer, fog tighter, paths closing behind.
- **Middling roll:** success with a small cost.
- **Maw #2 (private to Collin):** once, mid-challenge.

| Failure | Result |
|---|---|
| 1st | Spores: **DC 15 Con, 3d8 poison, half on success**, party-wide |
| 2nd | Path loops back to their own tracks: each PC spends **2 Hit Point Dice**, no recovery |
| 3rd (overall) | Bamboo closes; **Otis is gone**. The way stays shut until **Collin asks the reeds aloud**. Nothing answers; it opens. The maw closes. |

### Beat 4 — The Grove-Coil (boss, cut short)
**Arena:** 80 × 80 ft open clearing; bamboo ring around the edge; one entry; snake coiled across the far side. On overall failure, Otis is **Grappled** in plain view.
- **Targets Otis first either way. Swallows him only if the challenge failed.**
- **Whisper comes** when the first PC drops to 0 HP (**not before end of round 2**), at **end of round 4**, or **immediately if Collin calls out to Luwei** (any round). Record whether he called.
- **This time everyone hears words.** The coils release. **His words command the audience.** Vaxen's blade reacts. **End of session.**
- Venom counters stay until treated or a rest.

### Backup — Luwei's audience (only with real time; otherwise opens Session 33)
**Setting:** clearing where murky water pours into a 30-ft pit walled in vines and roots. **Manner:** patient, never raises his voice; invitations are commands. *(Luwei's page doesn't list the antlers and legs from Collin's account; reconcile if he appears whole.)*
*Wants:* Collin back in place; Alexandra by Collin's request; the proof. *In the way:* Collin's pride, the party. *Learned from:* the fire talk and grievances.
1. First words turn on **whether Collin called out**.
2. Grievances: the silence, the skull. **Engine as a pointed hint only**; he asks nothing.
3. Quotes something said at the fire.
4. **Alexandra:** asks Collin what her fate should be.
5. **One question** on the old world or the aboleth (T-004). Keep to canon: the aboleth is not "The Eldest."
6. If Silver Moon comes up: a passing blip, so long as fighting stays far from the old ones' lairs.
7. **Terms:** proof of Mithbarakaz's double-dealing. He hints the money for the aboleth's favor must come from Mithbarakaz. He never says "blackmail." Gives leave to hunt the Ettercap.
8. Hook out: Mithbarakaz, Silver Moon, the aboleth.

| Collin says | Luwei |
|---|---|
| Asks Luwei to take her | Pleased. "A favor, then." The debt is noted; she becomes his lever. |
| Refuses | Mild amusement. "As you wish." Terms stand; she stays in his hands. |
| Demands her release | He yields, and remembers. The party leaves with her. |
| Asks to speak with her first | Permits a short scene. |
| Deflects | "I am patient." Terms still land. |

## 5. Fantastic locations
- **The Jade Fragment:** humid marsh, towering bamboo, fog that never lifts. Muffled footsteps; bamboo creaking; reeds whispering without wind; wet stone and green rot.
- **The Tenders' village:** reed-woven huts around a central fire. Quiet, watchful, slightly too tidy.
- **The fog:** tightening each lap; paths close behind.
- **The clearing:** see Beat 4.

## 7. Relevant monsters
**Ozhkaar:** Gladiator (2024 Monster Manual, CR 5), as written. Reskin as a green dragonborn.

**Grung Handler** (2024-style update of Grung Elite Warrior, MotM; about CR 2, deliberately overtuned) — Small Humanoid, Neutral
**AC** 13 · **HP** 49 (9d6 + 18) · **Speed** 25 ft., Climb 25 ft. · **Initiative** +3
STR −2, DEX +3 (save +5), CON +2, INT +0, WIS +0, CHA +1
**Skills** Athletics +2, Perception +2, Stealth +5, Survival +2 · **Immunities** Poison; Poisoned · **Senses** Passive Perception 12 · **Languages** Grung
*Traits.* **Amphibious.** **Standing Leap:** long jump 25 ft., high jump 15 ft., no running start needed.
**Poisonous Skin:** A creature that starts a Grapple on the grung or hits it with an Unarmed Strike makes a **DC 12 Con save**. *Failure:* Poisoned until the end of its next turn.
*Actions.* **Multiattack:** two Dart attacks. **Dart:** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing plus 5 (2d4) Poison.
**Hunting Chirr:** One creature within 60 ft. that the grung can see makes a **DC 12 Wis save**. *Failure:* the target is **Marked**, and drakes have Advantage on attacks against it. Only one creature can be Marked at a time; a new Mark replaces the old one (one token on the map). *Failure or Success:* Each drake within 30 ft. of the grung can use its Reaction to move up to half its Speed toward the target.
*Bonus Action.* **Sic:** One drake within 30 ft. that can see the grung uses its Reaction to move up to its Speed and make one Bite.

**Green Guard Drake** (2024-style update, MotM; CR 2) — Medium Dragon
AC 14 · HP 52 (7d8 + 21) · Speed 30 ft., Swim 30 ft. · Initiative +0
STR +3, DEX +0, CON +3, INT −3, WIS +0, CHA −2 · Resistances Poison · Darkvision 60 ft.
*Traits.* Amphibious. **Off the Leash:** if its handler dies, the drake has Advantage on attacks and must attack the nearest creature, friend or foe, until it dies.
*Actions.* **Multiattack:** one Bite and one Tail. **Bite:** Melee Attack Roll +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) Piercing. **Tail:** Melee Attack Roll +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning.

**Grove-Coil (reworked)** — Gargantuan Monstrosity, Unaligned. Replaces the current D&D Beyond entry.
AC 16 · HP 247 (17d20 + 68) · Speed 40 ft., Swim 40 ft. · Immunities Poison; Poisoned, Prone
STR +5, DEX +0 (save +4), CON +4 (save +8), INT −4, WIS +1 (save +5), CHA −3 · Perception +5 · Darkvision and Tremorsense 60 ft.
*Traits.* **Two Turns:** Head and Coils roll separate initiative and share one HP pool. A condition on one half leaves the other acting. Marsh-Born. Grove-Bound.
*Head action.* **Bite:** Melee Attack Roll +9, reach 15 ft. Hit: 21 (3d10 + 5) Piercing, then Venom.
*Coils action (one of).* **Constrict:** DC 16 Str, one creature within 15 ft. Failure: 22 (4d10) Bludgeoning and Grappled (escape DC 16). Success: half, not Grappled. Holds one at a time; doesn't re-Constrict a held target. **Swallow:** the Grappled creature is swallowed: Blinded, Restrained, 10 (3d6) Acid at the start of each Coils turn. 30+ damage from inside in one turn frees it. **Whip:** if neither applies. Melee Attack Roll +9, reach 10 ft. Hit: 18 (2d12 + 5) Bludgeoning and pushed 10 ft.
*Venom.* The target's counter rises by 1, then it makes a DC 16 Con save against (counter)d12 Poison. Failure: full and Poisoned until the end of its next turn. Success: half. Lesser Restoration or similar magic, a DC 15 Medicine action, or a short or long rest resets the counter to 0.
*Legendary Actions (3; regain at the start of the Head's turn).* **Whip.** **Slither:** moves up to half Speed without provoking. **Spit (Costs 2):** 60-ft cone, DC 16 Con. Failure: counter +1, then (counter)d12 Poison and Poisoned until the end of its next turn. Success: half of (current counter, minimum 1)d12; counter doesn't rise.
