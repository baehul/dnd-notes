# Session 33 — Prep (Lazy DM)

**Party:** 6 PCs, level 7. [[Lady Alexandra Aurum|Alexandra]] is missing, held in [[Luwei]]'s hands. [[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn|Sylas]] and [[Bloos]] are on the Lair island.
**Shape:** resumes the Session 32 plan where play stopped. Lost in the fog → the Grove-Coil, cut short by Luwei's words → the audience.
**Lesson (carried from S32):** Collin has no standing without Luwei's favor.
**Clock (K-001, DM-only):** about 14 true days to Operation Green Thunder; the party believes about 7. Session 32 cost less than a day; this session costs about one. Silver Moon armistice: "within the month" (K-003).

## 1. Arc fit
Arc 2, The Terramancers' Bargain → minor arc The Whisper and the Deep → adventure The Jade Fragment (second session).
- **Owes:** Luwei's terms land (T-001); Alexandra's status stops being a blank (T-005); the glowing hair gets used (I-017).
- **Services:** Otis's standing with Luwei (T-002, T-036); Twilight, unserviced for 4 sessions; Collin's place under his patron (H-001, H-007).
- **Handoff:** terms in hand (the reeds, the Ledger, Mithbarakaz's money for the aboleth); Alexandra's fate decided by Collin's answer; leave to hunt the Ettercap; next destination off the Jade Fragment toward Mithbarakaz, Silver Moon and the aboleth.
- **Locked:** the egg and the reeds hearing everything stay DM-only. Luwei's dragon form stays hidden unless he chooses to reveal himself (DM's call at the table; Collin already met him face to face, per his player's private background). The Engine co-opt is seeded, not sprung. The mole (T-009) stays unidentified and is never explained by the reeds. Dawn (H-003) stays unresolved.

## 2. Characters
- **[[Collin McCambridge|Collin]]:** the lesson. Whether he calls out to Luwei changes when the fight ends and how the audience opens. Answers what Alexandra's fate should be. The party learns here that he watches Mithbarakaz for Luwei.
- **[[Otis Thulu|Otis]]:** the Grove-Coil's first target; taken on an overall fog failure (**cleared with his player**). Makes amends for the crushed eye by taking Whisper Reeds home to plant in his own garden on the Lair.
- **[[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]]:** his beat is at the audience. Luwei addresses him as a priest of Night, and offers him the one question on the old world or the aboleth.
- **[[Vase the Fragile|Vase]]:** promised to calm the patron; his chance is in the audience. Luwei feigns not knowing Vase is Mithbarakaz's nephew and ward (it is on Vase's published page) and weighs him when the Ledger comes up.
- **[[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]]:** the blade wants a death in battle; the cancelled Grove-Coil fight is its beat. At the audience Luwei addresses his terms to Vaxen as the party's named leader, passing over Collin.
- **[[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]]:** treats venom (**DC 15 Medicine** action); practical craft in the fog. The reeds will grow beside his fenced plot.

## 3. Strong start
The village is behind them and the fog has already closed over it. Collin's fist pulls forward, the hair warm against his palm, gold-green light leaking between his fingers. The bamboo creaks without wind. Behind them, where the path was, there is only reed.

## 4. Scenes

### Scene 1 — Lost in the fog (skill challenge, PR-SKILL-01)
**Goal:** reach the island's heart. **6 successes before 3 failures, DC 18.**
- Lap the table; each skill once; players pitch skills; spells grant **advantage** only. **No advantage for Collin.** The party stays together.
- **The hair:** following it is **1 automatic success**.
- **Forks (max 2, after laps 1 and 2):** two openings, no roll. One can be a route Collin remembers. Webs on one, to plant the Ettercap.
- **Each lap:** whispers nearer, fog tighter, paths closing behind.
- **Middling roll:** success with a small cost.

| Failure | Result |
|---|---|
| 1st | Spores: **DC 15 Con, 3d8 poison, half on success**, party-wide |
| 2nd | Path loops back to their own tracks: each PC spends **2 Hit Point Dice**, no recovery |
| 3rd (overall) | Bamboo closes; **Otis is gone**. The way stays shut until **Collin asks the reeds aloud**. Nothing answers; it opens. (This is not the Grove-Coil's "calls out to Luwei" trigger, which needs him called by name.) |

- **Reveals:** the island answers to someone; asking is what opens it.
- **If skipped:** go straight to the clearing; the hair still points the way.

### Scene 2 — The Grove-Coil (boss, cut short)
**Arena:** see Locations. On an overall fog failure, Otis is **Grappled** in plain view at the start.
- **Targets Otis first either way. Swallows him only if the fog challenge failed.** This pays off Collin's private visions of mouths aimed at Otis (T-036).
- **Luwei's words come** when the first PC drops to **0 HP** (not before the **end of round 2**), at the **end of round 4**, or **immediately if Collin calls out to Luwei** (any round). Record whether he called.
- **Everyone hears words this time.** The coils release. His words command the audience. Vaxen's blade reacts.
- Venom counters stay until treated or a rest.
- **Reveals:** Luwei can stop his island's beast with a word; he is watching.
- **If skipped:** his words come in the fog instead and summon them.

### Scene 3 — Luwei's audience (Negotiation, PR-SOC-01)
*Wants:* Collin back in his place; Alexandra by Collin's request; the proof against Mithbarakaz. *In the way:* Collin's pride, the party. *Learned from:* the S32 fire talk and grievances (the silence, the skull).
1. **Unseen at first.** Only voice, whiskers and coils in the fog. He may reveal himself later in the scene (DM's call). **First words** turn on whether Collin called out.
2. **Grievances:** the silence, the crushed skull. **The Engine as a pointed hint only**; he asks nothing. **No quoting of the fire talk.**
3. **Twilight:** Luwei addresses him as a priest of Night and asks what his goddess wants with the Ancient Ones. **Curious and noncommittal**; nothing is settled about how the two stand.
4. **One question** on the old world or the aboleth (T-004), offered to Twilight. Keep to canon: the aboleth is not "The Eldest." On the aboleth, he lets slip that a Cult of Night study is under way there, a hint toward Dawn; nothing about who is in it.
5. **Alexandra:** the hair pulls toward her here; she is in his hands. He asks Collin what her fate should be (table below).
6. **Terms:**
   - **The reeds:** Otis takes Whisper Reeds home to plant in his own garden on the Lair, as amends for the crushed eye. Luwei says nothing of what the reeds do and does not name them aloud.
   - **The Ledger:** proof of Mithbarakaz's double-dealing. Luwei heard Collin tell the party about Mithbarakaz's double-dealing (S32) but says nothing; he waits to see whether Collin admits he told them. Speak of Collin's watching Mithbarakaz only to Collin and obliquely, beyond what Collin already said. Never say "blackmail."
   - **The aboleth:** he hints the money for the aboleth's favor must come from Mithbarakaz.
   - **The Ettercap:** leave to hunt it on the Jade Fragment.
7. **Time in view:** one line putting both clocks before the party: the Crusade's move they believe is about 7 days off (K-001), and the Silver Moon armistice "within the month" (K-003). If Silver Moon comes up, Luwei treats it as a passing blip, so long as fighting stays far from the old ones' lairs.
8. **Carrot (reserve):** restoring Collin's disguise, offered only if Collin plays along.
9. **Hook out:** Mithbarakaz, Silver Moon, the aboleth.

| Collin says | Luwei |
|---|---|
| Asks Luwei to take her | Pleased. "A favor, then." The debt is noted; she becomes his lever. |
| Refuses | Mild amusement. "As you wish." Terms stand; she stays in his hands. |
| Demands her release | He yields, and remembers. The party leaves with her. |
| Asks to speak with her first | Permits a short scene. |
| Deflects | "I am patient." Terms still land. |

- **Reveals:** Luwei's terms; Alexandra is alive and his; a Cult of Night study at the aboleth's home; Luwei knows of the Harmony Engine (hint only).
- **If skipped:** the terms can come as his words at the end of the Grove-Coil, with the audience opening Session 34.

## 5. Locations
- **The fog:** humid marsh, towering bamboo, fog that never lifts. Muffled footsteps; bamboo creaking; reeds whispering without wind; wet stone and green rot. Tightens each lap; paths close behind.
- **The Grove-Coil's clearing:** **80 × 80 ft**, open; a bamboo ring around the edge; one entry; the snake coiled across the far side.
- **The audience:** a clearing where murky water pours into a **30-ft pit** walled in vines and roots. Luwei is patient and never raises his voice; his invitations are commands. Unseen at first: coils, whiskers and voice in the fog. If he reveals himself, his dragon form becomes known to the party (update Current State and the Luwei page in the write-back).
