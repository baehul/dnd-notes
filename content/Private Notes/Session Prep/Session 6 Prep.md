## Combat Mechanics
### Crane Power Surge (Player Action)

When a player reaches a crane's control point and uses their Action to "surge" it, it affects the corresponding quadrant.

- **The Effect:** A massive electromagnetic pulse overloads the area. All construct enemies within that quadrant are immediately destroyed (pulled apart by the magnetic forces).
    
- **The Shrapnel Hazard:** The violent destruction of the armor sends metal flying everywhere. Any player character inside the surged quadrant must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw**. On a failed save, they take **3d6 piercing damage** from the shrapnel, or half as much on a successful one.
    

### The Henchman Actions (Initiative Count 20)

The henchman cannot use the same action two rounds in a row. Since your map has clear zones, these actions will target specific quadrants to force the players to constantly reposition.

**1. Magnetic Pull (Target: One Quadrant)** The henchman cranks a dial, temporarily hyper-magnetizing one of the quadrants (A, B, C, or D) that hasn't been surged yet.

- **Mechanic:** Any creature in that quadrant wearing medium/heavy metal armor or wielding heavy metal weapons must make a **DC 13 Strength saving throw**.
    
- **Failure:** The character is pulled 15 feet toward the center of the quadrant and falls Prone. If they are within 5 feet of an Animated Armor, the armor gets advantage on its next attack against them.
    

**2. Deploy Scrap Minions (Reinforcements)** The henchman drops unstable cores into the piles of junk to slow the players down, likely targeting the heavily populated "Zone B" on your map.

- **Mechanic:** Summons **1d4+1 Scrap Minions** in a single quadrant.
    
- **Scrap Minion Stats:** AC 13. **HP: 1** (Minion rule: they die if they take any damage). Speed: 30 ft. _Melee Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ **1d6 + 2 bludgeoning damage**.
    

**3. Override Protocol** The henchman pushes the constructs beyond their limits to protect the cranes.

- **Mechanic:** Up to three Animated Armors of the henchman's choice immediately use their Reaction to move up to 15 feet and make a single slam attack against a player.
    

**4. Electrify Controls (Defensive Measure)** If players are getting too close to a crane's control panel, the henchman electrifies the ground around it.

- **Mechanic:** The henchman targets one crane control panel. Any creature within 10 feet of that panel must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw**, taking **2d6 lightning damage** on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. That 10-foot radius becomes difficult terrain until Initiative 20 of the next round.

## Session Prep
### 1. Review Characters

- **Collin McCambridge (Eobard Aurum):** His true identity is at extreme risk; his brother August Aurum is part of the attacking force.
    
- **Vaxen the Unkillable:** Challenged Lord August Aurum to a midnight duel; August's arrival fulfills this challenge.
    
- **Cletus Ironplow:** Possesses the critical intel that Professor Jitterhop is the Cult of Night informant assisting The Crusade.
    
- **Otis Thulu:** Deeply angered by House Aurum's propaganda and displacement camps; primed for aggressive action against Ephraim and August.
    
- **Twilight Emberrest:** Cleric support; heavily invested in the philosophy of the Cult of Night, which is about to be rocked by Jitterhop's betrayal.
    
- **Vase the Fragile:** Has forged charity documents and gathered gala gossip; returning to his uncle's emissary with the Ancient Ones.
    

### 2. Strong Start

- **Action:** Mid-escape from the Sun Spire Scrapyard.
    
- **Mechanic:** Group Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Strength (Athletics) checks (DC 13) to successfully board Lancaster's escape pod/ship while under fire from Wolfram Zoloto's remaining forces.
    
- **Transition:** The pod blasts off into the void of the In-Between Isles, transitioning from combat to a tense, cramped voyage.
    

### 3. Outline Scenes

- **Scene 1: The Cramped Voyage (Roleplay/Skill Challenge).**
    
    - **Detail:** The party travels in Lancaster's pod to the In-Between Isles tavern.
        
    - **Roleplay Hook:** Time to discuss the recovered Harmony Engine notes.
        
- **Scene 2: The Resistance Meeting (Social).**
    
    - **Detail:** Arriving at the nondescript tavern used by the Resistance.
        
    - **NPCs present:** Broomhilda Bonebelly, Gnarl Thulu, Ancient Ones Emissary.
        
    - **Goal:** Convince the factions of the immediate threat of Project Orchestra.
        
- **Scene 3: The Lair Awakening (Exploration & Lore).**

	- **Trigger:** The party places the Harmony Engine down inside the tavern during the Resistance meeting.
	    
	- **Visual:** The music in the tavern syncs perfectly with the Engine's hum. The walls breathe; the furniture shifts. The Djinn emissary or Broomhilda Bonebelly recognizes the ancient magic at play.
	    
	- **Lore Drop:** An NPC explains the concept of the "Orphaned Lair" and that the Engine's Pre-Shattering Terramancy is acting as a surrogate master.
	    
	- **Mechanic:** A PC must make a DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) or Intelligence (Arcana) check to successfully "attune" the Engine to the party's collective will, establishing them as the Lair's new masters.
	    
	- **The Choice:** As the Lair bonds, it instinctively shifts to protect its new masters. The players must choose its default mobile form.
        
- **Scene 4: The Aurum Assault (Combat).**
    
    - **Detail:** Duke Ephraim and Lord August Aurum breach the island with a vanguard force.
        
    - **Hazard:** Incoming evocation artillery from August Aurum (DC 15 Dexterity Save vs. Fire/Force damage).
        
    - **Goal:** Hold the line while the Resistance leaders organize a retreat.
        
- **Scene 5: The Collision (Action/Escape).** * **Detail:** The drifting orbit of a neighboring Tectonic Island intersects with the tavern's island.
    
    - **Mechanic:** The collision causes massive seismic shear; DC 14 Dexterity or Strength Saving Throws to avoid being knocked prone or taking 2d6 bludgeoning damage from falling debris.
        
    - **Resolution:** The Resistance forces hold off the Aurum guards, allowing the party to escape onto the colliding island with the Harmony Engine and their new Mimic Lair.
        

### 4. Secrets & Clues

- The Tavern the Resistance uses is a living entity made of mimics posing as furniture and patrons.
    
- The Harmony Engine's imperceptible, constant tonic chord acts as a pacifying or commanding frequency for certain magical entities or structures.
    
- House Aurum tracked the escape pod by tracing the raw magical resonance of the Harmony Engine through the broken Ley Lines.
    
- **DM Input Required:** What specific clues do you want to drop regarding the customization options for the new Lair?
    

### 5. Fantastic Locations

- **Lancaster's Pod:** Cramped, smelling of ozone and dry gin, illuminated by flashing arcanotech warning lights.
    
- **The Mimic Tavern:** Shifting floorboards, furniture that subtly breathes, walls that change texture when touched.
    
- **The Collision Edge:** A chaotic boundary where atomized magical mist rises violently as two rock masses grind against each other in the void.
    

### 6. Important NPCs

- **Lancaster Slickwhistle:**
    
    - **Appearance:** Handsome Drow elf, impeccably dressed but battered.
        
    - **Goal:** Deliver the Engine to the Resistance leadership safely.
        
    - **Roleplay Note:** Suave, English accent; running on adrenaline and deeply concerned about the Crusade's timeline.
        
- **Duke Ephraim Aurum:**
    
    - **Appearance:** Older human, blonde hair, golden eyes, arcanotech monocle, cigarette holder.
        
    - **Goal:** Reclaim the Harmony Engine at all costs.
        
    - **Roleplay Note:** Precise, cold German accent; infuriated by the breach of his Spire.
        
- **Lord August Aurum:**
    
    - **Appearance:** Tall, brutish human.
        
    - **Goal:** Fulfill the duel to the death with Vaxen.
        
    - **Roleplay Note:** Arrogant, loud, lacks social grace but wields terrifyingly potent evocation magic.
