### Step 1: Review Characters

- **Vaxen the Unkillable:** Tricked by Twilight into believing Lord August Aurum used enchantment magic to force him to flee their midnight duel.
    
- **Twilight Emberrest:** Secretly charmed Vaxen to ensure their escape; currently maintaining a high-stakes deception.
    
- **Vase the Fragile:** Eager to deliver all gathered intelligence to his uncle, Mithbarakaz, completely unaware the Silvered Blizzard funds both The Crusade and The Resistance for personal wealth.
    
- **Collin McCambridge:** Distant and distracted after a vision of High Baron Magnus Aurum welcoming him home and glowing with golden light during the escape.
    
- **Cletus Ironplow:** Plans to bring the Harmony Engine notes to Professor Jitterhop, unaware Jitterhop is the Crusade informant.
    
- **Otis Thulu:** Angered by House Aurum's propaganda and the displacement of Goliath refugees.

### Step 2: The Strong Start

- **The Adrenaline Crash:** The party is cramped inside Lancaster's escape ship, hurtling through the void toward the Resistance tavern. Lancaster Slickwhistle, coming down from torture and a dry gin martini, demands a rapid alignment on what intel they will share with the fractured Resistance leadership.
    
- **The Ticking Clock:** Give the players a strict out-of-character time limit (e.g., 10 minutes) to plan their story and align on who holds the Harmony Engine.
    
- **The Consequence:** If they take too long, the ship's damaged power core fails.
    
    - **Mechanic:** The ship plummets. Everyone must make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw** or take **2d6 bludgeoning damage** (half on success) as the ship violently crash-lands into the docks of the Witch's Hut island instead of cruising smoothly.
### Step 3: Outline Scenes

- **Scene 1: The Resistance Debrief (Social/Roleplay)**
    
    - **The Flow of the Conversation:** Start with Lancaster Slickwhistle delivering the hard intel: The Crusade is executing **Project Orchestra**, a plan to use the Harmony Engine to perfectly fuse the Tectonic Islands of the Upper Crust, sealing it into a solid shell. If they succeed, the In-Between Isles and the Underworld will be permanently plunged into total, eternal darkness, starving out everyone below.
      
      Immediately after Lancaster drops this bomb, the faction leaders should erupt into an argument, each viewing the Harmony Engine through their own selfish lens:

	- **The Cult of Night (Represented by Broomhilda Bonebelly):**
	    
	    - **What she sees:** Proof that the war is already lost. If the Crusade can manipulate pre-Shattering Terramancy, fighting is statistical suicide.
	        
	    - **What she wants:** To take the Harmony Engine notes, evacuate the Monastery of Night, and retreat deep into the Underworld to preserve their knowledge. She will argue that the party’s actions just kicked a hornet's nest they can't survive.
	        
	- **The Blood Horde (Represented by Gnarl Thulu):**
	    
	    - **What he sees:** A ticking clock and a weapon of mass destruction. If the Crust seals, his people will never reclaim their ancestral lands.
	        
	    - **What he wants:** An immediate, all-out, suicidal frontal assault on Cittadella Lumina or the Sun Spire using the Harmony Engine to smash the Crusade's islands apart. He will call Broomhilda a coward and demand the party hand the Engine over to the Horde's War Council.
	        
	- **The Ancient Ones (Represented by the Djinn Emissary):**
	    
	    - **What they see:** A liability. This device makes the Resistance a massive target.
	        
	    - **What they want:** To confiscate the Harmony Engine and lock it away in one of their impenetrable hoards (like Mithbarakaz’s Silver Moon or Luwei’s swamp). They want to hide it, wash their hands of the politics, and wait out the Crusade.
            
- **Scene 2: The Witch's Hut & The Engine (Exploration/Lore)**
    
    - **Setup:** The Harmony Engine, placed inside the tavern, begins to hum. The Witch's Hut reacts, its walls and furniture shifting.
        
    - **The Reveal:** The Hut is able to interface with the Engine's pre-Shattering Terramancy. It cannot activate the "world-fusing" capability, but it unlocks the Weavesilk navigation and Ley Line manipulation functions.
        
    - **Mechanic:** A PC must make a **DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) check** to stabilize the connection between the Hut and the Engine, tethering the building to the device's power.
        
- **Scene 3: The Aurum Assault (High-Stakes Skill Challenge)**
    
    - **Setup:** Duke Ephraim and Lord August Aurum breach the island, having tracked the Harmony Engine's magical resonance. They bring overwhelming force.
        
    - **The Goal:** Survive 3 rounds until a neighboring Tectonic Island collides with their current location.
        
    - **The Threat Level (Deadly):** Emphasize that fighting head-on is suicide.
        
    - **Mechanics (Skill Challenge):** Require 5 Successes before 3 Failures to successfully coordinate the evacuation of the Resistance leaders and secure the Hut.
        
        - _Dodge Artillery:_ **DC 16 Dexterity Save** vs. August's Evocation blasts (**4d6 Fire damage** on failure).
            
        - _Barricade Doors:_ **DC 15 Strength (Athletics)** against advancing House Aurum Vanguard constructs.
            
        - _Command the Hut:_ **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion/Intimidation)** to force the panicked Witch's Hut to detach from its foundations.
            
- **Scene 4: The Collision and the New Base (Transition/Reward)**
    
    - **Setup:** A massive Tectonic Island grinds against the tavern's island.
        
    - **Mechanic:** **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** to leap across the atomized mist at the Edge as the current island crumbles.
        
    - **The Reward:** The party successfully brings the Harmony Engine and the Witch's Hut onto the new island. The Hut, now powered by the Engine, morphs to fit its new environment.
        
    - **Player Choice:** The players get to choose the new architectural form and internal layout of their mobile base.
