### **Room 1: The Magnetic Graveyard (The Entrance Guardian)**

**Goal:** Infiltrate the Docks/Scrapyard undetected to reach the Palace entrance.

**How to Run It:** Do **not** run this as a combat. Combat takes too long and drains HP too early. Run this as a **Skill Challenge**.

- **The Objective:** The party needs **4 Successes** before **3 Failures**.
    
- **The Scene:** Mountains of rusted metal. Giant cranes with glowing blue runes are lifting scrap automatically. Robotic "Scrap-Sentries" (looking like crude metallic dogs) patrol the perimeter.
    
- **Example Challenges (DC 13):**
    
    - _Stealth (Dex):_ Avoid the Sentries' sensors.
        
    - _Acrobatics (Dex):_ Vault across a moving conveyor belt.
        
    - _Arcana (Int):_ Predict the magnetic pulses of the cranes to avoid getting stuck to them (foreshadowing!).
        
    - _Sleight of Hand (Dex):_ Create a distraction by throwing a bolt to misdirect a Sentry.
        
- **Failure:** If they get 3 failures, they still arrive at the Palace door, but they have tripped a "Silent Alarm." This adds +2 DC to checks in the next room, or gives the enemies in the final room Advantage on Initiative.
    

### **Room 2: The Workshop Gala (Roleplay & Puzzle)**

**Goal:** Retrieve the **Cell Keycard** and the Spy’s **Arcane Focus**.

**The Setup:** The players enter the factory floor, now dressed as a ballroom. The Villain is hosting the party, but his private Workshop overlooks the gala from a balcony.

- **Objective A (The Keycard):** The players must identify the Head Jailer (enjoying the buffet) and swipe the key.
    
    - _Action:_ Persuasion to distract him, Sleight of Hand to swipe the key, or Deception to spill a drink on him and take it while "cleaning up."
        
- **Objective B (The Focus):** The players must sneak up to the Workshop balcony.
    
    - _The Mini-Puzzle:_ The Workshop door is locked by a magical rune cipher. The players might notice the pattern of the factory lights matches the cipher (Observation/Investigation) or simply break in (Thieves' Tools).
        
    - _The Find:_ Inside, they find the Spy’s **Crystal Focus** sitting on a workbench being analyzed. Grabbing it might require a Dex Save to swap it with a weight (Indiana Jones style) or it triggers the alarm immediately.
        

### **Room 3: The Empty Cell (The Setback)**

**Goal:** Rescue the Spy and realize the escape route is blocked.

**The Scene:** They reach the detention block. Using the Keycard, they open the cell.

- **The Spy:** He is beaten but relieved. "Thank the gods! Did you bring my Focus? I can't cast without it."
    
- **The Setback:** When you ask about the magical device, he says: _"I hid it in the Scrapyard before they caught me! Inside the chassis of an old Destroyed Warforged. Also... my ship didn't land at the docks; I crashed it there. We have to fix it to leave."_
    
- **The Turn:** The alarm blares (if it hasn't already). The guards aren't coming to the cell; they are rushing to seal the exits. The party must sprint back to the Scrapyard before the Villain locks the whole facility down.
    

### **Room 4: The Magnetic Defense (The Climax)**

**Goal:** Defend the Spy while he repairs the ship and retrieve the device.

**The Setup:** Back at the Scrapyard (Room 1). The Spy finds his crashed ship and the hidden device. He starts working on the engine.

- **The Objective:** Survive **3 Rounds** of combat while the Spy fixes the ship.
    
- **Enemies:**
    
    - **2x Palace Guards** (Use _Guard_ stats, but give them AC 16).
        
    - **1x Sentry Bot Alpha** (Use _Animated Armor_ stats, but describe it as a clanking robot).
        
- **The Gimmick (The Cranes):**
    
    - There is a control console nearby. A player can use their Action to make an Intelligence (Arcana or Technology) check (DC 12).
        
    - **On Success:** They can control a Magnetic Crane to target an enemy. The enemy must make a DC 13 Strength Save.
        
    - **Fail Save:** The enemy is pulled 20 feet into the air and is **Restrained**. They can be dropped on their next turn for **2d6 bludgeoning damage**. (This is hugely rewarding for creative players).
        

### **Room 5: The Narrow Escape (Reward/Resolution)**

**Goal:** Blast off.

**The Scene:** After 3 rounds (or when the enemies are dead), the ship’s engines roar to life.

- **The Cavalry:** The Villain himself kicks open the warehouse doors, flanked by elite soldiers (way too strong for Level 3s to fight).
    
- **The Action:** The players must leap onto the moving ship ramp. (Optional final DC 10 Athletics check just for tension—if they fail, a friend catches their hand).
    
- **The Result:** The ship blasts out of the scrapyard, leaving the Villain shaking his fist in the dust. The Spy hands them a bag of gold (The Reward) and explains what the mysterious Device actually does (The Revelation).
