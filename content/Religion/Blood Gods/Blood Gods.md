The **Blood Gods** is the collective, catch-all term used by external observers and scholars to denote the disparate pantheon of tribal, familial, and ancestral deities revered by the various groups composing **[[The Blood Horde (Fictional Organization)|The Blood Horde]]**. The term is rarely used by the followers themselves, who instead reference their specific patron deities, spirits, or venerated ancestors by name or title.

# Theological Identity
The Blood Gods do not constitute a single, unified religion or centralized pantheon. Their theology is fundamentally decentralized, mirroring the tribalistic structure and familial focus of [[Blood Horde|the Horde]] itself. The sheer number of deities and spirits within the collective is considered innumerable. They include ancient totemic spirits, gods associated with specific natural elements or locations on lost ancestral lands, and powerful, deified ancestors of major bloodlines.

While their domains are unique to the tribe or clan that venerates them, the deities overwhelmingly revolve around core cultural aspects essential to life and survival within [[Blood Horde|the Horde]]. These domains include: **War and Combat**, **Honor and Fealty**, **Conquest and Reclamation**, and **Primal Nature**.

# Worship and Rituals
Worship of the Blood Gods is as varied as the deities themselves, though all practices are generally visceral and intensely community-focused. 

The requirements of the Blood Gods vary drastically between tribes. Some are appeased through war dances and feats of martial prowess (e.g., public duels or tests of endurance), while others are believed to demand more costly forms of reverence. The deities most focused on war and conquest are known to demand blood sacrifice or significant physical offerings to grant favor and ensure victory in battle. This practice is most often cited by external critics and is the primary source of the collective title, "Blood Gods".

Reverence for ancestral spirits is a universal practice, often involving blood oaths and ceremonies dedicated to honoring the dead who fell in the lands targeted for reclamation.
