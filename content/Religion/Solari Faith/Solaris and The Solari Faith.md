**Solaris** is the supreme deity of the [[Ecclesia Solari]] and the central figure of worship for the majority of the [[Upper Crust]]. The Solari Faith is a religion centered on light, order, and divine judgment, teaching that the physical sun is the literal manifestation of Solaris's watchful eye and divine power.

# Mythology
Central to Solari theology is its interpretation of [[The Shattering of the World]]. While other cultures view the cataclysm as a tragedy or a failure of ancient magic, according to the Solari Faith, the world was once a singular, chaotic mass where the wicked and the righteous lived intermingled, allowing darkness to corrupt the hearts of the innocent. In an act of supreme but necessary judgment, Solaris struck the world, shattering the crust to physically separate the good from the evil.

The faith emphasizes Solaris's two faces. To the faithful, he is the Benevolent Father, providing warmth, growth, and protection. To enemies and the shadow, he is the Scouring Fire, a wrathful judge who burns away corruption and seals away the darkness.

The faith acknowledges the existence of other entities (such as the [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|Night Goddess]]), but denies their divinity. These entities are classified as "Idols of Shadow": weak, parasitic beings that bring only confusion and darkness.

# Depictions
Solaris is depicted in religious iconography as a towering, perfect humanoid male composed of blinding molten gold and white fire whose head is the Sun. Theologically, he is considered one and the same as the sun. The sun is not merely his creation; it is his physical body in this world.

# Rituals and Worship
The daily life of a Solari follower is regimented by the movement of the sun.
## Daily Prayers
1. **The Greeting (Dawn):** As the sun breaches the horizon, followers must face east and offer a short prayer of welcome, thanking Solaris for returning to guard them and "opening the eye of the world."
2. **The Exaltation (Zenith):** At high noon, when shadows are smallest, work stops for prayer. This is a prayer of submission to Solaris's greatness and power.
3. **The Gratitude (Dusk):** As the sun sets, followers thank Solaris for his guidance through the day and pray for the strength to endure the coming night.
## The Sanctity of Fire
Because the night is viewed as a time of spiritual siege where sin and dark thoughts fester, fire is considered sacred, a terrestrial piece of Solaris left behind to guard the faithful. It is a strict custom that the hearth fire in a home must _never_ go out between dusk and dawn. A dark house is invited ground for the corrupt and wicked. To cleanse oneself of minor sins or bad thoughts, followers will mark their foreheads with cooled white ash from this sacred fire, symbolizing the burning away of their impurities. 

## Sacrificial Burning
The "Unclean" must be purged. This involves the ritualistic burning of objects (and sometimes people) deemed corrupted by darkness. Moldy grain, books containing heretical ideas, or items touched by "monstrous" entities are cast into consecrated public braziers to be purified by flame.

## Eschatology
The most distinctive aspect of the Solari faith is its treatment of death. For the faithful, cremation is the only holy rite. The burning of the body releases the soul from the earth, allowing it to rise on the smoke to rejoin Solaris in the sun. Burial is reserved for criminals, heretics, and enemies of the faith. To be buried is to be damned. The Solari faith believes that a soul trapped beneath the earth cannot rise to the sun and is left to wander the cold, dark void forever.
