The **Way Of Night** is a philosophical and religious movement centered on its foundational sacred text, [[Way Of Night (Book)|Way Of Night]]. Adherents regard the Way of Night direct doctrinal guide communicated by [[The Goddess Whose Name Is Night]], the religion's primary deity.

Scholarly debate exists regarding the movement's classification; some analysts categorize it as a traditional religion, while others regard the Way Of Night as primarily a system of thought and behavior. The doctrine emphasizes the fluidity and unknowability of the truth instructing followers to maintain an open mind toward all possibilities and reject the acceptance of any absolute certainty. 

Unique among religious traditions, the Way Of Night actively encourages its adherents to study, respect, and worship other deities and systems of faith. This practice is rooted in the movement's core tenet that followers should not accept the Way of Night itself as the sole or absolute truth.

# Core Doctrine and Theology
The theology of the Way of Night is fundamentally defined by a philosophy of skepticism and non-absolutism. Unlike most organized faiths, the central tenets are intentionally fluid, reflecting the nature of its sacred text, the _Way of Night_.
## Fluidity of Truth
The foundational principle of the Way of Night is the rejection of any claim to **absolute, singular truth**. Adherents are taught that the nature of reality is in constant flux, and any attempt to codify it leads only to misunderstanding. This fluidity in belief is directly responsible for the textual variability of the sacred book, as the faith embraces individual contribution and dissent as necessary components of spiritual inquiry.
## The Goddess Whose Name Is Night Paradoxes
The faith contains two unique paradoxes. While followers maintain reverence for [[The Goddess Whose Name Is Night]] as the source of the text, the doctrine actively encourage followers to doubt all sources of truth, including [[The Goddess Whose Name Is Night]]. 

In addition, followers are encouraged to test their faith against outside sources. This drives active polytheistic worship amongst adherents of the faith in order to drive religious study. Adherents claim this has allowed the faith to continue to flourish as it allows followers to validate the wisdom contained within the *Way Of Night* and fold positive aspects of other faiths into the Way Of Night.
