## Summary
### The Lecture and the Notes

The party arrived at the busy campus of the [[Monastery of Night]], surrounded by religious chanting, loud experiments, and dangerous creatures. They located [[Professor Jitterhop]] mid-lecture, where he was performing a demonstration with a [[Resonance Crystals|Resonance Crystal]]. [[Professor Jitterhop|Jitterhop]] demonstrated to the class that concentrated magic can be contained while still flowing, creating fields of magical energy similar to the [[Ley Lines]].

Following the lecture, [[Cletus Ironplow]] approached[[Professor Jitterhop|Jitterhop]] to discuss [[The Terramancers|Terramancy]] and presented the recovered notes regarding the [[Harmony Engine]]. Upon reviewing the documents, [[Professor Jitterhop|Jitterhop]] explained that [[Ley Lines]] act similarly to a web or gelatin, holding [[Tectonic Islands]] in suspension. He explained that land possesses a "memory" of its original position prior to [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]]. The [[Harmony Engine]] utilizes [[The Terramancers|Terramancy]] to return an island to its original location relative to the planetary core. [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] and [[Professor Jitterhop|Jitterhop]] noted the presence of Infernal text within the notes, an uncommon pre-Shattering language, suggesting that [[The Terramancers|Terramancers]] may still be active.

### The Scavenger Hunt Begins

To further study the device, [[Professor Jitterhop|Jitterhop]] requested the party gather four specific items from around the campus. The list included:
- a ley line sieve from Engineer Thrumm
- a super-acoustic tuning fork from Scholar Vra'rath
- a resonance dampener from Biomancer Vesper
- a pure resonance crystal from Sendryr Ogami

The party temporarily diverted to Cletus's dorm room to collect old notes and hand-knit sweaters. They observed that the dorms were unusually cold due to an ongoing failure in the campus steam heating system.

### The Steam Pipe Trunk Distribution Venue

The party located Engineer Thrumm, who was actively attempting to manage a steam elemental.  The party helped him dispatch the elemental and quickly patch the broken pipes in return for the ley line sieve. Thrumm asked the party to tell [[Professor Jitterhop]] to stop stretching school resources thin by continuing his work with [[The Resistance]].

### A Walk In The Park
The party searched for a place to rest after their harrowing battle with the steam elemental. They saw encountered all sorts of happy folk from the [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night]] as they eventually made their way to a park.  During this time, they realized that [[Broomhilda Bonebelly|Broomhilda Bonebelly's]] absence was beginning to become conspicuous.

As they made their way back to campus, [[Collin McCambridge]] utilized his familiar, Jeeves, to secretly observe [[Professor Jitterhop]]. He observed that the professor was referencing a second set of notes and was wearing a ring highly similar to his own [[Ring of Restless Revelation]].

## New NPCs
- [[Professor Jitterhop]]: The eccentric professor who is the foremost expert on [[The Terramancers|Terramancy]] this side of the Isles. He works as the lead researcher for [[The Resistance]] though his commitment to the cause is questionable.
