## Summary
### The Forum and the Void-Worm Theory

The party entered **the Forum**, a massive underground amphitheater within the [[Monastery of Night]] dedicated to debates and public discussions. Scholar Vra'ath, a Githyanki academic, was leading a passionate lecture on his "Void-Worm" theory. He posited that the shattered world currently resides inside the stomach of a cosmic worm, arguing that the chaotic frequencies of the void are merely the entity's indigestion. Vra'ath presented a Harmonic Tuning Fork, claiming it was necessary to find the "antacid frequency" to cure the sickness. [[Vase the Fragile]] engaged in the debate, warning that any attempt to re-piece the world could trigger violent and destructive consequences.

### The Biomancy Department Incident

While the debate continued, [[Otis Thulu]], [[Twilight Emberrest]], and [[Cletus Ironplow]] investigated the Biomancy Department, a chaotic laboratory overgrown with strange plant life. [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] made physical contact with a large elephant ear plant and ingested its sap, temporarily granting him the ability to communicate with the surrounding flora. He engaged in a conversation with an entity calling itself Arbor Imperatus, mistakenly believing it to be the World Tree. The sentient plant expressed anger over a lack of "subjects" and violently attempted to absorb [[Otis Thulu|Otis]]'s life force. In a panic, [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] destroyed the plant, reducing it to ash. [[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]] and [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] safely guided a catatonic [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] away from the department. Utilizing this distraction, [[Collin McCambridge]] slipped away from the rest of the group to return to [[Professor Jitterhop]] alone, telling the party he was going to retrieve [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]]


### Securing the Tuning Fork

[[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] found himself challenged to a duel by a Minotaur student who fiercely defended Scholar Vra'ath's theories. As both parties drew their swords, Vra'ath attempted to de-escalate the situation. [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] played the confrontation off as a theatrical act, while [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] successfully convinced the Scholar to give him the Harmonic Tuning Fork to calm the combatants. Utilizing the distraction, the party managed to slip away from the amphitheater with the tuning fork in their possession. The party realized that they could not find [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]].
### Meetings with Sendryr and Jitterhop

[[Collin McCambridge]] utilized his familiar to secretly observe [[Professor Jitterhop]]. Through the familiar, [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] witnessed [[Professor Jitterhop|Jitterhop]] conversing with [[Sendryr Ogami]] and overheard them discussing intelligence regarding an unknown faction that was actively destroying [[Tectonic Islands]] to expand their own territory. After the exchange, [[Sendryr Ogami|Sendryr]] left the room and ran into [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]]. She mentioned she believed she had met [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] before, acknowledged his connection to [[Luwei]]. She also inquired about [[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]] and [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] and whether they were safe before departing for the Temple of Night, inviting the party to see her soon.

After [[Sendryr Ogami|Sendryr]] left, [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] confronted [[Professor Jitterhop]] privately in his laboratory, having also noted the professor speaking to himself in an unfamiliar language and referencing a second set of notes. [[Professor Jitterhop|Jitterhop]] admitted his primary interest in the [[Harmony Engine]] was to further "Project Genesis," an initiative to create artificial [[Tectonic Islands]] and solve the housing crisis. When [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] pointed out that [[Professor Jitterhop|Jitterhop]] wore a ring identical to [[Ring of Restless Revelation|his own]], the professor confessed to working with outside scientists. He stated he uses the ring to communicate in Abyssal with entities he believes are [[The Terramancers|Terramancers]] residing in the [[Underworld]].

## The Harmony Engine's True Potential

The rest of the party arrived at the laboratory, interrupting [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] and [[Professor Jitterhop]]'s conversation. During this sudden intrusion, [[Twilight Emberrest]] noticed [[Collin]] acting suspiciously and observed him hastily attempting to put his ring away to hide it.

[[Professor Jitterhop]] then addressed the gathered group, sharing his findings from the translated notes. He explained that the [[Harmony Engine]] was fundamentally designed to piece the shattered world back together exactly as it existed before [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]]. He noted that the device can combine landmasses to create increasingly larger [[Tectonic Islands]] and navigate them through the void, though it cannot move an island that is larger than the one upon which it currently resides. Furthermore, he revealed that the [[Harmony Engine]] is capable of adjusting environments and local terrain; advanced functions that [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]] apparently did not realize the device possessed.

The party dropped off the objects they had already collected with the professor and resolved to acquire the final two pieces, deciding to seek out [[Sendryr Ogami]]'s component last.

## The Resonance Dampener

The party reconvened and returned to the Biomancy Department to retrieve the Resonance Dampener. They navigated a jungle-like testing area filled with giant carnivorous fauna to locate Professor Vesper, who possessed the device. The surrounding flora proved to be highly sensitive to noise, reacting aggressively to any sound the party made. Despite triggering the hostile plant life, the party successfully retrieved the Resonance Dampener, though they sustained significant damage during the extraction.
