## Summary

### The Temple of Night

The party traveled to an older, somber section of the [[Monastery of Night]] to locate [[Sendryr Ogami]]. While traversing the area, they observed several individuals recovering from battle injuries, a stark reminder of the ongoing conflict. They found [[Sendryr Ogami|Sendryr]] among a congregation, listening to a priest recite a newer verse: _"A blind man, stares at the empty sky. Does the sun still burn without his eyes; or does he make the dark? He is Night."_ The group split up, with some joining the gathering while others maintained a watch outside.

### Meeting with Sendryr

Meeting privately with [[Sendryr Ogami]], the party shared their intelligence regarding a mole within the [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night]]. [[Sendryr Ogami|Sendryr]] revealed information regarding the recent demonstration of the [[Ostinato Accumulator]] by [[The Crusade]], which sounded eerily similar to the device [[Bloos]] described affecting his home.

[[Sendryr Ogami|Sendryr]] admitted her suspicions pointed toward [[Professor Jitterhop]], citing his sudden breakthroughs with "Project Genesis" and his collaboration with an unknown outside contact. Despite this, she stressed that [[The Resistance]] could not risk alienating him, as his technological and magical weaponry were strictly necessary to keep pace with their enemies. She also believed that if "Project Genesis" was to work, it could bring a peaceful resolution to the conflict.

### The Sacred Relic

Later, while eating dinner, the party realized that the specific [[Resonance Crystals|Resonance Crystal]] [[Professor Jitterhop]] had asked them to retrieve was actually a sacred relic housed within the temple. This revelation caused a sudden uproar among the surrounding congregation. To deescalate the tension, [[Sendryr Ogami]] promised to try and find a solution in the morning after the party got some much-needed rest.

### The Engine and Future Paths

Following the uproar, the party chose to reveal to [[Sendryr Ogami]] that they were currently in possession of the actual [[Harmony Engine]]. With this truth uncovered, Sendryr assisted the party in discussing the four distinct options ahead of them for their next destination: traveling to the [[Underworld]] to track down [[Professor Jitterhop]]'s mysterious contacts; visiting [[Collin McCambridge]]'s patron, [[Luwei]], among the [[Ancient Ones]]; seeking out [[Vase the Fragile]]'s father, Captain Clay Argilla, to utilize his pirate fleet; or attending the [[Blood Horde]] War Council.

### A Conversation with Jitterhop

[[Collin McCambridge]] and [[Otis Thulu]] spoke with [[Professor Jitterhop]], who provided [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] with a specific phrase spoken in Infernal, suggesting it might allow him to learn more from the entity/entities haunting his dreams through the [[Ring of Restless Revelation]]. Furthermore, [[Professor Jitterhop|Jitterhop]] revealed that his outside contacts were highly angered upon learning he had discovered the [[Harmony Engine]] notes, leading the professor to theorize that his mysterious contacts and the original architects of the notes are rival factions.
