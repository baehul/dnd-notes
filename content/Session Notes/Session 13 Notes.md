## Summary

### The Emberrest Residence
[[Twilight Emberrest]] returned to his home, initially believing he had forgotten his keys before utilizing his enchanted key to gain entry. Upon his arrival, illusion magic triggered, and a crow familiar delivered a welcoming message from his wife, [[Dawn]]. The interior of the house was animated with various chaotic arcane projects and scattered books. Twilight carefully navigated the room to pull a master lever, which shut down the ongoing experiments and left only the ambient lighting active. 

After securing the main floor, Twilight led the party to the basement to prepare for their rest. As they settled in, the group noted that [[Collin McCambridge]] had gone to sleep wearing the [[Ring of Restless Revelation]]. Before resting, Twilight and [[Vase the Fragile]] stepped aside to share a private conversation.

### Visions and Morning
While sleeping, [[Collin McCambridge]] experienced a profound vision facilitated by the ring. He observed several cloaked and horned figures gathered around a model of the shattered world. Communicating mostly through pantomime, the figures expressed an eagerness to meet him. They directed Collin to find a specific compass, which he must use in conjunction with his ring to locate them. In the physical world, [[Otis Thulu]] noticed Collin beginning to levitate in his sleep and safely caught him just as the vision abruptly ended. The following morning, the party awoke and shared a communal breakfast of spider egg omelettes.

### The Arena Trial
The group traveled to an arena to meet with [[Sendryr Ogami]], locating her as she spoke to a congregation in the center of the grounds. Certain members of the [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night]] expressed reluctance to hand over their [[Resonance Crystals|Resonance Crystal]] to the party and [[Professor Jitterhop]]. To secure the crystal, the congregation decreed that the party must first pass a test to prove their worthiness.

### DM Secrets & Context
* **The Lestirmek:** The cloaked and horned figures from Collin's vision are the Lestirmek (The Devils), an extremist faction of [[The Terramancers]]. Following their previous agent's failure, they are currently unaligned and actively using the [[Ring of Restless Revelation]] to recruit Collin to their cause. 
* **The Compass:** The compass the Lestirmek instructed Collin to find is a required component to track their physical location in the void, bridging the gap between his psychic visions and their actual coordinates.
