## Summary

### Sendryr's Challenge

The party undertook the challenge set before them by [[Sendryr Ogami]] and [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult]], hoping to secure a [[Resonance Crystals|Resonance Crystal]]. The central feature of the arena was a pedestal holding a bag, guarded by attacking lions and hindered by invisible moving runes. Recalling advice from [[Sendryr Ogami]] to exist in both light and dark, the party opened one eye and closed the other. This revealed the runes and exposed a blue glow emanating from beneath the central pedestal.

Upon completing the challenge, [[Sendryr Ogami|Sendryr]] explained that the challenge was an attempt to impress upon the party the need to consider multiple truths. She gave the party the [[Resonance Crystals|Resonance Crystal]] [[Professor Jitterhop]] needed to complete his [[Harmony Engine]] prototype as well as a few other magical items to help them on the journey ahead.

### The Prototype Engine

The party returned to [[Professor Jitterhop]], who had constructed a prototype of the [[Harmony Engine]]. In the initial test, the prototype began to glow and emit discordant sounds, attempting to "tune in" to its surroundings. However, the device failed to reach a final concordance. The group deduced that the prototype was attempting to connect to the [[Monastery of Night]], a massive [[Tectonic Islands|Tectonic Island]] and move it immediately to its original location before [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]]. They concluded that manipulating an island of that magnitude would require a significantly larger or purer [[Resonance Crystals|Resonance Crystal]]. Recalling research conducted by [[Broomhilda Bonebelly]], [[Professor Jitterhop|Jitterhop]] theorized that a [[Lairs|Lair]] could be utilized to bind to the engine and control the movement of the [[Tectonic Islands|Tectonic Island]] through the [[Ley Lines]].

### Infernal Confessions

Upon returning to their island, [[Collin McCambridge]] confessed to the group that he had been communicating with infernal beings in his sleep. The party resolved to seek out these beings, intending to make contact before [[House Aurum]] could do the same.

As they formulated their plans, [[Lancaster Slickwhistle]] made an unexpected arrival at their location.

### DM Secrets & Context

- The infernal beings communicating with [[Collin McCambridge]] in his sleep are the Lestirmek (The Devils), an extremist faction of [[The Terramancers]].
    
- The Lestirmek are utilizing the [[Ring of Restless Revelation]] to recruit [[Collin McCambridge]] after the failure of their previous agent, [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]].
    
- The prototype [[Harmony Engine]] constructed by [[Professor Jitterhop]] is likely functioning exactly as originally designed, possessing a "memory" of the original world to perfectly fuse islands.
    
- [[Professor Jitterhop]]'s involvement with the [[Harmony Engine]] prototype is highly sensitive, as he is secretly acting as an informant for [[The Crusade]] and assisting [[Lady Dagna Cuprum]] with the Ostinato Accumulator.
