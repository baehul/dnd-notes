## Summary
### Lancaster's Intelligence Briefing
[[Lancaster Slickwhistle]] returned to the party. His car looked dented and corroded. 

Lancaster delivered a grim update regarding their allies, confirming that their sponsors ([[Broomhilda Bonebelly]], [[Gnarl Thulu]], and the Djinn) are alive, but have been captured by [[The Crusade]]. He also provided critical intelligence regarding [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]], who has been officially ousted from his position. Consequently, his work on [[Project Orchestra]] and weapons research & manufacturing has been transferred to [[House Cuprum]].

Lancaster warned that [[The Crusade]] is now utilizing the [[Ostinato Accumulator]], a massive device as large as the [[Sun Spire]] itself, which requires an immense amount of power that the group must be acquiring at a high cost.

### A Change of Course
Lancaster expressed deep concern regarding the [[Ancient Ones]], noting that they were displaying unusual impatience and a hunger for knowledge regarding the party's discoveries and the [[Harmony Engine]], despite Lancaster having kept that information strictly secret. Suspecting a leak, though doubting it was [[Collin McCambridge]]'s patron, Lancaster advised delaying their return until the mole could be identified.

He provided historical context on [[The Terramancers]], describing them as pre-Shattering idealists who believed their magic could forge a utopia, and explained that pre-Shattering [[Lairs]] were powerful entities with strong capabilities to serve their creators/masters including moving to where they were needed. The party shared their discoveries regarding the communication rings held by [[Collin McCambridge]] and [[Professor Jitterhop]]. Lancaster attempted to use Collin's ring in his sleep, but the device failed to work for him. The following morning, Lancaster departed the island to investigate his suspicions regarding the [[Ancient Ones]]. 

To stall for time, the party agreed to travel to the Sinking City to do their research in the Library of Lairs. [[Twilight Emberrest]] commanded the [[Lairs|Lair]] via the [[Harmony Engine]], and the island physically initiated its journey through the void, pulling on the memories of [[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]], [[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]], and [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]].

### Downtime
During the journey, the party engaged in various research and crafting tasks. 

[[Vase the Fragile]] investigated the "Void-Worm" theory, concluding there were no corroborating texts beyond Scholar Vra'ath's own work, confirming only that [[The Shattering of the World]] was caused by a massive magical burst]. Vase also successfully completed the construction of an Instrument of Illusions. 

Meanwhile, [[Collin McCambridge]] dedicated his time to learning the Infernal language.

[[Vaxen the Unkillable]] researched the heirs of [[House Aurum]], noting that [[Eobard Aurum]] was one of the few recorded as having died in battle. In private, [[Twilight Emberrest]] spoke with [[Vaxen the Unkillable]], quietly designating the warrior as the group's new leader. Vaxen subsequently experienced a dream featuring a jovial, battle-scarred warrior, and was later approached by [[Otis Thulu]], whose steadfast friendship provided Vaxen with a renewed sense of purpose and a reason to keep fighting for his companions.
