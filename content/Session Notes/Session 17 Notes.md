## Summary
### The Swamp Trek and Its Hazards
The party continues traversing the dense swamp toward the [[Sinking City]] aboard a raft poled by [[The Ferryman]]. As a thick fog rolls in, [[The Ferryman]] warns of the dangers of traveling at night and directs the group toward a specific hut. Along the way, the party must navigate treacherous natural hazards, including fields of soporific lotus and a swarm of flesh-eating glasswing moths. Utilizing magic to locate a safe path and physically poling the raft to assist [[The Ferryman]], they manage to push through the dangers to safety.

### Auntie Dorian's Diagnosis
The group arrives at the hut of [[Auntie Dorian]], a voodoo witch doctor. Though [[The Ferryman]] refuses to enter, [[Auntie Dorian]] warmly invites the party inside for dinner. During the meal, poltergeist-like activity shakes the bottles within the hut. [[Auntie Dorian]] explains that the local spirits remain furious about the party's prior actions in the swamp, noting that their "souls are heavy." She diagnoses that the party members have somehow turned their backs on their roots and families, warning that attempting to reach the city in this state will result in their deaths.

### The Witching Hour Preparation
To appease the "empty" spirits - representing acts of killing, drinking, and taking - [[Auntie Dorian]] brews a strong alcoholic concoction to pacify them. She instructs the party to travel to a nearby clearing at midnight, break the "shells" of the spirits, and capture six dangerous "Mudhollow" spirits in jars to pour into her cauldron. Before they leave, she paints a protective mana line down their faces, a process that causes [[Collin McCambridge]] immense psychic and physical pain.

### The Clearing
Carrying the concoction and the cauldron, the party walks toward the clearing. During the trek, strange phenomena plague the group: [[Otis Thulu]] tastes blood in his mouth, and [[Cletus Ironplow]] hallucinates visions of dying flowers on his parents' farm. Upon entering the clearing and initiating the ritual, the Mudhollow spirits begin to manifest. The encounter begins with the unsettling revelation that the Mudhollows are taking on the appearance of faces deeply familiar to the party members.
