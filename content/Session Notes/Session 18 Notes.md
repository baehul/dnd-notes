## Summary

### The Mudhollow Ritual
At midnight, the party entered the swamp clearing to conduct the ritual prescribed by [[Auntie Dorian]]. As the ritual commenced, six Mudhollow spirits manifested, taking the physical forms of deeply familiar figures from the party's past. 

A physical skirmish broke out, compounded by intense psychological warfare as the spirits actively taunted the party members.

[[Cletus Ironplow]] was berated by his father for abandoning his family farm, while [[Vaxen the Unkillable]] was mocked by his deceased relatives for his newfound desire to survive. [[Twilight Emberrest]] faced manifestations of the party who dismissed his philosophical platitudes, and [[Otis Thulu]] was reprimanded by his father for his continuous violence and disconnect from the First Tree. [[Vase the Fragile]] faced [[Captain Clay Argilla]], who accused him of endangering the world, and [[Collin McCambridge]] was confronted by his father and brother, who labeled him a danger to his companions.

### The Golden Eruption
Despite the psychological onslaught, the party successfully fought the spirits, broke their protective shells, and captured them within the prepared jars. They emptied the captured spirits into the cauldron, successfully fulfilling the requirements of the ritual. 

During the intense skirmish, [[Collin McCambridge]] was rendered unconscious and pushed to the absolute brink of death. Just as he appeared to succumb, he miraculously revived and erupted in a blinding golden light. With his eyes and veins radiating uncontrolled magical energy, Collin turned to the party and simply stated, "I guess we should talk."
