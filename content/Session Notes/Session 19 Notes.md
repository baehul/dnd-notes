## Summary

### The Mudhollow Ritual's Price
[[Auntie Dorian]] completes the ritual amidst chanting and flashes of lightning, causing the swamp to go silent. The party drinks from the cauldron to finalize the rite. Before allowing them to depart, [[Auntie Dorian]] warns the group that the ancient magic demands a service in return, instructing them that they must return to see her before they leave the region.

### Collin's Confession
Taking advantage of a quiet moment, [[Collin McCambridge]] reveals his true connection to [[House Aurum]]. He confesses that he was the steward to [[Lord Eobard Aurum]] and was closely bonded with him. Following Eobard's disappearance, [[High Baron Magnus Aurum]] covered up the truth, forcibly altering Collin's appearance to resemble the missing heir and subsequently having Collin's father and brother assassinated to protect the secret. Collin confirms he still does not know how the real Eobard died.

### Visions and Suspicions
When [[Vase the Fragile]] embraces Collin, Vase is struck by a sudden vision of a gnome scholar in a library full of ancient tomes. Upon making a horrifying discovery and attempting to warn someone named "Mulvin," the gnome is suddenly assassinated by a physically identical counterpart wielding a knife. Shortly after, Collin informs the group that he is feeling a pull from his patron and insists they must visit him soon.

### The Ferryman's Intel
The party reunites with [[The Ferryman]], who mentions he recently transported someone else with golden eyes like Collin's. He also shares that a secretive group of three individuals, described only as one small, one medium, and one large, recently traveled through the swamp. The party theorizes this group might be representatives traveling in secret from the primary [[Upper Crust Houses]], suspecting the potential involvement of figures such as [[Lady Alexandra Aurum]] of the [[Ecclesia Solari]].

### Entering the Sinking City
Arriving at the rim of the [[Sinking City]], the party is forced to dive down a waterfall to access the streets below. [[The Ferryman]] provides them with a golden tooth to use as a summoning contact, which [[Cletus Ironplow]] successfully catches mid-air despite suffering a painful fall into the water. Upon entering the city, the vibrant flora of the swamp transitions into a bleak, grayscale environment choked by thick, colorless fog. Observing the heavy presence of [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company]] emblems, Collin carefully conceals his eyes to avoid recognition.

### The Rat Race Agency
Seeking a safe haven, the party utilizes a contact known to Collin, [[Twilight Emberrest]], and [[Vaxen the Unkillable]], arriving at the Rat Race Private Detective Agency. When a prospective client arrives at the agency, Vase disguises himself to intercept them, only to discover that the client is the very same gnome he witnessed being murdered in his vision.
