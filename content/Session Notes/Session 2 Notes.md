## Summary
### The Vision
Following the accidental dislodging of the barrel on the deck of _The Lead Belly_, the container cracked open, releasing the energy of raw [[Resonance Crystals]]. [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] was immediately struck by a psychic backlash, collapsing into a seizure. While unconscious, Collin experienced a vivid vision of the world's [[Ley Lines]] and [[Tectonic Islands]] revolving around a core, violently pulled together and glued by molten gold pouring from a massive coin replacing the sun.

In the vision, a familiar figure appeared, speaking with a voice Collin recognized: _"My prodigal ninth son, returning to the fold. You should have stayed hidden, it would have been safer for you, easier for me. Cleaner for both of us. I have much experience cleaning up your messes. This all could have been yours."

[[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]] and [[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]] managed to revive Collin just as [[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe|Captain Arabella]] rushed onto the deck. Twilight successfully convinced her that the commotion was merely someone tripping, concealing the loss of the cargo. [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] noted that [[Resonance Crystals]] of this grade are typically use for powerful rituals and should be under heavy security. 

### The Hazardous Waste Hold
Concerned about meeting her delivery deadline after veering off-course, [[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe|Captain Arabella]] decides to take a faster route. During this travel, Twilight observed an unusual volume of [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|Golden Sails Company]] ships in the area. The captain decides that it might be better for the group to hide in the hazardous waste hold as the traffic around the ship has increased. 

[[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe|Captain Arabella]] warned the group of a lever that activates an acid cleanse mechanism but promised not to pull it. Cletus attempted to jam the cleansing lever mechanism to prevent accidental activation. While the group is preparing, [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] inspected some of the barrels aboard the ship and noticed they were marked with some writing in a language he did not recognize.

A [[House Cuprum]] ship was spotted passing _The Lead Belly_ twice, forcing the party to enter the hazardous hold sooner than expected. The party descended into the dark hold, lit only by dim red emergency lights. The area was filled with trash and acid burns. 

### The Inspection
_The Lead Belly_ was forcibly stopped and boarded by [[House Cuprum]] forces. The boarders were suspicious of the passengers seen on deck earlier. The [[House Cuprum]] forces commandeered _The Lead Belly_, escorting it to their destination island, a journey taking roughly 8 to 10 hours.

Upon docking, the ship was inspected by [[Lady Dagna Cuprum]]. The inspection revealed that Arabella was short one barrel, delivering only two instead of the contracted three. It was revealed that Arabella had shorted the delivery on a previous run due to price disputes.

The [[House Cuprum]] inspectors, noting the discrepancy and hearing noises from below, ordered the ship to be inspected. Consequently, the order was given to run the "Biological Waste Cleanser" to sterilize the hold while the party was still trapped inside.

## New NPC's
 - **[[Lady Dagna Cuprum]]**: A high-ranking member of House Cuprum who oversaw the receipt of the crystal shipment.
