## Summary

### The Missing Professor
[[Vase the Fragile]], operating under the guise of the Rat Race Private Detective Agency's resident detective, was approached by [[Mulvin Thistlethorn]] regarding his missing brother, [[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn]]. Sylas, an ancient history professor researching [[The Terramancers|Terramancy]], failed to arrive at the library as planned. Mulvin last saw his brother at Smoke's Speakeasy, where they were preparing to use their acquired library passes. [[Vase the Fragile]] accepted the investigation for a fee of 10,000 GP, securing 3,000 GP upfront. The agency's actual assistant subsequently warned the group that failing to solve the case would provoke the ire of the real detective.

### Navigating the Sinking City
While organizing their efforts, [[Collin McCambridge]] accidentally established a psychic telepathic link connecting himself, [[Otis Thulu]], [[Cletus Ironplow]], and [[Vase the Fragile]]. The group also examined a steam matrix device utilized in the [[Sinking City]]. Attached directly to the buildings, the device prevents them from slowly sinking into the mud by extracting humidity from the air to generate stabilizing steam lift. The party noted the unusual design choice of the matrix lacking a continuous magical fuel source, requiring constant refueling instead.

### Gathering Leads and the Ambush
The party split to gather intelligence. [[Otis Thulu]] engaged with [[Blood Horde]] forces and uncovered details regarding "Operation Green Thunder," an unsanctioned military effort to reclaim an island controlled by [[House Argentum]]. Meanwhile, [[Twilight Emberrest]] learned from a local bar owner that Sylas was likely captured by forces aligned with the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]]. Investigating the alley where the kidnapping occurred, the party discovered signs of a struggle, large footprints, and the magical residue of Abjuration and Illusion magic. As they examined the scene, thieves wielding bats dropped their invisibility and ambushed the group from the shadows.
