## Summary
### The Archives Office
The party visited the Archives office to secure library passes in their ongoing search for [[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn]] and [[Mulvin Thistlethorn]]. Speaking with clerks Bill and Agnus, they learned the waitlist was approximately three months long, unless expedited by a pass from the Rat Mayor. They also observed a heavy presence of upper-crust citizens and learned that the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]] was currently conducting security renovations at the Library.

### GSC Trade Office Intel
Seeking a faster route, the party investigated the local [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]] Trade Office, noting heavy security outside. While [[Vase the Fragile]] gathered information from locals on the street, [[Collin McCambridge]] re-entered the office using the alias "Cassius Lackey." By charging a lookup fee to [[House Aurum]], Collin persuaded a clerk named Codsworth to retrieve Mulvin's file. The records indicated that Mulvin had redeemed his tickets at the Library that morning and was currently renting a warehouse near the docks.

### The Warehouse Ambush
Arriving at the docks, the party located the block of warehouses. Collin identified magical abjuration and darkness wards protecting one of the buildings. After bypassing the lock and sending his familiar inside, Collin spotted a gnome chained in the basement. As Collin attempted to leave, a large celestial being named Romanis seized him, dangling him over the basement drop. The rest of the party rushed inside just as a massive explosion—part of a [[Blood Horde]] operation—detonated outside at the docks.

### A Deal with Varius
The captive Mulvin warned the party of their mistake. The figure overseeing the ambush shifted form, revealing himself to be [[High Inquisitor Varius]], who had secretly initiated the party's contract at the detective agency. Varius ordered Romanis to release Collin and struck a deal with the group. In exchange for the GSC receipt proving Sylas was currently in the Library, Varius provided the necessary GSC paperwork for the party to enter and retrieve the professor. Furthermore, he paid them handsomely, included a bonus for two weeks of silence, and promised to supply holy texts to [[Vase the Fragile]] upon their return. The party concluded the exchange and stepped outside to find the harbor actively burning from the explosion.
