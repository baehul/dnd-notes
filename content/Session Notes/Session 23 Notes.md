## Summary
### The Harbor Explosion & Blood Horde Encounter
The party rushed toward the arcane fire at the docks, observing unique fire colors and a burning metal ship leaving the harbor. They discovered [[Blood Horde]] forces hastily loading a commandeered [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company]] ship with [[Resonance Crystals]] and weapons, noting the event as an unexpected victory against [[The Crusade]]. 

[[Twilight Emberrest]] and [[Cletus Ironplow]] healed an unconscious orc, who recognized [[Otis Thulu]]. The orc revealed that the [[Blood Horde]] found exactly what [[The Crusade]] had been hiding, mentioning that a woman with a bomb had attempted to stop them. He urged [[Otis Thulu]] and [[Vaxen the Unkillable]] to join the fight, disclosing an upcoming operation to attack and reclaim an unnamed territorial island from [[House Argentum]] in two weeks. The party declined the call to arms, and both groups fled the scene as authorities arrived.

### Securing the Passes
The party returned to the chaotic [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company]] trade office. Utilizing the expedited paperwork provided by [[High Inquisitor Varius]], [[Collin McCambridge]] met with the clerk Codsworth under his alias, "Cassius Lackey". The group successfully secured anonymous, full-access library passes and proceeded to the archives.

### The Library's Lower Levels
Entering the populated upper levels of the library, the party found only basic information regarding aboleths and the [[Underworld]]. Seeking more substantial knowledge, they crossed the boundary into the unguarded, uncleaned lower levels where safety was not guaranteed. 

While making camp in the unsafe sector, [[Cletus Ironplow]] and [[Collin McCambridge]] reset the command word of their Driftglobe to "Liesht" for illumination. During the night watches, [[Otis Thulu]] and [[Vaxen the Unkillable]] fed a passing mouse. In a subsequent watch, the party was surrounded by a large swarm of mice. While attempting to follow one of the creatures, [[Collin McCambridge]]'s familiar, Jeeves, fell into a pit.
