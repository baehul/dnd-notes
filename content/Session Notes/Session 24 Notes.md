## Summary
### The Fog-Choked Level
The party pressed on through the unguarded lower levels of [[The Library of Layers]], descending into a floor drowned in thick fog. Water dripped somewhere out of sight, its echo carrying through rooms they could not see the far walls of. The air, against all expectation, smelled faintly pleasant.

### The Maintained Level
A level down, the decay broke. The party came upon a section that was surprisingly well-kept and, by every sign they could find, safe — and across its floor ran old, dried prints of swamp mud. There were two sets. One pair was unmistakably gnomish. The other was larger, belonging to a dwarf or a small human, and to the party's eye possibly a woman's.

The tracks wandered the section without much order, but they lingered, and the party could read from them exactly which shelves the two had stopped at. Several books had been pulled down from those shelves. One had been left lying open.

### Theories of the Shattering
The open book was a survey of the known theories on the cause of [[The Shattering of the World]]. It set the two dominant hypotheses against one another: the Inside-Out theory, which held that the phenomenon began at the world's core and expanded outward, and the Outside-In theory, which held that it began at the surface and tunneled inward. Its author came down on neither side, arguing instead for a combination of the two — that energy drawn from inside the planet had been used by someone, or something, outside it.

### The Scavengers
Descending a third time, the party heard gnawing coming from an adjoining room. They found the source: large parasitic creatures swarming over the carcass of a great reptilian beast, stripping it as they fed. The fight that followed was a harrowing one, but the party killed them to the last.

Only once the room fell quiet could they examine the carcass properly, and what they found was not the scavengers' work at all. A gigantic hole had been blown clean through the beast. Whatever had killed it had done so with overwhelming force, and had been down here before them.

With the level below them proven hostile, they climbed back up to the maintained floor and made camp there to take a long rest.

### DM Secrets & Context
* The larger, feminine set of muddy footprints belongs to [[Lady Alexandra Aurum]], descending ahead of the party with the gnome.
* The reptilian beast was the resident predator of that level. Alexandra killed it on her way down — the hole blown through the carcass is her work, and the parasites merely found the body afterward.
