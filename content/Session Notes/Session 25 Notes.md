## Summary
### A Troubled Rest
During the short rest on the maintained floor of [[The Library of Layers]], [[Cletus Ironplow]] gave [[Otis Thulu]] a magic ring. As they rested, Otis dreamed of a bamboo forest where he saw a crocodile with strange, unnatural eyes and was swallowed by the ground.

### The Vault Floor
The party found a rusted vault door. It was forced open with a charge of [[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]]'s Chime of Opening while [[Otis Thulu]] and [[Vaxen the Unkillable]] slammed against it.  The party found a floor dominated by a vast, seemingly bottomless hole. Every shelf and book in the room plunged into the dark, save for a glass case holding three scrolls (*Reduce*, *Imprisonment*, and *Catnap*) and, to the party's confusion, an old fish suit. Vaxen was left dangling over the drop; [[Twilight Emberrest]] cast the scroll of *Reduce* on him so the rest of the party could haul him back up.

### The Floor of Trees
The next floor replaced shelves with living trees which proved to be books that had transformed. Someone had already hacked and slashed a path through to the far staircase, and the party recovered a vial of unidentified golden magical residue left in their wake. [[Collin McCambridge]] warned the group that they might be trailing others after the same quarry, and urged caution. The party located and dispelled a powerful tripwire alarm spell left behind as well.

Amongst the tree-books, [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] found a record of tribes - he searched for those who followed "the world tree": the Ironroots, Branchbow, Tusktooth, Thorntusks, and the River. [[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]]'s tribe was also listed in the record of tribes, with its earliest ancestor recorded only as "The First Of Their Name."

### The Floor of Vines
On the floor below, strange plants reacted violently to flashes of light that seemed to be triggered by a magical ward. The plants flared with a hypnotic glow that charmed or stunned whoever it caught. The effect struck multiple times, snaring a different party member each time, seemingly triggered by loud noise. [[Vase the Fragile]] cast *Magic Mouth* on an arrow and fired it across the room, using its shouting to bait the flashes so the party could slip out. The spell is still active and will keep triggering the plants until someone dispels it.

### The Floor of Water
The lowest floor the party reached was flooded with soothing water that eased their wounds. [[Cletus Ironplow]] searched out and found books on an ancient deep-sea being: the oldest known Aboleth of the western oceans, a six-eyed entity called **The Six-Eyed Tentacle of the Deep Icy Waters**. The texts described it as having made contact with humans in ages past, granting boons in exchange for service, and being regarded by ocean-dwelling creatures as a deity .

Drinking the water proved to carry its own magic. [[Collin McCambridge]] and [[Twilight Emberrest]] each gained the ability to *spider climb* for 24 hours, though the water left everyone feeling as though they badly needed rest. [[Cletus Ironplow]] and [[Otis Thulu]] found small flowers sprouting from their head, granting them advantage on Charisma checks for a day. [[Vase the Fragile]] gained advantage on sight-based Perception checks for the same span. [[Vaxen the Unkillable]] felt strangely fortified after his first drink, but a second sent him into a deep sleep.

### DM Secrets & Context
* Otis's dream was a vision of anger sent by Luwei, whose domain is a bamboo forest: in a previous session, Otis crushed a crocodile skull with a dragon's eye embedded in it, not realizing it was a message from Luwei intended for Collin McCambridge (Luwei's chosen "eyes" outside his island). Otis destroyed it, mistaking it for a bad omen.
* The Six-Eyed Tentacle of the Deep Icy Waters is the Aboleth the party will eventually need to track down in pursuit of an Aboleth eye.
* The golden magical residue is the aftermath of Lady Alexandra Aurum's spellcasting — she and the gnome are still descending ahead of the party.
