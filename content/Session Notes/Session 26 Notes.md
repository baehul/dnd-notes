## Summary

### A Rest Beside the Cenote
The party made camp on a dry bank beside the overflowing underground cenote where they had ended their last descent, the soothing waters of [[The Library of Layers]] still lapping nearby. The rest was not a quiet one. [[Collin McCambridge]] and [[Vase the Fragile]] fell to arguing: Collin had recognized that one of the Library's floors was devoted to the [[Solaris and The Solari Faith|Solari Faith]] — a subject [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] had wanted to study — and had decided on his own that the group could not spare the time to linger there, settling instead for retrieving a single book in the hope of placating him.

### The Library's Bargain
As they rested, the book Collin had recovered began to **speak to Vase** — a voice bound to the Library itself. It had watched a **gnome** gathering books from the shelves, and it had sensed the party doing the same. It asked that every book be returned to its place and that none be carried out of the Library. After some back-and-forth, [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] vowed that the party would leave the Library with no books at all. In exchange, the voice granted him a black vial of a blank, ink-like substance — an [Essence of the Mindless](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/11915333-essence-of-the-mindless), which leaves whoever drinks it helpless before the next spell cast upon them. Vase later shared its nature with the rest of the party.

### The Whisper in the Reeds
[[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] was seized by what looked like a night terror. When [[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]] tried to wake him, she was instead drawn into the dream alongside him. There, Collin's patron — a presence the party came to know as **the Whisper in the Reeds** — demanded to know why [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] had defaced his symbol. Collin explained that Otis had been frightened and confused and had not understood what it was. Unmoved, the patron demanded that both Collin *and* Otis come to him.

The patron also revealed that he had been quietly working to uphold his end of his bargain with Collin — to deliver [[House Aurum]] into Collin's hands — and that a member of the house was somewhere below them in the Library. The party realized that Collin's [[Ring of Restless Revelation]] had been blocking the patron's messages all this while. Talking it through afterward, and weighing everything they had found in their descent, the group all but confirmed the one below them to be **Alexandra Aurum**.

### The Ruined Floor and the Mending Desk
Descending further, the party reached a floor of shattered shelves and destroyed books, its ruined volumes concerned with the [[Way Of Night (Religion)|Way of Night]]. Amid the wreckage, a single desk stood intact — bound to the Library's magic and able to cast *Mending*, restoring a ruined book to its original written state. Recognizing what such a thing could do for their [[Lair]] — restoring ancient texts they might yet recover — the party worked to pry it loose and haul it away. As they did, it discharged a burst of arcane energy that seriously wounded everyone present, but they succeeded in securing the desk.

### Into Free-Fall
The party pressed on into a room laced with staggeringly intricate arcane wards — layered *contingency* and *symbol* magic woven together across every surface. When they attempted to dispel one of the traps, the whole lattice answered at once: the floor gave way beneath them, and the session closed with the party plunging into free-fall.

### DM Secrets & Context
* Collin's patron is **[[Luwei]]**, the Whisper in the Reed. The party knows him only as "the Whisper in the Reeds" — they do not have his true name.
* The "defaced symbol" is the crocodile skull with the embedded dragon's eye that Otis crushed (Session 25's vision) — Luwei's message to Collin, destroyed before Collin ever saw it.
* The Aurum sibling descending ahead of the party is **[[Alexandra Aurum]]** — the feminine muddy footprints (Session 24) and the golden spell residue (Session 25) are hers. The layered abjuration wards fit her as well (Alexandra is a powerful abjurer). The party has now all but named her; the patron confirmed only that "a member of the house" lay further below.
