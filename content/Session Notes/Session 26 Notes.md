## Summary

### A Rest Beside the Cenote
The party made camp on a dry bank beside the overflowing underground cenote where they had ended their last descent, the soothing waters of [[The Library of Layers]] still lapping nearby. The rest was not a quiet one. [[Collin McCambridge]] and [[Vase the Fragile]] fell to arguing: Collin had recognized that one of the Library's floors was devoted to the [[Solaris and The Solari Faith|Solari Faith]] — a subject [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] had wanted to study — and had decided on his own that the group could not spare the time to linger there, settling instead for retrieving a single book in the hope of placating him. Vase's interest was not idle: the party was under contract with a very high-ranking figure in the Solari church, and he felt that knowing more about the faith could only help them.

### The Library's Bargain
As they rested, the book Collin had recovered began to **speak to Vase** — a voice bound to the Library itself. It had once been a librarian, but it no longer remembered its own name, so Vase gave it a nickname. It had watched a **gnome** gathering books from the shelves, and it had sensed the party doing the same. It asked that every book be returned to its place and that none be carried out of the Library. After some back-and-forth, [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] vowed that the party would leave the Library with no books at all. In return, the book died, pouring its whole essence into a black vial of a blank, ink-like substance — an [Essence of the Mindless](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/11915333-essence-of-the-mindless). Vase later shared its nature with the rest of the party.

### The Whisper in the Reed
While [[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]] kept watch, [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] began to glow green and was seized by what looked like a night terror. When Twilight tried to wake him, she was instead drawn into the dream alongside him. There, Collin's patron — a presence the party came to know as **the Whisper in the Reed** — demanded to know why [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] had defaced his symbol. Collin explained that Otis had been frightened and confused and had not understood what it was. Unmoved, the patron demanded that both Collin *and* Otis come to him. Collin, for his part, remarked that the patron's old ways of reaching him, the glowing eyes and the curses, had grown dated, and that the two of them ought to communicate more directly.

The patron also revealed that he had been quietly working to uphold his end of his bargain with Collin — to deliver [[House Aurum]] into Collin's hands — and that a member of the house was somewhere below them in the Library. He reminded Collin that he was there only because the patron allowed it, and that crossing him again would go badly. The party realized that Collin's [[Ring of Restless Revelation]] had been blocking the patron's messages all this while. In the morning the party talked things over and agreed they had to work more as a team, which was no easy thing: more of Collin's secrets had come out yet again, and when Vase told everyone about the book that had spoken to him, then died and become the vial, the others worried he was losing it. Weighing everything they had found in their descent, the group all but confirmed the one below them to be **Alexandra Aurum**. Bats had been seen flying through the Library, Collin knew someone whose familiar was a bat, and Alexandra was an abjurer, which fit the elaborate alarm spell they had seen.

### The Ruined Floor and the Mending Desk
Descending further, the party got past a dam that stood in their way and reached a floor of shattered shelves and destroyed books, its ruined volumes concerned with the [[Way Of Night (Religion)|Way of Night]]. Amid the wreckage, a single desk stood intact — bound to the Library's magic and able to cast *Mending*, restoring a ruined book to its original written state. Recognizing what such a thing could do for their [[Lair]] — restoring ancient texts they might yet recover — the party worked to pry it loose and fit it into their Bag of Holding. As they did, it discharged a burst of arcane energy that seriously wounded everyone present, but they succeeded in securing the desk, which for now stays stored in the Bag of Holding until it can be brought to the Lair.

### Into Free-Fall
The party pressed on into a staircase room covered in runes and laced with staggeringly intricate arcane wards — layered *contingency* and *symbol* magic woven together across every surface. In trying to dispel one of the traps, they accidentally set it off, and the whole lattice answered at once in a cascade of traps: the floor gave way and the room itself fell. The party understood that whoever had laid the wards now knew they were there, and the session closed with the party plunging into free-fall.
