## Summary

### The Fall
Still in free-fall from the collapsing floor, the party plunged toward a great rune waiting on the ground far below. [[Collin McCambridge]] realized who had likely laid such wards, and who might be waiting at the bottom, which gave him a moment's pause before he cast *Feather Fall* on everyone. The drop slowed to a gentle, steady descent, with loose paper and the remnants of ruined books drifting past as the room fell apart around them. [[Otis Thulu]] prayed silently to the world tree, and [[Twilight Emberrest]] dispelled the rune with *Dispel Magic*. It simply vanished.

They landed on solid floor just as another large bang sounded. A great door ahead blew open, and the tunnel behind it crumbled and collapsed.

### A Message on the Wall
The chamber was noticeably older than any floor the party had crossed. Its stone floor was weathered, and three other corridors led out of it. Colorless and flickering like a hologram, the image of [[Lady Alexandra Aurum]] appeared, which Collin recognized at once. She wore ecclesiastic clothing, with a braid and a pendant.

The message was addressed to Varius. She said he had navigated the traps she had set, including a web rune she had left for him as a gift, and she warned him to come no further. She accused him of treachery, of poisonings and betrayals, and said she had been tracking him since he used her name to enter the gala for [[Project Orchestra]]. Her uncle, she added, would surely have told her that her absence had been noticed. Her own magic, she said, was disobeying her. She would no longer be held, her real family needed her, and her loyalty lay with them rather than the church, which had had its chance. The image then vanished.

### A Case of Mistaken Identity
The party puzzled over it. Collin said the party had nothing to do with any poisonings, and the party recalled having sneaked into the gala using Alexandra's name. Vase and Twilight remembered that another group had been at the gala, and Collin came to believe Alexandra might be conflating the party with them. Cletus recalled the Inquisitor they had met, and the party knew him as [[High Inquisitor Varius]]. [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] and [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] observed that Varius could transform into anyone, even several people at once.

Collin also spoke of his own past. He said that the [[Resonance Crystals]] imbued into his body had caused a side effect that appeared later and cost him his magic. He said he did not know whether Alexandra's claim that the church had tampered with her magic was true, but guessed she was going through something similar, and that it might leave her weaker, more volatile and more desperate. Twilight agreed she might be more desperate.

### The Book Retrieval System
Cletus examined the pipes and tubes in the chamber and found them to be dual-walled and hollow at the center, with a badly rusted closed slot. He judged it an ancient retrieval system for sending books from one part of the library to another, one that had not been used in a long time and could carry nothing larger than a textbook. Magic ran along the outside of the tubing, behaving like pressure, so closing certain outlets might cause a blast of magic elsewhere. Twilight worried that some of the information the party and their rivals both sought had already been moved out of the library.

The other corridors offered no way down, and the most recent footprints led toward the collapsed one. Collin sent his familiar Jeeves into the collapse, and Jeeves felt airflow, suggesting open space beyond, though not how far. Collin proposed using the tubes to blow the collapse open with built-up pressure, and Cletus agreed to try. Five tubes led off the machine: three into open corridors, one into the collapsed corridor, and one going down. The plan was to close every valve except the one for the collapsed corridor, so the pressure would be released there. That meant three valves in the three rooms and a final valve in the main chamber.

### A Nap and a Survey
Cletus, Twilight and Vase used the [Scroll of Catnap](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/3360220-catnap) the party had found, sleeping briefly and waking refreshed, while Collin and Otis scouted the three rooms. Jeeves stayed by the sleepers, and [[Watson, Cloud Parrot|Watson]] circled overhead to guard them from falling debris.

The first room was bare of books, with a rusty, moss-covered valve visible across it. The second held a mud pit and a chasm, its pipe ending broken at the center of the floor. Glowing golden runic circles sat on pillars on either side, and an arcane eye scanned the room. In the third, the valve was intact, and water fell over the edge into a dark void on the far side. When the sleepers woke, Jeeves was found perched on Twilight's nose.

### The First Valve
The first valve was protected by a transparent, reflective sphere with a golden rune at its center. A rock Otis threw at it rebounded and disintegrated against the wall behind him. On Twilight's suggestion, the whole party spread around it and threw stones together until the rune distorted, fizzled and shattered. Otis, with Twilight's help, forced the rusted valve shut, and the pipe shook and went still.

### The Mud Room
The second room held narrow beams over a mud pit, with three lever components waiting on the far side. Two watching eyes stood guard over the crossing, and their gazes carried the effects Snare and Tithe. One eye slammed anyone who moved beneath its gaze to the floor, and the other tore whatever a watched person held out of their hands and flung it into the mud. Party members repeatedly slipped off the beams and struggled in the mud, and the crossing was slow and exhausting.

Collin proposed sending the components across in the Bag of Holding, so that if the bag was taken, someone could jump in the mud and retrieve it, and the three components were stored inside. In the end, Cletus took up the bag, made himself and the bag invisible, and crossed unnoticed by the eyes. He brought everything across, the lever was reassembled and attached, and a second valve was closed.

### The Third Valve and the Ghost
The third valve was held by a ghost, whose hands gripped it while a lightning rune bound to his back fed a shimmering ward around him. The lightning leapt to anything metal, and to anyone carrying metal. Collin worked out that the rune's power had nowhere to go, and that the water falling beyond could draw it off. The party laid a line of metal objects toward the water, and when the lightning ran along it, the ghost's ward faltered long enough for Otis, who had stripped off everything metal, to close the valve. The ward fell away from the ghost, his hands let go, and the electricity stopped.

Freed, the ghost seemed to come to his senses and said the ordeal had been agony. He could not say how long he had been held, only that it felt like hours, perhaps a few days. He seemed to be a librarian of some ancient order. With dread, he asked whether a terrible woman had finally left. He said he could feel her tearing apart parts of the library and grieved for it, that others of his kind still survived, and that he sensed a portion of the library had just been cut off from the rest. Vase told him he had met one of his friends, a book, who had sacrificed himself.

### A Last Rite
Collin urged that they move on, while Twilight believed their opponents were acting in the library right now, and that catching them mid-action would be the party's best chance. Vase pitied the ghost and, drawing on an old-path rite, moved the nearby water while telling him a simple story of the sun and the moon and the first forest. The tale plainly pleased him, and, worn out, he smiled and faded into the library. Twilight rested a hand on Vase's shoulder as he rejoined the group.

### The Blast
Back in the main chamber, only the final valve, on the great pipe leading to the library below, remained. Cletus turned it while the others took cover, and the whole system shook. Steam hissed from the pipe, a deep rumble rolled toward the collapsed tunnel, and a great blast of arcane magic, steam and fire broke through several feet of stone. As the smoke cleared, the party saw a golden light shining from further down the opened tunnel.
