## Summary

### Back at the Tunnel
The party stood at the mouth of the tunnel they had blasted open deep within [[The Library of Layers]], golden light spilling from somewhere further in. [[Vaxen the Unkillable]] woke and rejoined the group, groggy and asking what had happened. He said he had been terribly sleepy, and [[Twilight Emberrest]] teased him about his long nap.

Curious about the glow, [[Collin McCambridge]] asked whether he should send his familiar Jeeves ahead to look, and the group agreed. [[Vase the Fragile]] noted that Jeeves could fly and that people tend not to look up.

### Down the Staircase
Jeeves flew down a spiraling stair of stone steps, and the glow brightened as he went. At the limit of what he could sense, a woman's voice drifted to him, but he could not travel far enough to look past the bend. The party followed quietly. Farther down, the passage opened toward a chamber where they could pick out stone shelving and a burning lantern suspended overhead. Twilight remarked that whatever came of their conversation, they would have to approach the woman ahead.

### Collin's Confession
Before going further, Collin turned to face the others. He said he had told only part of the truth about himself at [[Auntie Dorian]]'s hut, and that he believed this was why the spirits had attacked them there. He said he still counted Callum and Cole as his father and brother and considered himself more McCambridge than Aurum, but that he was Eobard Aurum, heir of the Aurum family, and that the woman waiting below was his sister, [[Lady Alexandra Aurum|Alexandra]]. Above all else, he said, he wanted her dead, along with the rest of his family.

Vase backed away in alarm and pointed out that this was hardly the first time Collin had claimed to be telling the truth at last. He argued that it would serve the Resistance better to take Alexandra alive as a source of intelligence, and said he had no side except the Resistance's. [[Otis Thulu]] observed that she was still an Aurum. Collin said he was willing to try to capture her, but that his goal had not changed: if she tried to flee unsubdued, he would take the killing shot. Twilight said he was not sure killing was the answer. He thanked Collin for the truth but noted the odd timing, and he promised to heal and support everyone and to deal no killing blows himself. Later, Vase told Collin quietly that he was angry about the lies but still wanted to be his friend, and that he would back him regardless.

### Making a Plan
Twilight reminded the party that a gnome might still need saving, though he suspected the gnome had turned against them. Vase proposed subduing Alexandra first and talking afterward, and offered the Essence of the Mindless he carried as a way to make her more compliant. Twilight proposed that everyone throw all they had at her first, so that she would spend her defensive magic before he tried to hold her with his strongest magic.

While they talked, a deep boom and the sound of something collapsing echoed from the room ahead. The party let Vaxen lead them in, and they crept slowly and quietly toward the room.

### The Threshold
Vaxen, in front, bumped into something invisible and was pitched headlong into the room. From around the corner a woman's voice remarked that she had been waiting through their long discussion. A glowing rune appeared underfoot, and behind them shimmering energy sealed the tunnel shut before turning transparent. Alexandra appeared before them as a projected visage. Twilight tried to unravel the barrier and found he could not.

### Alexandra Speaks
Alexandra addressed the party as though [[High Inquisitor Varius]] were among them. She remarked that Varius had come with a crowd when she would have expected him to keep to himself, and she wanted to know which of them he was. Turning to Collin, she said that wearing the likeness of her dead younger brother, Eobard, was a cruel stroke even from Varius, and that she meant to finish matters with him right there.

Collin, taken aback, asked whether his father had told them all that he was truly dead. Alexandra narrowed her eyes and told him that a man of faith ought to be above deceit and above defiling the memory of the dead. Her composed, stone-still bearing faltered. The party heard a sound like breaking crystal, and her image seemed to fracture into shifting colors as golden light spilled out of her through the cracks for a moment. She added that she was surprised Varius kept such foolish company, that she had more reading to get back to, and that she would leave them to consider where they had gone wrong in turning on her.

### Baiting the Lady
Twilight urged Collin to bait her, and Collin obliged. He mocked the idea that some Resistance rabble could have brought down the Aurum heir, and asked whether she truly thought he was dead. He jeered that she had been fooled by the workings of the [[Ecclesia Solari]], where, he said, the family had simply planted her as a pawn; such gullibility, he added, he would have expected from August rather than from her. He said he had crossed paths with August, who had failed to know him at all. Alexandra bristled at being likened to August. She observed that she had apparently been kept ignorant of yet another scheme, but brushed that aside. The church had abandoned her, she said, and here was her opportunity to claim her rightful place in the house, with her research ensuring she would never be pushed aside again.

Collin asked about her Resonance Crystals, the ones both of them had been fitted with as children. Alexandra fell silent and stared at him, then shut her eyes and steadied her breathing. Under her breath she seemed to be reassuring herself that Father could mend everything if she only accomplished one task properly. Collin pressed her: what did she think had become of him, and why would Father have told the world he was dead? He said he had lost his magic. Alexandra refused to believe him, insisting that he was an illusion and that this was only another deception.

### The Barrier Holds
For a moment a blue glow, the color of resonance crystal, shimmered around Alexandra and then drew back in. She turned and glared at the party. Weaving magic around herself, she hurled a glowing rune-circle at a stalactite overhead; it fastened there and released an eye, akin to the Snare and Tithe eyes met earlier. She vanished from view, though flashes of magic could be seen. The barrier remained. Collin called out that he could help her, and that his magic had returned. His words echoed back to him muffled, so the party knew sound passed through the barrier only faintly.

### The Wall
Trapped in a small corridor, Twilight asked what color Collin's eyes glowed when he cast, then used a small trick to make them flash gold. Those among the party who were more magically adept could tell the wall in front of them was not permanent but fading. Collin judged that it would not last much longer, while the wall behind them would hold far longer. Vaxen charged the front wall with all his might, and fine cracks branched across its surface. Encouraged by Collin, he charged again. The party braced for a fight, noting that the newly dropped eye was unlike the ones they had met earlier. Then the wall gave way and they rushed into the chamber.

### The Burning Books
Alexandra stood in the chamber with a gnome of some kind beside her, burning stacks of books with golden fire. A long and difficult fight followed, in which the party wore her down and brought her to the ground. During it, she declared that it made no difference whether he was Eobard or Varius: nobody would keep her from her birthright. She also boasted that she could not be spared, since only she and a foolish uncle of hers had any grasp of this magic.

Nearby was [[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn|Sylas]], entombed in a shell that resembled resonance crystal, with a knife lodged in his back; he seemed to be held in a kind of suspended stillness. The party tried to save the books as they burned. [[Cletus Ironplow]] rushed among the burning volumes and smothered the flames on some. Twilight set books into Cletus's Bag of Holding, among them one that held a diagram of a device strikingly like the [[Harmony Engine]]. Vase, with [[Watson, Cloud Parrot|Watson]] helping, gathered books as well.

### Eobard
With Alexandra on the ground, Collin met her eyes and pleaded with her to come with them. He said she was mistaken if she thought only she understood Terramancy: he had spoken with the [[The Terramancers|Terramancers]], knew them, and had picked up some of their language, so her value to her family was smaller than she imagined. He urged her to join them and do something greater: by helping the party she would help herself and everyone else, and together they would uncover Terramancy's history and find out the purpose of [[Project Orchestra]].

Alexandra answered by calling him Eobard and acknowledging that it was really him. She said Father had been furious ever since Eobard died, that she had no idea what had become of him, and that Father would hunt them to the world's edge for having tarnished his name. She also claimed that the church was aware of what she had done and had been poisoning her, turning their Resonance Crystals against her. Collin answered that Father had never tracked him down; Father had known full well that Eobard vanished rather than died, and had lied to shield the family name. Alexandra looked broken.

### The Pendant
Twilight knelt beside her, spoke a prayer of the night, told her she should listen to her brother, and tended her wounds. Alexandra pushed herself upright, her eyes wet, and confessed to Collin that the weight of the family name had become a cage she could not measure up to, yet could not bring herself to discard. She urged him to return to his hiding and his fight, far away from Father, and leave her to find some peace at last.

From within her vestments she drew a scroll and tossed it in Collin's direction. Then she wrenched from her neck a pendant stamped with a crown above a crossed sword and staff. Runes like those the party had seen some floors above flared to life and began to keen, and a rumble and a blast sounded from below. Alexandra fell catatonic. The fight was over, and Twilight proposed that Otis and Vaxen each grab Sylas and carry him out.
