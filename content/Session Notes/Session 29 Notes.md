## Summary

### Out of the Falling Library
[[The Library of Layers]] was coming apart around the party. Explosions rolled up from far below, runes flared along the walls, and golden fire had already burned away a great many books. [[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn|Sylas]] stood sealed in his crystal shell with the knife still in his back, and [[Lady Alexandra Aurum|Alexandra]] sat quiet and unresponsive, zoned out the way a person can after something terrible. [[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]] ran to Sylas and took hold of the crystal to carry him out, calling for [[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]]'s help, while [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] gathered Alexandra up and followed [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]], who had picked up the scroll Alexandra threw his way.

The books were the last hurdle. [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] felt the vial of the Essence of the Mindless he carried flare hot near the volumes the party had saved, and he reminded the others of the Way of the Librarians, which holds that what has been taken must be returned. The party pulled the books out of [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]]'s Bag of Holding and left every one of them behind as the room began to crumble.

### The Scroll
Collin recognized the scroll as teleportation magic. It would not carry them off the island, he explained, but it could take them somewhere familiar: the city, the swamp, even the lair. He passed word of the plan by mind link to Vase, Twilight and Otis. Collin feared that [[High Inquisitor Varius]] would kill them if they went back to him, and Twilight would rather let Varius believe them dead. The party chose [[Auntie Dorian]]'s hut instead, since she had told them they owed a repayment before they left the region.

When Collin read the scroll, blue light rose from the ground around the party. The scroll burned away, and Collin's hair and eyes turned fully gold, with golden light spilling out from beneath his wraps. The light faded into the sounds of the swamp, and the party stood sunk in mud in a clearing along the route they knew from the Mudhollow ritual, the hut a walk away. Collin was sick into the water, drained by the magic, and Vase took his first step only to be flung briefly into the air before dropping face first into the muck. They set off through the mud toward the hut in the late afternoon.

### Auntie Dorian's Hut
Through the trees they saw the hut, the reeds beside it dried and withered, and heard the old woman crooning inside. Otis knocked. Auntie Dorian welcomed them warmly, saying her bones had told her only that morning to expect guests, though not so soon. She seemed to take in Alexandra and Sylas without reaction, remarking only that her guests' friends were not very talkative, and she set a teapot bubbling for everyone. Alexandra took her cup in both hands and sipped, staring into the distance.

The party asked whether Auntie Dorian could do something about the crystal and the knife. They argued over whether to pull the knife, until Twilight pointed out that his healing might not be enough if the wound had done real harm. The party agreed to rest first and puzzle over Sylas later.

### The Restless Blade
Cletus reminded Auntie Dorian that she had told them they owed the spirits a debt. She said it was a small one: they were to help gather berries and roots for the next morning's breakfast, and carry something for her. From a cupboard she took a bundle wrapped in leather, twine and vines. Inside lay a fine dark steel dagger, bone-white along its edge, which she said held a restless spirit in need of help finding rest.

She began to hold it out to Collin, then paused and turned to Vaxen instead, saying the spirit sensed a kinship with him. As he reached for it the blade creaked like bone breaking and rejoining, and grew from dagger to sword to greatsword. A voice spoke from it, delighted to have found a mighty warrior to lead it to a glorious end. It said it had lost its own name in battle long ago and told Vaxen to call it the Restless Blade, restless because it sought that end. Vaxen, nearly in tears, promised to do anything for it. Auntie Dorian said that helping the spirit find rest was the only debt she asked, along with the breakfast foraging.

### Auntie Dorian's Lessons
Vase, quill and pad in hand, pressed Auntie Dorian for guidance. She called herself a cook rather than an alchemist, said the spirits were her friends, and told him that the harder one tries to make someone a friend, the harder it becomes. Some spirits, she added, prefer silence, especially those long used to being alone. Vase wrote it all down.

### The Lone Apple Tree
Twilight and Otis went out into the mud to forage. Otis, longing for apples, came upon a single apple tree with ruby red fruit, standing in a meadow where the air felt brighter and cleaner than the swamp around it. The fruit tasted of amber and of home. Twilight felt his hackles rise at how out of place it was, yet the meadow was warm, like honey and apple pie. Otis took apples for everyone, tossed his core aside so more might grow, bowed to the grass and thanked the tree as the world tree, and felt a deep rootedness in his stomach as another apple dropped neatly into his pouch. Twilight found a cluster of bright blue berries like neon raspberries, and the two gathered roots as well.

They returned to find the others at the hut. Auntie Dorian was delighted with the berries, but when Otis offered the apples she asked, a little oddly, whether he had picked them all. She said the party's service was complete. With the sun sinking she warned them to decide quickly whether to leave or stay, since the swamp was dangerous in the dark. When Otis wondered aloud whether their families would find them, Alexandra's eyes snapped up to him and then dropped back down. After a quick vote the party agreed to stay the night.

### Dinner and Prayer
Auntie Dorian set the party grinding vine, root and bugs for a crust, and served roast goose followed by an apple pie made from Otis's apples. As the sun set, Alexandra rose and asked to step outside to pray. Vase hurried after her with a note he had written, but she gave no sign that she saw it. Collin followed her out and linked minds with her to vouch for Vase.

On the balcony she faced the setting sun and prayed in Celestial, burned a pinch of incense in her palm with golden fire, and marked her forehead with the ash. She said that someone in her family might still keep the prayer, though it had done her no good. She said she had nowhere to go, having failed the church and betrayed her family, and that Collin had made that plain to her in the Library. She allowed a faint smile at Collin's golden hair and eyes, remarking that parading around with them was hardly wise, and said that Father had told everyone Collin was dead. She added that August had also become hard to be around, and that perhaps Father had told the others as well. Collin told her that Vase wanted to learn about every religion, including the Way of Night, though Collin could not teach him the Solari faith. She went back inside. Collin judged her prayer part habit and part sincere, and saw only defeat in her.

### The Watch
Collin took the first watch with Vase, and Vaxen offered to take a later one. Collin quietly passed the [[Ring of Restless Revelation]] to Vase. Alexandra took a chair beside the hearth and kept the fire going, and when Collin urged her through the mind link to rest, she would not hand the tending of the fire over to people she called heathens.

Vaxen attuned to the blade and dreamed of glorious combat with it in hand. On his watch he spoke with it, and it told him that the greatest end was to fall in battle and be remembered, that those who lived to protect others were forgotten, and that his family and friends were weak. It urged him to lose his fear of dying. Before the night ended it told him that Auntie Dorian had tried to steer it toward another man, whom it judged too eager to live, and that it had chosen Vaxen instead.

### Otis's Dream
Otis dreamed of the apple tree. Its branches were bare, its roots rotted through, and its fruit split open with rot. The water and grass around it were fouled. Voices spoke, one saying that the first tree would fall and another that someone had lost her sight. Otis caught hold of a single green leaf that still held a faint life and swore he could save the tree. He woke with the leaf in his hand, wondering whether it was the world tree he had seen.

### Vase's Vision
Wearing the ring, Vase sank in his sleep into a cavern of obsidian and magma lit by the blue glow of Resonance Crystal. A hooded, horned figure with a crystal staff was speaking with a strange one-eyed creature, and both turned toward him. Collin, Vaxen and Otis watched Vase float slightly off the floor, the ring glowing.

The figures asked whether Vase had found his way to them, then said he was not the one they had spoken with before, a person they called Aurum. Understanding their speech through magic, Vase gave his name and said he wanted to understand their culture and Terramancy, and that Aurum was pursuing his own ventures. They spoke of a compass, known to Aurum, that would point the way to them, and said they had hidden it in the Library, deep below, with their notes. The notes given to the Duke, they said, had been given freely; the rest he would have to find in the Library. They hoped to work together to heal the world. Vase's understanding faltered as they spoke of restoring the world and making peace, and their whispers crawled into his mind until the vision broke. Vaxen caught him as he fell back. Vase later told the others that the figures had known Collin as the Aurum boy.

### The Compass and the Island
When the party woke, they weighed what the vision meant. Twilight asked Alexandra whether she had come across a compass. She said she had found a small relic, no longer than her arm, and had sent it by her bat familiar, Isiah, to the nearest [[House Aurum]] safehouse in the city. By now it would be with the house's agents and heading to her father, [[High Baron Magnus Aurum|Magnus Aurum]], along with the rest of what she had gathered. It was already lost, she said, and she went back to staring at the fire. Collin remembered the shard of earth the [[The Terramancers|Terramancers]] had once pointed out to him.

Twilight argued against chasing the compass now: better to return to the island, where Alexandra's knowledge and Sylas might help. Cletus recalled his idea of a device that would let the lair navigate with the ring as an anchor, and said he had read part of a book mentioning Aboleths, including the oldest known Aboleth, who lived in the Western Oceans, where there may once have been water before the Shattering. Twilight proposed that Collin's patron might know more about the old world, and the party agreed to go to the island and visit him.

### Back to the Island
Over breakfast Auntie Dorian told the party that silence is the greatest teacher. She took the golden tooth that [[The Ferryman]] had given them and threw it into the water to summon him, then told Otis not to lose his leaf. The Ferryman soon arrived, joked with the party a little on the ride, and dropped them at their island. After so long away, the party found the place swampier than when they left, its grassy hills turned muddier.
