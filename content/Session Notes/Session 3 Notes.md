## Summary
### The Acid Pit
Trapped within the hazardous materials hold of _The Lead Belly_, the party faced an immediate threat as the "Bio-Cleanse" protocol initiated. Acid began to flood the chamber from the ceiling and floor, accompanied by the release of two Gelatinous Cubes that attacked the group.

While the party fought for survival against the rising acid and the oozes, [[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe]] argued with [[Lady Dagna Cuprum]] on the deck above. In a desperate bid to protect the ship and its passengers, Arabella sacrificed her payment for the delivery, successfully convincing [[House Cuprum]] to depart. The party managed to defeat the oozes, recovering two emerald "ooze cores" valued at approximately 500 gold pieces each.
### Forgeries and Suspicion
Following the battle, [[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe|Captain Arabella]] questioned the group regarding their access to the Charity Ball, specifically asking if they possessed invitations. When the group denied having them, Arabella grew suspicious, wary that they were concealing information from her after she had sacrificed a significant sum of money for their safety.

[[Vase the Fragile]] attempted to smooth relations by offering one of the recovered ooze cores to the Captain. Taking advantage of the distraction, Vase slipped into Arabella's quarters to search for papers related to [[House Cuprum]]. Although Arabella nearly caught him, Vase managed to memorize the signature on the document before leaving. Utilizing this information, Vase successfully forged documents stating the party is a group in need of charity, signed by [[House Cuprum]]. In addition, [[Collin McCambridge]] created a an impeccably-forged invitation from [[House Aurum]].
### The Voyage to the Spire
To further distract [[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe|Captain Arabella]], the group encouraged [[Vaxen the Unkillable]] to speak with her. This resulted in a prolonged interaction where Arabella appeared to attempt to seduce [[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]], who remained oblivious to her advances and instead lamented the tragic history of his family.

During the subsequent long rest, the party set watches:
- [[Vase the Fragile]] and [[Otis Thulu]] discussed Otis's religious views regarding the World Tree.
- [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] and [[Twilight Emberrest]] heard the distinct sound of ropes tightening and swaying for sixty seconds.
- [[Cletus Ironplow]] hallucinated the sounds of farm animals before falling asleep on his watch.

## Loot & Acquisitions
- **Ooze Core:** An emerald worth 500 GP recovered from the Gelatinous Cubes. Originally two were recovered, but one was given to [[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe|Captain Arabella]] as recompense for her lost payment.
