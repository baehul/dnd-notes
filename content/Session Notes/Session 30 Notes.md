## Summary

### Home to a Swampier Island
The party returned to its island with [[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn|Sylas]] still sealed in his crystal shell. The place had taken on the look of the swamp it had been moored beside: the grassy hills had gone darker and muddier, and thick vines had begun to creep up the sides of the tower. [[Lady Alexandra Aurum|Alexandra]] did not need carrying. She trudged through the mud on her own while the party, kept above it, bore Sylas inside.

In the tower, [[Bloos]] was sitting before the display case that held the [[Harmony Engine]], gazing at it as he had all week. He admitted he had spent the time eating every piece of clothing in the rooms he could open, and [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] said he had brought sweaters from his dorm for him. [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] and [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] agreed it was time to leave the sinking city behind, and Otis asked the Harmony Engine to part the island from the other and float away. It did so cleanly, though the line between the two islands had grown so blurred that the party could not be sure it had not carried off a piece of the other. Alexandra asked Collin for a room where she might retire, and he took her to his own.

### Bloos and the Shell
Bloos soon forgot the Engine and stared at the shell around Sylas. To the party it looked like plain resonance crystal, but Bloos saw a beautiful glow and many layers in it, and colours of blue and gold around the knife. Delighted, he asked to keep it in his room. When the party let him touch it, a sound like cracking glass rang out, and he remarked that it was very fragile.

Cletus, who thought himself good at pulling knives out of people, looked closer at the wound and saw it was not so simple. Drawing the knife might close the surface wound, but Sylas would still be left stuporous with deep trauma, and the stasis around him was a far more complex system than a knife in a back.

### Three Stages
Working it out would take three stages: first, understanding what was happening with the shell and the knife; second, separating the two and clearing away the shell; and third, steadying the knife and drawing it out. It would take days, and more hands would make it faster. The party agreed it could not return to the Library, since [[High Inquisitor Varius]] would kill them if they went back. Cletus pointed out that they had left Sylas's brother, [[Mulvin Thistlethorn]], a prisoner of Varius and the [[Ecclesia Solari]], and argued that they would have to leave him for the moment, since marching up to Varius would only cost them Sylas. The party agreed to wait for Alexandra before deciding how to free him.

The party remembered that Bloos could perceive magic and auras, which might help if his attention could be kept on the shell. [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] argued that since the island was already under way, they might as well begin. The island drifted until the sinking city dropped out of sight, then settled to rest and wait for instruction.

### Two Names
While Collin was away with Alexandra, [[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]] raised something with the others. Collin's true identity had been in the air for a day or so, and nobody knew his birthday. Twilight thought learning it a fair first step toward knowing him. Vase, who now knew the party had travelled for weeks beside an Aurum, did not take the idea kindly. He argued that Collin had put the party and their loved ones in danger by hiding it, and that someone might have recognised him at the [[Sun Spire]] or the [[Monastery of Night]]. Twilight urged the others to understand Collin's reasons before judging him, and Vase concluded that his dealings with Collin would be strictly business from then on. Cletus observed that the party was in as much trouble for having stolen the Harmony Engine as for unknowingly travelling with an Aurum.

Bloos wept as he told his own story. His home island had fallen apart, and he had flown away carrying only himself, his clothes, his staff and all the sweaters he could manage. He admitted he had tried to get into each party member's room during the week, and that a giant named Mungus had once given him a sweater without knowing it. He said too that he had once been another person before being cursed into this form, so he had no birthday as such. He had learned the library was very good at producing things, and by tossing in a coin he made a small pastry appear. Bloos offered to arrange a surprise party while the others spoke to Collin, and Twilight told him to prepare materials in case Collin wanted one. The party went to find Collin.

### A Talk in Collin's Room
Alexandra sat staring into the fire in Collin's room as the party approached. Collin, asked his birthday, said he did not know when it was. He, Callum and Cole had celebrated a day, but he did not really call it a birthday. The others traded their own birthdays and small stories: Cletus said he had been conceived during a Harvest Festival, Vase recalled exchanging small gifts, and Vaxen asked what a birthday was. Collin's hair and eyes were still gold. Twilight then spoke earnestly. He said he was a strange person to lead such a talk, but the party was too deep in this together to part now, and it was all right to be upset or to disagree. He asked that anyone with grievances air them, so they might understand one another better.

Asked why he had not told them sooner, Collin said he had hoped his suspicion about a certain woman was wrong, and that when her voice made it undeniable, he had wanted to confront and kill her. He had not named her, in case she overheard. Now he was no longer sure that was what he wanted. He added that the party might be better protected without him, especially with Alexandra here, since his father did not let go of what he controlled, and that his patron's shadowing was the only reason he was still alive. As [[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]] puffed out his chest, he heard a voice in his head: [[The Restless Blade]] told him that someone was relying on him to die on that person's behalf. Vaxen asked aloud whether it wanted him to die for someone, but the blade gave no further answer.

### The Patron's Eye
Collin warned that his patron was unhappy with Otis. Otis had squashed an eye that was meant to summon Collin, though Otis protested that it had been a strange, unnatural eye inside a crocodile skull. The patron had invited Otis in particular, disliked people knowing his identity, and might be displeased if more people came than he had asked for. The party decided that all of them would go. Vase said he helped Collin because it was right and Collin was his friend, but that the lies had hurt him, and he added that he had an uncle to return to and that the party were among the first few people he had ever met.

Twilight pressed Collin to consider what it meant to bring down the Aurum family: killing them, or foiling their plans by helping the people they hurt. He reminded him that he had brought his sister along. From behind Collin's door, Alexandra asked to step outside to conduct her prayers, saying she had been a prisoner since the day of her birth. Vase told her she was free and not a prisoner. She went down the stairs, and the party heard Bloos shout surprise and then apologise, since she was not Collin. She walked on, and the door opened and closed behind her.

### The Ninth Child
Collin took Vase aside, out of Alexandra's hearing, to say what he had not wanted her to feel bad about. He was the ninth child, and a time stasis had been used just before his birth so that his birthday would fall on the ninth day of the ninth month. His other siblings all shared a birthday on the third day of the third month, and only his older brother [[Lord Romauld Aurum|Romauld]], the third son, and Collin himself had had their birthdays celebrated. Vase thanked him for telling him and said he was sorry.

Vase joked about Twilight's soapbox speeches, then said he thought Twilight was doing well. Collin said he did not know how to answer how much kinder the party was being to him than he thought he deserved. Vase offered him a saying of the old path about redemption and told him he was worth another chance. They agreed to speak again later.

### A Surprise
Twilight had Bloos change his decorations, and the sign was crossed out until only Collin's name remained. When Collin came downstairs, Bloos and the party called out surprise. Vase tossed a few coins into the air and a cake appeared. Collin broke into tears, and the party told him to have some cake. When Bloos fluttered off, Collin asked in disbelief why they were all being so nice to him. Alexandra then came back with ash on her forehead, looked at Collin crying beneath the sign, announced that she was retiring to her room, and went back up.

### Setting Course
Otis said he would not go to Collin's patron unless Collin came with them, and Twilight told Collin the choice was his. Collin put his hand on the Harmony Engine, and the island began to move. As at the Monastery of Night, the Engine drew on a place Collin knew: the island was set for [[The Jade Fragment|the Jade Fragment]], the island of Collin's patron. Collin felt a pull like someone tugging on a sorrow in his chest, and the party knew the journey would take about six days.

### Collin's History
Collin gathered the party and asked that Alexandra be within earshot for all of it. He said that some of what he had told them was true, but that the most important parts were false. In the Aurum household where he had been Eobard, the McCambridges had been butlers. Cole had been his steward and Callum's father, and the man Collin thought of as his own father. Callum and Cole had been more family to him than his blood family. From a young age he and his siblings had been tormented by surgeries and experiments that imbued resonance crystals into their skin. The Aurum family was the family of magic, and the practice had run through the house since the Shattering, though no one spoke of its side effects. Collin had lost control of his magic, which infuriated his father, who thought him not trying hard enough. His father killed Callum and Cole, believing they were stunting his magic and his growth.

Later Collin learned that his father was planning something terrible for him and was speaking with members of the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]]. He did not know whether his father meant to be rid of him or to imbue him with more crystals, but he ran, slipping off on a GSC ship. Around then his visions began, and they led him to his patron's island. He begged the patron to help him bring down his family, and the patron brought him into [[The Resistance]].

### Alexandra's Reply
Alexandra stood listening, still faintly vacant, and a few tears fell though her face stayed still. When he was done she spoke: the Aurum children were given what they were told was the privilege of higher power, and it affected each of them in a different way. She was glad to know the church had not betrayed or poisoned her, as she had thought, though it meant she had been wrong to betray the church. Again, she said, she had failed the only family she knew. She would go back to her room, which she supposed was Eobard's. She looked at his golden hair and eyes and remarked that it was astonishing a fool like him had escaped their father's notice wandering about with such colours, and wondered whether the family was perhaps not so special as it believed.

### Alexandra's Door
Bloos came down to say that a few sweaters had made him feel better, and to ask whether the party wanted help with the shell, since webs of cracks were running through it. Twilight called Collin, and the two went looking for Alexandra. It did not take long: a door marked with an A had appeared upstairs, smelling of incense, in a room that had not existed before. Alexandra answered in Aurum pajamas, her intricate hair undone. When Collin asked what she had done to the man, she said she had stabbed him with a knife and that a shell of crystalline stasis had formed around him on its own. She said she had stolen the knife from Varius, who had meant to stab Sylas himself, and that she had believed it an ordinary knife. She then asked to go back to sleep and closed the door.

### Runes in the Crystal
The party gathered around Sylas. Twilight studied the cracking crystal and the knife and saw that small layers could be peeled from the shell like the shell of a boiled egg, and that doing it correctly would free the area around the knife. Vaxen, who had found some very large sweaters in his room, dangled them before Bloos and bargained that he could have them if he concentrated on the shell, and Bloos agreed. He called out hidden runes and strings of magic joining the shell to the knife, and with that the first stage was passed.

### The Shell Rearranges
Collin used what Bloos had pointed out to pick at the runes, and crystal began to fall to dust around Sylas. But as it loosened, the shell reverberated with a tone that gave Collin a piercing headache, and then a discordant hum filled the air. It would take music to dull it. Vase tried an instrument, but he could not hit the right note even with Cletus's help, and the shell shifted and rearranged itself.

### Alexandra's Wardrobe
At dawn the next day Alexandra was outside praying, looking a little more composed, and told Collin she needed new clothes. Collin offered a little gold to the library and asked it to provide some, but nothing happened. He sensed that the clothing was far too costly and that the library simply did not like her, and that the room was the full extent of the hospitality she would get. Twilight, thinking of his wife, [[Dawn]], offered the skin-tight bodysuit he had bought for her, and Alexandra recoiled with disgust. Collin left with her to wash their clothes, and Alexandra called Vaxen servant boy and ordered him to wash hers, which he did not take well.

### The Cradle
Otis, lent a set of carpenter's tools by Cletus and strengthened by him, built a sturdy cradle to hold the shell together while the party took it apart. Vase, with Cletus's help, loudly yelled at exactly the frequency needed to still the vibration, and Twilight picked apart the remaining sections until the shell held only the pieces around the knife. Sylas stirred.

### The Knife
Twilight found that the knife had struck Sylas's spine, so that drawing it could do worse damage than leaving it, and the party knew any slip would now be worse. Vase calmed Sylas through his half-conscious state, Collin linked minds with him to soothe him, and Vaxen held him steady in the cradle. Cletus gingerly drew the knife with Collin guiding his hand. The shell shattered and fell to dust, and the knife crumbled into resonance dust in Cletus's hand. A single slip left Sylas bleeding from the back. Twilight steadied him and guarded him against slipping away, and Cletus closed some of the wound. Sylas lay unconscious but alive and stable, and the party could not tell when he would wake. They collected the dust from the shell, and Cletus collapsed from the strain.

### A Guest in the Library
When the party woke, they found Sylas sprawled across a library table amid a great deal of blood, and no one remembered moving him to a bed. Downstairs, Bloos was chatting happily with [[Lancaster Slickwhistle]], who sat with an espresso martini in the library at an hour few people kept. He greeted them warmly as old acquaintances and remarked that it had been almost two weeks. Cletus asked how he kept finding their island, but Lancaster did not answer. Instead he asked how much of the outside world they had heard.

### The Armistice
Lancaster told them the Crusade was asking the Resistance to sign a small armistice, a kind of ceasefire. Representatives of the [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|Cult of Night]], the [[Ancient Ones]] and the [[Blood Horde]] were on their way, though the Horde's delegates came from a disparate fringe faction rather than from [[Omeez Tzikhe]], since its main Tribal Council had not approved. The Crusade would send token representation, though the church and the upper houses had been insistent. A businessman with friends on both sides was brokering the peace, and one of the Ancient Ones had offered a neutral base: [[Silver Moon]]. The meeting was ceremonial and would happen within the month. The Blood Horde disliked the ceasefire because its lands were not returned, and a makeshift group of orcs, goliaths and other bands would stand in as its representatives. Vase feared that gathering so many leaders on one island was dangerous, with island-moving weapons being built for warfare, and Collin disliked it too. The party was about ten days from Operation Green Thunder.

### Varius and the Ecclesia
Lancaster said the church seemed to be moving in an interesting direction, that a High Inquisitor of some sort had asked for the pause, and that he himself had not been allowed into the upper houses since the ceasefire. Cletus said the party had met a very high-ranking Inquisitor and asked why Varius would hire them to fetch Sylas just before a push for peace. Lancaster said it was strange that Varius had spoken to the party, gone back to the [[Ecclesia Solari]], and asked for the war to pause while he completed an important investigation.

The Cult of Night had put Lancaster on temporary leave, he said, because someone in the Resistance had objected to the investigations he was pursuing among the Ancient Ones. He thought it no longer mattered. He hinted that [[Sendryr Ogami|Sendryr]] was cautious about antagonising anyone.

### Lancaster Moves In
Lancaster complimented Collin's new hairstyle and asked whether he might wait for the party in their library and make it his base while he looked into private matters of his own in the evenings. Vase invited him to stay without charge. When Vase asked whether he would fetch some rare components, powdered moonstone and drawn silver wire, Lancaster declined, with admiration for the initiative. He turned back to Bloos, who began telling him of Mungus. Collin said Alexandra was staying with them for the time being, and went to talk to her.
