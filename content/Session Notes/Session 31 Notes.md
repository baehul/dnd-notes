## Summary

### A Garden Divided
The island drifted on toward [[The Jade Fragment]], and the party settled into a few quiet days of downtime. [[Professor Sylas Thistlethorn|Sylas]] still lay unconscious on the library table where he had been freed, and [[Lancaster Slickwhistle]] was lounging in the library, put on the shelf by the [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|Cult of Night]] while hostilities were paused. He said he would be gone by the end of the day.

[[Otis Thulu|Otis]] spent the day spreading seeds across the island, with [[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]] helping and then working out beside him. Still inspired by the swamp the island had been moored beside, Otis let his growth run wild, and it crept into the tidy experimental plot where [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] was testing irrigation methods for the days when the party would be away. Cletus cut back everything that crossed into his section along a straight line and spent the day building a fence. When the two argued about it later in front of Sylas, Otis insisted that his plants would help Cletus's and that touching biomes did no harm, and Cletus insisted the fence stay.

### Two Recipes
The party went over its magic item recipes. [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] wanted to put his time into the Marionette's Fang, which was meant for him in particular. [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] argued that the Self-Mending Desk was worth having for all of them, since it could bring even ancient tomes back from ruin. The materials were costly and the Jade Fragment would offer no bustling shop to buy them from, so the party agreed to pay for them rather than wait. The Armor of Acid Resistance was judged too niche, and the Pearl of Power was not a priority. The library conjured a plain silver stickpin for Collin at no cost.

Collin soaked the large tooth of a puppeteer parasite in nerve-quieting oil until its writhing nerves lay still, then used jeweler's tools to deepen the grooves in the tooth. Vase, with carpenter's tools lent by Cletus, began work on the desk by easing its old binding, and Cletus said he would help finish it the next morning.

### The Noon Prayer
Around noon [[Lady Alexandra Aurum|Alexandra]] stepped out of the library to pray to [[Solaris and The Solari Faith|Solaris]]. Vase, who had asked her whether the Solari must convert the unknowing, asked instead to learn how she prayed. She was reluctant and a little exasperated, but she lit incense in her palm with golden fire and spoke the prayer in Celestial while he copied her stillness. When he rubbed soil on his forehead, she brushed it off and marked him with ash instead, telling him never to use soil when he should use ash. He fumbled the words of the response, and she gave him the right one before returning to her room.

### A Visitor in the Library
[[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]] spent the morning tending Sylas. He found that Sylas had lost a great deal of blood and that his spine was damaged, though healing might wake him by evening. While he worked, Lancaster wandered over and asked about his friend. Twilight said the gnome was a researcher who might know about Terramancy and the [[Harmony Engine]], and that both [[House Aurum]] and the church had been after him. Lancaster remarked that the church was not an upper house but its own thing, rather like the Cult of Night if it worshipped the sun and were far stricter. He added that Alexandra looked somewhat familiar and that Collin's changed look suited him better.

Alone with Lancaster, Twilight asked what he made of the Resistance, mentioning the [[Blood Horde]] raid on ships. Lancaster had heard rumblings of it and thought it fortunate that it came before the ceasefire. Twilight called the Resistance shattered and fragmented, and Lancaster called it a three-headed dragon whose heads would not agree. Twilight asked how he coped, and Lancaster passed on advice from an old partner: working with a team is miserable, which is why he worked alone, but working alone is worse. He said he had tried to talk sense into [[Sendryr Ogami|Sendryr]], who would jump at any chance for peace, and hoped to return with more definite information. When Twilight said the party was heading toward the [[Ancient Ones]], Lancaster warned that each one was the most self-serving being he had ever met, even those who claimed otherwise. He advised finding out what the one in question wanted and helping it, since they cared more about balancing a ledger than about kindness or malice, and an Ancient One in one's debt was priceless.

### The Blade at Practice
Vaxen asked the library for something to practice on, and paper cutouts appeared hanging from the branches. As he hacked at them, his sword spoke. It said it liked being used, that its bearers often died while it lived on, and that it could not die in battle unless someone bore it into one. Its energy slowly drained as he slashed. It felt a pull toward Otis, who was busy with a tree, and said he would give it a good fight. Vaxen turned flustered and tried to keep it focused on the dummies. Later he noticed more books in the library than before, many in the style of upper-house volumes and some written in Celestial, and he and the blade argued over whether practice fights counted. He threw the blade to the ground in a huff, felt its curse call him back, and apologized.

### The Marionette's Fang
That evening Collin finished the Marionette's Fang. As he worked, he felt the light fade from his eyes and the resonance crystals in his arms siphon out through his fingers into the fang. As his strength failed, a spark of green ran through his whole body, and a green psionic blade took shape at the fang's edge.

### Seeds and Cellars
Otis decided to graft the little green leaf from his dream onto a tree rather than plant it in soil. He nestled the stem into a notch in one of the trees growing in the library and prayed that the children of the World Tree had taken from the tree and now wished to give back. It would take a few days to be sure, but it showed no sign of trouble and looked as though it might be growing. By the next day it had fully taken hold and looked more vibrant than the rest of the tree, with a little insect life around it.

Cletus, wishing for a windowless room to experiment in, found a rusty door under vines at the base of the tower, with old stone stairs leading down into a lightless cellar. He began an experiment growing plants and seeds there, and fungus and soft purple glowing crystals appeared to light the room. He meant to return and see what came of it.

### Putting Sylas to Bed
Twilight told the others that he had healed Sylas partway and that he would wake by evening. Since it only affected when, he chose not to heal him further to wake him early, and he carried Sylas up to a proper bed. As he did, a small gnome-sized door with an F on it appeared. The party looked at the Pearl of Power recipe, which they thought might be made from the dust and sand left of the crystal shell, but the pearl itself had to be found and the library could not produce it, so they set it aside. Lancaster's emerald car was seen near the tower, and by the next morning it and Lancaster were gone.

### The Desk Installed
The next morning Cletus bought the materials and finished the desk with Vase. Cletus did the construction and the magical enchantment, while Vase handled most of the looks: flames and dragons, like a decal on a toy car. Once it was installed in the library, a tiling effect rippled through the tower and a hum showed the desk had joined the lair.

### Sylas Wakes
A small voice called from Sylas's room. The room had grown larger for company, the bed was raised a little, and a wheelchair stood in the corner. Sylas was a nervous gnome, closer to middle-aged and a little on the older side, in a blood-stained smock like an archaeologist's. He did not know where he was or who these people were. Twilight introduced himself, and Collin read Sylas's thoughts to get past his suspicion. Sylas remembered meeting his brother that morning to go into the [[The Library of Layers|Library]], after which his memories were a wash of static. Collin judged them lost to the damage and the stasis rather than to magic.

Twilight gave the big picture first: people tied to the upper crust had wanted Sylas's information, had pretended to be his brother, had gone into the Library with him, and had trapped him in stasis. When Twilight said upper crust, Sylas's gaze fixed on Collin for a moment. Sylas worked it out aloud, that the bad guys had gone in with him, put him in stasis, left, and then hired the party to find him. Twilight called that the party's working theory and said there might have been a betrayal among them. Sylas asked for [[Mulvin Thistlethorn]], and Twilight admitted his brother had been taken. Collin sensed that the [[Ecclesia Solari]] held him, and Sylas cried out that it was all terrible.

Sylas had been researching Terramancy, but he could not see why that mattered enough to hurt anyone. When Twilight told him the rooms holding his research had been collapsed and the books destroyed, he said he would never have taken the money had he known. Because Sylas was in abject terror, Twilight calmed him, touching him near his scar so he would feel the healing. Sylas then explained that he had been a history professor until a donor to his university offered a very generous grant, after which he became a finder of antiquities. One day a large sum of money and a letter had appeared on his desk, asking him to look into Terramancy and the Terramancers, to find any artifacts or writings they had left behind, and to reach the sender through a drop-box of the [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|Golden Sails]]. He had never met the sender. Collin glimpsed in his mind a well-dressed rat-folk gentleman with a top hat and cane, who did not seem dangerous. Sylas also said that the people before the Shattering were more advanced than anyone thought, but lacked the resonance crystals that the Shattering exposed.

Twilight healed Sylas's legs a little, and Sylas said he could not feel them. Twilight offered him the party's own research, urged him to rest and heal, and said the party would keep him safe and supply what he needed, and that he could leave whenever he chose. When Sylas asked to be dropped back in the [[Sinking City]] for his appointment, Twilight refused, calling it unsafe. Twilight said the party did not know where Mulvin was, only that whoever held him kept crossing their path, and Sylas said he would help however he could if they got Mulvin back.

### A Tour of the Island
Twilight told Sylas that the island ran on Terramancy and was moving along a ley line, then took him out to see. Sylas saw [[Bloos]] staring at the Harmony Engine and supposed Bloos was the one casting the Terramancy. The party said he only liked the light, and Sylas stayed puzzled by how closely Bloos seemed tied to the artifact. Collin muttered that it might prove useful later to start claiming Bloos was the only pivotal one.

The party gave Sylas copies of its notes and let him read outside, where quills and paper appeared for him whenever he reached for them. He watched Otis's wild growth, took it for a form of Terramancy that made small biomes, and was delighted. Cletus, introduced as the head arcanist, offered to upgrade his wheelchair. Sylas said he did not think his companion Winfrey could manage chairs well, then pulled out a small metal-coated orb that unfolded into an owl automaton about Cletus's size. He had found it on one of his expeditions, and it came from before the Shattering. Winfrey could carry him from one island to another. Cletus looked it over and thought a harness could be built to carry the chair, which would take about a day and some equipment, and Winfrey gave a recorded hoot in which Cletus sensed some intelligence. Sylas said that with his brother around he would be safer and could gather rarer finds, but for now he would stick to smaller antiquities.

### Vase and the Professor
Vase introduced himself to Sylas, who took him for a research assistant. Vase explained that the library could produce books a person had read, and Sylas pulled one down, an artificers' magazine he had read before. Vase asked about pre-Shattering history and mentioned that his uncle was around then, but Sylas said he was still fuzzy and had not done research in a long time. He told Vase that the lead of a lab only seemed intimidating, and that Vase might run his own lab one day. Twilight then took Sylas aside for a talk about his health. He told him he had been found in stasis with a dagger in his spine. Injuries to the spine usually meant never walking again, but stasis might have preserved his body, so Twilight could not rule out his walking. He believed Sylas would, though not soon.

### A Talk in Alexandra's Room
That evening Collin knocked on Alexandra's door. She made him wait a moment, then received him sitting upright on the edge of her bed. He asked whether she had gone into the Library for House Aurum or for the church. She said she had known Terramancy was being used in the Crusade's efforts, that her family had been cut out of them because of the failures of her uncle, [[Duke Ephraim Aurum|Ephraim]], and that her father was desperate to return to the race. House Cuprum had been given command of the [[Sun Spire]]. She said the houses had been asked to put forward a weapon, that [[House Argentum]] had not been interested, and that Ephraim had had nothing to show but a few notes. She said her father had given away what little power Ephraim had left, and that Ephraim now sailed the islands drinking.

She said that she had believed the church was poisoning her and that its people had already used her name to get into the event, which had been part of why she turned from them. Collin had since shown her that she had thrown away her ties because of a misunderstanding, though they had abused her connection long before. She had seen a chance to win back her father's good graces by delivering a victory for House Aurum. She told Collin that she had gone into the Library disguised as Sylas's brother Mulvin, the gnome who went in with him, and that her spur-of-the-moment plan could have been better thought out. She had sent what she could not understand, the artifacts and writings, with her familiar Isaiah directly to the House Aurum safehouse in the Sinking City, and kept what she could understand, while Sylas read what he could. Collin let slip that he had seen something of what had happened between her and the gnome, without saying how or mentioning Vase.

### Old Resentments
Collin asked about her magic. She said she was no fighter and had learned a variety of protective magics to keep herself safe, but that they had grown rigid and less responsive to her. She was sure it was nothing once the poison left her body, though there was no poison. Collin said he had run when he overheard that his father meant to do something to him with the Golden Sails. She said she had not been given the special training he got, and that Collin's father had bestowed gifts on him that the other children had not received. Collin said gifts was not how he would put it, called [[Lord Romauld Aurum|Romauld]] terrible, and asked whether a healthy relationship with a father would produce someone like him. She said he had been born lucky and not in the way the other children were, and that she had borne all the consequences of being an Aurum child without the privileges, respect or adoration he received. Collin said he had not chosen the role forced on him, and that he had not known her familiar was a bat or its name.

He admitted that he had been ready to kill her in the Library, that he no longer thought he blamed her, and he apologized. He said his father had taught him to see the other children as competition standing between him and greatness, and he now saw that this had been his father's view put on him. She called him Eobard and said she was not ready to forgive him and that some part of her would always resent that he had been special and she had not. Collin told her that at the next island she could reach, she was free to leave, or she could stay. She said the Library was the best place for her to hide for now, since she had found no clean way to hide golden hair and eyes without potions or constant concentration, and she had spent days trying to pass as a gnome. Then she asked why Collin kept company with Otis and the others, whom she called things, and wondered whether the orc would eat her. Collin told her to stop calling them that: some he kept for protection, some because they were useful or entertaining, and Otis was the nicest person he had ever met. He urged her to try one real conversation with Otis, and suggested she join the party's shared suppers if she wanted a hot meal.

### Twilight and Vaxen
At the end of that evening the party knew it would likely land the next day. In the morning Twilight went to see Vaxen, who said his sword was talking to him and guiding him. Vaxen introduced Twilight to the blade as the one who kept urging them not to die, and the blade answered that it would bear that in mind. Twilight asked whether the sword wanted Vaxen to die. Vaxen said it might be his destiny.

Twilight said they were probably nearing Collin's patron that day, that there was a ceasefire with the Resistance, and that he had seen the Blood Horde. He asked Vaxen whether he was the leader. Vaxen said Twilight had assigned him the role, so he was, and Twilight had to agree. Twilight confessed he worried about problems that were not physical and asked whether he could rely on Vaxen for those too. Vaxen said any problem could be solved with violence and looked to his sword for approval. Twilight sighed that someone else might be better suited to such problems, and left.

### A Lady Wants Breakfast
Downstairs Twilight found Alexandra sitting stiffly at a table. He got her name wrong, and she corrected him: it was Alexandra, or simply My Lady. She said she had had nothing but tea and biscuits for days and asked for a proper meal. Collin, watching from above, came down to help her look for food. They knocked on the doors of the island's two gardeners, Cletus and Otis. She noted that Otis had a very large door and Cletus a small one. She addressed Cletus as dwarf, observed that no fruit had been set out, and demanded the gardeners fulfill their duties.

The party agreed to cook breakfast together. Cletus offered barley, Otis and Twilight went foraging, and Alexandra was asked to find a table. She was appalled to be set to laying plates and silver, which she called a servant's job, but she agreed to hold the plates. Vase and Bloos were in a corner, where Vase was throwing coins to conjure sweaters from the library and feeding them to Bloos to see how many he could eat. Collin told her he preferred Collin and did not need the honorifics of a family that did not care for him. She said she was still a lady.

At the table she ate daintily and praised the breakfast to Twilight and his staff. She also praised Cletus's spread, which included potatoes he had not planted. She asked Otis to say the giant's rhyme from fairy tales, which she said Collin had told her was his favorite, and Otis said it, though he had never said it before. Collin proposed that the party call her Lady Alexandra if she preferred, and Otis agreed. Vaxen was confused about what titles meant.

Twilight suggested everyone give their titles. Vase said his family name was Argilla. Collin said he was technically Lord Eobard Lucidean Aurum, heir to the Aurum name and ninth-born son, but asked the party not to call him that. Cletus said he was Cletus Ironplow the 14th, which he rarely mentioned. Alexandra said Collin had many more titles, and told them how he used to puff out his chest when they were announced, and how he hung about with a steward boy. Asked by Vase about a pirate captain, she said she thanked her stars she had never met him, and that an Aurum escort of ships would defeat him. She said the Argentum were weak, dainty elves who lacked cunning and strength. When Vaxen did not use her title, she warned him that unless he addressed her properly he would not be heard. When the table turned to prayer, Alexandra refused to lead such a company of unbelievers and night-folk in prayer, saying it would defile a sacred rite, and went to her room.

### Sylas at the Stairs
Otis asked the Harmony Engine to help, and grooves appeared in the stairs so that Sylas could ramp his chair down. The party thought the lair itself was accommodating him. Sylas asked to join them, said he avoided the grumpy lady upstairs, and asked to sit outside to watch the island land. He said he would not go far in any case, since the slope was too much for his chair. Twilight mentally told the Harmony Engine that Sylas could not leave the island. Sylas was interested in seeing the docking, but Cletus decided not to give him the harness yet, so that he would get no ideas about adventuring on this particular island.

### The Jade Island
Collin asked whether to leave Alexandra behind without telling her anything, though she would very likely wander onto the island. Vase argued that they should take her, since the party might find a way to disguise her too and he could learn more from her, and Collin agreed that she was safest with them. Twilight asked whether she might be desperate enough to accept whatever Collin accepted to hide from his family, and Collin did not answer directly. He said his patron had asked only for him and Otis, disliked strangers learning its identity, and might kill the others who came, and that the party could not stop it. Vase said he was a people person and could calm the patron down.

The island slowed like a car braking, and off in the distance appeared a jade green island covered in bamboo, with a strange fog hanging over it as if to hide whatever lived there. Collin went up to Alexandra's room and asked her to come see how he had stayed hidden. She remarked drily that they were apparently about to walk into the frightening green island. Outside, Sylas cried out in amazement as the two islands fused. The fog was impenetrable except for a few reeds and trickles of water, it muffled sound, and the wind howled through the bamboo like whispers at the edge of hearing. As Collin approached, the fog and reeds did not clear a path, and the island felt as uninviting as on his first visit. The party decided to go in together, with Alexandra.
