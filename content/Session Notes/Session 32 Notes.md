## Summary

### Fog and Spears
The party seemed to have lost its way in the fog of [[The Jade Fragment]], with [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] struggling to find a path, and [[Lady Alexandra Aurum|Alexandra]]'s magic seemed to be acting strangely. They came upon a clearing and tried to get their bearings, and it was there that the ambush began.

The party was soon deep in a fight. Grung handlers harried [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] and [[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]] with darts, drakes snapped at Collin, and a blinded dragonborn leader, [[Ozhkaar]], stumbled through the press. In the thick of it, Twilight searched the fog for anyone else in hiding and felt sure that nobody was there, Alexandra included.

Ozhkaar swung his spear blindly at Collin, but as he heard whispers in the wind he pulled the blow aside. After a short appeal to his lord, he said in Draconic that the party must lay down their weapons and come to the village, where their fate would be decided. The grung and drakes stood down, though they still ringed the party in.

### A Missing Companion
Alexandra had vanished in the ambush. Locking eyes with the leader, Collin let his psychic daggers fade and asked where his sister was, adding that [[Luwei]] would be very interested to see her, and him. The leader said that the lord would tell them where she could be found if he granted them an audience. He said she had been encased in a protective spell that he could not break, and that he had prayed to the lord to take her for him.

Collin asked whether he questioned what Luwei had told Collin to do. The leader asked why Collin had brought a party of outsiders, and a human, onto the island, and warned him to be careful. He said he had not been told who Collin was, that the island itself had shown them there were intruders, and that his patrol had been told to protect the island from them. Collin answered that someone among the party was of great interest to his patron, who did not trust that person.

### The Road to the Village
As they were marched along, Twilight healed Collin and quietly told him that he trusted him. The leader did not know Collin, and Collin knew him no better: he had met very few people on the island, which kept its people carefully apart. Quietly, as they walked, Collin passed word to the others about how to carry themselves. They were to speak of his patron only as his patron, claim to know nothing, give no name or title until they were given one, and come as humbly as they had ever come to anyone.

Whispers brushed the edge of Collin's hearing, like those of his first visit, in a language he had once been unable to hear. Alone among the party, Collin saw a branch hung with vines and moss form a toothed mouth that snapped shut over Otis as he passed beneath it, before the branch was only a branch again.

### The Village
The party came to a small village of wood and bamboo, with wooden wind chimes and carvings of draconic faces on its houses. Grung, kobolds, dragonborn and drakes lived there. As Ozhkaar led the party through it, the village fell silent and the villagers stared and whispered. He brought them to a fountain in front of a larger hut with smoke rising from its chimney, asked them to wait, and went inside while two scraggly kobold guards with small spears stared at them, curious rather than menacing.

Collin told the party that he seldom met others who served his patron, who kept his followers apart, and that the way he travelled had spared him these people. He reversed his earlier advice and urged honesty, since the patron probably already knew everything. He told [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] that his many trips to [[Mithbarakaz]] had been because Mithbarakaz was dealing with the upper crust and double-dealing both sides. The island was as large as the [[Monastery of Night]], so this was not its only village. A hunchbacked, grandmotherly kobold elder came out with Ozhkaar, leaning on a bamboo staff topped with a jade crystal. She greeted them in halting Common and said that she rarely spoke it, with so many guests. Collin offered to speak Draconic, she apologized for their difficult introduction to the village's hospitality, and he answered in Common.

### The Hall of Elders
The elder told Ozhkaar that he might return to his patrol, and noted that one of the party was missing. Ozhkaar said she had been taken in the ambush and had vanished. The elder spoke of a companion and of a sister, then plucked a hair from Collin's head. Collin said the missing woman was his sister. Ozhkaar apologized for any injuries his people had caused and said they would not cross paths again unless the lord willed it.

The feast was held in a simple hall where villagers sat on bamboo mats, and the room fell to a hush as the party entered. Collin thanked the hosts and said the party had nearly died coming in, because their warriors were so strong, and a chuckle ran through the crowd. Jade plates and cutlery were laid out, a fire was lit in a pit in the floor, and food came: berries, rat, squirrel, snake and crocodile. Collin studied the room and found no one openly hostile, though a few people seemed uninterested or were avoiding the feast. They wore the garb of tenders, personal servants of Luwei who attended him and maintained the village's bamboo groves. Collin decided to wait until a group of them talked together, then approach.

### Questions Over Supper
The villagers sat close and questioned the party, more like a panel of questioners than a social gathering. Vase juggled orbs of light and played fast music on his instrument, and an elder asked whether it was popular outside the Jade Fragment. Asked about the war, Vase said a ceasefire between [[The Crusade]] and [[The Resistance]] might be coming, though that would not mean the war ended, and he told them of a magical technology that could allow clear communication across great distances between islands. [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] told them he had invented a harness that let birds carry chairs, and when an elder held up a plate, he blasted it with lightning. The room fell quiet for a moment, then broke into applause. The elders said many powerful wizards had come from the village, gifted by the Lord with pieces of his magic.

### The Tenders
When a few tenders gathered apart, Collin made his way over. He cut the mental link with his companions and joined the minds of three tenders instead, thanking them for the show that had put his friends at ease and calling himself one of the master's eyes. They were standoffish. They said the master had not spoken of him and that he should not presume to stand at their station. Collin said he had news of the outside world for the master and told them the party had learned of magic that might let islands grow and merge, returning the world toward its earlier order, from the days when his patron had held more than this one isle. The tenders said the master would speak with him if he wished to, and that they did not point him in any direction. They said the Lord summoned them, and not the other way round, and that the Lord was the Jade Fragment itself. Their master had sent them to the village some time before, to be ready for visitors about whom he had not yet decided how he felt. Collin said the party would leave after the feast. The link with the tenders did not last.

### Ribbik
Collin went over to a young kobold tender named [[Ribbik]], who was hurrying to scarf down food and stuff more into her robes before she left. She said she was new, having served only a couple of months, and that this was not her home village. Collin said that the master would value his news and asked whether she could help get it to him. Ribbik said that something mattered deeply to the master and had made him erratic, and that she had not seen him in some time. She also said that the party was being tested, that they had upset him and that he was not happy with them, but that they had to make him happy with them to tell him what they knew. Collin thanked her, and she hurried out.

### A Gift for the Road
An elder hung on Otis's arm and asked whether he and his kin fought in the war. Otis said they did, though not really for the master. The elder, struggling with the D sound in Draconic, mangled the name of the Blood Horde into something that sounded like Blood Whores. Otis said he was from the [[Blood Horde]], and steered him away from the hordes part.

As the elder spoke, Collin alone saw the ground sink and open into a maw that would have swallowed Otis whole, before it vanished. An angry whisper and a buzzing came with it, at the edge of his hearing. The elders gave the party a stick with live rats tied to it by the tails, for the road. The elder who had brought them in then bid them goodbye. As the party walked out, she gave Collin a hair that glowed gold-green and told him that it should help. When he closed his fist around it, it tugged lightly in one direction, like a compass. The party began to make its way out of the village.
