## Summary
### The Gala Descent

[[Lancaster Slickwhistle]] abruptly proceeded downstairs to the charity gala after a dry gin martini seemed to restore him to his health. After briefly losing track of him, the party located him wearing a mask and dancing within the crowd. [[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]] and [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] entered the party, while other party members attempted to distract them from approaching [[Lancaster Slickwhistle|Lancaster]]. [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] gathered gossip from the attendees, noting an inside joke originating from [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]]'s speech. [[Lancaster Slickwhistle|Lancaster]] instructed the group to meet at the workshop door in two minutes, taking advantage of a guard break. [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] noted the strange absence of the [[Duke Ephraim Aurum|Duke Ephraim]] as well as [[Wolfram Zoloto]], his head of security, and began to grow suspicious.

### The Downstairs Workshop

The party stealthily descended into the workshop. A scrying crystal orb acting as a surveillance camera was bypassed when [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] cast an illusion over it. While [[Lancaster Slickwhistle|Lancaster]] hurriedly collected his belongings, the party looted the room and recovered numerous magical artifacts. [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] discovered notes detailing the [[Harmony Engine]]. [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] found a magical bag that had a large number of potions as well as some artificing notes. He also, found [[Lancaster Slickwhistle|Lancaster]]'s magical focus. [[Lancaster Slickwhistle|Lancaster]], beginning to grow suspicious as well, informed the group that their timeframe was shorter than anticipated, prompting a hasty escape down the scrapyard freight elevator.

As the party left, [[Vaxen the Unkillable|Vaxen]] was stopped by [[Lord August Aurum]], reminding him of their duel later that night. [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] narrowly avoided an encounter with him as they continued on towards the scrapyard.

### The Scrapyard Ambush

Returning to the scrapyard, the party noted how empty the scrapyard was and couldn't shake the feeling they were being watched or followed. [[Lancaster Slickwhistle|Lancaster]] took the party to where he originally hid the [[Harmony Engine]], a junk pile where his ship had crash landed, but found the junk pile was missing. [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] used a detect magic spell to following the strong magical essence of the [[Harmony Engine]] to another nearby junk pile. There, the party began to dig for the [[Harmony Engine]], however, [[Wolfram Zoloto]] seemed to already be waiting for them and combat initiated.
## Loot & Acquisitions
- A collection of [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]]'s notes on the [[Harmony Engine]]
- A variety of yet unidentified magic items
