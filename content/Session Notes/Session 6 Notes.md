## Summary
### The Getaway

The party successfully uncovered the escape vehicle and the [[Harmony Engine]]. During the fight, [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] experienced a vision similar to the one he experienced in [[Session 2 Notes|Session 2]], hearing the voice of [[High Baron Magnus Aurum]] telling him "Welcome home son.". He glowed with golden light and [[Wolfram Zoloto]] began to laugh manically. The party executed a daring getaway from the Sunspire as Wolfram self-destructed.

### Deciphering the Intel

[[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] and [[Twilight Emberrest|Twilight]] determined they need to question [[Lancaster Slickwhistle|Lancaster]] regarding his intelligence on the events at the Sunspire, specifically concerning a planned demonstration. The party also discussed mentions of a traitor operating within [[The Resistance]]. During this time, [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] seemed distant and distracted.

### The Harmony Engine Notes

The party reviewed the recovered notes pertaining to the [[Harmony Engine]]. The notes detailed [[The Terramancers|Terramancy]], an ancient and outdated study focused on the shaping of the earth. The party noted that while [[Professor Jitterhop]] researches this field, the engine's mechanics might exceed his capabilities, and Duke Ephraim Aurum is not known to be a researcher of Terramancy.

The notes, which included text in Primordial, indicated that the [[Harmony Engine]] can move, impact, shape, and connect [[Tectonic Islands]]. The device contains [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company#Weavesilk|Weavesilk]] within a cube structure, allowing it to see and align entering and exiting [[Ley Lines]] similarly to how [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|GSC]] sails function.

The group deduced that the engine represents the culmination of years of secret work. [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] theorized that since [[House Aurum]] is in charge of weaponry, the engine could be used as a weapon of mass destruction by colliding one tectonic island into another. He resolved to bring the notes to [[Professor Jitterhop]] for further analysis.

## Loot & Acquisitions

- **The [[Harmony Engine]]**: An arcanotech device capable of moving and shaping tectonic islands - perhaps much more.
