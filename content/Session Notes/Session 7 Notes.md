## Summary
### The Crash Landing

The party traveled aboard [[Lancaster Slickwhistle]]'s escape ship, which experienced flickering magical energy as his arcane focus, a multi-layered inscribed orb worn on his wrist, stopped channeling power. During the flight, the party informed [[Lancaster Slickwhistle|Lancaster]] about a mole within their ranks. Due to the depleted energy from the arcane focus, the ship violently crash-landed onto the tavern island.

### The Faction Debate

Surviving the crash, the party reunited with [[Broomhilda Bonebelly]], [[Gnarl Thulu]], and the Djinn Emissary. They relayed that [[Captain Arabella Montclaire-Smythe]] had sold them out and had been supplying [[Resonance Crystals]] to [[House Cuprum]], fully compromising the island. The party presented the [[Harmony Engine]], a device [[Lancaster Slickwhistle|Lancaster]] noted was only half-complete and intended for [[Project Orchestra]], a project [[The Crusade]] seemed heavily invested in. The faction leaders immediately argued over the artifact's fate: [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult of Night]] wished to study it, the [[Blood Horde]] wanted to use it as a weapon in the war, and the [[Ancient Ones]] sought to protect and hide it.

### The Witch's Hut Awakening

When the [[Harmony Engine]] was dropped onto the table, it clicked into the center and began connecting with a presence within the tavern and island. The structure reacted, transforming its interior into a witch's hut, occasionally pulsing into forms of a library and the original tavern. The party discovered the [[Harmony Engine]] acted as a control mechanism, allowing them to move the tectonic island back and forth through the void.

### The Aurum Assault and Escape

The island was suddenly besieged by [[House Aurum]] ships, leading to the destruction of the party's remaining moored vessels. As the enemy fleet, including [[August Aurum]], began boarding, [[Vaxen the Unkillable]] utilized the [[Harmony Engine]] to hurdle a neighboring [[Tectonic Islands|Tectonic Island]] directly toward the attacking ships. 

The Djinn Emissary managed to transport the party to the new island just as the collision occurred, launching their original island away.  [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]], desperate to reclaim the engine, launched a fireball at the group, but the awakened witch's hut absorbed the damage to protect them. The party narrowly escaped, but the battle left [[Broomhilda Bonebelly|Broomhilda]] and [[Gnarl Thulu|Gnarl]] unconscious, the Emissary surrounded, and [[Lancaster Slickwhistle|Lancaster]]'s fate uncertain.
