## Summary

### Bloos's Bad Day
[[Bloos]], a giant humanoid moth, was comfortably eating an oversized sweater when a deep, unnatural mechanical rumble shook the earth. A blinding light appeared near the [[Sun Spire]], emitting a blinking white glow. As the light pulsed, [[Bloos|Bloos's]] island fell apart, the rock and dirt turning into a glowing slurry of raw earth and arcane energy. Terrified and clutching his meal, [[Bloos]] frantically took flight into the void just before his home was completely destroyed.
### Taking Watch

Following the party's narrow escape from [[Duke Ephraim Aurum]], the party attempted to determine where they were and how they could use the [[Harmony Engine]] to shift and navigate the [[Tectonic Islands|Tectonic Island]] they found themselves on. [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] and [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] both got a feeling that this task may be beyond them. [[Otis Thulu|Otis]], remembering a fable from his childhood, gave the [[Harmony Engine]] to the damaged Witch's Hut behind them.

The party determined to rest and recover from the previous battles. During the first watch, [[Vase the Fragile|Vase Argilla]] confronted [[Collin McCambridge]] about why the [[House Aurum|Aurum]] weapons did not damage him. [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] explained that he grew up serving the children of [[House Aurum]]. Once his magical abilities were discovered, his father and brother were killed, and he was forcibly conscripted into their arcane experiments before he was able to make his escape. Though [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] could sense that [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] was still not telling the whole truth, he was satisfied that [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] was not the mole within [[The Resistance]].

During the second watch, [[Twilight Emberrest]] reflected on whether their wife, [[Dawn]], was safe with a possible mole in [[Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night|The Cult]]. Meanwhile, [[Cletus Ironplow|Cletus]] reviewed the notes recovered from the [[Sun Spire]]. He concluded that fully comprehending the [[Harmony Engine]] would require an expert in the field of [[The Terramancers|Terramancy]], noting the documents lacked any instructions on how a user might physically navigate with the device.
### The Metamorphosis and the Arrival

The party's newly acquired [[Lairs|Lair]] completed its structural transformation. Shifting away from the form of a witch's hut, the building stabilized into a towering magical library, miraculously generating bedrooms for each member of the group.

As the party explored the tower, [[Vaxen the Unkillable]] had a vulnerable moment with [[Otis Thulu|Otis]], sharing that he may not be as eager to die as he was before. This conversation was interrupted when [[Bloos]] crash-landed into the bushes outside the new tower. Weeping and hungrily consuming a sweater, the moth was brought inside by [[Otis Thulu]].

During this time, Collin attempted to open the puzzle cube he recovered from [[Duke Ephraim Aurum|Duke Ephraim's]] vault - failing to do so, he was briefly exposed to the aura of a Resonance Crystal and struck with a sharp, agonizing pain. As [[Otis Thulu|Otis]] opened the door to [[Collin McCambridge|Collin's]] bedroom to inform him about [[Bloos]], he noticed [[Collin McCambridge|Collin's]] eyes briefly turn golden.

### Charting the Course

[[Bloos]] became instantly mesmerized by the light of the [[Harmony Engine]]. In a trance-like state, he explained that he was a human druid cursed two centuries ago, and recounted the recent destruction of his island by an unknown force at the [[Sun Spire]]. He also informed the party that their new [[Tectonic Islands|Tectonic Island]] was currently sitting stationary, sitting directly atop a cross of [[Ley Lines]] that he was able to see due to his sensitivity to light. The group realized that their [[Lairs|Lair]] was able to connect ot the [[Harmony Engine]] and navigate it, following the magical tethers of the [[Ley Lines]]. After a debate regarding their next steps, the party commanded the [[Lairs|Lair]] to take them toward the [[Monastery of Night]].

During this time, [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] and [[Vase the Fragile|Vase]] snuck away to [[Collin McCambridge|Collin's]] room. There, [[Collin McCambridge|Collin]] revealed [[Duke Ephraim Aurum|Ephraim's]] puzzle cub and enlisted [[Vase the Fragile|Vase's]] assistance in opening it. The session concluded precisely as the exterior gave way, exposing what lay inside...

## New NPCs

- [[Bloos]]: A cursed, giant humanoid moth and former druid whose island was recently destroyed by a mysterious light near the [[Sun Spire]]. Eats sweaters and easily mesmerized by bright lights.
