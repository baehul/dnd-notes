## Summary

### The Puzzle Box and the Ring

Collin McCambridge experienced a profound vision and successfully opened the infernal puzzle box he had acquired. Inside the box, he discovered a mysterious ring. Afterward, Collin and Cletus Ironplow reviewed the recovered notes regarding the Harmony Engine, during which Collin demonstrated having specific insights into Duke Ephraim Aurum.

### Life in the Library

During their downtime, Vase the Fragile and Twilight Emberrest discussed a copy of the Cult's book that had manifested within the magical library. Otis Thulu expressed feeling disconnected from the World Tree, though he noted a growing, special connection to the library itself. Meanwhile, Cletus and Otis started competing gardens on the island.

### Restless Dreams

Collin spent a night wearing the newly acquired ring and experienced a strange encounter in his sleep, which left him exhausted the following day. He spent a second night with the ring and encountered the entity again, though he struggled to understand its language. During this second interaction, the entity established itself as an enemy of House Aurum.

### Arrival at the Monastery

The party discussed their plans regarding the Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night. Their mobile island approached and physically docked with the mainland of the Monastery of Night. After traveling overland for some time, the group encountered a mushroom farmer heading toward the University, driving a cart pulled by a giant star-nosed mole. The party then officially arrived at the Monastery.

## New NPCs

- **Mushroom Farmer**: A local encountered on the way to the University, driving a cart pulled by a giant star-nosed mole.
    

## New Locations

- **Monastery of Night Mainland**: The landmass surrounding the headquarters of the Cult of the Goddess Whose Name Is Night, where the party's island physically docked.
    

## Loot & Acquisitions

- **Mysterious Ring**: A ring recovered by Collin McCambridge after opening the infernal puzzle box.
