**The Universal Constant**, also referred to as **The Law Of Threes**, refers to the pervasive metaphysical, geological, and cultural phenomenon wherein the number three acts as the fundamental stabilizing force of the universe. It is widely accepted by scholars, arcanists, and theologians not as a mere superstition, but as an observable axiom of reality, similar to gravity or the passage of time.
# Metaphysical Manifestations
The stability of the number three is most visibly observed in the physical structure of the world following [[The Shattering of the World]].
- **Geological Structure:** The world fractured and reorganized itself into exactly three distinct [[Strata]]: the [[Upper Crust]], the [[In-Between Isles]], and the [[Underworld]].
- **Temporal Event:** [[The Shattering of the World|The Shattering]] itself, which reshaped reality, occurred over a period of exactly three days.
# Arcane Mechanics
Arcane theory holds that numbers divisible by three represent stability, flow, and completion.
- **Spellcasting:** Successful spellcasting generally requires verbal, somatic, and material components arranged in triads.
- **Magical Stability:** Rituals intended to be permanent are invariably cast in cycles of three to ensure they bind correctly to the broken [[ley lines]]. As an example, the wards on [[Golden Sails Trading & Shipping Company|Golden Sails]] ships are cast and enchanted over the course of three days.
# Culture and Superstition
The Universal Constant deeply influences daily life, architecture, and etiquette across the [[In-Between Isles]] and the [[Upper Crust]].
## Superstitions:
- **The Good News, The Bad News, and One More Thing**: Whenever sharing news with someone, one should always give three pieces of new. Only giving two pieces of news is unlucky as it is incomplete and it invites bad news.
- **Always Travel By Three:** Traveling in groups of four is considered profoundly bad luck, viewed as inviting one of the members to befall a tragedy causing them to leave the group. Groups of three or a number divisible by three are considered to be good luck.
- **Powers of Three:** While any number divisible by three is considered lucky, powers of three (3, 9, 27, etc.) are viewed as the most powerful and holy numbers, representing exponential stability.
## Architecture
- **Triangular Design:** Architecture in both the [[Upper Crust]] and the [[In-Between Isles]] favors the triangle over the square. Arches, windows, and doorways are typically triangular or pointed arches. Right angles are often avoided in sacred or important structures, as the square (4 sides) implies stagnation or bad luck.
